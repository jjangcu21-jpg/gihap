300. Which of the following is NOT a violation of the special protection duty for school buses (on a one-lane road)?

  1. ①Passing through without stopping when children or toddlers are getting off.
  2. ②Slowing down and passing through before reaching the bus, when approaching from the opposite direction.
  3. ③Blowing the horn and passing through while children or toddlers are getting on or off.
  4. ④Not overtaking a school bus displaying the child transport sign while driving.
Show answer

Answer
④ Not overtaking a school bus displaying the child transport sign while driving.

Reference
Article 51 of the Road Traffic Act (Special Protection of Children's School Buses) ① When a children's school bus is stopped on the road and devices such as flashing lights are in operation to indicate that children or infants are getting on and off, the driver of a car traveling in the lane where the children's school bus is stopped and the lane right next to the lane must stop temporarily before reaching the children's school bus, check for safety, and then drive slowly. ② In the case of paragraph 1, on roads without a center line or on roads with one lane in each direction, drivers of cars traveling in the opposite direction must also stop temporarily and check for safety before reaching the children's school bus and then drive slowly. ③ Drivers of any vehicle may not pass a children's school bus traveling on the road with a sign indicating that it is carrying children or infants.
