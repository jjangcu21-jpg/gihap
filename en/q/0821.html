821. You plan to make a left turn toward the Business City and Terminal. What are the two safest ways to proceed in the given situation?

  1. ①Switch to the first lane in advance to prepare for the upcoming left turn as instructed by the navigation.
  2. ②As lanes 1 and 2 go into an underpass, let the vehicle to your rear pass, and then safely change to lane 3.
  3. ③Use the SATNAV repeatedly to confirm the route.
  4. ④Quickly change lanes without signaling since your vehicle is ahead of the vehicle to your rear.
  5. ⑤Maintain a safe distance in case the vehicle ahead of you brakes suddenly.
Show answer

Answer
② As lanes 1 and 2 go into an underpass, let the vehicle to your rear pass, and then safely change to lane 3.
⑤ Maintain a safe distance in case the vehicle ahead of you brakes suddenly.

Reference
Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act [Appendix Table 28] You should refrain from operating the navigation system while driving as this will divert your attention and increase the risk of an accident. In addition, it is helpful to develop the habit of checking road signs while driving rather than relying solely on the navigation system to ensure safe driving.
