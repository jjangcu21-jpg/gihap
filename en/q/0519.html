519. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, the driver of a vehicle is traveling on an expressway without carrying a vehicle breakdown sign (safety tripod sign) inside the vehicle. What will happen?

  1. ①An administrative fine of KRW 20,000 will be charged.
  2. ②A traffic penalty of KRW 30,000 will be imposed.
  3. ③A criminal fine of up to KRW 300,000 will be charged.
  4. ④No fine or penalty will be imposed.
Show answer

Answer
① An administrative fine of KRW 20,000 will be charged.

Reference
Under item 12 of Appendix 6 to the Road Traffic Act Enforcement Decree, drivers of passenger cars, buses/vans, etc. who violate the requirements for expressways in Article 67(2) of the Act face an administrative fine of 20,000 won.
