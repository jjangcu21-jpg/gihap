886. What does this safety sign signify?

  1. ①A roundabout ahead
  2. ②Look out for U-turning and left-turning vehicles
  3. ③An intersection without traffic signals
  4. ④A left-bending road
Show answer

Answer
① A roundabout ahead

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules Annex 6, Caution Sign 109 Rotational intersection sign installed on the right side of the road 30 to 120 meters before the intersection.
