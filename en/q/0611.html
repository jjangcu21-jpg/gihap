611. Which of the following is the safest method of driving a loaded dump truck when going down a hill?

  1. ①Save fuel by driving in neutral gear.
  2. ②Because stepping on the brake pedal several times does not produce the desired braking effect, step on it all at once.
  3. ③Maintain adequate distance from the vehicle driving ahead to guard against sudden stopping.
  4. ④Honk loudly and increase speed.
Show answer

Answer
③ Maintain adequate distance from the vehicle driving ahead to guard against sudden stopping.

Reference
In a loaded dump truck, there is a risk that the brake system may rupture due to the weight of the load, so you must maintain a low gear, use the engine brake, and apply the brake pedal frequently to minimize strain on the brake system and maintain a sufficient safety distance.
