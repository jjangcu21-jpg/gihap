15. What are TWO inappropriate explanations for the "Good Driving Mileage" system?

  1. ①It is a system that provides practical incentives to those who comply with and practice traffic laws.
  2. ②If a driver receives a suspension order, the penalty can be deducted from the accumulated points.
  3. ③Those who have not paid the traffic penalty or administrative fine can participate in the no-violation, no-accident pledge of the mileage system.
  4. ④In case of causing a traffic accident or violating traffic laws during the pledge period, the person cannot take the pledge again.
Show answer

Answer
③ Those who have not paid the traffic penalty or administrative fine can participate in the no-violation, no-accident pledge of the mileage system.
④ In case of causing a traffic accident or violating traffic laws during the pledge period, the person cannot take the pledge again.

Reference
Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act, Appendix 28: When a driver is subject to license suspension, the earned mileage points may be deducted from accumulated penalty points. Any license holder may join the program, but a person with unpaid traffic or administrative fines may not make the pledge. A new pledge is allowed even after a traffic accident or traffic-law violation during the pledge period (Notice on Standards for Awarding Driver's License Preferential Points).
