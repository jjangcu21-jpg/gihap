184. The driver of a passenger car who commits a violation of a traffic signal between ( ) and ( ) in a school zone receives double the demerit points. Which of the following belongs in the pair of brackets?

  1. ①6 AM, 6 PM
  2. ②7 AM, 7 PM
  3. ③8 AM, 8 PM
  4. ④9 AM, 9 PM
Show answer

Answer
③ 8 AM, 8 PM

Reference
Article 12 (Designation, cancellation and management of child protection zones) Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix Table 28] Standards for cancellation and suspension of driver's license 4. Notwithstanding the above table, penalty points specified in the relevant items shall be imposed on drivers who commit the following violations between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. in child protection zones and protection zones for the elderly and the disabled. Violation that falls under any of subparagraphs 1 and 3: 120 points B. Violations falling under any of subparagraphs 3-2, 9, 14, 15, or 25 (excluding Article 27, Paragraph 7 of the Act): Double the penalty points imposed for violations under the relevant subparagraph.
