725. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①It is safe to travel in a clockwise direction at a roundabout.
  2. ②When entering a roundabout, slow down or come to a complete stop before entering.
  3. ③Within a roundabout, a vehicle that is already inside the roundabout must yield the right of way to a vehicle entering the roundabout.
  4. ④Proceed without turning on the turn signal when exiting a roundabout.
  5. ⑤If a vehicle is signaling to enter a roundabout, the vehicle behind it must not obstruct the progress of the front vehicle.
Show answer

Answer
② When entering a roundabout, slow down or come to a complete stop before entering.
⑤ If a vehicle is signaling to enter a roundabout, the vehicle behind it must not obstruct the progress of the front vehicle.

Reference
At roundabouts, traffic must proceed counterclockwise. When entering a roundabout, you must slow down or stop temporarily, and if there is another vehicle already proceeding, you must give way to that vehicle. If there is a car signaling with a hand, direction indicator, or light to pass through a roundabout, the driver of the car behind that signal must not impede the progress of the vehicle ahead that gave the signal. When entering or exiting a roundabout, turn signal lights must be turned on (Article 25-2 of the Road Traffic Act).
