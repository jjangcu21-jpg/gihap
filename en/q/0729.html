729. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Turn on the left-turn signal, maintain a safe distance, and exit to the right when appropriate.
  2. ②Watch out for the traffic ahead and behind you as you exit the off-ramp.
  3. ③If you miss the off-ramp, reverse the vehicle and try to get back to the intended route.
  4. ④You need not use the turn signal if there are no vehicles on the off-ramp.
  5. ⑤Increase the distance between you and the car ahead to avoid skidding.
Show answer

Answer
② Watch out for the traffic ahead and behind you as you exit the off-ramp.
⑤ Increase the distance between you and the car ahead to avoid skidding.

Reference
The driver of any vehicle must not change the course of the vehicle if there is a risk of interfering with the normal passage of other vehicles traveling in the direction being changed (Road Traffic Act, Article 19, Paragraph 3). If a driver intends to change course, he or she must signal until the action is completed using a turn signal lamp. (Article 38, Paragraph 1 of the Road Traffic Act). You must not change lanes suddenly, ensure a sufficient safe distance, and turn on the turn signal in the direction you wish to advance before proceeding.
