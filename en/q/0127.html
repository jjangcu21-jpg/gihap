127. Which TWO statements are correct regarding penalties imposed on drunk drivers?

  1. ①Fully inebriated drivers with a blood alcohol concentration of 0.08% or higher will have their driver's license revoked and receive punishment in accordance with the law.
  2. ②Those who refuse to take the alcohol breathalyzer test or who have caused fatalities or injuries while driving with a blood alcohol concentration of 0.03% or higher will have their driver's license revoked and receive punishment in accordance with the law.
  3. ③Those who are caught driving with a blood alcohol concentration of at least 0.03 percent but less than 0.08 percent will have their driver's license suspended for 120 days and receive punishment in accordance with the law.
  4. ④In a case wherein the driver causes an accident for the first time resulting in property damage while driving with a blood alcohol concentration of at least 0.03 percent but less than 0.08 percent, his/her driver’s license is revoked.
Show answer

Answer
① Fully inebriated drivers with a blood alcohol concentration of 0.08% or higher will have their driver's license revoked and receive punishment in accordance with the law.
② Those who refuse to take the alcohol breathalyzer test or who have caused fatalities or injuries while driving with a blood alcohol concentration of 0.03% or higher will have their driver's license revoked and receive punishment in accordance with the law.

Reference
In the case of simple drunk driving with a blood alcohol concentration of 0.03 percent or more but less than 0.08 percent, the driver's license will be suspended for 100 days and criminal punishment will be imposed. If a drunk driver with a blood alcohol concentration of 0.03 percent or more causes a traffic accident resulting in personal injury, the driver's license will be revoked.
