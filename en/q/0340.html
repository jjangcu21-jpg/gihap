340. Which of the following is in the correct range of traffic penalties and demerit points (excluding surcharges) imposed on a car driver who drives above 60 km/h at around 15:00 in a silver zone?

  1. ①KRW 60,000, 60 points
  2. ②KRW 90,000, 60 points
  3. ③KRW 120,000, 120 points
  4. ④KRW 150,000, 120 points
Show answer

Answer

Reference
