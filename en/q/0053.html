53. Which of the following is incorrect regarding provisions on autonomous driving systems?

  1. ①The Road Traffic Act stipulates that “driving” does not include the use of an autonomous driving system in accordance with the original use of motor vehicles and horses.
  2. ②The prohibition of using a mobile phone does not apply to drivers driving a vehicle operated under an autonomous driving system.
  3. ③The traffic penalty for a driver who fails to respond promptly to the take-over request from the autonomous driving system is a fee of KRW 40,000.
  4. ④An “autonomous driving system” encompasses a variety of automated apparatus, software, and peripheral devices that allow a vehicle to move by recognizing and judging the surroundings and road information without the manipulation of the driver or a passenger.
Show answer

Answer
① The Road Traffic Act stipulates that “driving” does not include the use of an autonomous driving system in accordance with the original use of motor vehicles and horses.

Reference
Article 2, Paragraph 26 and Article 56-2 of the Road Traffic Act, and Appendix 8 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act. No. 38-3 “Driving” means using a vehicle, horse or tram on the road according to its original method of use (including using a steering or autonomous driving system). The driver of a car equipped with an autonomous driving system that does not correspond to a fully autonomous driving system must drive by directly operating the steering, braking, and other devices in response to the direct driving request of the autonomous driving system without delay. When a driver drives using an autonomous driving system, the provisions of Article 49 (1) 10, 11, and 11-2 do not apply. Article 2, Paragraph 1, Item 2 of the Act on Promotion and Support of Commercialization of Autonomous Vehicles “Autonomous driving system” refers to automation equipment, software, and all devices related thereto that enable the vehicle to operate by recognizing and judging surrounding conditions and road information, etc., without driver or passenger intervention.
