557. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following cannot be regarded as a mobility aid chair vehicle (i.e. a vehicle with a width of no more than 1 meter)?

  1. ①A manual wheelchair
  2. ②An electric wheelchair
  3. ③A mobility scooter
  4. ④An electric bicycle
Show answer

Answer
④ An electric bicycle

Reference
Article 2 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act (Mechanisms and Devices Excluded from Cars and Horses) ① In Article 2, Paragraph 10 and Item 17, Item 5) of the Road Traffic Act (hereinafter referred to as the “Act”), “Mechanisms and devices prescribed by the Ordinance of the Ministry of the Interior and Safety, such as strollers, wheelchairs for walking assistance, and walkers for the elderly” refers to the following devices and devices with a width of 1 meter or less. 1. Baby stroller 2. Chair car for walking assistance (referring to manual wheelchairs, electric wheelchairs, and medical scooters in accordance with the standards for medical devices determined by the Minister of Food and Drug Safety pursuant to Article 19 of the Medical Device Act) 3. Walkers for the elderly 4. Play equipment (limited to those used by children) pursuant to Article 11 (3) of the Act 5. Non-powered carts 6. Two-wheeled vehicles, A motorized bicycle or bicycle that the driver dismounts and pulls or carries to travel. 7. Mechanisms and devices used for road repair and maintenance, road construction, etc. (limited to those that do not carry people or transport cargo)
