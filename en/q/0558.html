558. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following statements regarding novice drivers is correct?

  1. ①Novice drivers are defined only for Class 1 regular licenses.
  2. ②Refers to someone who has received only a motorized bicycle license for which less than 2 years have passed since the date of issuance.
  3. ③If a driver's license is revoked before 2 years have passed after the date of issuing the first driver's license and then reissued, the record of driving before the revocation is added to the driver's driving history.
  4. ④A person with a driver’s license issued no more than two years previosuly is considered a novice driver.
Show answer

Answer
④ A person with a driver’s license issued no more than two years previosuly is considered a novice driver.

Reference
Article 2, Paragraph 27 of the Road Traffic Act, “Novice driver” refers to a person for whom less than two years have passed since the date of first receiving a driver’s license (if the driver’s license was canceled before two years have passed since the date of first receiving the driver’s license, this refers to the date of receiving the driver’s license again). In this case, if a person who has only received a motorcycle license receives a driver's license other than a motorcycle license, he or she is deemed to have received a driver's license for the first time.
