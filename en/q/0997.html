997. Which of the following correctly describes the driver's law-abiding driving behavior as seen in the video? (Refer to the website)

Official video ↗
  1. ①The driver turned on the headlights.
  2. ②The driver safely overtook the other vehicle.
  3. ③The driver observed the speed ​reduction standards for abnormal weather conditions.
  4. ④The driver drove slowly to avoid splashing water onto the pedestrians on the side.
Show answer

Answer
① The driver turned on the headlights.

Reference
Drivers must obey the speeds prescribed by Article 17 of the Road Traffic Act on wet roads. In adverse weather, reduce the maximum speed by 20% when rain makes the road wet, and by 50% when heavy rain, snow or fog reduces visibility to 100 m or less. The driver in the video was speeding. Article 13 requires keeping to the right, but the driver traveled left of the center line. The driver also violated the duty to avoid splashing standing water onto pedestrians.
