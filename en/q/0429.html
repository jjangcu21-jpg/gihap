429. Two vehicles are driving in the rain, and the rear vehicle sees the front vehicle stopping and applies the brakes. Which of the following are NOT correct? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Hard braking can cause the vehicle to nose dive due to increased friction between the road surface and tires.
  2. ②Free running distance is increased due to reduced friction of the road surface.
  3. ③Aquaplaning and partial braking may occur simultaneously, causing the vehicle to slide out of the lane.
  4. ④The higher the tire wear rate, the shorter the braking distance.
Show answer

Answer
② Free running distance is increased due to reduced friction of the road surface.
④ The higher the tire wear rate, the shorter the braking distance.

Reference
① During sudden braking, the vehicle leans forward due to friction between the tires and the road surface. ② On rainy roads, the braking distance becomes longer as the friction between the tires and the road surface decreases. ③ Hydroplaning and biased braking may occur, causing the steering direction to change and causing the driver to deviate from the lane. ④ As a car’s tires wear out, the braking distance becomes longer.
