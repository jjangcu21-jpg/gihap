754. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Since there are no vehicles in the direction of travel, you can stop on the right side of the road.
  2. ②Even if you are driving in a school zone, you need not observe the speed limit if no children are present.
  3. ③Observe the maximum speed limit indicated by the safety sign as you drive.
  4. ④Drive with caution and watch your surroundings, as children may suddenly jump into the road.
  5. ⑤You should speed up and drive as fast as possible to exit school zones.
Show answer

Answer
③ Observe the maximum speed limit indicated by the safety sign as you drive.
④ Drive with caution and watch your surroundings, as children may suddenly jump into the road.

Reference
Since it is within a child protection zone, the maximum speed must be within 30 km/h, and when you spot a child, you must pay attention to the child's movements and look ahead. Accidents in child protection zones are caused by failure to follow laws, such as failure to drive safely, violation of pedestrian protection obligations, illegal parking and stopping, and traffic signal violations. When pedestrians cross, the driver must stop, wait until they finish crossing, check that it is safe, and then proceed.
