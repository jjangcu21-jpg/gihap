581. Which of the following is NOT an advisable practice for cyclists (excluding electric bicycles)?

  1. ①A child riding a bicycle on a sidewalk
  2. ②Cycling on a sidewalk with a safety sign indicating that cyclists are allowed
  3. ③Cycling on a sidewalk because the road is damaged
  4. ④Cycling along the middle of the road as there are no cars around
Show answer

Answer
④ Cycling along the middle of the road as there are no cars around

Reference
In cases ①, ②, and ③, you can pass on the sidewalk. (Article 13-2, Paragraph 4 of the Road Traffic Act)
