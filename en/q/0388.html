388. Which of the following are the correct methods of turning right at an intersection on a road with three lanes in one direction? (Select TWO)

  1. ①When turning right, you should turn on the turning signal at the intersection just before proceeding to indicate your intended direction of travel.
  2. ②Even if the cross light at the right-side crosswalk is green, you can proceed if there are no pedestrians.
  3. ③If the right-turn signal is red, you cannot proceed even if there are no pedestrians.
  4. ④It is safer to turn right from the second lane on a road with three lanes in one direction.
Show answer

Answer
② Even if the cross light at the right-side crosswalk is green, you can proceed if there are no pedestrians.
③ If the right-turn signal is red, you cannot proceed even if there are no pedestrians.

Reference
When turning right at an intersection, even if the crosswalk pedestrian traffic light on the right side of the road is green, you can pass as long as it does not interfere with pedestrian traffic. However, if a vehicle auxiliary traffic light is installed on the side of the pedestrian traffic light, passing when the auxiliary traffic light is red may constitute a traffic violation, so it cannot pass. If a pedestrian is on the crosswalk, even if the vehicle and the pedestrian are far away, the vehicle cannot pass because it interferes with pedestrian traffic.
