37. Which of the following is correct regarding the obligations of a driver who has obtained a conditional driver’s license upon installing an ignition interlock device under the Road Traffic Act?

  1. ①A person who has obtained a conditional driver’s license upon installing an ignition interlock device and who drives without registering it with the commissioner of the city or police agency of his/her municipality will have his/her license suspended.
  2. ②A person who has driven a vehicle without an ignition interlock device or with such device that fails to meet the installation standards shall be ordered to take corrective measures within one month.
  3. ③A person who drives a motor vehicle with an ignition interlock device, etc. knowing that the device is less effective as it was dismantled, tampered with, or otherwise for maintenance will have his/her license suspended.
  4. ④After the period of license disqualification due to drunk driving, a person may only drive a vehicle with an ignition interlock device from the time of obtaining a conditional driver’s license upon installing an ignition interlock device.
Show answer

Answer
④ After the period of license disqualification due to drunk driving, a person may only drive a vehicle with an ignition interlock device from the time of obtaining a conditional driver’s license upon installing an ignition interlock device.

Reference
Article 50-3 of the Road Traffic Act: Compliance requirements for drivers who have received a conditional driver's license with a drunk driving prevention device, Article 93 (Cancellation/Suspension of Full Driving Permit) of the same Act, Paragraph 1 - If you drive a car, etc. equipped with a drunk driving prevention device without registering it with the city/provincial police office, your license will be revoked. (No. 21) - If you drive a car, etc. that is not equipped with a drunk driving prevention device or is equipped with a drunk driving prevention device that does not meet the installation standards, your license will be revoked. (No. 22) - If you drive a motor vehicle equipped with a drunk driving prevention device knowing that its effectiveness has been reduced by disassembly, manipulation, or other methods for maintenance, your license will be revoked. (No. 23)
