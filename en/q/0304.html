304. Below are descriptions related to school zones. Which of the following is NOT true?

  1. ①Traffic accidents in these areas may be excluded from the special exemption provided under the Special Traffic Accident Act.
  2. ②The speed limit for vehicles can be set at 30 km/h or less.
  3. ③The traffic penalty and demerit points are three times higher than those on general roads.
  4. ④Parking and stopping are prohibited.
Show answer

Answer
③ The traffic penalty and demerit points are three times higher than those on general roads.

Reference
Article 12, Paragraph 1 of the Road Traffic Act, Article 3, Paragraph 2, Item 11 of the Special Act on Traffic Accidents, and Annex Table 10 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act. Illegal acts and amounts committed in child protection zones and elderly and disabled protection zones.
