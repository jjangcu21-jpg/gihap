820. Which of the following are the TWO safest ways to proceed in the given situation?

  1. ①Maintain a greater following distance than usual.
  2. ②Apply the brakes very hard before entering the tunnel to slow down in advance.
  3. ③Since there is no snow inside the tunnel, accelerate while driving through it.
  4. ④Drive along the tire tracks of the vehicle in front, as snowy roads are very slippery.
  5. ⑤Shift to neutral to prevent skidding.
Show answer

Answer
① Maintain a greater following distance than usual.
④ Drive along the tire tracks of the vehicle in front, as snowy roads are very slippery.

Reference
In order to drive safely on snowy roads, you must avoid sudden braking, rapid acceleration, or sharp steering, and ensure a sufficient safety distance. Additionally, to prevent skidding, it is advisable to drive following the wheel tracks of the car in front and use a low gear whenever possible. In particular, tunnel sections, including tunnel entrances and exits, have a high risk of freezing, so operation must be slowed down.
