385. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is NOT a correct statement regarding the speed of motor vehicles, etc.?

  1. ①Separate legal speed limits are applied to various roads depending on whether it is a general road, an automobile road, or an expressway, and on the total number of lanes.
  2. ②There are no minimum speed limits on regular roads.
  3. ③You must drive at a reduced speed in poor weather conditions.
  4. ④If the speed limit set by the variable speed limit sign and the speed limit set by a speed limit safety sign are different, the latter must be followed.
Show answer

Answer
④ If the speed limit set by the variable speed limit sign and the speed limit set by a speed limit safety sign are different, the latter must be followed.

Reference
Variable speed limit signs must be followed.
