804. You are driving straight at 30 km/h. Which of the following are the TWO safest ways to drive?

  1. ①Drive through the crosswalk at no more than 30 km/h to avoid collision with the parked cars.
  2. ②Slow down as you approach and stop at the stop line just before the crosswalk.
  3. ③There are no pedestrians on the crosswalk, so proceed at 30 km/h.
  4. ④Keep driving as there are no pedestrians on the crosswalk.
  5. ⑤Drive slowly while paying attention to vehicles entering the road from the right.
Show answer

Answer
② Slow down as you approach and stop at the stop line just before the crosswalk.
⑤ Drive slowly while paying attention to vehicles entering the road from the right.

Reference
The location of the presented situation is a children's protection area in a residential area. When entering a crosswalk without traffic lights in a child protection zone, you must stop before entering, regardless of whether pedestrians are crossing. In particular, if there are cars parked near the crosswalk, pedestrians trying to cross cannot be seen, so even if you plan to pass slowly after stopping, you must be prepared for pedestrians to enter. Drivers of vehicles attempting to enter the road from a place other than the road must first stop, but some drivers enter the road forcibly. Therefore, if you see a car trying to enter the road from a place other than the road, you must drive slowly and be careful.
