798. Which of the following are the safe ways to drive when a vehicle is trying to head in the 12 o’clock direction in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Stop to yield to the passenger vehicle about to enter the roundabout.
  2. ②Turn on the left-turn signal and enter the truck apron.
  3. ③Turn on the right-turn signal and drive in the 12 o’clock direction.
  4. ④If the vehicle misses the chance to get out from the roundabout, it should circle around one more time to exit in the right direction.
  5. ⑤The vehicle is trying to head in the 12 o'clock direction, so turn signals are not required.
Show answer

Answer
③ Turn on the right-turn signal and drive in the 12 o’clock direction.
④ If the vehicle misses the chance to get out from the roundabout, it should circle around one more time to exit in the right direction.

Reference
Article 25-2 of the Road Traffic Act (Roadway through roundabouts) ① Drivers of all vehicles must travel counterclockwise at roundabouts. ② Drivers of all vehicles must slow down or stop temporarily when entering a roundabout, and if there is another vehicle already proceeding, they must give way to that vehicle. ③ In accordance with paragraphs 1 and 2, if there is a car signaling with a hand, direction indicator, or light to pass through a roundabout, the driver of the car behind that signal shall not impede the progress of the car in front that has signaled. Article 38 of the Road Traffic Act (Vehicle Signals) ① Drivers of all vehicles must signal with their hands, turn indicators, or lights when turning left, turning right, crossing, U-turning, moving slowly, stopping or reversing, or when attempting to change course while proceeding in the same direction, or when entering or exiting a roundabout, with their hand, direction indicator, or light until the action is completed. If a passenger car or truck tries to force its way in without giving way, it should refrain from advancing toward 12 o'clock and then turn 360 degrees counterclockwise and advance toward 12 o'clock.
