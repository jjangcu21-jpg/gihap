161. Which of the following is NOT true about pedestrian-first roads?

  1. ①On a pedestrian-first road, pedestrians can only walk along the right edge of the road.
  2. ②Drivers are obligated to exercise various forms of pedestrian protection such as slowing down and coming to a complete stop.
  3. ③A driver who fails to fulfill his/her duty to protect pedestrians is subject to a traffic penalty of KRW 40,000 and a penalty of 10 points based on the standards for passenger vehicles.
  4. ④If deemed necessary for pedestrian protection, the chief of a police station can limit the vehicle speed to 20 km/h or less.
Show answer

Answer
① On a pedestrian-first road, pedestrians can only walk along the right edge of the road.

Reference
Pedestrians can travel on all parts of the road on the pedestrian priority road. In this case, pedestrians must not intentionally obstruct the progress of cars and horses (Road Traffic Act, Article 8, Paragraph 3, No. 1). Drivers of cars and horses permitted to pass on pedestrian-only roads must drive their cars or horses at the walking speed of pedestrians or temporarily stop so as not to endanger pedestrians or impede their passage (Article 28, Paragraph 3 of the Road Traffic Act). If the city/provincial police chief or police chief deems it necessary to protect pedestrians on a pedestrian priority road, the speed of cars and horses may be limited to 20 kilometers per hour (Road Traffic Act, Article 28-2).
