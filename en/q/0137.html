137. According to the Road Traffic Act, drivers of ( ) are prohibited from driving their motor vehicles and the like in a row or side by side without good cause and in a manner that endangers other persons and causes danger to traffic. Anyone who violates this provision will be punished by ( ). Choose the correct pair for the parentheses.

  1. ①self-balancing scooters - imprisonment with labor for not more than 1 year or criminal fine of not more than KRW 5 million
  2. ②two-wheeled vehicles - imprisonment with labor for not more than 6 months or criminal fine of not more than KRW 3 million
  3. ③special-purpose vehicles -imprisonment with labor for not more than 2 years or criminal fine of not more than KRW 5 million
  4. ④motorcycles - imprisonment with labor for not more than 6 months or criminal fine of not more than KRW 3 million
Show answer

Answer
③ special-purpose vehicles -imprisonment with labor for not more than 2 years or criminal fine of not more than KRW 5 million

Reference
Article 46 (Prohibition of Joint Dangerous Activities) Paragraph 1 of the Road Traffic Act: Drivers of automobiles, etc. (excluding personal mobility devices) shall not cause harm to others or create traffic hazards by two or more drivers jointly driving two or more vehicles in a line, one behind another or side by side, on the road without justifiable reasons. Additionally, you may be punished by imprisonment for up to 2 years or by a fine of up to 5 million won. Electric two-wheeled parallel vehicles are personal mobility devices and are not subject to this provision above. Article 2, Paragraph 21 of the Road Traffic Act: “Cars, etc.” refers to automobiles and motorized bicycles. Article 2, Paragraph 18 of the Road Traffic Act: “Motor vehicle” refers to a vehicle that is driven using a motor rather than using a railroad or constructed line (a towed vehicle is also considered a part of a vehicle) and refers to the following vehicles. go. The following vehicles according to Article 3 of the Automobile Management Act. However, motorized bicycles are excluded. 1) Passenger car 2) Van 3) Truck 4) Special vehicle 5) Two-wheeled vehicle B. Construction machinery in accordance with the proviso to Article 26 (1) of the Construction Machinery Management Act
