Road signs · 2 pts

945. What does this road surface marking indicate?

  1. ①Vehicles must yield.
  2. ②There are obstacles on the road surface.
  3. ③Vehicles must not stop inside the markings.
  4. ④Parking is allowed.
Show answer

Answer
③ Vehicles must not stop inside the markings.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules, Appendix 6, road marking 524: Keep-clear area. Prohibits stopping within a marked area in a plaza, the center of an intersection, or a similar location.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
