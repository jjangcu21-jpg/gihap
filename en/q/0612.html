612. Which of the following is the correct method of preventing traffic accidents caused by poor cargo loading?

  1. ①Put a cover over the cargo you are carrying.
  2. ②There is no need to check fixed attachments such as spare tires.
  3. ③Keep the cargo tied down loosely so you can get it on and off quickly.
  4. ④There is no need to tie down the cargo if you are not going too far.
Show answer

Answer
① Put a cover over the cargo you are carrying.

Reference
When moving cargo, even if it is a short distance, you must firmly secure the cargo and cover it to prevent it from moving. Before departure, check and confirm whether there are any abnormalities in attachments such as spare tires, and depart after correcting them.
