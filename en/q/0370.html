370. Which of the following vehicles are granted the right of way at a roundabout?

  1. ①Vehicles moving on the section of the road within the roundabout
  2. ②Vehicles trying to turn left before entering the roundabout
  3. ③Vehicles trying to turn right before entering the roundabout
  4. ④Vehicles trying to turn left or right before entering the roundabout
Show answer

Answer
① Vehicles moving on the section of the road within the roundabout

Reference
Article 25-2 of the Road Traffic Act (Roadway through roundabouts) ① Drivers of all vehicles must travel counterclockwise at roundabouts. ② Drivers of all vehicles must slow down or stop temporarily when entering a roundabout, and if there is another vehicle already proceeding, they must give way to that vehicle. ③ In accordance with paragraphs 1 and 2, if there is a car that signals with a hand, direction indicator, or light to pass through a roundabout, the driver of the car behind the car must not obstruct the progress of the car in front that gave the signal.
