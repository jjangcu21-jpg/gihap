57. Which two statements are NOT correct regarding the management of EVs?

  1. ①The valid period for the vehicle inspection of non-commercial passenger vehicles is 6 years.
  2. ②When driving long distances, check and charge the battery in advance.
  3. ③Refrain from accelerating or suddenly stopping immediately after charging.
  4. ④It is more efficient to use an air heater than a heated seat or a heated handle.
Show answer

Answer
① The valid period for the vehicle inspection of non-commercial passenger vehicles is 6 years.
④ It is more efficient to use an air heater than a heated seat or a heated handle.

Reference
① Except for new cars, the validity period of car inspection for non-commercial passenger cars is 2 years (Appendix 15-2 of the Enforcement Rules of the Automobile Management Act). ④ In the case of electric vehicles without an internal combustion engine, a lot of electrical energy is used to operate the heater. Therefore, it is recommended to use heated seats and a heated steering wheel. ② Consider the remaining battery capacity and travel distance to ensure that it does not run out of charge while driving. ③ Immediately after charging, the battery temperature rises. At this time, sudden acceleration and sudden stops consume a lot of electrical energy, reducing battery efficiency.
