529. Which two statements about ignition interlock devices (IIDs) are correct?

  1. ①An “ignition interlock device” is a device that prevents a vehicle from starting if the driver is intoxicated, as prescribed by the Minister of Interior and Safety.
  2. ②Drivers holding a conditional license with IID restrictions must install the device on their vehicle and register it with the provincial or municipal police agency.
  3. ③The period of the device’s installation begins from the date on which the driver’s period of disqualification ends.
  4. ④In cases where a person drives a personal mobility device after drinking, if he/she receives another license revocation for drunk driving within five years, he/she must obtain a conditional driving license requiring the installation of an alcohol ignition interlock device.
Show answer

Answer
① An “ignition interlock device” is a device that prevents a vehicle from starting if the driver is intoxicated, as prescribed by the Minister of Interior and Safety.
② Drivers holding a conditional license with IID restrictions must install the device on their vehicle and register it with the provincial or municipal police agency.

Reference
① Article 2, Paragraph 34 of the Road Traffic Act ② Article 50-3, Paragraph 1 of the Road Traffic Act ③ The attachment period is calculated from the ‘next day’ when the driver’s license disqualification period ends (Article 80-2, Paragraph 2 of the Road Traffic Act). ④ Excluding cases of driving a personal mobility device (Article 80-2, Paragraph 1 of the Road Traffic Act).
