726. Which of the following are the correct ways to change lanes in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①A passenger car driving in the second lane can switch to the first lane.
  2. ②A passenger car driving in the first lane cannot switch to the second lane.
  3. ③You can switch from the entry lane directly to the first lane.
  4. ④A passenger car traveling in the second lane cannot switch to the entry lane.
  5. ⑤Lane changes are allowed in all lanes.
Show answer

Answer
② A passenger car driving in the first lane cannot switch to the second lane.
④ A passenger car traveling in the second lane cannot switch to the entry lane.

Reference
The driver of a car or horse must not change the course of the car or horse in places where safety signs are installed and changing course is specifically prohibited. However, this does not apply if there are obstacles due to damage to the road or road construction (Article 14, Paragraph 5 of the Road Traffic Act). You can change lanes in the white dotted line section, but you must not change lanes in the white solid line section. Also, even when there are double dashed and solid lines, you can only change lanes on the side with the dotted line.
