256. Emergency vehicles should be properly outfitted, and they should convey that they are undertaking an emergency operation by turning on their sirens or flashing lights. Which of the following vehicles is exempted from this obligation?

  1. ①Vehicles cracking down on illegal parking
  2. ②Fire trucks
  3. ③Ambulances
  4. ④Police cars cracking down on violations of speed limit
Show answer

Answer
④ Police cars cracking down on violations of speed limit

Reference
An emergency vehicle must have the structure of an emergency vehicle specified in the standards necessary for the safe operation of automobiles under the Automobile Management Act, and must sound a siren or turn on warning lights when it is intended to be subject to special provisions for priority passage and emergency vehicles and other special provisions stipulated in the law. However, this does not apply to emergency vehicles used to crack down on vehicles that violate speed regulations, and vehicles used for official purposes to provide security for domestic and foreign dignitaries.
