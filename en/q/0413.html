413. Which of the following is the safe method of driving when a vehicle loses control on a frozen road?

  1. ①Steer into the skid.
  2. ②In the case of a car with manual transmission, shift to high gear.
  3. ③Operate the steering wheel in the opposite direction of the skid.
  4. ④Stop the car using the parking brake.
Show answer

Answer
① Steer into the skid.

Reference
When your car skids on an icy road, it is safer to steer the steering wheel in the direction of the skid.
