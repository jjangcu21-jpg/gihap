461. At which point do lanes start decreasing in the zone requiring lane changes for safe passage due to road construction?

  1. ①Starting point of a warning zone
  2. ②Starting point of a transition zone
  3. ③Starting point of a work zone
  4. ④Starting point of a termination zone
Show answer

Answer
② Starting point of a transition zone

Reference
A road work zone consists of advance warning, transition, work, and termination areas. In the transition area, the number of lanes decreases and drivers need to change lanes. For safe passage, change lanes in advance and slow down.
