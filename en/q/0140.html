140. Which of the following is not subject to legal action for reckless driving under the Road Traffic Act?

  1. ①Violating the maximum speed limit
  2. ②Violating a prohibition of crossing the road, making a U-turn, or moving backward
  3. ③Cutting in a lane
  4. ④Honking continuously
Show answer

Answer
③ Cutting in a lane

Reference
Road Traffic Act ① Speed violation under Article 17 Paragraph 3 ② Violation of crossing, U-turn, and reverse prohibition under Article 18 Paragraph 1 ④ Creation of noise without justifiable grounds under Article 49 Paragraph 1 No. 8, and ③ is Article 23, cutting in is not a violation of reckless driving.
