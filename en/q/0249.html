249. Which of the following are the TWO correct methods of turning right safely at an intersection?

  1. ①Use the turn indicator at least 30 m before where you want to turn right.
  2. ②Move into the right-hand lane carefully if there is a solid white line on the road.
  3. ③Turn right after checking your left side to make sure that there are no other vehicles headed in the same direction as you.
  4. ④Turn right quickly while paying attention to the traffic in the area.
Show answer

Answer
① Use the turn indicator at least 30 m before where you want to turn right.
③ Turn right after checking your left side to make sure that there are no other vehicles headed in the same direction as you.

Reference
If you are approaching an intersection and a solid white line is drawn, you must not change course in that section and must turn slowly while paying attention to other traffic. And when turning right, at intersections without traffic lights, you must yield the lane to vehicles that have the right of way.
