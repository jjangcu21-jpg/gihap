920. What does this safety sign signify?

  1. ①Bicycle thoroughfare only
  2. ②A road for bicycles and pedestrians
  3. ③Children must be accompanied by a guardian in a school zone
  4. ④A crossing for bicycles
Show answer

Answer
② A road for bicycles and pedestrians

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 6] Instruction sign No. 303 This is a bicycle/pedestrian combined road sign and is installed on both sides of the necessary points within the section or location of the bicycle/pedestrian combined road.
