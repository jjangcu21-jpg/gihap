116. Which of the following drivers is most likely to cause an accident?

  1. ①A driver who is focused only on driving
  2. ②A driver with a habit of sudden starts, sudden braking, and sudden lane changes
  3. ③A driver who keeps a safe distance from bicycles and two-wheeled vehicles
  4. ④A driver who is a patient
Show answer

Answer
② A driver with a habit of sudden starts, sudden braking, and sudden lane changes

Reference
Be considerate of inexperienced drivers and give way to them. Repeated sudden starts, hard braking and abrupt lane changes increase the risk of a crash.
