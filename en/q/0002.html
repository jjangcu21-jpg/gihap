2. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is NOT true about driver's license issuance?

  1. ①A driver’s license must be issued within 30 days of passing the driver's license test.
  2. ②A driver’s license cannot be issued in English.
  3. ③A mobile driver’s license can be issued.
  4. ④A lost driver’s license can be reissued.
Show answer

Answer
② A driver’s license cannot be issued in English.

Reference
Articles 77 to 81 of the Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules and Article 80 of the Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules If a driver's license is lost or has become worn or unusable, it may be reissued by applying to the city/provincial police chief as prescribed by Ordinance of the Ministry of the Interior and Safety.
