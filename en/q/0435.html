435. Which of the following is NOT an appropriate action by a driver while driving on a road during a typhoon accompanied by strong winds and heavy rain?

  1. ①Maintain at least double the normal distance from the car in front, as the braking distance is longer.
  2. ②Upon passing the flooded area, it is better to pass without stopping.
  3. ③Avoid areas with a high risk of flooding such as riversides and streams when parking.
  4. ④It is safe to park a vehicle by a wall or under a large sign.
Show answer

Answer
④ It is safe to park a vehicle by a wall or under a large sign.

Reference
Since the braking distance is longer, keep the distance from the car in front at least twice the usual distance to prevent collision accidents. When passing through a flooded area, it is advisable to pass without stopping as much as possible because if you stop in the middle, rainwater may enter the muffler and cause the engine to turn off. When parking your car, it is best to avoid places with a high risk of flooding, such as riversides or near rivers, and park your car in elevated areas as much as possible. Avoid staying next to a wall that may collapse or under a large sign, as it can be dangerous. Cars parked in the basement of buildings that are expected to flood should be moved to a safe place.
