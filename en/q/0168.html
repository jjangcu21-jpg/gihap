168. If a vehicle is stopped on the railroad crossing due to vehicle failure while passing through, which of the following is NOT proper behavior by the driver?

  1. ①Immediately evacuate all passengers.
  2. ②Use emergency signal devices.
  3. ③Notify a railway official.
  4. ④Quickly check the cause of the vehicle’s failure.
Show answer

Answer
④ Quickly check the cause of the vehicle’s failure.

Reference
Article 24 of the Road Traffic Act (Passing through a railroad crossing) If the driver of any car or streetcar becomes unable to operate the car or tram within the crossing due to a breakdown or other reasons while passing through a railroad crossing, he/she must evacuate the passengers immediately and notify railroad or police officials of the fact by using an emergency signal or other means.
