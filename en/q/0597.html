597. Which of the following is an eco-friendly, economical method of driving?

  1. ①Reduce air pressure in the tires.
  2. ②Start the air-conditioner at a low level
  3. ③Do not replace the oil filter and air filter when replacing the engine oil.
  4. ④Keep the fuel tank filled to about half its capacity.
Show answer

Answer
④ Keep the fuel tank filled to about half its capacity.

Reference
Maintain proper tire pressure, start air conditioner operation at high speed and maintain low speed, and thoroughly manage consumables such as air cleaner. And to reduce the weight of the car, remove unnecessary luggage, empty the trunk, and fill the car with only half of the fuel.
