451. Which of the following are the most appropriate actions to take if the Hi-Pass device malfunctions and fails to read? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Turn on the emergency flashers, temporarily stop your vehicle, and have a toll ticket issued in a non-automated toll lane.
  2. ②Upon reaching the toll gate of your destination, explain where you entered the expressway to the toll collector and pay the toll.
  3. ③Use the Hi-Pass lane and go through the toll gate of your destination, and the toll will be automatically collected.
  4. ④Detach the card from your Hi-Pass device at the toll gate of your destination and hand it over to the toll collector to pay the toll.
Show answer

Answer
② Upon reaching the toll gate of your destination, explain where you entered the expressway to the toll collector and pay the toll.
④ Detach the card from your Hi-Pass device at the toll gate of your destination and hand it over to the toll collector to pay the toll.

Reference
At the destination toll booth, explain the entry location to the payment manager and pay the fee.
