377. According to the Road Traffic Act, the driver of ( ) must ( ) in front of a railway crossing and then pass through after confirming that it is safe to cross. Which of the following best fills the parentheses?

  1. ①any vehicle - slow down
  2. ②any motor vehicle or heavy equipment - slow down
  3. ③any vehicle or tank - stop
  4. ④any vehicle or tram - stop
Show answer

Answer
④ any vehicle or tram - stop

Reference
When the driver of any car or tram wishes to pass a railroad crossing, he or she must stop temporarily in front of the railroad crossing and check for safety before passing. (Road Traffic Act Article 24: Crossing railway tracks)
