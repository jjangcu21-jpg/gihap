343. Which of the following lights must be turned on when stopping on a road at night (except for two-wheeled vehicles)?

  1. ①Headlights and taillights
  2. ②Indoor lights and sidelights
  3. ③License plate lights and headlights
  4. ④Taillights and sidelights
Show answer

Answer
④ Taillights and sidelights

Reference
Article 19 (2) of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act The types of lights that the driver of a car or tram must turn on when stopping or parking on the road pursuant to each subparagraph of Article 37 (1) of the Act shall be classified as follows. 1. Automobiles (excluding two-wheeled vehicles): Taillights and side lights specified in automobile safety standards
