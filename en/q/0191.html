191. Which of the following is correct regarding the protection of pedestrians crossing roads?

  1. ①You have no responsibility to protect pedestrians on sections of a road other than an intersection.
  2. ②You have no responsibility to protect pedestrians crossing the road illegally.
  3. ③You have to protect pedestrians even when they are crossing the road illegally.
  4. ④You have no responsibility to protect pedestrians crossing a one-way road illegally.
Show answer

Answer
③ You have to protect pedestrians even when they are crossing the road illegally.

Reference
<Road Traffic Act Article 27 (Protection of Pedestrians)> ⑤ When a pedestrian is crossing a road on which a crosswalk is not installed pursuant to Article 10 (3), the driver of any car or tram must temporarily stop at a safe distance to allow the pedestrian to cross safely.
