449. In the event of a traffic accident on an expressway, what is the most appropriate measure for preventing secondary accidents?

  1. ①First and foremost take photos of the accident for insurance purposes.
  2. ②Explicitly let the other driver know that he/she is at fault and claim for insurance accordingly.
  3. ③The vehicle must be stopped at the rightmost edge of the road, and a warning sign must be placed in accordance with the regulations prescribed by the Ministry of the Interior and Safety.
  4. ④Turn on the hazard lights and call the relevant authority inside your vehicle.
Show answer

Answer
③ The vehicle must be stopped at the rightmost edge of the road, and a warning sign must be placed in accordance with the regulations prescribed by the Ministry of the Interior and Safety.

Reference
Article 67 of the Road Traffic Act (Matters to be observed by drivers on highways, etc.) ② Drivers of automobiles operating on highways, etc. must always carry a sign for a broken-down automobile in accordance with Article 66 in order to ensure traffic safety and smooth traffic flow, and when the automobile cannot be driven due to a breakdown or other unavoidable reasons, the driver must stop the automobile at the right edge of the road and install the marker as prescribed by Ordinance of the Ministry of Public Administration and Security.
