570. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following is NOT true about riding a bicycle?

  1. ①If a road is divided into a roadway and a sidewalk, bicycle riders must travel on the roadway.
  2. ②To turn right at an intersection, bicycle riders must use the rightmost lane and slow down before making the turn.
  3. ③To turn left at an intersection, bicycle riders must use the centerline or leftmost lane and slow down before making the turn.
  4. ④Bicycle riders must use the bicycle lane if there is any.
Show answer

Answer
③ To turn left at an intersection, bicycle riders must use the centerline or leftmost lane and slow down before making the turn.

Reference
At the Ministry of Government Legislation's public inquiry (Regarding Article 25, Paragraph 3 of the Road Traffic Act, the National Police Agency's response to a question about how cyclists turn left at intersections), a hook-turn means that cyclists cannot turn left immediately according to a left turn signal at an intersection, but must turn left in two steps by sticking to the right edge of the road in advance according to the straight signal in the direction of travel and going straight in two steps.
