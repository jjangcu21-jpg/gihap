715. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Proceed with caution, as the two-wheeled vehicle ahead may suddenly enter into the middle of the road.
  2. ②Speed up as the road is not busy.
  3. ③Maintain a safe distance from the two-wheeled vehicle.
  4. ④Keep honking repeatedly and speed up to overtake.
  5. ⑤Stay close to the two-wheeled vehicle to avoid crossing the centerline.
Show answer

Answer
① Proceed with caution, as the two-wheeled vehicle ahead may suddenly enter into the middle of the road.
③ Maintain a safe distance from the two-wheeled vehicle.

Reference
Since two-wheeled vehicles may move toward the center of the road, you must maintain a sufficient safe distance and slow down.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
