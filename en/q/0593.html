593. Which of the following is the most appropriate method of improving a vehicle’s fuel efficiency?

  1. ①Always fill up the fuel tank completely.
  2. ②When replacing engine oil, replace the oil filter and the air filter as well.
  3. ③When making a stop, step on the brake pedal once with strong force.
  4. ④Frequently use the accelerator and brake pedals.
Show answer

Answer
② When replacing engine oil, replace the oil filter and the air filter as well.

Reference
Engine oil has the function of improving engine performance and extending its lifespan through lubrication, cooling, sealing, and cleaning inside the engine. If you continue to drive the car with insufficient engine oil, the moving parts inside the engine may become stuck, causing engine failure. If the replacement period is exceeded, the viscosity of the engine oil increases, which has a negative effect on fuel efficiency. Before driving for a long period of time, it is recommended to inspect and change the engine oil according to mileage and time in use, and replace the oil filter and air filter as well.
