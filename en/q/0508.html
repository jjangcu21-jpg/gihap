508. According to the Road Traffic Act, what is the correct penalty for a person who drives a passenger car with a blood alcohol concentration (BAC) of 0.03 percent or higher but less than 0.08 percent (for a first offense within 10 years)?

  1. ①Imprisonment for up to 1 year or criminal fine of up to KRW 5 million
  2. ②Imprisonment for up to 2 years or criminal fine of up to KRW 10 million
  3. ③Imprisonment for up to 3 years or criminal fine of up to KRW 15 million
  4. ④Imprisonment in the range of 2 years - 5 years or criminal fine of KRW 10 million or more (up to KRW 20 million)
Show answer

Answer
① Imprisonment for up to 1 year or criminal fine of up to KRW 5 million

Reference
Article 148-2 (Penalty Provisions) of the Road Traffic Act ③ Any person who drives a car or tram while intoxicated in violation of Article 44 (1) shall be punished according to the following classifications. 1. A person with a blood alcohol concentration of 0.2% or more shall be punished by imprisonment for 2 to 5 years or a fine of 10 million to 20 million won 2. A person with a blood alcohol concentration of 0.08% or more but less than 0.2% shall be punished by imprisonment for 1 to 2 years or a fine of 5 million to 10 million won 3. A person with a blood alcohol concentration of 0.03% or more but less than 0.08% will be punished by imprisonment for not more than one year or by a fine of not more than 5 million won.
