379. Which of the following is the most correct action to take when the driver of the vehicle ahead stretches his/her arm horizontally out of the left side of the vehicle?

  1. ①Slow down as the front vehicle is expected to turn right.
  2. ②Switch to an upper lane as the front vehicle is expected to cross.
  3. ③Overtake the front vehicle as it is expected to make a U-turn.
  4. ④Slow down as the front vehicle is expected to change lanes.
Show answer

Answer
④ Slow down as the front vehicle is expected to change lanes.

Reference
According to Appendix 2 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act, when turning left, crossing, U-turning, or trying to change course to the left while proceeding in the same direction, when you reach a point of at least 30 meters (at least 100 meters on an expressway) before reaching the point where you want to perform the action (the edge of the intersection if turning left), extend your left arm horizontally and extend it outside the left side of the vehicle body, or extend your right arm outside the right side of the vehicle body, bend your elbow and raise it vertically, or turn on the turn indicator or light on the left side. Manipulate.
