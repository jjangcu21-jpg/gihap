573. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following persons may NOT use a sidewalk while riding a bicycle?

  1. ①A person registered as physically handicapped according to the Act on the Welfare of Persons with Disabilities
  2. ②A child
  3. ③A person with a plaster cast due to a bodily injury
  4. ④A person of distinguished service to the state with Disability Rating 1 through 7 according to the Act on the Honorable Treatment of and Support for Persons of Distinguished Service to the State
Show answer

Answer
③ A person with a plaster cast due to a bodily injury

Reference
Article 13-2 of the Road Traffic Act (Special Provisions for Bicycle Passage) Paragraph 4, Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules Article 14-4 (Persons with physical disabilities who can ride a bicycle on the sidewalk)
