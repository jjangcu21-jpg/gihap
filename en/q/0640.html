640. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following applies to the driver of a small towing vehicle?

  1. ①The driver of a small towing vehicle need not fasten his/her seat belt as he/she is on emergency duty.
  2. ②The driver of a small towing vehicle is allowed to use a mobile phone for his/her routine work while driving.
  3. ③The driver of a small towing vehicle is required to obtain a Class I driver’s license for special licenses (small towing vehicles) and carry it at all times while working.
  4. ④The driver of a small towing vehicle may exceed the speed limit when in transit to an accident site.
Show answer

Answer
③ The driver of a small towing vehicle is required to obtain a Class I driver’s license for special licenses (small towing vehicles) and carry it at all times while working.

Reference
Drivers of small tow vehicles must also comply with all driver requirements under the Road Traffic Act and must obtain and possess a small tow vehicle license when driving.
