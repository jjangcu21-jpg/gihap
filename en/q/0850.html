850. Which of the following are the TWO most appropriate ways to proceed in the given situation?

  1. ①Speed up in the third lane to get through so that the ambulance can pass through quickly.
  2. ②Yield to the ambulance even though the traffic light is green.
  3. ③Check for any trailing emergency vehicles after the ambulance passes.
  4. ④Accelerate and follow the red car through the intersection.
  5. ⑤Honk at the ambulance driver and continue driving through.
Show answer

Answer
② Yield to the ambulance even though the traffic light is green.
③ Check for any trailing emergency vehicles after the ambulance passes.

Reference
When an emergency vehicle approaches at or near an intersection, drivers must stop temporarily to avoid the intersection. (Article 29 of the Road Traffic Act). ③ Due to the nature of emergency vehicles, there is a high possibility that they will be dispatched in groups rather than individually, so to prevent accidents, they must yield to the preceding emergency vehicle at the intersection and check for the presence of a following vehicle before departing.
