602. Which of the following is the most appropriate method of reducing fine dust from vehicle exhaust gases?

  1. ①When starting, step hard on the accelerator pedal.
  2. ②Start smoothly instead of accelerating quickly.
  3. ③When driving, accelerate and stop on a frequent, repeated basis.
  4. ④When stopping or parking, keep the engine running.
Show answer

Answer
② Start smoothly instead of accelerating quickly.

Reference
For eco-friendly driving, you should avoid sudden starts, sudden braking, and rapid acceleration. When driving, drive at a constant speed, but it is not advisable to repeat acceleration and stopping frequently. Also, do not continue idling when stopping or parking.
