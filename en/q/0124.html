124. Which of the following is NOT a correct explanation of the punishment criteria for drunk driving?

  1. ①In case of the first violation, if the blood alcohol concentration is 0.2% or more, imprisonment for 2 to 5 years or criminal fine of KRW 10 million - 20 million
  2. ②Refusing a field sobriety test once may result in imprisonment with labor for 1 year or more but not more than 5 years, or criminal fine of KRW 5 million or more but not more than KRW 20 million
  3. ③In case of a second violation involving a blood alcohol concentration of 0.05%, imprisonment for up to 1 year or criminal fine of up to KRW 5 million
  4. ④Upon the first violation at a blood alcohol level of at least 0.08% but less than 0.2%: imprisonment with labor for at least 1 year but not more than 2 years or criminal fine of at least KRW 5 million but not more than KRW 10 million
Show answer

Answer
③ In case of a second violation involving a blood alcohol concentration of 0.05%, imprisonment for up to 1 year or criminal fine of up to KRW 5 million

Reference
Road Traffic Act, Article 148-2 (Penalties): A person previously sentenced to a fine or a heavier penalty for violating Article 44(1) or (2) who violates either paragraph again within 10 years after the sentence became final is subject to the following penalties. This includes a person whose sentence has expired and applies to drivers of motor vehicles or trams, excluding personal mobility devices. 1. Violation of Article 44(2): imprisonment for 1 to 6 years, or a fine of KRW 5 million to 30 million. 2. Violation of Article 44(1) with a blood alcohol concentration of 0.2% or higher: imprisonment for 2 to 6 years, or a fine of KRW 10 million to 30 million. 3. Violation of Article 44(1) with a blood alcohol concentration of at least 0.03% but below 0.2%: imprisonment for 1 to 5 years, or a fine of KRW 5 million to 20 million.
