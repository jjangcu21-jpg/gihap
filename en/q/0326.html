326. Which of the following statements is INCORRECT regarding a silver zone?

  1. ①A traffic penalty is imposed for speeding between 8 AM and 8 PM.
  2. ②Green traffic lights for pedestrians are longer that those on general roadways.
  3. ③No road parking lots can be installed.
  4. ④Larger traffic lights are installed so that they can be seen more easily by the elderly.
Show answer

Answer
④ Larger traffic lights are installed so that they can be seen more easily by the elderly.

Reference
Considering the physical condition of the elderly, the length of the walking signal is set based on the average walking speed of the disabled and is longer than the walking signal on general roads (see Article 6 (2) of the Rules on Designation and Management of Protection Zones for Children, the Elderly, and the Disabled; 2023 Traffic Signal Installation and Operation Manual, Part 2, Chapter 2, Walking Signal Operation). Traffic lights cannot be installed larger than specified.
