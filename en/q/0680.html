680. Which of the following is an appropriate action for the driver of a recovery vehicle?

  1. ①In some instances, it is permissible for the driver of a recovery vehicle to use a police radio scanner.
  2. ②The driver of a recovery vehicle must obey the Road Traffic Act.
  3. ③A recovery vehicle may violate the speed limit when it is on the way to the scene of an accident.
  4. ④In the event of an accident, a recovery vehicle may proceed without regard for traffic signals.
Show answer

Answer
② The driver of a recovery vehicle must obey the Road Traffic Act.

Reference
Article 2, item 22 of the Road Traffic Act limits emergency vehicles to categories such as fire engines, ambulances, blood supply vehicles, and police vehicles; recovery tow trucks are not included. Recovery tow trucks therefore cannot use the exceptions granted to emergency vehicles (such as exceptions to traffic signals or speed limits) and must obey the Road Traffic Act. It is illegal for their drivers to intercept police radio communications.
