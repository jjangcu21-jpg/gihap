964. Which of the following correctly describes this building number plate?

  1. ①"Pyungchon-gil" is the name of the road and "30" is the building number.
  2. ②"Pyungchon-gil" is the main entrance and "30" is the basic number.
  3. ③"Pyungchon-gil" is the start of the road and "30" is the building number.
  4. ④"Pyungchon-gil" is the road identifier and "30" is the specific address.
Show answer

Answer
① "Pyungchon-gil" is the name of the road and "30" is the building number.
