474. In which of the following situations is a driver allowed to overtake a vehicle?

  1. ①When the vehicle ahead is overtaking another vehicle
  2. ②When the vehicle ahead stops or it is driving slowly to prevent a dangerous situation
  3. ③When the vehicle ahead is driving next to another vehicle in the left lane
  4. ④When the vehicle ahead is driving slowly to secure a safe distance from another vehicle
Show answer

Answer
④ When the vehicle ahead is driving slowly to secure a safe distance from another vehicle

Reference
The driver of any vehicle may not overtake the vehicle in front if another vehicle is traveling parallel to the vehicle in front on the left side of the vehicle in front, or if the vehicle in front is overtaking or intends to overtake the other vehicle. (Articles 21 and 22 of the Road Traffic Act)
