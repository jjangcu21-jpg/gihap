270. Which of the following are the TWO correct methods of yielding to an emergency vehicle on the road?

  1. ①All vehicles must pull over to the left side of the road.
  2. ②If the emergency vehicle is climbing up a narrow slope and you are facing it, the emergency vehicle must pull over to the right to make way for you.
  3. ③Near an intersection, pull over at some distance from the intersection.
  4. ④When an emergency vehicle approaches from a place other than an intersection or its vicinity, the roadway shall be yielded so that the emergency vehicle can take priority.
Show answer

Answer
③ Near an intersection, pull over at some distance from the intersection.
④ When an emergency vehicle approaches from a place other than an intersection or its vicinity, the roadway shall be yielded so that the emergency vehicle can take priority.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 29 (Priority Passage for Emergency Vehicles) ④ When an emergency vehicle approaches at or near an intersection, drivers of cars, horses and streetcars must avoid the intersection and stop temporarily. ⑤ When an emergency vehicle approaches from a place other than that specified in paragraph (4), the drivers of all cars and streetcars must give way to allow the emergency vehicle to pass first.
