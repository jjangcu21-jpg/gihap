307. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following correctly describes the driver's duty regarding the special protection of school buses?

  1. ①If a school bus for children with a red flasher being operated passes next to your lane, it is necessary to stop temporarily.
  2. ②You cannot overtake any school bus for children passing on the road.
  3. ③If you violate this duty, you will incur 15 demerit points on your driver's license.
  4. ④This duty does not apply when a school bus for children stops on a road with one lane in one direction with its red flasher activited.
Show answer

Answer
① If a school bus for children with a red flasher being operated passes next to your lane, it is necessary to stop temporarily.

Reference
Article 2, Paragraph 19 of the Road Traffic Act, Article 51 of the Road Traffic Act, Automobile Management Act, and Rules on Performance and Standards of Automobiles and Automobile Parts ① When a children's school bus is stopped on the road and devices such as flashing lights are in operation to indicate that children or infants are getting on or off, the driver of a car traveling in the lane where the children's school bus is stopped and the lane immediately next to the lane must stop temporarily before reaching the children's school bus, check for safety, and then drive slowly. ② In the case of paragraph 1, on roads where a center line is not installed and roads with one lane each way, the driver of a car traveling in the opposite direction must also stop temporarily and check for safety before reaching the children's school bus and then drive slowly. ③ Drivers of any vehicle may not overtake a children's school bus traveling on the road with a sign indicating that it is carrying children or infants. Appendix 28 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act. Violation of the obligation to provide special protection for children on school buses will result in 30 driver's license penalty points.
