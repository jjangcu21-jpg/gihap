779. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①The tractor has stopped, so you may honk continuously to rush the tractor driver to proceed.
  2. ②As soon as the vehicle on the opposite side passes through, cross the centerline and overtake to the left.
  3. ③Drive cautiously, anticipating that the truck on the right may suddenly start moving while it is stopped.
  4. ④If there is space on the right of the tractor, use it to overtake.
  5. ⑤Since the tractor moves slower than cars, maintain a safe distance instead of aggressively attempting to overtake.
Show answer

Answer
③ Drive cautiously, anticipating that the truck on the right may suddenly start moving while it is stopped.
⑤ Since the tractor moves slower than cars, maintain a safe distance instead of aggressively attempting to overtake.

Reference
If there is slow-speed agricultural machinery in front, such as a cultivator or tractor, the speed is slower than that of a car, so you must carefully monitor the progress of the agricultural machinery before driving. It is dangerous to drive based on assumptions or try to overtake. Overtaking by crossing the center line is dangerous and is not a safe driving method.
