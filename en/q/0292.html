292. Which ministries are responsible for jointly establishing the procedure and criteria for designating priority zones under the Road Traffic Act?

  1. ①A school zone is jointly designated by the Ministry of Interior and Safety, Ministry of Health and Welfare, and Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, and Transport.
  2. ②A silver zone is jointly designated by the Ministry of Interior and Safety, Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, and Transport, and Ministry of Environment.
  3. ③A priority zone for persons with disabilities is jointly designated by the Ministry of Interior and Safety, Ministry of Health and Welfare, and Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, and Transport.
  4. ④A priority zone for people with mobility impairments is jointly designated by the Ministry of Interior and Safety, Ministry of Environment, and Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, and Transport.
Show answer

Answer
③ A priority zone for persons with disabilities is jointly designated by the Ministry of Interior and Safety, Ministry of Health and Welfare, and Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, and Transport.

Reference
Article 12(2) of the Road Traffic Act provides that procedures and standards for designating and removing child protection zones under paragraph 1 are set by a joint ministerial ordinance of the Ministry of Education, Ministry of the Interior and Safety, and Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport. Procedures and standards for designating protection zones for older people or people with disabilities are set by a joint ordinance of the Ministry of the Interior and Safety, Ministry of Health and Welfare, and Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport.
