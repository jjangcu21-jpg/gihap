312. Which of the following is true about the definition of the senior driver sign under the Road Traffic Act?

  1. ①A senior driver sign indicates that the vehicle is driven by a licensed driver aged 65 or older.
  2. ②The background of the sign should be blue and the text should be yellow.
  3. ③The front side should be made of rubber magnet for removal, and the back side should be made of reflective paper.
  4. ④Attach it to the front of the vehicle in a visible location that does not interfere with safe driving.
Show answer

Answer
① A senior driver sign indicates that the vehicle is driven by a licensed driver aged 65 or older.

Reference
Article 10-2 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act (Production and Distribution of Elderly Driver Signs) ① The Commissioner General of the National Police Agency may produce and distribute a sign indicating that the vehicle is being driven by a person aged 65 or older who has received a driver's license pursuant to Article 7-2 (1) of the Act (hereinafter referred to as “elderly driver sign”).
