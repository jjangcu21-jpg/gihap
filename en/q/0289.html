289. Which of the following are TWO correct statements about school zones?

  1. ①A school zone refers to a section of a road located within 100m of the main gate of an elementary school.
  2. ②A traffic penalty can be imposed for cars that are stopped or parked illegally in a school zone between 8 AM and 8 PM.
  3. ③The length of the green signal of traffic lights installed in a school zone is based on the fastest walking speed of an average child.
  4. ④The demerit points applied to drivers who fail to consider pedestrian safety are doubled in school zones between 8 AM and 8 PM.
Show answer

Answer
② A traffic penalty can be imposed for cars that are stopped or parked illegally in a school zone between 8 AM and 8 PM.
④ The demerit points applied to drivers who fail to consider pedestrian safety are doubled in school zones between 8 AM and 8 PM.

Reference
A child protection zone refers to a certain section of the road within 300 meters of the main entrance to an elementary school, and the walking time of the traffic signal installed within the child protection zone is based on the average walking speed of children.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
