653. Which of the following is a towed motor vehicle designed for the transport of extremely large cargo as a single or a combined double unit, and equipped with a device for weight balancing?

  1. ①A semi-trailer
  2. ②A low-bed trailer
  3. ③A module trailer
  4. ④A center-axle trailer
Show answer

Answer
③ A module trailer

Reference
① Semi-trailer - A towed car that is partially mounted on top of the tow car and shares a significant portion of the weight of the car and load with the tow car. ② Low-floor trailer - A towed car that is suitable for the transportation of heavy objects and has the structure of a semi-trailer. Most of the trailer’s upper deck has a height above the ground of 1,100 mm or less and are manufactured lower than the height of the coupler of the tow car. ④ Center axle trailer - A towed vehicle with one or more axles whose center of gravity in an evenly loaded state is in front of the center of the vehicle axle, the connection device with the towing vehicle is not bent in the vertical direction, and a load less than 10 percent of the total weight of the vehicle or 1,000 kilograms is shared with the towing vehicle.
