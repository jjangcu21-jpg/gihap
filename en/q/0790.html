790. Which of the following are the safest ways to make a U-turn in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Make a U-turn within the U-turn area marked by white dotted lines when the signal allows, starting with the car in front of you.
  2. ②There is no need to watch out for right-turning vehicles from a left side road onto the main road.
  3. ③You can make a U-turn at any time regardless of the signal, as long as you do not obstruct traffic on the opposite side of the road.
  4. ④There is no need to yield to a car making a U-turn first from immediately behind you.
  5. ⑤Be sure to look before making a U-turn, as there may be cars disobeying the traffic signal on the opposite side of the road.
Show answer

Answer
① Make a U-turn within the U-turn area marked by white dotted lines when the signal allows, starting with the car in front of you.
⑤ Be sure to look before making a U-turn, as there may be cars disobeying the traffic signal on the opposite side of the road.

Reference
This is an intersection where you can make a U-turn when the left turn signal changes, and you must be prepared for the danger caused by vehicles going straight on the opposite road in violation of the signal immediately after the left turn signal changes and vehicles turning right from the opposite side road and entering the main road in a hurry. So, even if the signal changes to allow for a U-turn, you need to check your surroundings with your own eyes, make U-turns one after another, and pay attention to the vehicles in front and behind your vehicle even when making a U-turn at a U-turn signal.
