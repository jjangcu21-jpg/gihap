469. Which of the following are TWO phenomena caused by the tires of a vehicle driving at high speed?

  1. ①Vapor lock
  2. ②Standing wave
  3. ③Fading
  4. ④Aquaplaning
Show answer

Answer
② Standing wave
④ Aquaplaning

Reference
A standing wave phenomenon occurs when the tire pressure of a vehicle traveling at high speed is insufficient, and a hydroplaning phenomenon occurs when the tires of a vehicle traveling at high speed are worn and pass through a pool of water. The fade phenomenon refers to a phenomenon that occurs when the brake pedal is used excessively, the friction coefficient of the pad and lining is lowered, and the braking force deteriorates, leading to slipping. Vapor lock and fade phenomena are phenomena that occur due to abnormalities in the braking system.
