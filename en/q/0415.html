415. Which of the following is the safest method of stopping on a snowy/icy road?

  1. ①Apply the brake hard.
  2. ②Stop quickly using the foot brake and the parking brake at the same time.
  3. ③Use the engine brake only to slow down until the car comes to a complete stop.
  4. ④After slowing down with the engine brake, step lightly on the brake pedal several times.
Show answer

Answer
④ After slowing down with the engine brake, step lightly on the brake pedal several times.

Reference
Because snowy or icy roads are slippery, it is safer to slow down with the engine brake and then apply the foot brake several times when stopping.
