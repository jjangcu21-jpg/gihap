Road signs · 2 pts

941. Which of the following statements regarding this road surface marking is INCORRECT?

  1. ①A raised crosswalk is present in the area.
  2. ②A crosswalk shaped like a block-ladder and a speed bump with height of 10cm.
  3. ③A sign that can be installed to caution drivers.
  4. ④These markings can be installed on all roads.
Show answer

Answer
④ These markings can be installed on all roads.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules Annex 6 Raised crosswalk marking (road marking 533) Indicating that this is a crosswalk on roads where the speed limit needs to be limited to 30 kilometers per hour or less.
