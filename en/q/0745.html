745. Which of the following are the unsafe ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①You cannot pass through a variable lane.
  2. ②Drive with caution as there is a foggy area 1 kilometer ahead.
  3. ③Drive on the shoulder to avoid the average speed camera ahead.
  4. ④Trucks may use the second lane to overtake.
  5. ⑤Passengers on trucks are not obligated to wear a seat belt on expressways.
Show answer

Answer
③ Drive on the shoulder to avoid the average speed camera ahead.
⑤ Passengers on trucks are not obligated to wear a seat belt on expressways.

Reference
There is a high risk of an accident if there is sudden braking or rapid deceleration on the highway. The driver of a car (excluding two-wheeled vehicles) must wear a seat belt when driving the car, and all passengers in the seat must also wear a seat belt (in the case of infants and young children, this refers to a seat belt after installing child protection equipment.
