178. According to the Road Traffic Act, which is a case of violations by passenger vehicle drivers related to pedestrian safety and which have a different traffic penalty amount compared to other cases? (Excluding specific zones, e.g., school zones)

  1. ①Interrupting pedestrians crossing the road when the walk signal is lit
  2. ②Entering a pedestrian-only road
  3. ③Throwing an object onto the road from a moving vehicle
  4. ④Failing to protect children, visually impaired, etc.
Show answer

Answer
③ Throwing an object onto the road from a moving vehicle

Reference
Appendix 8 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act: Illegal acts and amount (driver) ①, ②, ④ are 60,000 won, and ③ is 50,000 won.
