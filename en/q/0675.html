675. Which of the following is the most appropriate method for the driver of a recovery vehicle to tow a broken down vehicle with an RR layout (Rear engine Rear wheel drive)?

  1. ①Tow by lifting the front part of the vehicle.
  2. ②Tow by lifting the rear part of the vehicle.
  3. ③It does not matter which part is lifted.
  4. ④Tow by lifting the entire vehicle.
Show answer

Answer
② Tow by lifting the rear part of the vehicle.

Reference
RR (Rear engine rear wheel drive) rear wheel drive vehicles have the engine at the back and are rear wheel drive, so the rear part of the car body is lifted and towed to prevent damage.
