480. Which of the following are true about wearing seat belts? (Select TWO)

  1. ①The penalty for a driver failing to wear a seat belt is an administrative fine of KRW 30,000.
  2. ②Whiplash injuries are more common with two-point seat belts.
  3. ③The penalty for a child under the age of 13 not wearing a seat belt is a traffic penalty of KRW 60,000.
  4. ④There are two, three, and four-point seat belts.
Show answer

Answer
② Whiplash injuries are more common with two-point seat belts.
④ There are two, three, and four-point seat belts.

Reference
① If the driver does not wear a seat belt, a fine of 30,000 won will be imposed. ② In general, whiplash injuries to the neck occur more often in the two-point type. ③ If a child under the age of 13 does not wear a seat belt, a fine of 60,000 won will be imposed. ④ Seat belts are classified into 2-point, 3-point, and 4-point types depending on how they are worn.
