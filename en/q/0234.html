234. In which of the following areas is overtaking a vehicle possible?

  1. ①Intersection
  2. ②Centerline (yellow broken line)
  3. ③Inside the tunnel (white solid line)
  4. ④On the bridge (white solid line)
Show answer

Answer
② Centerline (yellow broken line)

Reference
Article 22 of the Road Traffic Act (Time and Place of Prohibition of Overtaking), Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act [Appendix 6] 501 Intersections, solid yellow line sections, inside tunnels, on bridges, and places designated by city/provincial police chiefs are places where overtaking is prohibited.
