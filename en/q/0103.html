103. Which of the following is the correct method of entering a travel lane from an acceleration lane of an expressway?

  1. ①Stop temporarily to check the traffic flow, and then quickly enter the expressway.
  2. ②Stop temporarily before entering the expressway and abruptly enter when there are vehicles passing on the road.
  3. ③Enter the expressway even if there is not much space to enter, in consideration of the vehicle behind.
  4. ④Use the acceleration lane, maintaining a certain speed, and enter the expressway when enough space is secured.
Show answer

Answer
④ Use the acceleration lane, maintaining a certain speed, and enter the expressway when enough space is secured.

Reference
When entering a highway, you must use the acceleration lane and gradually increase your speed. If you enter slowly or pause, it can be dangerous because acceleration is difficult. You must make sure there is enough space to enter and accelerate to enter.
