299. Which of the following are true about school zones and driving in these zones? (Select TWO)

  1. ①A school zone may limit the vehicle speed to 30 km/h if necessary to ensure children's safety. Drivers must observe the speed limit when driving in a school zone.
  2. ②In a school zone, drivers of motor vehicles, motorcycles, and trams must observe the speed limit and come to a full stop when children are crossing.
  3. ③Roads near alternative or international schools are not designated as school zones; therefore, drivers must slow down when driving in these areas as children may be jaywalking.
  4. ④Speed limit and pedestrian crossing signs can be placed with priority in school zones. Drivers must slow down when a child is standing by the centerline.
Show answer

Answer
① A school zone may limit the vehicle speed to 30 km/h if necessary to ensure children's safety. Drivers must observe the speed limit when driving in a school zone.
② In a school zone, drivers of motor vehicles, motorcycles, and trams must observe the speed limit and come to a full stop when children are crossing.

Reference
Article 12 of the Road Traffic Act (Designation, cancellation and management of child protection zones) Paragraph 1: If the mayor, etc. deems it necessary to protect children from the risk of traffic accidents, he or she may restrict the speed of cars and trams to within 30 kilometers per hour. No. 4: Foreign schools and alternative schools may also be designated as child protection areas. Paragraph 5 No. 2: Safety signs regarding speed limits and crosswalks may be installed with priority in child protection areas for the safety of children.
