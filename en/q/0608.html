608. Which of the following is NOT true about an FCEV inspection?

  1. ①Hydrogen is a flammable gas; therefore, FCEVs require regular inspection.
  2. ②FCEV inspections must be conducted in a well-ventilated area.
  3. ③During an inspection, it is crucial to check for hydrogen leak in the gas pipeline and charging inlet.
  4. ④The driver of an FCEV is not responsible for conducting safety inspections of the vehicle.
Show answer

Answer
④ The driver of an FCEV is not responsible for conducting safety inspections of the vehicle.

Reference
According to Article 7 of the Traffic Safety Act (Obligations of Vehicle Drivers, etc.), persons driving vehicles, etc. must check whether the vehicle in question poses any obstacles to safe operation in accordance with the provisions of the law and drive safely so as not to cause danger or damage to pedestrians and cyclists.
