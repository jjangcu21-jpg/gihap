976. Which of the following is the most dangerous situation in the video? (Refer to the website)

Video questions play in the Gihap app or on the official site.

Official video ↗
  1. ①The possibility of a collision with the two-wheeled vehicle intending to turn right from the right edge of the road
  2. ②The possibility of a collision with the black vehicle on the right
  3. ③The possibility of a collision with the white vehicle on the opposite side waiting to turn left
  4. ④The possibility of a collision with the pedestrian standing on the left side of the crosswalk
Show answer

Answer
① The possibility of a collision with the two-wheeled vehicle intending to turn right from the right edge of the road

Reference
In the video, the driver did not turn on the right turn signal when trying to turn right. This means that information was not conveyed to traffic participants on the road. In particular, the driver of a car trying to turn right tends to make a rather wide turn due to the difference between the trajectory of the front wheels and the trajectory of the rear wheels when turning right. If there is space to the right, a motorcycle rider may see the vehicle ahead without its turn signal and assume that it will continue straight, although it is about to turn right. This misunderstanding can directly cause a collision.
