668. The following are measures that the driver of a recovery vehicle may take at the scene of an accident. Which one is the most advisable?

  1. ①Advise the parties involved in the accident to pursue a civil settlement.
  2. ②Immediately tow the crashed vehicle without consulting the parties involved in the accident.
  3. ③Just prepare for towing without taking any safety measures, as visibility is good in daytime.
  4. ④Help the driver of the vehicle that crashed to calm down.
Show answer

Answer
④ Help the driver of the vehicle that crashed to calm down.

Reference
You must not be involved in accident handling, such as urging parties involved in a traffic accident to reach an agreement, and safety measures must be taken even during the day.
