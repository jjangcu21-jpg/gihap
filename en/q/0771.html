771. Which of the following traffic safety facilities and actions are the most appropriate in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Crosswalk - You can cross while riding on your bike.
  2. ②Bike crosswalk - Ride the bicycle to cross a roadway with a bike crossing.
  3. ③Pedestrian signal - Pedestrians should start crossing quickly when the green traffic light is flashing.
  4. ④Pedestrian signal - If a bike signal is not installed, bicycle riders must follow the pedestrian signal.
  5. ⑤Traffic light - If a bike signal is not installed, bicycle riders must follow the traffic signal.
Show answer

Answer
② Bike crosswalk - Ride the bicycle to cross a roadway with a bike crossing.
④ Pedestrian signal - If a bike signal is not installed, bicycle riders must follow the pedestrian signal.

Reference
When the green light among pedestrian traffic lights is flashing, pedestrians must not start crossing, and pedestrians crossing must quickly complete the crossing or stop crossing and return to the sidewalk (Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 2]). When riding a bicycle, follow the directions of the vehicle traffic lights in places where bicycle traffic lights are not installed. If a bicycle crossing signal light is not installed on the bicycle crossing road, riders of bicycles and personal mobility devices follow the directions of the walking signal light (Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 2]).
