158. Which of the following is NOT a desirable attitude for a driver using a drive-thru store?

  1. ①Follow the safety instructions of the attendant at the drive-thru store.
  2. ②Follow the instructions on the signage installed at the drive-thru store.
  3. ③Stop by encroaching upon a crosswalk while waiting in line for the drive-thru store.
  4. ④Exercise caution while navigating the entrance and exit areas of the drive-thru store, paying attention to safety facilities.
Show answer

Answer
③ Stop by encroaching upon a crosswalk while waiting in line for the drive-thru store.

Reference
Ride purchase stores (drive-thru stores) have recently become a social transportation issue. These queues not only affect traffic congestion but also threaten traffic safety. Even if you are queuing at a drive-through store, you must not stop by encroaching on the crosswalk.
