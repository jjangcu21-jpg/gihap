548. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, who among the following does NOT qualify for a conditional driver’s license requiring the installation of an alcohol ignition interlock device?

  1. ①A person who has violated the drunk driving laws and not more than 5 years have passed since he has had his/her license revoked for driving a motorized bicycle while intoxicated.
  2. ②A person who has violated the drunk driving laws and not more than 3 years have passed since he has had his/her license revoked for driving a personal mobility device while intoxicated.
  3. ③A person who has violated the drunk driving laws and not more than 5 years have passed since he has had his/her license revoked for refusing to comply with a police officer’s request to submit to an alcohol test while intoxicated.
  4. ④A person who has violated the drunk driving laws and not more than 3 years have passed since he has had his/her license revoked for interfering with an alcohol test while intoxicated.
Show answer

Answer
② A person who has violated the drunk driving laws and not more than 3 years have passed since he has had his/her license revoked for driving a personal mobility device while intoxicated.

Reference
Road Traffic Act, Article 80-2(1): A person whose license was revoked for again violating Article 44(1), (2), or (5) within five years of an earlier such violation must obtain a conditional license requiring an alcohol interlock from the city/provincial police chief before driving a motor vehicle. This applies to violations involving motor vehicles or trams, excluding personal mobility devices.
