456. You are driving on an expressway and there is ongoing construction ahead. Which of the following are NOT true? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Stay cautious when driving toward and by a construction site.
  2. ②Drive faster than what is indicated on the construction speed limit sign.
  3. ③Try to refrain from excessive overtaking or cutting in front of cars.
  4. ④Drive at the same speed when approaching the construction site so as not to cause unnecessary traffic disruptions.
Show answer

Answer
② Drive faster than what is indicated on the construction speed limit sign.
④ Drive at the same speed when approaching the construction site so as not to cause unnecessary traffic disruptions.

Reference
In construction sections, the speed is lower than the road speed limit, so you must drive at a reduced speed according to the speed limit sign installed at the construction site.
