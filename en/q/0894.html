Road signs · 2 pts

894. Which of the following statements regarding this safety sign is correct?

  1. ①It should be installed on a road with many cars going straight.
  2. ②It should be installed to the left of an area that should not be entered.
  3. ③Where such signs are installed, one must make a U-turn and drive in the opposite direction.
  4. ④The sign means that drivers should use other roads such as the ones to the right or left.
Show answer

Answer
④ The sign means that drivers should use other roads such as the ones to the right or left.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules Appendix 6, Regulatory Sign 212, Prohibition to Go Straight Sign This is a regulatory sign that prohibits cars from going straight, and is installed on the right side of the road at the point where cars are to be prohibited from going straight.
