181. Which of the following is NOT appropriate behavior on the part of a driver toward pedestrians?

  1. ①Watch out for children in areas with the "school zone" sign.
  2. ②Drive slowly to protect pedestrians who are about to cross a crosswalk.
  3. ③Pedestrians crossing the road illegally must also be protected.
  4. ④Stop if there is a child crossing the road without a guardian.
Show answer

Answer
② Drive slowly to protect pedestrians who are about to cross a crosswalk.

Reference
Article 13, Paragraphs 1 and 2 of the Road Traffic Act: Before crossing the sidewalk, you must pause, look at the left and right sides, and then cross so as not to impede the passage of pedestrians.
