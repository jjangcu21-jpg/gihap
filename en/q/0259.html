259. According to the Road Traffic Act, which TWO are considered emergency vehicles?

  1. ①A fire truck going to a repair shop for repairs
  2. ②A vehicle transporting patients who are injured or in a critical condition, or transporting blood for transfusion
  3. ③An ambulance transporting a patient discharged from a medical institution
  4. ④A vehicle authorized by the commissioner of the City/Do (Province) police agency to transport urgent postal mail, etc.
Show answer

Answer
② A vehicle transporting patients who are injured or in a critical condition, or transporting blood for transfusion
④ A vehicle authorized by the commissioner of the City/Do (Province) police agency to transport urgent postal mail, etc.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 2, item 22: Emergency vehicles are vehicles being used for their original emergency purpose: (a) fire engines, (b) ambulances, (c) blood-supply vehicles, and (d) other vehicles prescribed by Presidential Decree. Enforcement Decree Article 2, item 5 includes official vehicles used to protect domestic or foreign dignitaries; item 10 includes vehicles transporting urgent mail.
