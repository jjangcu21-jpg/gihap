400. According to the Road Traffic Act, on an expressway with five lanes in one direction, up to which lane is considered the left lane based on the standard of traffic flow in each lane? (Assuming smooth traffic flow and no designated lanes or acceleration/deceleration lanes)

  1. ①Lanes 1 to 2
  2. ②Lanes 2 to 3
  3. ③Lanes 1 to 3
  4. ④The second lane only
Show answer

Answer
② Lanes 2 to 3

Reference
Exclude lane 1 and divide the remaining lanes into two groups. The left-side lanes are the group closer to lane 1. If the number of remaining lanes is odd, exclude the middle lane from this group.
