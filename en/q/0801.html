801. The driver is driving straight ahead. Which of the following are the TWO safest ways to drive?

  1. ①Accelerate to prevent the white vehicle from entering.
  2. ②Drive slowly and pay attention as the white car may be driving straight ahead.
  3. ③Traffic is allowed to pass on the left side during road maintenance, so stay on the left.
  4. ④Anticipate that the white car will stop and proceed while paying attention to the vehicles in the opposite direction.
  5. ⑤The red car in the opposite direction may enter the left-turn lane, so stop and observe the situation if necessary.
Show answer

Answer
② Drive slowly and pay attention as the white car may be driving straight ahead.
⑤ The red car in the opposite direction may enter the left-turn lane, so stop and observe the situation if necessary.

Reference
Article 13 of the Road Traffic Act. If driving on the right side is impossible due to road maintenance work, you can drive on the left side in accordance with the Road Traffic Act. At this time, extreme caution is required for cars traveling in the opposite direction. If a car in the opposite direction attempts to turn left, the likelihood of a collision is high, so it must stop if necessary. Also, in the presented situation, the white car is traveling and can take one of the following actions: stop, turn right, go straight (crossing the center line), or turn left (crossing the center line). In particular, there are cases where some drivers cross the center line on side roads and drive straight or turn left, so it is dangerous to predict that all drivers of cars entering from the front will stop or only turn right.
