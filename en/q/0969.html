969. Which of the following vehicles shown in the video complies with the Road Traffic Act to protect children crossing a road in a school zone? (Refer to the website)

  1. ①The red car
  2. ②The white truck
  3. ③The blue truck
  4. ④The orange taxi
Show answer

Answer
③ The blue truck

Reference
Article 27 of the Road Traffic Act (Pedestrian Protection), Article 49 of the Road Traffic Act (Matters to be observed by all drivers, etc.) In child protection zones, you must reduce your speed and drive slowly because you do not know when and at what moment a child may appear. A safe distance must be maintained because if the car in front suddenly brakes due to a child suddenly appearing, the car following may collide. When passing by children, you must drive slowly and maintain sufficient distance.
