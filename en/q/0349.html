349. Which of the following statements about parking and stopping on a slope under the Road Traffic Act is incorrect?

  1. ①When parking, operate the parking brake and use wheel chocks or blocks to prevent the vehicle from slipping.
  2. ②When stopping, if the driver remains seated and directly operates the brake, no additional anti-slip measures are required.
  3. ③When parking, operate the parking brake and turn the steering wheel toward the road.
  4. ④The parking brake alone is insufficient when stopping and leaving the driver’s seat temporarily; rubber or plastic must be used to prevent the vehicle from slipping.
Show answer

Answer
③ When parking, operate the parking brake and turn the steering wheel toward the road.

Reference
Article 11, Paragraph 3 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act. Pursuant to Article 34-3 of the Act, the driver of a vehicle must take any of the following measures after activating the parking brake system of the vehicle when he or she intends to stop or park on a slope (including stopping or parking on a slope other than the road). However, this excludes cases where the driver operates the brakes directly without leaving the driver's seat. 1. Install chocks, chocks, or other rubber, plastic, etc. on the wheels in the downhill direction of the slope to prevent slipping accidents. 2. Turn the steering device toward the edge of the road (referring to the side closest to the car). 3. Take other measures to prevent slipping accidents in a manner equivalent to subparagraph 1 or 2.
