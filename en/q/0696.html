696. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Pass through the road quickly, as there are pedestrians on the right side of the road.
  2. ②Check the traffic situation via the road mirror for areas that are difficult to see.
  3. ③Look carefully before entering the intersection and drive slowly, as there are vehicles in the alley on the left.
  4. ④Keep honking to warn others when driving on an uphill road that curves to the right as it is difficult to see what is ahead.
  5. ⑤Even if there is a vehicle coming down the road from the opposite direction, the vehicle going uphill has the right of way, so proceed without slowing down.
Show answer

Answer
② Check the traffic situation via the road mirror for areas that are difficult to see.
③ Look carefully before entering the intersection and drive slowly, as there are vehicles in the alley on the left.

Reference
It is safer to clearly check the safety of the alley on the left through the roadside convex mirror and drive slowly before entering the intersection to pass through the intersection without traffic lights. Since there are pedestrians on the right, it is safer to ensure safety and proceed by slowing down or pausing rather than sounding the horn. According to Article 20 of the Road Traffic Act, when driving facing each other on a narrow, sloping road, the vehicle going up must give way.
