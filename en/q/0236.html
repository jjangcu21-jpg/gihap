236. Below are various explanations of the punishment criteria for driving a vehicle (except for personal mobility devices) in violation of the maximum speed on the road. Which of the following is correct?

  1. ①Anyone who drives three or more times at a speed of over 100 km/h above the speed limit is subject to a criminal fine of up to KRW 5 million or detention.
  2. ②Anyone who drives three or more times at a speed of over 100 km/h above the speed limit is subject to imprisonment for up to one year or a criminal fine of up to KRW 5 million.
  3. ③Anyone who drives twice at a speed of over 100 km/h above the speed limit is subject to a criminal fine of up to KRW 3 million.
  4. ④Anyone who drives at a speed of over 80 km/h above the speed limit is subject to a criminal fine of up to KRW 500,000 or detention.
Show answer

Answer
② Anyone who drives three or more times at a speed of over 100 km/h above the speed limit is subject to imprisonment for up to one year or a criminal fine of up to KRW 5 million.

Reference
①② Road Traffic Act Article 151-2, item 2: Driving more than 100 km/h above the speed limit on three or more occasions carries up to 1 year of imprisonment or a fine of up to KRW 5 million. ③ Article 153(2), item 2: Driving more than 100 km/h above the limit carries a fine of up to KRW 1 million or detention. ④ Article 154, item 9: Driving more than 80 km/h above the limit carries a fine of up to KRW 300,000 or detention.
