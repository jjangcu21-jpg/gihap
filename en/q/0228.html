228. Which of the following is the correct method of passing through an intersection with two left-turn lanes?

  1. ①Passenger cars must use only the first lane to turn left.
  2. ②Passenger cars must use only the second lane to turn left.
  3. ③Large buses must use only the first lane to turn left.
  4. ④Large buses must use only the second lane to turn left.
Show answer

Answer
④ Large buses must use only the second lane to turn left.

Reference
A car attempting to turn left at an intersection with two or more left turn lanes must turn left within the left turn lanes according to the lane classification other than highways in Table 9 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act.
