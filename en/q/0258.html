258. Which of the following is necessary for a general vehicle to be recognized as an emergency vehicle when transporting a patient in critical condition?

  1. ①The car should be authorized by the chief of the district police station.
  2. ②The driver should turn on the headlights or emergency lights.
  3. ③The car does not need to get special permission because it is transporting a patient in critical condition.
  4. ④The car must be escorted by another vehicle.
Show answer

Answer
② The driver should turn on the headlights or emergency lights.

Reference
In an emergency situation where an emergency vehicle cannot be called and a patient whose life is in danger must be transported by a regular vehicle, and the vehicle must be driven to a hospital, etc. while receiving concessions from drivers of surrounding vehicles, in order to receive special treatment as an emergency vehicle, the vehicle must turn on its headlights or emergency lights or indicate in other appropriate ways that it is being driven for urgent purposes.
