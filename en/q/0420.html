420. If you witness a fire caused by a traffic accident while driving inside a tunnel, which of the following is the most appropriate method of responding?

  1. ①It is safe to stop your vehicle even if traffic is still flowing.
  2. ②If traffic is at a standstill, stop the vehicle and wait inside until the fire is extinguished.
  3. ③If traffic is at a standstill, stop the vehicle and leave the scene with your car keys.
  4. ④If there is a lot of smoke after disembarking, lower your body as much as possible and move in the opposite direction of the smoke following the emergency lighting.
Show answer

Answer
④ If there is a lot of smoke after disembarking, lower your body as much as possible and move in the opposite direction of the smoke following the emergency lighting.

Reference
Correct response when witnessing a fire due to a car accident, etc. while passing through a tunnel: ① If traffic is possible, get out of the tunnel as quickly as possible. ② Even in the event of a fire, if visibility is adequate and traffic can move, move the vehicle outside. ③ If visibility is poor and traffic is congested or passage is impossible, stop the car in an emergency parking space or on the shoulder of the road. Turn off the engine and get out of the car with the key in place. Report to 119 using your cell phone or emergency phone in the tunnel and check if there are any injuries. If there is a lot of smoke, lower your body as much as possible and move in the opposite direction from where the smoke is coming from, following the guidance lights in the tunnel.
