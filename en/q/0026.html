26. Whom must the person likely to have his/her driver’s license suspended due to a violation of traffic laws according to the Road Traffic Act request when wishing to receive the recommended special traffic safety education? (Driving under the influence excluded)

  1. ①Chairperson of the Korea Road Traffic Authority
  2. ②Head of the local government having jurisdiction over one's domicile
  3. ③Head of a driver licensing office
  4. ④Commissioner of a City/Do police agency
Show answer

Answer
④ Commissioner of a City/Do police agency

Reference
Road Traffic Act, Article 73(3): A person in any of the following groups may apply to the city or provincial police chief for recommended special traffic safety training under the Presidential Decree, provided they have not taken that training during the preceding year. 1. A person whose license is to be suspended or has been suspended for traffic-law violations or other reasons apart from paragraph 2, items 2 and 4. 2. A person at risk of license suspension for traffic-law violations. 3. A person covered by paragraph 2, items 2–4 who has completed its mandatory special traffic safety training. 4. A license holder aged 65 or older on the intended training date.
