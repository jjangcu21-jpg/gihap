473. Which of the following is true about using turn signals?

  1. ①It is not a violation to change lanes without signaling if it does not obstruct the other vehicles.
  2. ②You must keep the turn signal on for a while after changing lanes.
  3. ③The traffic penalty for failure to signal lane change for passenger vehicles and buses is a fine of KRW 30,000.
  4. ④When changing lanes on an expressway, turn on the turn signal from 30 m before and keep it on until you have completed changing lanes.
Show answer

Answer
③ The traffic penalty for failure to signal lane change for passenger vehicles and buses is a fine of KRW 30,000.

Reference
① If you change your course without signaling, you are subject to a traffic law violation of failing to signal, even if you are not interfering with other traffic. ② After changing course, the signal must be stopped immediately. ③ Passenger cars and vans are subject to a fine of 30,000 won if they do not signal when changing course. ④ On an expressway, signal from 100 meters before the point where you begin changing lanes until the lane change is complete.
