595. Which of the following is the most advisable method of driving in order to improve a vehicle’s fuel efficiency?

  1. ①Drive with various tools and parts loaded so that you are prepared for a possible breakdown.
  2. ②Maintain a steady speed while driving.
  3. ③Perform quick starts, quick acceleration, and quick braking frequently.
  4. ④Drive with your tire pressure reduced by 30 percent for improved fuel efficiency.
Show answer

Answer
② Maintain a steady speed while driving.

Reference
Driving at a constant speed helps improve fuel efficiency.
