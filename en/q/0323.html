323. Which of the following is NOT a road facility that can be installed for the safety of the aged in a silver zone?

  1. ①Non-slip facilities, crash barriers
  2. ②Speed bumps, non-slip facilities
  3. ③Acceleration lane, silver zone sign
  4. ④Crash barriers, road reflector system
Show answer

Answer
③ Acceleration lane, silver zone sign

Reference
Article 7 (Installation of Sidewalks and Road Accessories) Paragraph 2 of the Rules for Designation and Management of Protection Zones for Children, the Elderly, and the Disabled The mayor, etc. may install any of the following road accessories in the protection zone or request the competent road management office to install them. 1. Protected area road signs according to the attached table 2. Road convex mirrors 3. Speeding prevention facilities 4. Anti-slip facilities 5. Protective fences 6. Other road accessories recognized by the mayor, etc. as necessary to protect children, the elderly, or the disabled from the risk of traffic accidents, and facilities that comply with the “Rules on Road Structure and Facility Standards”
