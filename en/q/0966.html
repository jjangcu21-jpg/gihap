966. Which of the following describes the most dangerous situation that may arise in the video? (Refer to the website)

Video questions play in the Gihap app or on the official site.

Official video ↗
  1. ①The large vehicle waiting on the right is about to move.
  2. ②The yellow vehicle on the other side is attempting to commit a traffic signal violation.
  3. ③The black vehicle turning right on the right is entering the first lane.
  4. ④A situation wherein a white passenger car in the opposite direction turns right without considering the outer wheel clearance.
Show answer

Answer
③ The black vehicle turning right on the right is entering the first lane.

Reference
When a car trying to turn right estimates the speed of a car going straight to be slow, there are many cases where it avoids parked cars and enters the first lane at once. Therefore, when there is a car turning right, it is necessary to refrain from making an absolute judgment that going straight has priority and to drive while anticipating the case where the driver of the right-turning car merges unsafely into your lane.
