439. According to the Road Traffic Act, at which reduced speed should a vehicle drive on an automobile road with two lanes in one direction when it is wet due to rain?

  1. ①80 km/h
  2. ②90 km/h
  3. ③72 km/h
  4. ④100 km/h
Show answer

Answer
③ 72 km/h

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules Article 19 (Speed of Cars, etc.) ② In case of bad weather due to rain, fog, snow, etc., notwithstanding paragraph 1, driving must be reduced in accordance with the following standards. 1. Cases where the vehicle must be driven at a speed reduced by 20/10 of the maximum speed. When it rains and the road surface is wet. If less than 20 millimeters of snow has accumulated
