310. Which of the following is NOT correct regarding the duty of a person who operates a school bus for children?

  1. ①When children are getting on a school bus, an adult guardian must be designated.
  2. ②After completing the trip, the operator must confirm that all children and infants have disembarked from the school bus.
  3. ③It is necessary to prepare and keep a record of seat belt wearing and confirmation of the accompanying guardian.
  4. ④A record of confirmation of wearing seat belts and accompanying guardians must be submitted to the supervisory authority of the facility operating the school bus every quarter.
Show answer

Answer
② After completing the trip, the operator must confirm that all children and infants have disembarked from the school bus.

Reference
Article 53, Paragraphs 3 and 6 of the Road Traffic Act When a person who operates a children's school bus puts a child or infant on a children's school bus, he or she must bring a guardian of adult age designated by the operator of the children's school bus with him. When a child or infant gets on or off the bus, the accompanying guardian must get out of the car and check that the child or infant gets on or off the vehicle safely, and must ensure that the child or infant remains seated while the bus is in operation. Necessary measures must be taken to protect children, such as ensuring that they wear seat belts. Article 53, Paragraph 6 of the Road Traffic Act. When operating a school bus for children pursuant to paragraph 3, a person who operates a school bus with a guardian may affix a sign indicating that the bus is riding with a guardian as prescribed by Ordinance of the Ministry of Public Administration and Security (hereinafter referred to as a "guardian riding sign"), and if anyone rides without a guardian riding with them, they must not affix a guardian riding sign. Paragraph 7 A person who operates a school bus for children must prepare and keep a record confirming the wearing of seat belts and accompanying guardians (hereinafter referred to as “safe driving record”) and submit the safe driving record every quarter to the head of the competent agency that supervises the facility that operates the school bus for children. ② is the duty of the children’s school bus driver.
