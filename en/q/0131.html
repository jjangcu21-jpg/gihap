131. Under the Act on Special Cases Concerning the Settlement of Traffic Accidents, what is the correct legal responsibility of a driver who causes a traffic accident resulting in injury or death while driving a vehicle in a condition of impaired normal driving due to drug or other substance abuse?

  1. ①The driver will be criminally charged despite being covered by liability car insurance and even after having settled with the accident victim(s).
  2. ②The driver will be exempted from criminal charges if he/she is covered by car insurance.
  3. ③The driver will be exempted from criminal charges if he/she is covered by comprehensive insurance.
  4. ④The driver will be exempted from criminal charges if he/she is covered by comprehensive insurance and he/she has settled with the accident victim(s).
Show answer

Answer
① The driver will be criminally charged despite being covered by liability car insurance and even after having settled with the accident victim(s).

Reference
As stipulated in Article 3(1) and Article 3(2), item 8, of the Act on Special Cases Concerning the Settlement of Traffic Accidents, if you drive while under the risk of being unable to drive normally due to the use of drugs and cause a traffic accident resulting in casualties, you will be punished by imprisonment for not more than 5 years or by a fine not exceeding 20 million won. This is an item subject to criminal punishment regardless of whether or not you have comprehensive insurance or liability insurance and whether there is an agreement.
