252. Which of the following is not a motor vehicle that is required to install or place a fire extinguisher?

  1. ①A passenger car with 5 or more seats
  2. ②A bus
  3. ③A truck
  4. ④A two-wheeled vehicle
Show answer

Answer
④ A two-wheeled vehicle

Reference
Article 11 of the Act on Installation and Management of Firefighting Facilities (Fire extinguishers installed or placed in automobiles) Paragraph 1 Among automobiles under Article 3, Paragraph 1 of the Automobile Management Act, a person who intends to manufacture, assemble, import, or sell a automobile that falls under any of the following items, or the owner of the automobile, shall install or furnish a fire extinguisher for the automobile. 1. Passenger vehicles with 5 or more seats 2. Passenger vans and buses 3. Trucks 4. Special vehicles (effective December 1, 2024)
