983. Which of the following driver behaviors is a violation of the Road Traffic Act? (Refer to the website)

Video questions play in the Gihap app or on the official site.

Official video ↗
  1. ①The driver observes the maximum speed limit of the road section.
  2. ②The driver safely changes lanes at a location where changing lane is permitted.
  3. ③The driver stops to protect pedestrians crossing the crosswalk.
  4. ④The driver stops at the stop line just before the intersection when the crosswalk signal turns red.
Show answer

Answer
④ The driver stops at the stop line just before the intersection when the crosswalk signal turns red.

Reference
Even when the crosswalk signal changed from green to red, the car's signal was red, so it had to stop at the stop line right before the crosswalk. All of the car drivers seen in this video violated traffic signals. (National Police Agency official notice issued on November 16, 2023) If the crosswalk stop line and the intersection stop line are close together and two vehicle traffic lights are following one signal system, and the vehicle signal is red after crossing according to the crosswalk signal and the vehicle passes the first stop line in front of the crosswalk and proceeds forward to the second stop line, whether it is a violation of Article 5 of the Road Traffic Act for signals and instructions is as follows. In a dual stop line in operation where two main signal lights are linked, stopping at the red signal at the first stop line in front of the crosswalk and then moving forward to the second stop line in front of the intersection while the vehicle signal light is still red after the pedestrian has completed the crossing is considered a violation of signals and instructions because the vehicle started without the green signal, which is the departure signal.
