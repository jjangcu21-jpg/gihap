818. Which of the following are the TWO safest ways to proceed in the given situation?

  1. ①Keep a lookout for a pedestrian suddenly crossing between the vehicles waiting for the green light.
  2. ②Enter the intersection quickly before the left-turn signal changes.
  3. ③Speed up to intimidate the jaywalking pedestrian.
  4. ④Turn on the hazard lights and slow down to protect the pedestrian.
  5. ⑤Change to lane 1 suddenly to avoid a collision with the pedestrian.
Show answer

Answer
① Keep a lookout for a pedestrian suddenly crossing between the vehicles waiting for the green light.
④ Turn on the hazard lights and slow down to protect the pedestrian.

Reference
Even if pedestrians jaywalk, drivers must be careful to ensure that pedestrians can cross safely.
