813. Which of the following are the TWO safest ways to proceed at the intersection in the given picture?

  1. ①The driver activated the left-turn signal when he/she was about to enter from lane A.
  2. ②The driver entering from lane A immediately entered lane a, made a turn, and proceeded north.
  3. ③The driver activated the right-turn signal when he/she was about to enter from lane B.
  4. ④The driver entering from lane B immediately entered lane b, made a turn, and then switched to lane B and headed north.
  5. ⑤The driver activated the left-turn signal while trying to exit after making a turn inside.
Show answer

Answer
① The driver activated the left-turn signal when he/she was about to enter from lane A.
② The driver entering from lane A immediately entered lane a, made a turn, and proceeded north.

Reference
The proposed intersection is a ‘spiral roundabout.’ Drivers entering from Lane A must yield to the turning car and then immediately enter lane a. In this case, the white dotted line must be used. The exit directions for Lane A are east and north. Drivers entering from lane B must yield to the turning car and then immediately enter lane b. The exit directions for Lane B are south and east. Also, when entering a roundabout, you must turn on the left turn signal, and when leaving a roundabout, you must turn on the right turn signal.
