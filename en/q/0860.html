860. Which of the following are the safe ways to drive when you are trying to switch to the first lane in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Look at the left side-view mirror to check the vehicles in lane 1.
  2. ②Use high-beam headlights to warn the vehicle that you are about to go to lane 1.
  3. ③Quickly switch to lane 1 as the agricultural machine may enter the road.
  4. ④Speed up since it is uphill.
  5. ⑤Maintain a safe distance as the two-wheeled vehicle in front may suddenly switch to lane 1.
Show answer

Answer
① Look at the left side-view mirror to check the vehicles in lane 1.
⑤ Maintain a safe distance as the two-wheeled vehicle in front may suddenly switch to lane 1.

Reference
If a safe distance is not secured, it is difficult to respond appropriately when the vehicle in front suddenly brakes or changes lanes. In particular, hill roads are more dangerous because you cannot see beyond the crest of the hill, so reduce your speed and leave a sufficient safe distance from the vehicle in front.
