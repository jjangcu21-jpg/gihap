430. A vehicle is approaching near the top of an uphill. Which of the following are TWO are safe ways of driving?

  1. ①Increase engine RPM (revolutions per minute) to reduce fuel consumption.
  2. ②At the top of the uphill, be sure to stop temporarily before starting.
  3. ③Maintain a safe distance from the front vehicle.
  4. ④Drive in a lower gear than a higher gear.
Show answer

Answer
③ Maintain a safe distance from the front vehicle.
④ Drive in a lower gear than a higher gear.

Reference
① If the RPM is high, fuel consumption is high. ② At the top of the uphill, you must drive slowly and do not necessarily have to stop. ③ It is best to increase the distance from the vehicle in front and maintain a safe distance. ④ It is better to drive in a low gear where the engine power is greater than in a high gear where the engine power is less.
