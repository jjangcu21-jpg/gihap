239. At an intersection, which of the following is the most correct method of turning left/right?

  1. ①When turning right, quickly pass through the intersection while carefully checking for pedestrians and bicycles that stop or proceed according to the signal.
  2. ②When turning left, you must pass through the outside of the intersection center.
  3. ③When turning right, reduce speed in advance while driving along the right edge of the road.
  4. ④When turning left at an intersection without traffic signals while a pedestrian is crossing the road, quickly pass in front of the pedestrian.
Show answer

Answer
③ When turning right, reduce speed in advance while driving along the right edge of the road.

Reference
When drivers of all vehicles wish to turn right at an intersection, they must first turn right while driving slowly along the right edge of the road. In this case, the driver of the car turning right must pay attention to pedestrians or bicycles stopping or proceeding according to the signal.
