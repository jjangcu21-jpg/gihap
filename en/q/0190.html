190. According to the Road Traffic Act, the driver of any type of vehicle must ( ) before reaching a crosswalk in a school zone with no traffic lights, regardless of pedestrians crossing. Which of the following best fills the parentheses?

  1. ①slow down
  2. ②stop
  3. ③slow down or stop
  4. ④reduce speed
Show answer

Answer
② stop

Reference
Article 27 of the Road Traffic Act (Protection of Pedestrians) ⑦ Drivers of all vehicles must temporarily stop in front of crosswalks installed in child protection zones where signals are not installed, regardless of whether pedestrians are crossing.
