408. Which of the following is NOT correct regarding overtaking according to the Road Traffic Act?

  1. ①To overtake another vehicle, you must pass on the left lane of the vehicle in front.
  2. ②If the centerline is a yellow broken line, it is possible to overtake when there is no vehicle in the opposite direction.
  3. ③In case of a reversible lane, overtaking is not allowed on the leftmost yellow broken line in the direction indicated by a traffic light.
  4. ④On an expressway with four lanes in one direction, vehicles driving on the right lane can move up to the first lane.
Show answer

Answer
④ On an expressway with four lanes in one direction, vehicles driving on the right lane can move up to the first lane.

Reference
① Article 21 of the Road Traffic Act (Overtaking Methods, etc.) Paragraph 1: Drivers of all vehicles must pass on the left side of the vehicle in front when passing another vehicle. ② Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules Annex 6, Center Line Marking (Road Marking No. 501) ③ When a variable lane is installed under Article 2 (Definition) No. 5 of the Road Traffic Act, the yellow dotted line on the far left of the direction of travel indicated by the traffic signal shall be the center line. ④ When overtaking in accordance with Article 16 Paragraph 1 and Article 39 Paragraph 1 of the Road Traffic Act and Appendix 9 of the Enforcement Rules, vehicles traveling in the right lane on a highway with three or more lanes each way cannot enter the first lane and may overtake using the immediately adjacent lane on their left.
