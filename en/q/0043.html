43. Which of the following CANNOT be considered a fake petroleum product for use as vehicle fuel?

  1. ①Gasoline mixed with methanol
  2. ②A product that contains less than 5% high-grade gasoline mixed with normal gasoline
  3. ③Diesel oil mixed with kerosene
  4. ④A product with less than 5% water mixed with diesel
Show answer

Answer
④ A product with less than 5% water mixed with diesel

Reference
Fake petroleum products defined in the Petroleum and Petroleum Substitute Fuel Business Act refer to those manufactured for the purpose of use as vehicle fuel by mixing petroleum products with other petroleum products (including petroleum products of different grades) or petrochemical products. Since there is no separate application standard for the mixing amount, even a small amount can be considered a fake petroleum product. The use of counterfeit petroleum products or fuels other than those intended for legitimate purposes leads to vehicle problems, traffic accidents, and environmental pollution due to an increase in harmful emissions. If water and sediment enters the product, such as gasoline or diesel fuel, it is considered a product of inadequate quality.
