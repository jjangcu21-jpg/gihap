764. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Drive while being extremely cautious about pedestrians with umbrellas.
  2. ②Speed up to pass quickly, as there are many pedestrians around.
  3. ③Even if you are not in a school zone, pay attention to pedestrian safety as you are in front of a school.
  4. ④Proceed slowly as there is an intersection ahead with no traffic lights and visibility is limited on both sides.
  5. ⑤Pedestrians may appear between parked cars, so honk to warn them.
Show answer

Answer
① Drive while being extremely cautious about pedestrians with umbrellas.
③ Even if you are not in a school zone, pay attention to pedestrian safety as you are in front of a school.

Reference
Even if it is not a child protection zone, on roads with many pedestrians and especially on rainy days, the visibility of pedestrians is limited due to umbrellas, so drivers should pay more attention to the safety of pedestrians. At intersections without signals, when visibility is not clear due to parked cars around, stop for a while instead of slowing down before entering, and avoid driving with the horn continuously sounding without a justifiable reason.
