98. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following best describes "yielding"?

  1. ①When driving continuously at slow speed, the driver must yield the lane by moving to the far left side of the road.
  2. ②When an emergency vehicle is approaching from behind, the driver must proceed quickly.
  3. ③At an intersection without a traffic light, the driver must yield the right of way to vehicles approaching from the left.
  4. ④Vehicles driving on a road with a yield sign must yield the lane to vehicles on other roads.
Show answer

Answer
④ Vehicles driving on a road with a yield sign must yield the lane to vehicles on other roads.

Reference
Even if an emergency vehicle is following you, you must yield the lane. In addition, traffic must be driven in accordance with the yielding driving standards at intersections without traffic control, and vehicles on roads with yield signs installed must yield the lane to other vehicles.
