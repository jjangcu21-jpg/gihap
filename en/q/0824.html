824. Which of the following are the TWO safest ways to proceed in the given situation?

  1. ①Quickly switch to the third lane to avoid vehicles that are changing lanes.
  2. ②Speed up to prevent vehicles in the first lane from merging.
  3. ③Use the horn and high-beam headlights to prevent vehicles from changing lanes.
  4. ④Yield to merging vehicles by slowing down.
  5. ⑤Slow down in advance as the braking distances are longer on wet roads.
Show answer

Answer
④ Yield to merging vehicles by slowing down.
⑤ Slow down in advance as the braking distances are longer on wet roads.

Reference
In traffic congestion, drivers may change lanes frequently to escape queues, so you must always drive while looking ahead, as route changes may occur frequently. Also, on rainy roads, the braking distance is longer than usual, so sufficient safety distance and deceleration are necessary.
