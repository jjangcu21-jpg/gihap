471. Which of the following should you adjust before you start driving in order to minimize injuries to the head and neck?

  1. ①Distance of the seat from the pedals
  2. ②Angle of the backrest
  3. ③Height of the headrest
  4. ④Seat height
Show answer

Answer
③ Height of the headrest

Reference
Drivers do not often adjust the headrest when adjusting the seat. Therefore, the headrest that can stabilize your head in case of impact in a traffic accident needs to be adjusted to fit your head.
