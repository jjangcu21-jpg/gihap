49. Which of the following is NOT correct regarding the characteristics of the fuel used in an LPG vehicle?

  1. ①It burns in a gaseous state, allowing the engine to operate smoothly.
  2. ②LPG for motor vehicles has a distinct odor.
  3. ③It is generally lighter than air and less explosive.
  4. ④It has a lower energy density compared to gasoline or diesel, resulting in lower output and lower fuel efficiency for vehicles equipped with an engine of the same capacity.
Show answer

Answer
③ It is generally lighter than air and less explosive.

Reference
It burns in a normal gaseous state, so the engine operates smoothly and the combustion efficiency is high, resulting in low emissions of pollutants such as carbon dioxide. LPG itself is colorless and odorless, but LPG for vehicles is mixed with a special scent to check for leaks. It is heavier than ordinary air and has a high risk of explosion. It has lower energy density than gasoline or diesel, resulting in lower output and lower fuel efficiency at the same displacement.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
