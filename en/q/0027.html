27. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, if a person who is blind in one eye intends to obtain a Class I driver's license for ordinary motor vehicles, the visual acuity of his/her other eye shall be at least ( ), he/she shall have altitudinal visual range of ( ) degrees and vertical visual range of 20 degrees, and there shall be no scotoma and hemianopia within the central visual range of 20 degrees. Which answer fits in the two ( )s?

  1. ①0.5, 50
  2. ②0.6, 80
  3. ③0.7, 100
  4. ④0.8, 120
Show answer

Answer
④ 0.8, 120

Reference
Article 45 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act (Standards for aptitude required for driving a car, etc.) However, if a person who is blind in one eye wishes to obtain a class 1 ordinary driver's license, the standard for aptitude required for driving a car, etc. must be that the visual acuity of the other eye is 0.8 or higher, the horizontal field of vision is 120 degrees or more, the vertical field of vision is 20 degrees or more, and there is no scotoma or hemianopia within 20 degrees of the central field of vision.
