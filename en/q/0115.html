115. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which TWO statements are NOT correct regarding personal mobility devices (PMDs)?

  1. ①The electric motor must not operate when traveling at speeds of over 25 km/h, and the device must weigh less than 30 kg.
  2. ②Anyone aged 13 or older may operate one without a license.
  3. ③A motor-driven bicycle powered solely by a motor is classified as a personal mobility device under the law.
  4. ④Personal mobility devices may freely use both bicycle lanes and sidewalks.
Show answer

Answer
② Anyone aged 13 or older may operate one without a license.
④ Personal mobility devices may freely use both bicycle lanes and sidewalks.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 2 (Definition) Personal mobility device refers to a motor-driven bicycle whose electric motor does not operate when driven at a speed of 25 kilometers per hour or more and whose vehicle weight is less than 30 kilograms, as prescribed by Ordinance of the Ministry of Public Administration and Security. A bicycle that can be moved solely by electric motor power is classified as a personal mobility device under the law.
