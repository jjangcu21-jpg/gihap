510. If a driver with a student license drives in inebriated state with blood alcohol content of at least ( ) percent, his/her student license shall be revoked. Which answer fits in ( )?

  1. ①0.03
  2. ②0.05
  3. ③0.08
  4. ④0.10
Show answer

Answer
① 0.03

Reference
According to Appendix 29 of the Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules, if a learner's license holder drives with a blood alcohol concentration of 0.03% or more, his or her learner's license will be revoked.
