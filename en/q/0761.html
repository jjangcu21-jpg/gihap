761. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Keep honking to pass a school zone, even if there are no pedestrians.
  2. ②Slow down to reduce the risk of an accident, as there may be vehicles crossing the centerline.
  3. ③Crossing the centerline is allowed, as there is an entrance to the parking lot in the building on the left.
  4. ④Slow down further to watch out for pedestrians, as the bus on the opposite side of the road obstructs your view.
  5. ⑤Parking is prohibited at speed bumps in a school zone, but stopping is allowed.
Show answer

Answer
② Slow down to reduce the risk of an accident, as there may be vehicles crossing the centerline.
④ Slow down further to watch out for pedestrians, as the bus on the opposite side of the road obstructs your view.

Reference
You must not continue sounding the horn without a justifiable reason, and even if you want to proceed to the left entrance, there is a center line, so you must not proceed beyond it. Speed ​​bumps in children's protection zones do not function as crosswalks. If there is a vehicle crossing the center line, it is safer to reduce the speed to avoid the risk of an accident. It is difficult to secure visibility due to the bus on the left, so proceed with special attention to the safety of pedestrians. Stopping and parking are prohibited in child protection areas except for permitted sections.
