295. Which of the following is the correct method of facilitating the safe boarding and disembarking of a child for a driver operating a school bus for children?

  1. ①Flashers must be used to ensure safety while the child is boarding the vehicle.
  2. ②If traffic is congested, turn off the flashers momentarily and allow the child to board.
  3. ③Drop off children around the school bus and leave immediately.
  4. ④Fastening a seatbelt is not required in a school zone.
Show answer

Answer
① Flashers must be used to ensure safety while the child is boarding the vehicle.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 53 (Obligations of Children's School Bus Drivers, Operators, etc.) ① A person driving a children's school bus must operate devices such as flashing lights only when children or infants are getting on or off the bus, and must display a sign in accordance with Article 51 (3) only when driving with children or infants. ② When a child or infant rides a children's school bus, the driver of a children's school bus must ensure that all children or infants on board are wearing seat belts before departing. When getting off, the driver must confirm that the children have reached at a safe place from cars, such as a sidewalk or roadside area, before departing.
