775. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Speed up as there are no speed limits on rural roads.
  2. ②Even if there is enough space to pass between your vehicle and the agricultural machine, stop for the safety of the person riding the cultivator.
  3. ③Maintain a safe distance from the agricultural machine by stopping temporarily or slowing down before approaching it.
  4. ④Never honk to avoid disturbing the agricultural machine driver.
  5. ⑤Stopping is prohibited in the roadside areas on both sides of the road, but parking is allowed.
Show answer

Answer
② Even if there is enough space to pass between your vehicle and the agricultural machine, stop for the safety of the person riding the cultivator.
③ Maintain a safe distance from the agricultural machine by stopping temporarily or slowing down before approaching it.

Reference
Rural roads are also considered roads under the Road Traffic Act (Article 2, Paragraph 1, C) of the Road Traffic Act. Even if there is no speed limit sign, the maximum speed on general roads is 60 kilometers per hour (Article 19, Paragraph 1, Item 1, Item B of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act). Since agricultural machinery noise is loud while operating agricultural machinery, a horn can be used to draw the attention of the agricultural machinery operator if necessary.
