545. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following are the maximum and minimum speed limits, respectively, to be observed in an automobile road?

  1. ①110 km/h, 50 km/h
  2. ②100 km/h, 40 km/h
  3. ③90 km/h, 30 km/h
  4. ④80 km/h, 20 km/h
Show answer

Answer
③ 90 km/h, 30 km/h

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules Article 19 (Speed of Cars, etc. and Streetcars) The maximum speed of cars on automobile-only roads is 90 kilometers per hour, and the minimum speed is 30 kilometers per hour.
