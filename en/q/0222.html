222. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following is the correct type of vehicle that may be driven in the left-side lane on roads other than expressways?

  1. ①Passenger cars and compact, small, and mid-sized buses
  2. ②Large passenger vehicles
  3. ③Trucks
  4. ④Special-purpose cars and two-wheeled vehicles
Show answer

Answer
① Passenger cars and compact, small, and mid-sized buses

Reference
Under Appendix 9 of the Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules, passenger cars and mini, small and medium-sized passenger vans/buses may use the left-side lanes on roads other than expressways.
