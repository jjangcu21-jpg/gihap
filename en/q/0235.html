235. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is the most appropriate method of slowing down at an intersection?

  1. ①Proceeding at a low speed such that the vehicle can be stopped immediately
  2. ②Proceeding while maintaining a speed of 30 km/h
  3. ③Proceeding at such a low speed that no accident occurs
  4. ④Proceeding at a speed that enables the driver to avoid any sudden stops by the vehicle in front
Show answer

Answer
① Proceeding at a low speed such that the vehicle can be stopped immediately

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 2 (Definition) No. 28. “Slow driving” refers to driving at a slow enough speed that the driver can immediately stop the car.
