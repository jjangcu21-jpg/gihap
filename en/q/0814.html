814. In the given situation, which of the following are the TWO safest ways to turn right?

  1. ①Turn right quickly when the forward-facing green light is on.
  2. ②Come to a complete stop and check for safety before turning right, as pedestrians may be crossing on the right.
  3. ③Stop because the dedicated right-turn signal is red.
  4. ④Even if the dedicated right-turn signal is red, you may turn right if it does not interfere with any pedestrians.
  5. ⑤Stop at the stop line and wait until the right-turn arrow signal turns green.
Show answer

Answer
③ Stop because the dedicated right-turn signal is red.
⑤ Stop at the stop line and wait until the right-turn arrow signal turns green.

Reference
Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act [Appendix Table 2] clearly states that a car intending to turn right must follow the three-color right turn light, despite other traffic lights. At intersections where three-color right turn lights are installed, proceeding through a red signal constitutes a traffic violation, and right turns are only possible when the right turn arrow light is on.
