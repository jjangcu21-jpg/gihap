146. Causing harm to the life and safety of others by repeatedly driving over the centerline or causing risk in the traffic flow are ( ) according to the Road Traffic Act. Which of the following fits in ( )?

  1. ①joint risky behavior
  2. ②reckless driving
  3. ③violent driving
  4. ④retaliatory driving
Show answer

Answer
② reckless driving

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 46-3 (Prohibition of Reckless Driving) Drivers of automobiles, etc. (excluding personal mobility devices) shall not commit two or more of the following acts in succession or continue or repeat one act to threaten or harm others or create traffic hazards.
