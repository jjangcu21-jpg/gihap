709. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Drive slowly or stop temporarily to protect pedestrians crossing the road.
  2. ②Proceed quickly as there is no obligation to protect pedestrians who are crossing the road.
  3. ③Anticipate that doors may potentially open on cars parked on the right side of the road.
  4. ④If there is not enough space for parking, parking in the red curb zone on the left side ahead is allowed.
  5. ⑤You can speed up as you need not watch out for people stepping out from behind parked cars.
Show answer

Answer
① Drive slowly or stop temporarily to protect pedestrians crossing the road.
③ Anticipate that doors may potentially open on cars parked on the right side of the road.

Reference
When pedestrians cross a road without a crosswalk, drivers must keep a safe distance and slow down or stop so pedestrians can cross safely. Anticipate that doors of parked vehicles may open. Both stopping and parking are prohibited along the red curb in front of a fire hydrant.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
