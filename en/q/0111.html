111. Which of the following are the proper ways of driving around a roundabout? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Vehicles already in the roundabout have priority.
  2. ②Enter a roundabout as quickly as possible.
  3. ③Turn on the hazard lights when entering a roundabout.
  4. ④The driving direction in a roundabout is counterclockwise.
Show answer

Answer
① Vehicles already in the roundabout have priority.
④ The driving direction in a roundabout is counterclockwise.

Reference
Article 25-2 (Method of passing through roundabouts) ① Drivers of all vehicles must travel counterclockwise at roundabouts. ② Drivers of all vehicles must slow down or stop temporarily when entering a roundabout, and if there is another vehicle already proceeding, they must give way to that vehicle.
