517. According to the Road Traffic Act, what is the correct penalty for a driver who, despite having reasonable grounds to be considered under the influence of alcohol, refuses to comply with a breath alcohol test legitimately requested by a police officer (for a first offense within ten years)?

  1. ①Imprisonment for at least one year but no more than two years, or criminal fine of up to KRW 5 million
  2. ②Imprisonment for at least one year but no more than three years, or criminal fine of up to KRW 10 million
  3. ③Imprisonment for at least one year but no more than four years, or criminal fine in the range of KRW 5 million - KRW 10 million
  4. ④Imprisonment for at least one year but no more than five years, or criminal fine in the range of KRW 5 million - KRW 20 million
Show answer

Answer
④ Imprisonment for at least one year but no more than five years, or criminal fine in the range of KRW 5 million - KRW 20 million

Reference
Article 148-2 (Penalty Provisions) of the Road Traffic Act ② Any person reasonably suspected of being intoxicated and who fails to comply with the measurement by a police official pursuant to Article 44 (2) (limited to persons driving a car, etc. or streetcar) shall be punished by imprisonment for not less than one year but not more than five years or by a fine not less than 5 million won but not more than 20 million won.
