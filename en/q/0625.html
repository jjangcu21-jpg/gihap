625. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following vehicles violates the safety standards of a truck's loading capacity?

  1. ①Length calculated by adding 2/10 of the vehicle length
  2. ②Width within the range of rearview for rear visibility
  3. ③3.9 m above the ground
  4. ④105% of payload depending on construction and performance
Show answer

Answer
① Length calculated by adding 2/10 of the vehicle length

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Decree, Article 22, implementing Article 39(1) of the Act: 3. Truck cargo weight must not exceed 110% of the rated payload for the vehicle’s structure and performance. 4. Cargo dimensions for trucks, two-wheeled vehicles and small three-wheeled vehicles must not exceed: (a) Length: vehicle length plus one tenth; for two-wheeled vehicles, the length of the seating or loading device plus 30 cm. (b) Width: the range that allows rearward visibility through the rear-view mirrors. If cargo is below mirror height, the cargo must be visible; if above, the rearward view must be visible. (c) Height from the ground: trucks 4 m, or 4.20 m on officially designated routes where road structure and traffic safety permit; small three-wheelers 2.50 m; two-wheelers 2 m.
