843. Which of the following are the TWO safest ways to proceed in the given situation?

  1. ①Be cautious, as a child may suddenly run out from behind the school bus.
  2. ②Since there are no pedestrians at the crosswalk ahead without a traffic light, proceed through it slowly.
  3. ③Come to a complete stop before the crosswalk, as children running on the right may suddenly dart out onto the crosswalk.
  4. ④In this zone, only school buses are allowed to park or stop temporarily.
  5. ⑤As children are getting on or off the school bus ahead on the right, stop before passing and then proceed carefully.
Show answer

Answer
① Be cautious, as a child may suddenly run out from behind the school bus.
③ Come to a complete stop before the crosswalk, as children running on the right may suddenly dart out onto the crosswalk.

Reference
② Drivers must temporarily stop in front of crosswalks installed in child protection zones where signals are not installed (if a stop line is installed, this refers to the stop line) regardless of whether pedestrians are crossing. (Road Traffic Act, Article 27, Paragraph 7) ④ Parking and stopping is prohibited within child protection zones (Road Traffic Act, Article 32, Paragraph 8). ⑤ The yellow flashing lights on children's school buses are used when stopping on the road and when the passenger door is closed for departure. (Article 48, Paragraph 4 of the Rules on Performance and Standards of Automobiles and Automobile Parts)
