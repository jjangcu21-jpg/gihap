375. Which of the following is a place where you should stop temporarily pursuant to the Road Traffic Act?

  1. ①Inside a tunnel and on a bridge
  2. ②At a busy intersection with no traffic lights
  3. ③On a descending section of a steep hill
  4. ④A curved section of a road
Show answer

Answer
② At a busy intersection with no traffic lights

Reference
There are different regulations for areas where overtaking is prohibited and areas where you must stop temporarily. Overtaking is prohibited in tunnels, on bridges, and on steep downhill roads and near bends in the road. Intersections where traffic is not being controlled and left and right cannot be checked, or where traffic is frequent, and where the Commissioner of the City/Provincial Police Agency has designated a safety sign as deemed necessary to prevent danger on the road and ensure safety and smooth traffic flow, are places where you must temporarily stop. (Road Traffic Act, Article 31, Paragraph 2)
