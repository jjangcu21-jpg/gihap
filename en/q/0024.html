24. Below are descriptions of the verification process for identifying individuals wishing to obtain a driver’s license according to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree. Which of the following is NOT correct?

  1. ①If the resident registration card has been lost, identification can be performed via the confirmation of application for the issuance of a resident registration card.
  2. ②An individual who cannot be verified by either identification certificate or fingerprint information is not permitted to take the test.
  3. ③Fingerprint information may be compared and verified using an electronic method without the applicant's consent.
  4. ④If the applicant refuses to verify his/her own identity, the issuance of a driver’s license may be denied.
Show answer

Answer
③ Fingerprint information may be compared and verified using an electronic method without the applicant's consent.

Reference
Road Traffic Act, Article 87-2, and Enforcement Rules, Article 57: An applicant who cannot present identification may, if they wish, have their identity verified by electronically comparing fingerprint information.
