630. Which of the following is the traffic penalty imposed on the driver of a 4.5-ton truck for driving with a person in the freight container?

  1. ①KRW 50,000
  2. ②KRW 40,000
  3. ③KRW 30,000
  4. ④KRW 20,000
Show answer

Answer
① KRW 50,000

Reference
According to Article 49, Paragraph 1, No. 12 of the Road Traffic Act, Appendix 8 of the Enforcement Decree, a fine of 50,000 won is imposed if a passenger rides in the cargo compartment of a truck exceeding 4 tons.
