100. Under the Act on the Promotion of Transportation Convenience of Mobility Disadvantaged Persons, which of the following is not considered a “pedestrian safety facility” for mobility disadvantaged persons?

  1. ①Speed reduction facility
  2. ②Bicycle lane
  3. ③Transportation information facilities such as public traffic information system
  4. ④Traffic signal for pedestrians' right of way
Show answer

Answer
② Bicycle lane

Reference
Article 21 (Installation of Pedestrian Safety Facilities) Paragraph 1 of the Act on Promotion of Mobility for the Transportation Disadvantaged The mayor or county head may install the following pedestrian safety facilities to enable pedestrians to walk safely and conveniently in pedestrian priority areas. 1. Speed ​​reduction facility. 2. Crossing facilities. 3. Transportation information facilities such as public transportation information notification facilities. 4 Traffic signals for pedestrian priority passage. 5. Stakes to deter vehicle entry. 6. Pedestrian route guidance devices such as acoustic signals for the transportation vulnerable. 7. Other facilities prescribed by Presidential Decree for the safety and convenience of movement of pedestrians.
