192. Who is NOT allowed to drive on the road according to the Road Traffic Act?

  1. ①A person using a wheelchair to aid his/her movement
  2. ②A person marching on the streets for an important social event
  3. ③A person cleaning or doing maintenance work on a road
  4. ④A person carrying an object that can hinder pedestrian thoroughfare, such as a ladder
Show answer

Answer
① A person using a wheelchair to aid his/her movement

Reference
<Article 7 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act (Persons or Processions Allowed to Pass on Roads)> In the first part of Article 9 (1) of the Act, “persons or processions prescribed by Presidential Decree” means any of the following persons or processions: 1. A person driving a large animal such as a horse or cow 2. A person carrying a ladder, lumber, or other items that may impede pedestrian traffic 3. A person performing work such as cleaning or repairing a road 4. A procession of a military unit or other similar organization 5. A procession carrying a flag or banner, etc. 6. A funeral procession
