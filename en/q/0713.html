713. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Slow down to proceed as there is a construction sign.
  2. ②Speed up and drive with the high-beam headlights on to alert the vehicle that crossed the centerline ahead of you.
  3. ③Turn on the hazard lights, and then speed up to proceed quickly.
  4. ④Slow down to avoid an accident and watch out for vehicles from behind.
  5. ⑤Stopping is not allowed at the right-side edge of the road.
Show answer

Answer
① Slow down to proceed as there is a construction sign.
④ Slow down to avoid an accident and watch out for vehicles from behind.

Reference
Since this is a road under construction, you must check safety and proceed slowly and with caution. Additionally, the safety of vehicles moving in front and behind is checked. Parking is permitted on the solid white lines along the road edge, but in construction zones, parking is prohibited and stopping is permitted.
