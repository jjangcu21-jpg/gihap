314. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following may be prohibited from entering or driving in a silver zone?

  1. ①Personal mobility devices (PMD), bicycles
  2. ②Truck-mounted drilling rigs and kick scooters for children
  3. ③Motorcycles and rollators with width of 1 m or less
  4. ④Road stabilizers and walking frames with width of 1 m or less
Show answer

Answer
① Personal mobility devices (PMD), bicycles

Reference
Article 12-2 of the Road Traffic Act (Designation, cancellation and management of protection zones for the elderly and the disabled) ① If the mayor, etc. deems it necessary to protect the elderly or the disabled from the risk of traffic accidents, designate a certain section of the roads around facilities or places under subparagraphs 1 through 3 and 3-2 as a protection zone for the elderly and a certain section of the roads around the facilities under subparagraph 4 as a protection zone for the disabled, and take necessary measures such as restricting or prohibiting the passage of vehicles, horses and trams. Bicycles are also considered vehicles.
