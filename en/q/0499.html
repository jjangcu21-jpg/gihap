499. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following statements regarding the administrative fine for parking violations is correct?

  1. ①The owner of a passenger car involved in a parking violation should pay an administrative fine of KRW 30,000.
  2. ②The owner of a van involved in a parking violation should pay an administrative fine of KRW 70,000.
  3. ③No administrative fine is imposed even if you do not pay the fine within the designated period.
  4. ④For parking violations that continue for more than two hours at the same location, additional administrative fines are imposed.
Show answer

Answer
④ For parking violations that continue for more than two hours at the same location, additional administrative fines are imposed.

Reference
In case of violation of Article 33 of the Road Traffic Act and Appendix 6 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act, a fine of 40,000 won is imposed for passenger cars and 50,000 won for vans. In case of violation of parking for 2 hours or more, an additional fine of 10,000 won is imposed, and additional fines and additional fines are imposed in case of non-payment.
