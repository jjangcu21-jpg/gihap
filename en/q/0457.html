457. Which of the following are the TWO correct measures to take when a fire starts inside a tunnel that you are driving in?

  1. ①Turn off the engine if you are getting out of the car to evacuate.
  2. ②Stand with your back to the wind when using a fire extinguisher.
  3. ③If you are unable to drive out of the tunnel, stop the car as close to the centerline as possible.
  4. ④Take the key out of the ignition if you are getting out of the car to evacuate.
Show answer

Answer
① Turn off the engine if you are getting out of the car to evacuate.
② Stand with your back to the wind when using a fire extinguisher.

Reference
① The engine must be turned off to prevent danger such as explosion. ③ Stop your vehicle near the side wall so that emergency vehicles can pass. ④ You must keep the car key in the ignition in order to take action in case another situation arises.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
