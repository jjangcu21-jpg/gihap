562. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following is true about overtaking?

  1. ①If the vehicle in front of you has another vehicle traveling side by side on its right, you cannot overtake the vehicle in front of you.
  2. ②Overtaking is allowed in recently built tunnels, bridges, and intersections.
  3. ③Overtaking is when you approach from behind and pass the vehicle on the left, traveling in the same direction.
  4. ④A passenger vehicle can use the exclusive bus lane on an expressway to overtake another vehicle.
Show answer

Answer
③ Overtaking is when you approach from behind and pass the vehicle on the left, traveling in the same direction.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 2 (Definition) No. 29, Article 21
