500. Which of the following is a scenario where the exemption according to the Act on Special Cases Concerning the Settlement of Traffic Accidents is applied because the driver who caused the accident has comprehensive insurance or he/she is insured through a mutual aid association?

  1. ①When the driver has violated his/her duty to drive safely, resulting in car damage or minor accident
  2. ②When the driver has caused an accident that resulted in loss of human life
  3. ③When the driver has fled the scene after causing an accident without attending to the injured
  4. ④When the driver has violated traffic signals, causing an accident involving minor injuries
Show answer

Answer
① When the driver has violated his/her duty to drive safely, resulting in car damage or minor accident

Reference
Article 4 of the Traffic Accident Handling Special Act
