359. Which of the following INCORRECTLY describes a safe method of using a roundabout?

  1. ①Slow down before entering a roundabout.
  2. ②Stop temporarily at the yield line and enter slowly.
  3. ③A vehicle entering the roundabout has the right of way, so the vehicle making a turn in the roundabout must yield.
  4. ④Turn counterclockwise.
Show answer

Answer
③ A vehicle entering the roundabout has the right of way, so the vehicle making a turn in the roundabout must yield.

Reference
Article 25-2 of the Road Traffic Act (Method of passing through roundabouts) ① Drivers must travel counterclockwise at roundabouts. ② When entering a roundabout, you must slow down or stop temporarily, and if there is another vehicle already proceeding, you must give way to that vehicle.
