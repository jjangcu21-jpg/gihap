313. Which of the following is NOT an authority that a commissioner of the City/Do (Province) police agency or a police chief can exercise in a silver zone?

  1. ①Prohibiting or restricting the passage of vehicles and horses
  2. ②Designating and operating a back road as a one-way street
  3. ③Setting a speed limit of 30 km/h for horses and vehicles
  4. ④Designating an on-road parking lot for the elderly on a road next to the gate of a house
Show answer

Answer
④ Designating an on-road parking lot for the elderly on a road next to the gate of a house

Reference
According to Paragraph 1 of Article 9 (Necessary Measures in Protection Zones) of the Rules for Designation and Management of Protection Zones for Children, the Elderly, and the Disabled, city/provincial police agencies or police chiefs may take the following measures by section and time zone in the protection zone: 1. Prohibiting or restricting the passage of cars and horses. 2. Prohibiting stopping or parking of vehicles and horses. 3. Limiting driving speed to 30 kilometers or less per hour. 4. Designating and operating side roads as one-way streets
