494. Which TWO things must be inspected before starting a long-distance drive?

  1. ①Cleanliness of the vehicle
  2. ②DMB operation status
  3. ③Oils and fluids
  4. ④Tires
Show answer

Answer
③ Oils and fluids
④ Tires

Reference
Before driving long distances, you should check tire wear, air pressure, various oils, wiper and washer fluid, lamps, etc.
