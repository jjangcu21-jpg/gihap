601. Which of the following is true about an FCEV?

  1. ①Hydrogen is highly flammable, and all drivers of FCEVs must complete special education as stipulated in the High-pressure Gas Safety Control Act.
  2. ②FCEVs burn hydrogen, causing air pollution.
  3. ③FCEVs are not equipped with safety equipment that is used to prevent explosions in case of an emergency such as fire.
  4. ④The driver of an FCEV must regularly inspect the vehicle to ensure safety and drive safely.
Show answer

Answer
④ The driver of an FCEV must regularly inspect the vehicle to ensure safety and drive safely.

Reference
① In accordance with Annex Table 31 of the Enforcement Rules of the High-Pressure Gas Safety Management Act, drivers who wish to newly drive a hydrogen large van (capacity of 36 or more passengers) must complete special training, but other drivers are not subject to this training requirement. ② Hydrogen cars do not burn hydrogen by driving the motor with electricity generated from a chemical reaction between hydrogen stored in a tank and oxygen to move the car. ③ Hydrogen cars are equipped with a safety valve to prevent explosion of the container when the temperature rises due to a fire or other reasons. In the event of an emergency, the safety valve is opened and hydrogen is released to the outside to prevent an explosion. ④ In accordance with Article 7 of the Traffic Safety Act, a person who drives a vehicle must check whether the vehicle has any problems in safe operation and drive safely as prescribed by the law.
