800. You are approaching an intersection while traveling straight from the left lane (first lane). Which of the following are the TWO safest ways to drive?

  1. ①There is no traffic in the opposite direction, so overtake the car ahead to the left side.
  2. ②Slow down and approach the taxi in the first lane with a safe distance.
  3. ③Honk to stop the taxi and quickly pass to its right side.
  4. ④Continue to switch to the third lane and stop.
  5. ⑤Slow down as you approach the taxi and pedestrians if you are switching to the second lane.
Show answer

Answer
② Slow down and approach the taxi in the first lane with a safe distance.
⑤ Slow down as you approach the taxi and pedestrians if you are switching to the second lane.

Reference
A taxi traveling in the first lane checks for pedestrians on the right, brakes, and operates the steering to the right. Therefore, drivers following behind must be careful of the taxi's sudden deceleration and maintain a safe distance. Also, when changing course to the right, it is expected that passengers will be boarded after stopping in the second lane to avoid parked cars, so you must slow down and approach the intersection.
