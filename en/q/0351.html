351. Which of the following are TWO statements correctly describe how a driver should behave?

  1. ①The passenger is responsible for his/her safety when exiting the vehicle.
  2. ②Even when parking temporarily to buy something, the driver must turn off the engine.
  3. ③Before exiting a vehicle, the driver must turn off the engine and check that it is safe.
  4. ④When parking in an area other than a parking lot, the parking brake must be left released so that the vehicle can be moved if necessary.
Show answer

Answer
② Even when parking temporarily to buy something, the driver must turn off the engine.
③ Before exiting a vehicle, the driver must turn off the engine and check that it is safe.

Reference
When a driver leaves the driver's seat, it is stipulated that the driver must take necessary measures to keep the car safely stopped, such as turning off the car's engine and using the brakes thoroughly, and to prevent unauthorized use by others.
