995. You want to exit from an expressway. What is the most appropriate way to do so? (Refer to the website)

Video questions play in the Gihap app or on the official site.

Official video ↗
  1. ①Cross lanes continuously without delay for a quick exit.
  2. ②Change lanes quickly by sharply reducing your speed.
  3. ③Reduce your speed from the deceleration lane while checking the speedometer.
  4. ④Pass through the exit road quickly by accelerating when there is no car in front in the deceleration lane.
Show answer

Answer
③ Reduce your speed from the deceleration lane while checking the speedometer.

Reference
Driving on the highway is bound to be stressful due to the high speed. Therefore, when exiting from the main lane to the off-ramp, there is a car following behind, so if you slow down quickly, there is a risk of a collision with the car behind, so be careful. When you leave the main lane and enter the deceleration lane, you must slow down clearly by looking at the speedometer and not relying on your senses.
