753. Which of the following are not defined as "actions obstructing parking in a parking space reserved for persons with disabilities" under the Act on the Guarantee of Convenience Promotion of Persons with Disabilities, Senior Citizens, Pregnant Women, and Nursing Mothers? (Select TWO)

  1. ①An act of obstructing a parking space reserved for persons with disabilities by stacking objects
  2. ②An act of stacking or parking objects in front, behind, or on either side of a parking space reserved for persons with disabilities
  3. ③An act of parking a car without a permit in a parking space reserved for persons with disabilities
  4. ④An act of obstructing a parking space reserved for persons with disabilities by erasing or damaging its lines and signs
  5. ⑤An act of parking a car with a permit in a parking space reserved for persons with disabilities, but the car is not occupied by a person with impaired mobility
Show answer

Answer
③ An act of parking a car without a permit in a parking space reserved for persons with disabilities
⑤ An act of parking a car with a permit in a parking space reserved for persons with disabilities, but the car is not occupied by a person with impaired mobility

Reference
Article 17 of the Act on Guarantee of Convenience for Persons with Disabilities, Elderly, Pregnant Women, etc. (Parking Areas for Persons with Disabilities, etc.) - No one shall park a vehicle in a parking area for persons with disabilities unless the parking sign for the area for persons with disabilities is attached pursuant to paragraph 2. The same applies even if a person with a walking disability is not riding in a car with a parking sign for a parking space reserved for the disabled. - No one may do anything that interferes with parking, such as stacking objects in a parking space exclusively for the disabled or blocking the passage. Article 9 of the Enforcement Decree of the Act on Guarantee of Convenience for the Disabled, Elderly, Pregnant Women, etc. (Acts Interfering with Parking in Parking Areas Exclusively for the Disabled) Acts interfering with parking pursuant to Article 17, Paragraph 5 of the Act are the following acts. 1. Obstructing parking by stacking objects in the disabled-only parking space 2. Stacking or parking objects in front, behind, or on both sides of the disabled-only parking space 3. Stacking or parking objects in the entrance to the disabled-only parking area 4. Obstructing parking by erasing or damaging the disabled-only parking zone lines and disabled-only signs 5. Other acts that interfere with parking in the disabled-only parking space
