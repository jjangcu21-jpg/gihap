839. Which of the following are the desirable ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Come to a complete stop before reaching the bicycle.
  2. ②Come to a complete stop at the stop line before reaching the crosswalk.
  3. ③Since the distance to the vehicle behind is short, accelerate to increase the distance.
  4. ④Since there are no people on the crosswalk, proceed through it as is.
  5. ⑤Honk continuously and proceed at a low speed.
Show answer

Answer
① Come to a complete stop before reaching the bicycle.
② Come to a complete stop at the stop line before reaching the crosswalk.

Reference
Risk prediction. Cars that violate stopping and parking in child protection zones are identified. Drivers passing through child protection zones should always remember that one of the physical characteristics of children is their ‘small stature’, which can hide them behind illegally stopped or parked vehicles. In the picture in question, a bicycle is trying to cross the road in front of the gray car on the left. In these cases, you must stop even if it is not a crosswalk. Article 12, Paragraph 3 of the Road Traffic Act. Drivers of cars, horses or streetcars must comply with the measures pursuant to paragraph 1 in child protection areas and operate while paying attention to the safety of children. Article 27, Paragraph 7 of the Road Traffic Act. The driver of any car or streetcar must temporarily stop in front of a crosswalk (referring to the stop line if a stop line is installed) where a signal is not installed among the crosswalks installed in the child protection zone under Article 12 (1), regardless of whether a pedestrian is crossing.
