332. Which of the following statements about senior protection zones under the Road Traffic Act is incorrect?

  1. ①The authority to designate, cancel, and manage such zones lies with the mayor, governor, or local administrator.
  2. ②The local police chief may designate and operate side streets as one-way roads.
  3. ③The local police chief may restrict traffic within senior protection zones.
  4. ④The mayor or governor may limit the driving speed to 30 km/h or less.
Show answer

Answer
④ The mayor or governor may limit the driving speed to 30 km/h or less.

Reference
Articles 3 and 9 of the rules on designation and management of protection zones for children, the elderly, and the disabled. It is the authority of the city/provincial police chief or police chief to limit the driving speed to 30 kilometers per hour.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
