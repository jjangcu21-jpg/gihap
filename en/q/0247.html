247. You are driving in a city that operates an exclusive median bus lane system. Which of the following are safe ways of driving? (Select TWO)

  1. ①You should continuously honk to prevent other vehicles from cutting in front of you.
  2. ②You should drive with caution as pedestrians on the right may try to jaywalk.
  3. ③You should slow down as pedestrians may cross the road from the bus station on the left.
  4. ④You should drive and pass through a median bus lane as quickly as possible before the traffic light turns red.
Show answer

Answer
② You should drive with caution as pedestrians on the right may try to jaywalk.
③ You should slow down as pedestrians may cross the road from the bus station on the left.

Reference
When driving on a city road in the central bus lane (BRT) section, you must be careful and drive slowly as there are many pedestrian jaywalking accidents due to the bus stop being located in the center and the crosswalk length being short.
