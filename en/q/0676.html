676. Which of the following is the most desirable way to drive a recovery vehicle when responding to an accident scene?

  1. ①Speedy arrival is the foremost priority, so it is acceptable to drive on the opposite side.
  2. ②It is acceptable to drive over the speed limit because a recovery vehicle is classified as an emergency vehicle.
  3. ③On a congested expressway, drive along the roadside while honking loudly.
  4. ④Drive safely to prevent more accidents, while striving to get to the scene of the accident as quickly as possible.
Show answer

Answer
④ Drive safely to prevent more accidents, while striving to get to the scene of the accident as quickly as possible.

Reference
When approaching a traffic accident scene, you must drive safely as the competition for towing will put you under psychological pressure and increase the possibility of causing a traffic accident.
