842. Which of the following are safe ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Move into the left-hand lane past the centerline to maintain a safe distance from the mobility aid device.
  2. ②Slow down and assess the situation before reaching the electric wheelchair.
  3. ③Accelerate quickly to pass the device before it enters the lane.
  4. ④Sound the horn repeatedly to incite the device to stop and refrain from entering the road.
  5. ⑤Stop temporarily to ensure the device’s safety as it is about to enter the road.
Show answer

Answer
② Slow down and assess the situation before reaching the electric wheelchair.
⑤ Stop temporarily to ensure the device’s safety as it is about to enter the road.

Reference
Risk prediction. The situation in the picture is that an elderly person using an electric wheelchair is trying to enter the roadway. At these times, many drivers move to the left and cross the center line to avoid the electric wheelchair. It is important to remember that this behavior violates road traffic laws and is a dangerous behavior with a high possibility of colliding with cars going in the opposite direction. Therefore, when an electric wheelchair or an elderly pedestrian is identified, the driver must maintain right-hand traffic, slow down before reaching the electric wheelchair or elderly pedestrian, and observe what they are doing. Also, in the situation shown in the picture, an electric wheelchair enters the roadway and is about to cross it, so pedestrians must be protected by temporarily stopping regardless of whether there are crossing facilities or not.
