661. According to the Regulations on the Performance and Standards of Vehicles and Automotive Parts, what is the maximum length of a motor vehicle? (Not referring to connected cars)

  1. ①13 m
  2. ②14 m
  3. ③15 m
  4. ④16 m
Show answer

Answer
① 13 m

Reference
Article 4 of the Rules on Performance and Standards of Automobiles and Automobile Parts (Length, Width and Height) ① The length, width and height of automobiles shall not exceed the following standards. 1. Length: 13 meters (16.7 meters in the case of connected cars) 2. Width: 2.5 meters (in the case of indirect visibility devices, ventilation devices, or windows that open outwards, the width of these devices is 25 centimeters for passenger cars and 30 centimeters for other cars. However, if the width of the towed vehicle exceeds that of the towing vehicle, the towing vehicle’s indirect-vision devices are subject to the following limit. (Must not exceed 10 centimeters on the outermost side of the towed vehicle) 3. Height: 4 meters
