195. According to the Road Traffic Act, which TWO correctly describe the rules for a pedestrian-only road?

  1. ①The driver of a motor vehicle or a horse allowed on a pedestrian-only road must travel at the same speed as the pedestrians.
  2. ②In principle, the driver of a motor vehicle or a horse may use a pedestrian-only road.
  3. ③The driver of a motor vehicle or a horse may not be allowed to enter a pedestrian-only road if and when deemed necessary by the chief of police.
  4. ④If there is a possibility that pedestrians could be exposed to danger, the driver of a motor vehicle or a horse on a pedestrian-only road must stop and remain stationary.
Show answer

Answer
① The driver of a motor vehicle or a horse allowed on a pedestrian-only road must travel at the same speed as the pedestrians.
④ If there is a possibility that pedestrians could be exposed to danger, the driver of a motor vehicle or a horse on a pedestrian-only road must stop and remain stationary.

Reference
Article 28 of the Road Traffic Act (Installation of pedestrian-only roads) ① City/provincial police chiefs or police chiefs may install pedestrian-only roads on roads when it is particularly necessary to protect pedestrian traffic. ② Drivers of cars and horses shall not travel on the pedestrian-only road under paragraph (1). However, if the city/provincial police chief or police chief deems it particularly necessary, he/she may permit the passage of cars and horses on the pedestrian-only road. ③ Drivers of cars and horses permitted to pass on pedestrian-only roads pursuant to the proviso to paragraph 2 shall drive their cars and horses at the walking speed of pedestrians or stop temporarily so as not to endanger pedestrians or impede the passage of pedestrians.
