Reference
Drivers who are overly confident in their overall driving behavior or who recklessly violate traffic laws are mostly drivers who lack the ability to predict risks. Drivers who do not understand the dangers of violating traffic laws or failing to make every effort to drive safely are more likely to violate laws and drive dangerously. The dangers you see on the road are not all. You must always try to see potential dangers and think about what driving behavior you can do for others to reduce the possibility of an accident.
