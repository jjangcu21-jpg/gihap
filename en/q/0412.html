412. Which of the following describes aquaplaning most appropriately?

  1. ①You must change tires with wider widths to reduce the risks associated with aquaplaning.
  2. ②Aquaplaning is more common in snow than rain. You must reduce speed.
  3. ③Tires with worn-down treads will experience less aquaplaning due to reduced traction.
  4. ④Underinflation can cause the tires to aquaplane more, especially at higher speeds.
Show answer

Answer
④ Underinflation can cause the tires to aquaplane more, especially at higher speeds.

Reference
Wide tires, low air pressure, and worn treads are more likely to cause hydroplaning, and new tires reduce the likelihood of hydroplaning.
