237. Which of the following is the correct method of turning right at an intersection with no traffic lights?

  1. ①Turn right as fast as possible.
  2. ②Wait until the vehicles entering the intersection ahead of you pass by, and then turn right.
  3. ③When a vehicle coming from the opposite direction is turning left, turn right at the same time with caution.
  4. ④There is no need to be mindful of other vehicles when turning right from a wide road onto a narrow one.
Show answer

Answer
② Wait until the vehicles entering the intersection ahead of you pass by, and then turn right.

Reference
When turning right at an intersection, you must turn right slowly and yield the lane to the left-turning vehicle that entered first. Also, be careful of other vehicles when turning right from a wide road to a narrow road.
