410. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is true about the standard for driving on an expressway with three lanes in one direction? (Assuming smooth traffic flow and no exclusive bus lanes)

  1. ①The first lane is the lane for overtaking a passenger car driving on the second lane.
  2. ②The first lane is the driving lane for a van.
  3. ③The roadside is the driving lane for emergency vehicles and tow vehicles.
  4. ④When operating an exclusive bus lane, the first lane is the driving lane for trucks.
Show answer

Answer
① The first lane is the lane for overtaking a passenger car driving on the second lane.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules Appendix 9: On expressways with at least three lanes in each direction, lane 1 is for passenger cars and mini, small and medium-sized passenger vans/buses overtaking. They may also use it without overtaking when road conditions, such as heavy traffic, inevitably limit speed to below 80 km/h. The left-side lanes are for those same passenger vehicle categories. The right-side lanes are for large passenger buses, cargo vehicles, special-purpose vehicles and construction machinery under Article 2, item 18(b).
