498. Which of the following statements regarding the standards for demerit points based on the effects of a traffic accident is correct?

  1. ①As for the personal injury of the driver who will receive an administrative punishment, demerit points will be imposed based on the rules regarding traffic accidents involving personal injury.
  2. ②In an accident between a car and a pedestrian, demerit points will not be imposed if the accident occurred due to mistakes committed by both parties.
  3. ③If the accident was unavoidable or had been caused clearly by a mistake of the injured, demerit points will be halved.
  4. ④In an accident between cars, demerit points are imposed only on the driver who committed a serious violation that caused the accident.
Show answer

Answer
④ In an accident between cars, demerit points are imposed only on the driver who committed a serious violation that caused the accident.

Reference
In case ①, penalty points are not calculated for the damage caused to the driver who is subject to administrative disposition. In case ②, the amount is reduced by 1/2. In case ③, no penalty points are imposed. (Appendix Table 28 of the Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules)
