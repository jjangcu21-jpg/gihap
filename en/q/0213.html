213. According to the Road Traffic Act, the width of a lane installed shall be ( ) m or wider. In such cases where an unavoidable need is recognized, such as for the installation of a left turn-only lane, it may be set to ( ) cm or wider. Which combination of answers fits in the two parentheses?

  1. ①5, 300
  2. ②4, 285
  3. ③3, 275
  4. ④2, 265
Show answer

Answer
③ 3, 275

Reference
According to Article 15 (Installation of Lanes) Paragraph 2 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act, the width of the lane shall be at least 3 meters, but in cases where it is deemed unavoidable, such as installation of a left turn only lane, it may be at least 275 centimeters.
