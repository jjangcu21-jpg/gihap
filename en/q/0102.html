102. What is the best way of proceeding when turning left at a congested intersection?

  1. ①If possible, enter the intersection while following the car in front.
  2. ②You may enter the intersection if the traffic light is green.
  3. ③If there is space available, you may enter the intersection even if the traffic light is red.
  4. ④Do not enter the intersection even when there is a green arrow.
Show answer

Answer
④ Do not enter the intersection even when there is a green arrow.

Reference
When entering an intersection with a traffic light, the driver of any vehicle must stop at the intersection according to the situation of the vehicle in front of the lane in which he or she wishes to proceed, and must not enter the intersection if there is a risk of interfering with the passage of other vehicles.
