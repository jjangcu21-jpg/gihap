994. In the video, which of the following situations poses the highest risk when overtaking a cultivator that is about to turn right? (Refer to the website)

Video questions play in the Gihap app or on the official site.

Official video ↗
  1. ①The truck on the right may slow down to enter the intersection.
  2. ②The red passenger vehicle on the left may slow down to turn right.
  3. ③The white passenger vehicle on the right may try to overtake the truck at the intersection while the cultivator is turning right.
  4. ④The cultivator may stop at the stop line to turn right.
Show answer

Answer
③ The white passenger vehicle on the right may try to overtake the truck at the intersection while the cultivator is turning right.

Reference
Article 19 of the Road Traffic Act (Securing a safe distance, etc.), Article 20 of the Road Traffic Act (Duty to yield way) ○ Near the entrance road, you must spot an approaching car early and consider the distance and speed difference from that car to determine whether your car should go first or let it enter in front of you. ○ In this case, there is a car trying to enter from the right, but the car behind is already trying to change course to the overtaking lane, so it is dangerous to change course to the left. Drive at a constant speed to make it easier for the car in the acceleration lane to merge.
