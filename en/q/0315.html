315. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is true about a violation that took place in a silver zone at 10 AM?

  1. ①If the driver of a dump truck violates the traffic signal, the traffic penalty is KRW 130,000.
  2. ②If the driver of a passenger vehicle obstructs a senior pedestrian, the traffic penalty is KRW 70,000.
  3. ③If a bicycle rider obstructs a senior pedestrian crossing a crosswalk, the traffic penalty is KRW 50,000.
  4. ④If the driver of a cultivator fails to fulfill his/her duty of guaranteeing pedestrian safety, the traffic penalty is KRW 30,000.
Show answer

Answer
① If the driver of a dump truck violates the traffic signal, the traffic penalty is KRW 130,000.

Reference
Fines are imposed for signal violations (130,000 won for dump trucks), obstructing pedestrians crossing the crosswalk (120,000 won for cars, 60,000 won for bicycles), and obstructing or failing to protect pedestrians (80,000 won for cars, 40,000 won for bicycles).
