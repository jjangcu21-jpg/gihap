740. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Move to the first lane to avoid the lane-changing truck.
  2. ②Check the traffic behind you and slow down to avoid colliding with the truck.
  3. ③Speed up as you exit the tunnel, as it is short and you can see the situation outside.
  4. ④Turn on the headlights when entering the tunnel.
  5. ⑤If the truck moves into the third lane and increases its distance from the passenger car ahead, follow as closely as possible behind the car.
Show answer

Answer
② Check the traffic behind you and slow down to avoid colliding with the truck.
④ Turn on the headlights when entering the tunnel.

Reference
The vehicle's direction must not be changed in places where safety signs are installed and direction changes are specifically prohibited. You can change course in the white dotted line section, but you must not change course in the solid white line section. In a tunnel, it is necessary to maintain a safe distance from the car in front to prevent accidents.
