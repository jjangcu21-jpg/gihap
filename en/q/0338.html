338. Which of the following is the least related to safe driving by a senior citizen?

  1. ①Taking sufficient rest prior to driving
  2. ②Periodic checks of health condition
  3. ③Verification of the route to the destination prior to driving
  4. ④Encouraging nighttime driving rather than daytime driving.
Show answer

Answer
④ Encouraging nighttime driving rather than daytime driving.

Reference
It is advisable for elderly drivers to get sufficient rest and periodically check their health before driving. It is best to check your destination route before driving and, if possible, avoid driving late at night.
