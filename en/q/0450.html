450. Which of the following is NOT a correct statement regarding the expressway construction section?

  1. ①In the case of construction that blocks a lane, caution is required due to expected congestion.
  2. ②In the case of a truck, drowsiness and neglecting to look forward can lead to a major accident.
  3. ③Various types of construction work including moving construction and stationary construction can be performed.
  4. ④The speed limit is set to 80 km/h.
Show answer

Answer
④ The speed limit is set to 80 km/h.

Reference
In the case of construction sections, the speed is limited to 80 kilometers per hour and 60 kilometers per hour for each section, so you must be aware of the speed limit signs and drive at a sufficiently reduced speed (Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport traffic management guidelines for road construction sites, traffic management standards for highway construction sites).
