114. Which TWO statements are correct regarding yielding at an intersection with no traffic control?

  1. ①When turning left, you must yield to vehicles going straight or turning right at the intersection.
  2. ②If you are about to enter the intersection, you are not required to yield to any vehicles that are already inside the intersection and which are turning left.
  3. ③If you are about to enter the intersection, you must not yield to any vehicles that are about to enter the intersection from a narrower road.
  4. ④When multiple vehicles assigned the same priority are about to enter the intersection at the same time, the vehicles on the right have the right of way.
Show answer

Answer
① When turning left, you must yield to vehicles going straight or turning right at the intersection.
④ When multiple vehicles assigned the same priority are about to enter the intersection at the same time, the vehicles on the right have the right of way.

Reference
The driver of a car wishing to turn left at an intersection without traffic control must yield the right-of-way to a car wishing to proceed straight or turn right at the intersection, and when cars with the same priority wish to enter the intersection at the same time, the driver of a car must yield the right-of-way to the car on the right-hand side of the road.
