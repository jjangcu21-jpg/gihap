339. Which of the following is NOT the correct explanation regarding criminal punishment if a driver of a passenger car causes an injury that will take 3 weeks to heal to an elderly person in the silver zone for failing to look ahead while driving?

  1. ①If a driver has comprehensive insurance, he/she is not subject to criminal punishment.
  2. ②Criminal punishment is applied when there is a road sign indicating a silver zone.
  3. ③If a victim does not want punishment, a driver is not subject to criminal punishment.
  4. ④If both parties reach an agreement, criminal punishment is not imposed.
Show answer

Answer
② Criminal punishment is applied when there is a road sign indicating a silver zone.

Reference
Act on Special Cases Concerning the Settlement of Traffic Accidents: Article 3(2) prohibits prosecution against the victim’s express wishes. Under Article 4(1), a driver covered by insurance or mutual aid cannot be prosecuted for the offense specified in the main text of Article 3(2). However, the exceptions listed in the proviso to Article 3(2) can still result in criminal punishment despite comprehensive insurance or the victim’s objection to punishment. The explanation states that the special exemption applies to senior protection zones: where comprehensive insurance applies and the victim does not want punishment, criminal punishment is not imposed.
