553. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following are the TWO least appropriate statements regarding acts intended to obstruct alcohol testing?

  1. ①A person who is reasonably suspected of being under the influence and who intentionally drinks more alcohol in order to interfere with the police officer's testing is considered to be obstructing the alcohol test.
  2. ②A person who has driven a motor vehicle and who commits an act of obstructing the alcohol test may be punished by imprisonment for up to 1 year or criminal fine of up to KRW 5 million.
  3. ③A person who is reasonably suspected of being under the influence and who uses substances such as medicine that may affect the blood alcohol concentration (as prescribed by the Ordinance of the Ministry of Interior and Safety) is considered to be obstructing the alcohol test.
  4. ④A person who is reasonably suspected of being under the influence and who commits an act of obstructing the alcohol test after riding a bicycle is not considered to be obstructing the alcohol test.
Show answer

Answer
② A person who has driven a motor vehicle and who commits an act of obstructing the alcohol test may be punished by imprisonment for up to 1 year or criminal fine of up to KRW 5 million.
④ A person who is reasonably suspected of being under the influence and who commits an act of obstructing the alcohol test after riding a bicycle is not considered to be obstructing the alcohol test.

Reference
Article 44 (Prohibition of driving while intoxicated) Paragraph 5 A person reasonably suspected of being intoxicated shall not, after driving a car, tram, or bicycle, drink additional alcohol or use items prescribed by Ordinance of the Ministry of Public Administration and Security, such as medicines that may affect the blood alcohol concentration, for the purpose of making the measurement pursuant to paragraph 2 or 3 difficult (hereinafter referred to as “acts interfering with alcohol intake testing”; hereinafter the same shall apply). Article 148-2 (Penalty Provisions) Paragraph 2 Any person who falls under any of the following items shall be punished by imprisonment for not less than one year but not more than five years, or by a fine not less than 5 million won but not more than 20 million won. 2. A person reasonably suspected of being intoxicated and who, in violation of Article 44 (5), drives a car, etc. or streetcar and then interferes with a breathalyzer test. Article 156 (Penalty Provisions) Any person who falls under any of the following items shall be subject to a fine not exceeding 200,000 won, detention, or minor fine. 12-2. A person reasonably suspected of being under the influence of alcohol and who interferes with a breathalyzer test after driving a bicycle, etc., in violation of Article 44 (5).
