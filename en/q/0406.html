406. What is the standard for driving on an expressway with three lanes in one direction? (Assuming smooth traffic flow and no exclusive bus lanes)

  1. ①Passenger cars are to use Lane 1 so they must use only Lane 1.
  2. ②A small passenger vehicle whose designated lane is Lane 2 should use Lane 1 when overtaking.
  3. ③Large vans must use the first lane.
  4. ④A truck with maximum load weight of 1.5 tons or less must drive in Lane 1.
Show answer

Answer
② A small passenger vehicle whose designated lane is Lane 2 should use Lane 1 when overtaking.

Reference
Under Appendix 9 of the Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules, on an expressway with three lanes in each direction, passenger cars and mini, small and medium-sized passenger vans/buses travel in lane 2, the left-side travel lane. To overtake from lane 2, they must use lane 1.
