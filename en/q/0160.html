160. Which of the following is NOT true about a driver's duty to protect pedestrians?

  1. ①A driver who sees pedestrians crossing a crosswalk must come to a full stop before he/she reaches the crosswalk.
  2. ②The driver must slow down while keeping a safe distance when passing by pedestrians on a narrow road with no markings.
  3. ③If a crosswalk in a school zone has no traffic lights, the driver has no duty to stop if there are no pedestrians crossing.
  4. ④The driver should not obstruct pedestrians crossing an intersection with no traffic lights.
Show answer

Answer
③ If a crosswalk in a school zone has no traffic lights, the driver has no duty to stop if there are no pedestrians crossing.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 27(1), (3), (4) and (7): ① Drivers of vehicles or trams must stop before a crosswalk, or its stop line if provided, when pedestrians are crossing or about to cross, so as not to obstruct or endanger them. Pedestrians include users who have dismounted and are pushing or carrying bicycles or similar devices under Article 13-2(6). ③ Drivers must not obstruct pedestrians crossing at or near an uncontrolled intersection. ④ Drivers must keep a safe distance and proceed slowly when pedestrians are in a road safety zone or when passing pedestrians on a narrow road without marked lanes. ⑦ At an unsignalized crosswalk in a child protection zone under Article 12(1), drivers of vehicles or trams must stop before the crosswalk or its stop line, regardless of whether pedestrians are crossing. (Added January 11, 2022.)
