711. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Speed up as the road is not busy.
  2. ②Drive within the prescribed speed depending on the weather conditions.
  3. ③Keep a close eye on traffic, as there is ongoing construction ahead.
  4. ④Drive across two lanes as the road surface is slippery.
  5. ⑤Drive closely behind a truck driving slowly.
Show answer

Answer
② Drive within the prescribed speed depending on the weather conditions.
③ Keep a close eye on traffic, as there is ongoing construction ahead.

Reference
When construction is underway ahead, unexpected situations may occur, so proceed while paying attention to the traffic situation. Slowdown regulations apply depending on weather conditions. Cases in which the vehicle must be driven at a speed reduced by 20/100 of the maximum speed are when the road surface is wet due to rain or when less than 20 millimeters of snow has accumulated. Cases where the vehicle must be driven at a speed reduced by 50/100 of the maximum speed is when the visibility is 100 meters or less due to heavy rain, heavy snow, or fog, when the road surface is frozen, or when snow has accumulated 20 mm or more. This is the case (Article 19, Paragraph 2 of the Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules).
