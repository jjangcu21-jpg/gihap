603. Which of the following is NOT a crucial component of an FCEV?

  1. ①A fuel cell stack
  2. ②A hydrogen tank
  3. ③An internal combustion engine generator
  4. ④A traction motor
Show answer

Answer
③ An internal combustion engine generator

Reference
A hydrogen car is a method of moving a car by driving a motor with electricity generated through a chemical reaction of hydrogen stored in a container with oxygen in a fuel cell system (stack).
