477. When you sense danger while driving and step on the brake pedal, which TWO will lead to the longest stopping distance? (i.e., the distance from the point where you press the brake pedal to the point where the vehicle actually stops)

  1. ①Relatively light vehicle weight
  2. ②Relatively higher speed
  3. ③Right after installing new tires
  4. ④Fatigue or drunk driving
Show answer

Answer
② Relatively higher speed
④ Fatigue or drunk driving

Reference
The so-called stopping distance of a car, which is when the driver senses danger and steps on the brake pedal to actually stop the car, becomes longer when the driver is fatigued or has been drinking, the heavier the car is, the faster the speed is, and the more worn the tires are.
