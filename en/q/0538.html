538. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following statements regarding the traffic safety education that a person who is 75 years of age or older must receive is INCORRECT?

  1. ①Traffic safety education for persons who are 75 years of age or older is provided by the Korea Road Traffic Authority.
  2. ②A person who is 75 years of age or older as of the renewal date of the driver’s license shall receive education within the period of renewal.
  3. ③If a person who is 75 years of age or older wants to obtain a driver’s license for the first time, the period of education is 1 hour.
  4. ④The education is conducted for 2 hours by means of lecture, audiovisual, and self-diagnosis of cognitive ability.
Show answer

Answer
③ If a person who is 75 years of age or older wants to obtain a driver’s license for the first time, the period of education is 1 hour.

Reference
Under Appendix 16, section 4 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act, traffic safety training for people aged 75 or older lasts 2 hours.
