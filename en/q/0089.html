89. Which TWO are the responsibilities of a driver?

  1. ①Stop the vehicle if there is risk of a traffic accident involving children.
  2. ②You must slow down when riding over a puddle so as not to splash others.
  3. ③Apply dark tinting since the brightness of vehicle windows is not regulated.
  4. ④Drive at a reduced speed when pedestrians are crossing a crosswalk.
Show answer

Answer
① Stop the vehicle if there is risk of a traffic accident involving children.
② You must slow down when riding over a puddle so as not to splash others.

Reference
Drivers must stop if they notice a risk of a child being involved in a traffic accident. They must also stop when a pedestrian is crossing a crosswalk. If a pedestrian is in a safety zone, drivers must pass slowly while keeping a safe distance.
