684. Which of the following are incorrect information that you can learn from the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①There is a crosswalk ahead.
  2. ②The traffic light ahead is green.
  3. ③There is an exclusive bicycle track on the right side of the road.
  4. ④The road speed limit is 30 km/h.
  5. ⑤The cars ahead are operating their brake pedals.
Show answer

Answer
④ The road speed limit is 30 km/h.
⑤ The cars ahead are operating their brake pedals.

Reference
This place is a children's protection area. Traffic speed in child protection areas may be limited to 30 kilometers per hour (Article 12, Paragraph 1 of the Road Traffic Act). However, there are many child protection zones with traffic speeds that are different from the 30 kilometers per hour standard. When you operate the brake pedal, the brake light installed on the rear of the car turns on. In the situation in the photo, there is no sign showing the maximum speed limit, and neither of the two vehicles ahead had their brake lights turned on.
