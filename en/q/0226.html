226. According to the Road Traffic Act, which two correctly describe the criteria for vehicles that may drive on exclusive lanes for high-occupancy vehicles?

  1. ①Passenger cars with 3 or more persons
  2. ②Trucks with 3 or more persons
  3. ③Buses with 3 or more persons
  4. ④Two-wheeled vehicles with 2 or more persons
Show answer

Answer
① Passenger cars with 3 or more persons
③ Buses with 3 or more persons

Reference
Annex Table 1 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act. Types of exclusive lanes and vehicles that can travel in the exclusive lane (Regarding Article 9 (1)) Passenger vehicles and vans with three or more occupants (excluding cars that can travel in the bus lane when high-occupancy vehicle lanes and bus lanes are installed simultaneously)
