Road signs · 2 pts

924. Which of the following is true about this road sign?

  1. ①It is a regulatory sign that informs the traffic direction and flow.
  2. ②It is a sign that directs vehicles turning right or left or traveling straight.
  3. ③A vehicle that is trying to turn left must use the outermost lane.
  4. ④A vehicle that is trying to turn left must switch to the leftmost lane and slow down.
Show answer

Answer
④ A vehicle that is trying to turn left must switch to the leftmost lane and slow down.

Reference
Appendix 6 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act, Traffic Classification Signs by Direction (Direction Sign No. 315) Instructs cars to turn left, go straight, or turn right. Article 25 of the Road Traffic Act (Intersection Passage Method) Paragraph 1 When drivers of all vehicles intend to turn right at an intersection, they must turn right while driving slowly along the right edge of the road in advance. In this case, the driver of a car turning right must pay attention to pedestrians or bicycles that stop or proceed according to the signal. Paragraph 2 When the driver of any vehicle intends to turn left at an intersection, he/she must drive slowly along the center line of the road in advance and make a left turn inside the center of the intersection.
