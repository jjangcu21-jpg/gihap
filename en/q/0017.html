17. Below are explanations of the standard for personal mobility devices (PMDs) according to the Road Traffic Act. Which one is correct?

  1. ①For vehicles with a motor, the motor should stop when the device reaches or exceeds a speed of 20 km/h.
  2. ②It includes electric bicycles that cannot move solely by motor power (PAS: Pedal Assist System).
  3. ③It is a vehicle with maximum rated output power of 11 kW or less, which weighs less than 35 kg.
  4. ④The weight of the vehicle should be less than 30 kg.
Show answer

Answer
④ The weight of the vehicle should be less than 30 kg.

Reference
Road Traffic Act, Article 2, item 19(b): Other vehicles fitted with an engine of 125 cc or less, or an electric motor with a maximum rated output of 11 kW or less, excluding electric bicycles under Article 2, item 1-2 of the Act on Promotion of Bicycle Use and outdoor mobile robots under item 21-3. Item 19-2 defines a personal mobility device as a motor-driven bicycle under item 19(b), prescribed by Ministry of the Interior and Safety ordinance, whose motor stops operating at 25 km/h or above and whose vehicle weight is less than 30 kg. PAS (Pedal Assist System) bicycles use pedals and an electric motor together and cannot run on motor power alone. Throttle-type electric bicycles can run on motor power alone and are classified in this explanation as personal mobility devices.
