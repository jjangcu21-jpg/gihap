4. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is a prerequisite for driving an emergency passenger vehicle with prescribed seating capacity of no more than 15 persons?

  1. ①Class I driver’s license for ordinary motor vehicles; 3 hours of traffic safety education
  2. ②Class I driver’s license for special motor vehicles (large towing vehicles); 2 hours of traffic safety education
  3. ③Class I driver’s license for special motor vehicles (recovery vehicles); 2 hours of traffic safety education
  4. ④Class II driver’s license for ordinary motor vehicles; 3 hours of traffic safety education
Show answer

Answer
① Class I driver’s license for ordinary motor vehicles; 3 hours of traffic safety education

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules Annex 18: A van with a passenger capacity of 15 passengers requires a Class 1 large-sized license or a Class 1 regular license, and people engaged in emergency vehicle work must receive new (3 hours) and regular traffic safety training (2 hours) pursuant to Article 38-2, Paragraph 2 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act.
