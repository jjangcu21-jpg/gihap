402. Under the Road Traffic Act, which of the following statements regarding the method of overtaking by passenger cars on a smoothly flowing three-lane one-way highway is INCORRECT? (No exclusive bus lanes)

  1. ①To overtake, a passenger car switches to Lane 1 and continues in Lane 1.
  2. ②A large passenger vehicle in Lane 3 switches to Lane 2 in order to overtake.
  3. ③A small van driving in the second lane should move into Lane 1 in order to overtake.
  4. ④A 5-ton truck uses Lane 2 to overtake.
Show answer

Answer
① To overtake, a passenger car switches to Lane 1 and continues in Lane 1.

Reference
When a passenger vehicle overtakes on a highway, it must use the first lane, and after overtaking, it must drive in the designated driving lane. (Appendix Table 9 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act)
