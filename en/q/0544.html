544. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following is NOT a case wherein a vehicle other than vehicles allowed on an exclusive-lane may pass along the exclusive lane?

  1. ①When an emergency vehicle is being operated for the originally intended emergency purpose
  2. ②When it is not possible to pass along except by using the exclusive lane due to road damage
  3. ③When a taxi temporarily passes along to take a passenger onboard, provided that it does not impede the traffic of vehicles in the exclusive lane
  4. ④When a package delivery truck temporarily passes along to unload goods
Show answer

Answer
④ When a package delivery truck temporarily passes along to unload goods

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Decree, Article 10 (Cases in which vehicles other than those designated for an exclusive lane may use that lane).
