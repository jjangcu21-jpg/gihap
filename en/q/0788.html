788. You want to turn left in the given situation. Which of the following are the TWO most dangerous ways to drive? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Turn on the hazard lights, pass through the safety zone to enter the first lane, and turn left.
  2. ②Once in the left-turn lane, stay as close as possible to the vehicle ahead and quickly turn left.
  3. ③Take caution of the vehicles behind you as you switch to the first lane.
  4. ④Switch to the first lane from the lane with white dotted lines, and then turn left.
  5. ⑤When switching to the left-turn lane, be mindful of the vehicle directly in front of you.
Show answer

Answer
① Turn on the hazard lights, pass through the safety zone to enter the first lane, and turn left.
② Once in the left-turn lane, stay as close as possible to the vehicle ahead and quickly turn left.

Reference
The safety zone sign indicates that there are obstacles on the road or that safety must be secured and that you cannot enter this zone. So you should not drive past the safe zone. Also, when changing directions, you must turn on the turn signal in advance, check front and rear, left and right, and change in the white dotted lane. Also, when changing directions, the vehicle in front or behind you may change direction quickly before your vehicle, so be careful about the vehicles in front and behind you as well. In the case of a lane where you can make a left turn and a U-turn at the same time, the car in front may make a U-turn, so it is necessary to drive defensively by turning left while ensuring a safe distance.
