666. Which of the following is a correct description of equipment attached to a recovery vehicle?

  1. ①The arm structure hung from the main body of the crane is called a hook.
  2. ②The device used to connect the recovery vehicle to a structure when towing is called the PTO switch.
  3. ③The structure attached to the front and rear sides in order to secure stability during tasks is called an outrigger.
  4. ④The device installed on the crane and on which a weighted item is hooked with a wire rope is called a boom.
Show answer

Answer
③ The structure attached to the front and rear sides in order to secure stability during tasks is called an outrigger.

Reference
① Crane boom: The arm part of the crane attached to the crane body ② Towing tripod: A device that connects to a rescue vehicle when towing a structure, PTO switch: The power required for the crane and rescue winch comes from the vehicle's PTO (power take-off device). ③ Outrigger: A structure attached to the front and rear sides to ensure stability during work. ④ Hook: A device mounted on a crane and hanging on a wire rope in the shape of a hook to hang heavy objects.
