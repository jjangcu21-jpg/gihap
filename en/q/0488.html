488. Which of the following is NOT a proper way to respond after a road kill?

  1. ①Do not touch the animal carcass to prevent exposure to potential diseases.
  2. ②Call any wildlife rescue center or a local government call center (area code + 120).
  3. ③Load the animal carcass on your vehicle to prevent a secondary accident.
  4. ④There is no need to file a report if necessary measures have been taken to prevent a secondary accident and facilitate traffic flow.
Show answer

Answer
③ Load the animal carcass on your vehicle to prevent a secondary accident.

Reference
According to the Korea Disease Control and Prevention Agency, animal carcasses should not be touched directly as there is a risk of infection. If an accident occurs, report it to the local government or road management office or the call center (living information counseling service) with area code +120 to receive help. Animals that have been in an accident are classified as objects under the current law, so there is no need to report if measures have been taken to prevent secondary accidents and ensure smooth traffic flow.
