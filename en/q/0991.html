Answer
② Drivers who fail to pay a toll may be charged 5 times the regular toll.

Reference
The single-lane Hi-Pass electronic toll lane speed is limited to 30 kilometers per hour or less, and the multi-lane Hi-Pass electronic toll lane is limited to 80 kilometers per hour or less. Additionally, if you travel on a toll road without paying the toll, an additional toll equivalent to 10 times the toll may be charged and collected (Article 14, Paragraph 5 of the Enforcement Decree of the Toll Road Act).
