62. What is the color scheme of the license plates attached to EVs and hydrogen fuel cell vehicles used for non-commercial or rental purposes (apart from hybrid vehicles)?

  1. ①Black letters on yellow background
  2. ②Black letters on blue background
  3. ③White letters on royal blue background
  4. ④Black letters on purple background
Show answer

Answer
② Black letters on blue background

Reference
Notification on standards for vehicle registration plates, etc. (Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport Notification No. 2017-245, partially revised on April 18, 2017) 1. Non-commercial use a. General use (including SOFA cars and cars for rental business): Purplish-black letters on a pinkish-white background. For diplomatic purposes (diplomatic, consular, embassy, embassy, national flag, agreement, representative): White letters on a royal blue background 2. For automobile transportation business: Black letters on a yellow background 3. Two-wheeled vehicle license plates: Blue letters on a white background 4. Electric vehicle license plates: Black letters on a blue background
