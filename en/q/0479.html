479. When a vehicle driving in front brakes suddenly due to a traffic accident ahead, which of the following are the TWO safest measures to avoid colliding with the vehicle?

  1. ①Maintain a longer distance from the vehicle ahead than the stopping distance.
  2. ②Turn on the emergency flashers and follow an emergency vehicle.
  3. ③Reduce speed and maintain sufficient distance from the vehicle ahead to prevent a collision.
  4. ④When there is danger, use both the foot brake and parking brake to shorten the braking distance.
Show answer

Answer
① Maintain a longer distance from the vehicle ahead than the stopping distance.
③ Reduce speed and maintain sufficient distance from the vehicle ahead to prevent a collision.

Reference
Maintain at least the stopping distance from the vehicle in front, slow down sufficiently to avoid colliding with the vehicle in front, and ensure a safe distance. (Road Traffic Act Article 19)
