564. According to the Act on Special Cases Concerning the Settlement of Traffic Accidents, which of the following is considered a speeding-related accident wherein the driver causing the accident can be prosecuted against the stated intention of the victim?

  1. ①An accident caused by driving at 110 km/h on an expressway where the speed limit is 100 km/h
  2. ②An accident caused by driving at 95 km/h on a general road with three lanes in one direction where the speed limit is 80 km/h
  3. ③An accident caused by driving at 100 km/h on an automobile road where the speed limit is 90 km/h
  4. ④An accident caused by driving at 82 km/h on a general road with one lane in one direction where the speed limit is 60 km/h
Show answer

Answer
④ An accident caused by driving at 82 km/h on a general road with one lane in one direction where the speed limit is 60 km/h

Reference
Special Act on the Settlement of Traffic Accidents, Article 3(2): For injury caused by occupational or gross negligence under paragraph 1, or an offense under Road Traffic Act Article 151 arising from vehicle traffic, the driver cannot be prosecuted against the victim’s express wishes. Exceptions apply when the offense results from any of the following: 1. Violating a traffic light signal or instruction under Road Traffic Act Article 5. 2. Crossing the center line contrary to Article 13(3), or crossing, making a U-turn or reversing contrary to Article 62. 3. Driving more than 20 km/h above the speed limit under Article 17(1) or (2).
