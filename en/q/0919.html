Road signs · 2 pts

919. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following is the correct way of passing a road where this road safety sign is installed?

  1. ①Electric scooters can pass on the road with this sign.
  2. ②Electric bicycles cannot pass on the road with this sign.
  3. ③Only bicycles are allowed on the lane with this sign.
  4. ④Vehicles can pass through the lane with this sign.
Show answer

Answer
① Electric scooters can pass on the road with this sign.

Reference
Appendix 6 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act, Bicycle-only lane sign (instruction sign No. 318) is installed on the designated lane to allow only bicycles and personal mobility devices to pass. According to Article 2 (Definition) of the Road Traffic Act, bicycles, etc. refer to bicycles and personal mobility devices. Bicycle refers to bicycles and electric bicycles pursuant to Article 2, Paragraph 1 and 1-2 of the Act on Promotion of Bicycle Use. According to Article 2-3 (Standards for Personal Mobility Devices) of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act, electric kickboards are personal mobility devices. Therefore, electric kickboards can travel in lanes designated for only bicycles and personal mobility devices.
