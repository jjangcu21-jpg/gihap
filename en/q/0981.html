981. Which of the following driver behaviors constitutes a violation of the Road Traffic Act? (Refer to the website)

Video questions play in the Gihap app or on the official site.

Official video ↗
  1. ①The driver slows down after checking the advance pavement markings of the crosswalk.
  2. ②The driver stops to protect a pedestrian who is about to cross the crosswalk.
  3. ③The driver turns on the indicator in the right-turn lane.
  4. ④The driver quickly enters the left-hand lane while turning right.
Show answer

Answer
④ The driver quickly enters the left-hand lane while turning right.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 27 (Protection of Pedestrians). Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 6] Types of safety signs, methods of making them, and installation and management standards. Road sign No. 529. Pedestrian crossing warning sign Article 38 (Vehicle signal) ① When turning left, turning right, crossing, U-turning, slowing down, stopping or reversing, or when attempting to change course while proceeding in the same direction, or when entering or exiting a roundabout, the driver of any vehicle must signal with his hand, direction indicator or light until the action is completed. The main character driver checked the crosswalk warning sign, drove slowly, and paused before reaching the stop line installed just before the crosswalk. In addition, in accordance with Article 38 of the Road Traffic Act, a signal was made using a direction indicator.
