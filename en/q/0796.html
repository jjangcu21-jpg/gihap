796. Which of the following descriptions regarding right-turn traffic at a "lane-reduced roundabout" in the given picture are correct? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Enter the roundabout and safely exit the roundabout to the right.
  2. ②Turn right while driving slowly in the rightmost lane of the roadway without entering the roundabout.
  3. ③Turning right immediately after entering a roundabout is a "violation of the right of way in an intersection."
  4. ④A vehicle must come to a complete stop at the crosswalk in the right-turn lane, regardless of whether there are pedestrians or not.
  5. ⑤The driver must make a large turn clockwise to turn right after entering the roundabout.
Show answer

Answer
② Turn right while driving slowly in the rightmost lane of the roadway without entering the roundabout.
③ Turning right immediately after entering a roundabout is a "violation of the right of way in an intersection."

Reference
* Road Traffic Act Article 25 (Method of passing through intersections) * Roundabout design guideline 2.2 (Roundabout type) “lane-reduction type roundabout” - Lane-reduction type roundabout: Classifies the movements of right-turning vehicles and straight-forward and left-turning vehicles, a type in which lane changes do not occur within the roundabout - Spiral roundabout: The movement of right-turn lanes is separated within the roundabout -Lane change suppressed two-lane roundabout: A type in which lane changes are suppressed through lane adjustment of existing roundabouts.
