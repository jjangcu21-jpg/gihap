505. Which of the following are the TWO situations subject to criminal prosecution in accordance with the Act on Special Cases Concerning the Settlement of Traffic Accidents?

  1. ①A driver without comprehensive insurance reaches a settlement with the victim after causing an accident resulting in property damage.
  2. ②A taxi driver who is a member of the Taxi Mutual Aid Association causes a traffic accident resulting in injury or death after driving over the centerline.
  3. ③A driver with comprehensive insurance causes a traffic accident resulting in injury or death after committing a violation of a traffic signal.
  4. ④A truck covered by cooperative insurance causes injury due to careless driving.
Show answer

Answer
② A taxi driver who is a member of the Taxi Mutual Aid Association causes a traffic accident resulting in injury or death after driving over the centerline.
③ A driver with comprehensive insurance causes a traffic accident resulting in injury or death after committing a violation of a traffic signal.

Reference
If you cause a traffic accident causing personal damage by crossing the center line or violating a traffic signal, you will be punished even if you are enrolled in comprehensive insurance or a mutual aid association (Articles 3 and 4 of the Traffic Accident Settlement Special Act).
