68. Which of the following is NOT a desirable mindset for drivers of autonomous vehicles?

  1. ①Since it is an autonomous vehicle, the drivers can drive even under the influence of alcohol.
  2. ②The driver must not drive an autonomous vehicle in an exhausted state.
  3. ③Even drivers of autonomous vehicles can take psychoactive medication and drive.
  4. ④The driver may use a mobile phone while driving an autonomous vehicle.
Show answer

Answer
① Since it is an autonomous vehicle, the drivers can drive even under the influence of alcohol.

Reference
Article 44 (1) of the Road Traffic Act (Prohibition of driving while intoxicated) No one shall drive a car, tram or bicycle while intoxicated. Article 45 (Prohibition of driving when overworked, etc.) The driver of a car, etc. or a streetcar shall not drive a car, etc. or a streetcar while intoxicated or in a state where there is a risk of being unable to drive normally due to overwork, illness, under the influence of drugs (narcotics, cannabis, psychotropic drugs, etc.), or for other reasons. Article 56-2 (Matters to be observed by self-driving car drivers, etc.) Paragraph 2 When a driver drives using an autonomous driving system, the provisions of Article 49 (Matters to be observed by all drivers, etc.), Paragraph 1, No. 10 (Prohibition of using mobile phones), No. 11 (Prohibition of viewing video display devices), and item 11-2 (Prohibition of operating video display devices) shall not apply.
