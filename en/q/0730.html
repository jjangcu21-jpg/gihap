730. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Switch to the third lane and overtake quickly, as there are no vehicles ahead in that lane.
  2. ②Use the high-beam headlights continuously on the truck ahead to make it yield.
  3. ③Tailgate closely behind the slow-moving truck ahead.
  4. ④Overtaking is prohibited in tunnels.
  5. ⑤Do not try to overtake, as there are many vehicles in the left lane.
Show answer

Answer
④ Overtaking is prohibited in tunnels.
⑤ Do not try to overtake, as there are many vehicles in the left lane.

Reference
The road surface is slippery due to snowy driving in winter, and you need to be especially careful because you will soon have to enter a tunnel. Drivers of all vehicles must pass on the left side of the vehicle in front when passing another vehicle (Article 21, Paragraph 1 of the Road Traffic Act). When changing the course of a car, you must not change the course if there is a risk of interfering with the normal passage of other cars coming in the direction you want to change (Road Traffic Act, Article 19, Paragraph 3). Additionally, you must turn on the left turn signal in advance, secure a safe distance, enter the left lane, and attempt to overtake. Overtaking is prohibited at intersections, inside tunnels, and on bridges (Article 22, Paragraph 3 of the Road Traffic Act). Therefore, the safest way to drive is not to overtake in the tunnel (④) and to avoid excessive overtaking since there are many vehicles on the left (⑤).
