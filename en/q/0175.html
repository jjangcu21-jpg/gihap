175. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following are TWO correct explanations of pedestrian protection?

  1. ①A cyclist walking his/her bicycle is not considered a pedestrian.
  2. ②A vehicle that is the first to enter an intersection where there is no traffic control has the right of way over pedestrians.
  3. ③The commissioner of the City/Do (Province) police agency can install a pedestrian-only road for pedestrians for their protection.
  4. ④Baby carriages are allowed on the exclusive road for pedestrians.
Show answer

Answer
③ The commissioner of the City/Do (Province) police agency can install a pedestrian-only road for pedestrians for their protection.
④ Baby carriages are allowed on the exclusive road for pedestrians.

Reference
A person walking while pushing a bicycle is also a pedestrian. Even a vehicle that entered an uncontrolled intersection first must yield to pedestrians.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
