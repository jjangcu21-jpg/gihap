705. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Drive while keeping in mind that pedestrians may appear from behind parked vehicles.
  2. ②Keep your distance from pedestrians on the right and stop temporarily or pass them slowly.
  3. ③Speed up and move quickly when the roads are slippery as the snow has been accumulated.
  4. ④Accelerate 20 percent above the maximum speed on snowy roads.
  5. ⑤Keep honking while driving to avoid sudden changes in pedestrian behavior.
Show answer

Answer
① Drive while keeping in mind that pedestrians may appear from behind parked vehicles.
② Keep your distance from pedestrians on the right and stop temporarily or pass them slowly.

Reference
Drivers of all vehicles must drive slowly at a safe distance when passing by pedestrians on roads where sidewalks and roads are not distinguished from each other and do not have a center line, and when pedestrian traffic is obstructed, they must slow down or stop temporarily to allow pedestrians to pass safely (Road Traffic Act, Article 27, Paragraph 6, Item 1). Drivers of cars and horses must drive on the right side of the center (if a center line is installed, refer to the center line; hereinafter the same shall apply) of the road (referring to the roadway in the case of a road with a separate sidewalk and roadway) (Article 13, Paragraph 3 of the Road Traffic Act). Speed ​​reduction regulations apply in preparation for abnormal weather. Cases where driving must be reduced by 20/100 of the maximum speed are when the road surface is wet due to rain or when less than 20 millimeters of snow has accumulated. Cases where driving is required to be driven at a speed reduced by 50/100 of the maximum speed is when visibility is 100 meters or less due to heavy rain, heavy snow, or fog, when the road surface is frozen, or when snow has accumulated 20 mm or more. This is the case (Article 19, Paragraph 2 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act).
