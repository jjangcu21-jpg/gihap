518. Which of the following is the appropriate penalty for driving a vehicle while covering the license plate?

  1. ①Imprisonment with labor for not more than 1 year or criminal fine of not more than KRW 10 million
  2. ②Imprisonment with labor for not more than 1 year or criminal fine of not more than KRW 20 million
  3. ③Imprisonment with labor for not more than 2 years or criminal fine of not more than KRW 10 million
  4. ④Imprisonment with labor for not more than 2 years or criminal fine of not more than KRW 20 million
Show answer

Answer
① Imprisonment with labor for not more than 1 year or criminal fine of not more than KRW 10 million

Reference
Article 10, Paragraph 5 and Article 81 (Penalty Provisions) of the Automobile Management Act No one shall cover the registration plate or make it difficult to read, and shall not drive such a vehicle.
