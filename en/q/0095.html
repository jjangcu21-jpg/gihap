95. Which of the following is true regarding wearing a seat belt before driving a vehicle (excluding two-wheeled vehicles)?

  1. ①If the passenger is a child under 13 years old and is not wearing a seat belt, the driver will be slapped with an administrative fine of KRW 100,000.
  2. ②If the passenger is 13 years old or above and is not wearing a seat belt, the driver will be slapped with an administrative fine of KRW 30,000.
  3. ③Only the driver and front passenger are obligated to wear seat belts when driving on general roads.
  4. ④All passengers are obligated to wear seat belts, but a child under 3 years old can be placed on the lap of the guardian.
Show answer

Answer
② If the passenger is 13 years old or above and is not wearing a seat belt, the driver will be slapped with an administrative fine of KRW 30,000.

Reference
Article 50(1) of the Road Traffic Act requires drivers of motor vehicles, excluding motorcycles, to wear a seat belt. Under Appendix 6 of the Enforcement Decree, the administrative fine for an unbelted passenger is KRW 60,000 if the passenger is under 13, or KRW 30,000 if the passenger is 13 or older.
