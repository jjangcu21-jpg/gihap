Road signs · 2 pts

884. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following is the correct explanation of this road safety sign?

  1. ①Auxiliary sign indicating that one side of the road is an area with risk of falling, such as a valley
  2. ②Regulatory sign indicating that one side of the road is an area with risk of falling, such as a riverside
  3. ③Caution sign that one side of the road is an area with risk of falling, such as a valley
  4. ④Indication sign indicating that one side of the road is an area with risk of falling, such as a riverside
Show answer

Answer
③ Caution sign that one side of the road is an area with risk of falling, such as a valley

Reference
Appendix 6 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act, Riverside Road Sign (Caution Sign No. 127) This indicates that one side of the road is a fall risk area such as a riverside, beach, or valley.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
