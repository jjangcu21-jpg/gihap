496. If a person notified to pay a traffic penalty fails to make the payment during the first designated period, which of the following is the correct amount that he/she has to pay within 20 days?

  1. ①Original traffic penalty + 10% late penalty
  2. ②Original traffic penalty + 20% late penalty
  3. ③Original traffic penalty + 30% late penalty
  4. ④Original traffic penalty + 40% late penalty
Show answer

Answer
② Original traffic penalty + 20% late penalty

Reference
A person who fails to pay the penalty within the payment period must pay the amount of the penalty notified plus 20/100 within 20 days from the day following the expiration of the payment period. (Article 164 (2) of the Road Traffic Act)
