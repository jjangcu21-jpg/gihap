381. As a vehicle is about to enter an intersection according to the traffic signal, a police officer gives a hand signal to stop. In this scenario, which of the following is the safest way to drive?

  1. ①Stop just before the stop line.
  2. ②Decelerate suddenly to slow down.
  3. ③Proceed according to the signal from the traffic light.
  4. ④Enter the intersection slowly.
Show answer

Answer
① Stop just before the stop line.

Reference
If the signals or instructions displayed by traffic safety facilities are different from the signals or instructions of police officials, etc. for traffic control, the signals or instructions of police officials, etc. must be followed.
