90. Which of the following are desirable behaviors when driving on a freeway? (Select TWO)

  1. ①If you are drowsy or exhausted from driving, pull the vehicle over on the shoulder to take a rest or a nap.
  2. ②You can watch entertaining videos while driving.
  3. ③You should take a rest and ventilate the vehicle regularly to stay awake.
  4. ④You should constantly check for hazards along the route before departure and even while taking a rest.
Show answer

Answer
③ You should take a rest and ventilate the vehicle regularly to stay awake.
④ You should constantly check for hazards along the route before departure and even while taking a rest.

Reference
Make a driving plan in advance, identify risk factors while resting rather than using a cell phone while driving, and prevent drowsy driving by taking regular breaks or ventilation rather than overcoming drowsy driving.
