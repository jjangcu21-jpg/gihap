368. Which of the following is a correct guideline with regard to passing through a roundabout?

  1. ①A car inside the roundabout has the right of way.
  2. ②A car about to enter the roundabout has the right of way.
  3. ③A car exiting the roundabout has the right of way.
  4. ④There are no set rules on who has the right of way.
Show answer

Answer
① A car inside the roundabout has the right of way.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 25-2 (Roadway Traffic Method) When entering a roundabout, you must slow down or stop temporarily, and if there is another vehicle already proceeding, you must give way to that vehicle.
