582. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following is NOT true about drivers of personal mobility devices?

  1. ①When crossing a crosswalk, get off the device and walk by either carrying or pushing the device.
  2. ②If there is no bicycle lane, ride the device on the far right side of the road.
  3. ③A self-balancing scooter is designed to carry more than 1 person.
  4. ④When driving at night, turn on the headlights and tail lights or wear other light-emitting devices.
Show answer

Answer
③ A self-balancing scooter is designed to carry more than 1 person.

Reference
Article 50 (10) of the Road Traffic Act: Drivers of personal mobility devices shall not drive personal mobility devices with passengers in excess of the passenger capacity prescribed by Ordinance of the Ministry of Public Administration and Security. Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules Article 33-3 Riding capacity for personal mobility devices 1. In case of electric two-wheeled parallel vehicles: 1 person
