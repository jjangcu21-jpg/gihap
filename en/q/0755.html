755. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Drive fast while continuously honking.
  2. ②Slow down sufficiently in advance to drive safely.
  3. ③Drive slowly even if you do not see any elderly people walking.
  4. ④For safety, it is advisable to tailgate the vehicle in front whenever possible.
  5. ⑤Speed up and quickly exit the silver zone as there is a crosswalk ahead.
Show answer

Answer
② Slow down sufficiently in advance to drive safely.
③ Drive slowly even if you do not see any elderly people walking.

Reference
This section is a senior citizen protection zone, so you must drive safely and slow down to 30 km/h. Also, even if you do not see pedestrians, you must drive slowly when approaching a crosswalk and be prepared for the appearance of pedestrians. Therefore, the safest driving method is to slow down in advance and pay attention to safety (②), and to drive slowly even if you cannot see the elderly (③). On the other hand, driving quickly while using the horn unnecessarily (①), following closely behind the car in front (④), or speeding up to get out of the zone (⑤) are all unsafe driving methods.
