440. Which of the following are TWO are safe methods to drive when lightning strikes?

  1. ①Park your vehicle under a large tree.
  2. ②Keep windows closed and stay in the car.
  3. ③Keep away from the outer walls of buildings as electricity may conduct down the wet surface.
  4. ④Evacuate the vehicle to avoid being in it if it gets hit by lightning.
Show answer

Answer
② Keep windows closed and stay in the car.
③ Keep away from the outer walls of buildings as electricity may conduct down the wet surface.

Reference
There is a high possibility that a large tree will be struck by lightning, and if that happens, there is a high possibility that the tree will fall and cause an accident, so it is best to avoid it. Even if lightning strikes a car, the inside of the car is safer than the outside.
