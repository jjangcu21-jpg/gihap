422. What do you call a problem that occurs when the brakes do not work due to bubbles that form in the brake fluid overheated by excessive braking?

  1. ①A standing wave
  2. ②A vapor lock
  3. ③A road holding
  4. ④An understeering
Show answer

Answer
② A vapor lock

Reference
Vapor lock is a phenomenon whereby bubbles form in the brake fluid due to frictional heat caused by excessive use of the foot brake, preventing braking.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
