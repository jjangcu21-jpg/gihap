970. Which of the following describes the most dangerous situation that may arise in the video?

Video questions play in the Gihap app or on the official site.

Official video ↗
  1. ①The vehicle waiting in lane 1 at the intersection is turning left.
  2. ②While switching to lane 2, your vehicle is about to collide with a vehicle driving in lane 2.
  3. ③The pedestrian standing behind a billboard suddenly enters the roadway to jaywalk.
  4. ④The pedestrian is about to cross a crosswalk without a traffic signal.
Show answer

Answer
③ The pedestrian standing behind a billboard suddenly enters the roadway to jaywalk.

Reference
It is difficult for drivers to see pedestrians behind billboards or signs because they are in blind spots caused by obstacles. At this time, there is a possibility that the pedestrian may jaywalk because he or she is not interested in the presence of a car coming from afar, or even if he or she spots the car, he or she misjudges that he or she can cross because the distance is quite far away. Additionally, it is important to remember that there is a lot of jaywalking in front of and behind crosswalks for defensive driving.
