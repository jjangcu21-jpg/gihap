746. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①When intending to move to another lane after entering a High-Pass lane, reverse to move to the intended lane.
  2. ②If you are not equipped with a Hi-Pass device, you must stop to pay in the Hi-Pass lane before passing through.
  3. ③If you intend to pay in cash or card, move to the correct lane in advance.
  4. ④You need not pay attention to oncoming traffic, as there is no need to stop in the Hi-Pass lane.
  5. ⑤It is safe to maintain speed within 30 km/h when passing a Hi-Pass tollgate.
Show answer

Answer
③ If you intend to pay in cash or card, move to the correct lane in advance.
⑤ It is safe to maintain speed within 30 km/h when passing a Hi-Pass tollgate.

Reference
You must not cross, make a U-turn, or go backwards on the highway (Road Traffic Act Article 62). Even without installing a Hi-Pass terminal, post-settlement is possible after passing in the Hi-Pass lane without stopping. You will not stop in the high-pass lane, but you must drive while paying attention to the situation ahead as the vehicle ahead may suddenly decelerate. When driving through the high-pass lane, it is safe to pass at a speed of 30 kilometers or less per hour.
