284. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is a correct action taken by a driver when a child is crossing in a crosswalk?

  1. ①Upon noticing a pedestrian crossing the crosswalk, check for safety and pass slowly.
  2. ②In the case of a crosswalk on a general road with no traffic light, the driver can pass the stop line as long as he/she does not enter the crosswalk area.
  3. ③In the case of a crosswalk on a general road with no traffic light, the driver can drive slowly behind crossing children.
  4. ④Pedestrian crossing signs are placed on both ends of a crosswalk, and all drivers must come to a full stop before reaching the crosswalk.
Show answer

Answer
④ Pedestrian crossing signs are placed on both ends of a crosswalk, and all drivers must come to a full stop before reaching the crosswalk.

Reference
A crosswalk sign instructs pedestrians to use the crosswalk. Drivers must stop temporarily in front of the crosswalk, and are installed on both sides of the road at the necessary points where the crosswalk is installed. When a child is passing through a crosswalk without a traffic light, pause in front of the crosswalk and wait for the child to pass.
