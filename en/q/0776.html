776. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Since the signals are not working, carefully observe the intersection before entering.
  2. ②When turning right, honk and overtake the cultivator on its left.
  3. ③Since the cultivator is moving slowly, speed up and turn right ahead of it.
  4. ④If you intend to go straight in the third lane, be sure to check the cultivator's position.
  5. ⑤Anticipate the cultivator going straight, and then quickly switch to the second lane and go straight.
Show answer

Answer
① Since the signals are not working, carefully observe the intersection before entering.
④ If you intend to go straight in the third lane, be sure to check the cultivator's position.

Reference
If there is a slow-speed agricultural machine in front, such as a cultivator, the speed is slower than that of a car, so you must carefully check the condition of the cultivator before driving. It is dangerous to anticipate ahead of time and change lanes abruptly when driving or trying to overtake.
