537. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following is true about using designated lanes?

  1. ①A passenger vehicle carrying 2 or more persons can use the high-occupancy vehicle lane.
  2. ②A 9-seater passenger vehicle carrying 6 or more persons can use the exclusive bus lane on an expressway.
  3. ③A 12-seater bus carrying 5 or more persons can use the exclusive bus lane on an expressway.
  4. ④A 16-seater personal vehicle can use all exclusive bus lanes found on roads other than expressways.
Show answer

Answer
② A 9-seater passenger vehicle carrying 6 or more persons can use the exclusive bus lane on an expressway.

Reference
Appendix 1 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act ① Three or more people must ride. ③ There must be 6 or more occupants. ④ Passage is possible only if conditions such as being a business van or being designated by the city/provincial police chief for school or commuting purposes are met. Large vans with 36 or more seats, commercial vans with less than 36 seats, and children's school buses that have received a report certificate can travel in bus-only lanes on roads other than highways.
