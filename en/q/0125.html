125. If you drive when you are tired, you will not be able to sense correctly the speed at which you are driving. Which of the following is correct?

  1. ①On a narrow road, you will feel as if you are going slower than you actually are.
  2. ②On roads with wide-open views, you will feel as if you are going faster than you actually are.
  3. ③You could underestimate the speed of a car coming toward you from far away and end up in an accident.
  4. ④There is no possibility of you mistaking a car stopped ahead on an expressway to be moving.
Show answer

Answer
③ You could underestimate the speed of a car coming toward you from far away and end up in an accident.

Reference
① On narrow roads, it feels faster than the actual speed. ② On an open road, it feels slower than it actually is. ④ A collision may occur on a highway when a stopped car in front is mistaken for a moving car.
