660. Which of the following describes a special recovery vehicle as defined by the Motor Vehicle Management Act?

  1. ①A vehicle that is structurally designed to tow other vehicles
  2. ②A vehicle that is structurally designed to tow and salvage other vehicles
  3. ③A vehicle that is structurally designed to salvage and tow a vehicle rendered immobile by a malfunction or an accident
  4. ④A vehicle that is designed to perform special tasks other than the three above
Show answer

Answer
③ A vehicle that is structurally designed to salvage and tow a vehicle rendered immobile by a malfunction or an accident

Reference
The detailed standards for each type of rescue type among special vehicles in Appendix 1 of the Enforcement Rules of the Automobile Management Act refer to a structure that can rescue and tow vehicles that are difficult to drive due to breakdowns, accidents, etc.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
