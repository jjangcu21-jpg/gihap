12. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following are the qualifications for taking a test for Class I driver's license for a large motor vehicle? (Excluding experience in driving a two-wheeled vehicle)

  1. ①A person who has been driving a vehicle for at least 6 months and who is 18 years or older
  2. ②A person who has been driving a vehicle for at least 1 year and who is 18 years or older
  3. ③A person who has been driving a vehicle for at least 6 months and who is 19 years or older
  4. ④A person who has been driving a vehicle for at least 1 year and who is 19 years or older
Show answer

Answer
④ A person who has been driving a vehicle for at least 1 year and who is 19 years or older

Reference
In accordance with Article 82, Paragraph 1, Item 6 of the Road Traffic Act, a Type 1 heavy-duty license cannot be obtained by anyone under 19 years of age or with less than 1 year of driving experience of a car (excluding two-wheeled vehicles).
