84. Which of the following is the most appropriate driving behavior for a driver?

  1. ①Drowsy driving entails the risk of a traffic accident, so it is important to stop the car on a roadside to rest when you are drowsy.
  2. ②Novice drivers should continuously drive in the overtaking lane on an expressway.
  3. ③Install and operate a device that interferes with the functions of traffic regulation equipment.
  4. ④Upon identifying a risk to traffic safety, inform others with emergency lights.
Show answer

Answer
④ Upon identifying a risk to traffic safety, inform others with emergency lights.

Reference
Resting on the road shoulder, continuously driving in the overtaking lane, and installing obstructive devices are not proper driving behavior.
