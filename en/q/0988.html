Show answer

Answer
③ Since the road surface is icy, press the brake pedal firmly.

Reference
Engine braking refers to deceleration in a car using the compression resistance of the engine or the friction resistance of the engine and transmission. When you take your foot off the accelerator pedal (accelerator) while running, the speed decreases even without pressing the brake, and the effect is greater in low gear. The two Korean terms used in the source are synonyms for engine braking. Drivers driving on snowy roads must prevent skidding when operating the brake pedal when approaching a place where there is a possibility of stopping. Therefore, when approaching a place where it is necessary to stop, you must use engine braking (engine braking) in advance to slow down and prevent slipping when operating the brake. Article 26. Paragraph 3 of the Road Traffic Act. Drivers of vehicles attempting to enter an intersection without traffic control at the same time must yield the right-of-way to vehicles on the right.
