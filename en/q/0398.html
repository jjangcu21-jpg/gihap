398. Under the Road Traffic Act, on an expressway with two lanes in one direction, if the first lane must inevitably be used with speed limit set to less than ( ) km/h due to an increase in traffic volume, vehicles are allowed to drive in the first lane even if they are not overtaking. Which of the following fills in the blank correctly?

  1. ①80
  2. ②90
  3. ③100
  4. ④110
Show answer

Answer
① 80

Reference
Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act [Appendix 9] Standards for passing vehicles according to lane All vehicles attempting to overtake on a highway with two lanes in each direction. However, if it is unavoidable to drive at a speed of less than 80 kilometers per hour due to road conditions such as an increase in vehicle traffic, you can drive without overtaking.
