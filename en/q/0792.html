792. Which of the following are the most dangerous ways to turn right in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Stop before the stop line and wait for the right-turn signal to change to green.
  2. ②Even after the right-turn signal turns green, keep your eyes on the road ahead to ensure safety.
  3. ③Even if the right-turn signal is red, stop temporarily and make the turn slowly if there are no pedestrians.
  4. ④There is no need to pay attention to crosswalks on the right side of the road immediately after turning right.
  5. ⑤Watch out for pedestrians who may suddenly run into the crosswalk from the right sidewalk.
Show answer

Answer
③ Even if the right-turn signal is red, stop temporarily and make the turn slowly if there are no pedestrians.
④ There is no need to pay attention to crosswalks on the right side of the road immediately after turning right.

Reference
Among vehicle signals, a red signal means that cars and horses must stop just before the stop line, crosswalk, or intersection. When a vehicle wishes to turn right, it must first stop just before the stop line, crosswalk, or intersection and then turn right without interfering with the traffic of other vehicles proceeding according to the signal. Nevertheless, cars and horses cannot turn right when the three-color right turn light is red. Therefore, if the three-color right turn signal at an intersection is red, you must stop before the stop line and wait until the signal changes to a green arrow. Additionally, it is necessary to pay attention to pedestrians who may jump out from the sidewalk on the right or between the front and back of vehicles parked next to them, as well as pedestrians in the crosswalk immediately after turning right.
