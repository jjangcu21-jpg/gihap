671. Which of the following terms refers to the phenomenon wherein the rear of a vehicle shakes from side to side during driving or sudden braking?

  1. ①Fishtailing
  2. ②Hydroplaning
  3. ③Standing wave
  4. ④Vapor lock
Show answer

Answer
① Fishtailing

Reference
‘Fishtailing’ is a phenomenon in which the rear body of the vehicle shakes from side to side like a fish’s tail fin during driving or sudden braking. ‘Hydroplaning’ refers to a condition in which steering ability is lost or impossible because the tires do not contact the road surface when driving on a wet road surface at high speed. ‘Standing wave’ is a phenomenon in which heat accumulates and deforms the tire contact area when a car is driven at high speed. ‘Vapor lock’ refers to a phenomenon in which air bubbles are generated in the brake fluid, preventing the brakes from operating properly.
