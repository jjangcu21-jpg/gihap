18. Which TWO actions would cause someone to be barred from acquiring a driver's license for 2 years? (If given a penalty more severe than a criminal fine)

  1. ①Driving without a license three times
  2. ②When taking a driver’s license test on behalf of another person.
  3. ③When charged for assaulting a police officer during an inspection, resulting in the revocation of one’s license.
  4. ④When causing three or more traffic accidents while driving under the influence of alcohol.
Show answer

Answer
① Driving without a license three times
② When taking a driver’s license test on behalf of another person.

Reference
According to Article 82 of the Road Traffic Act (Reasons for Disqualification of Driver's License), if you are booked and revoked for assaulting a police officer, the disqualification period is 1 year, and if you cause three or more traffic accidents while driving under the influence of alcohol, it is 3 years from the date of cancellation.
