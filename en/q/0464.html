464. Which of the following statements regarding a driver’s "Hypovigilance in driving" at nighttime is correct?

  1. ①Cognitive ability is better than usual.
  2. ②Eye movements become more active.
  3. ③It often occurs when driving on congested roads in a city.
  4. ④The eyes become used to a monotonous view and experience a type of sensory paralysis.
Show answer

Answer
④ The eyes become used to a monotonous view and experience a type of sensory paralysis.

Reference
Night Driving and Decreased Arousal Night driving vision is limited to the range illuminated by headlights, and compared to daytime vision, only the road surface and the tail lights of the car in front are visible, resulting in a very monotonous vision. Therefore, drivers unconsciously become accustomed to a monotonous field of view and fall into a state of numbness. In that case, eye movements inevitably become inactive and responses to stimuli become dull. As this phenomenon escalates, the response of muscles and brain waves also decreases, gradually leading to a state of drowsiness. Driving with reduced alertness like this is called ‘low alert driving’.
