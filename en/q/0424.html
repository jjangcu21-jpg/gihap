424. Which of the following is not a correct explanation of black ice in winter?

  1. ①It is a phenomenon wherein a thin ice film forms like a coating on the road surface.
  2. ②It is a phenomenon wherein pollutants in the air are mixed with snow and moisture on the asphalt surface, absorbed, and then frozen into black ice.
  3. ③It occurs mainly in places with low temperatures during the winter season, such as on bridges, tunnel entrances, shady roads, and in the shade of mountain corners.
  4. ④It refers to the phenomenon wherein the snow melts and seeps into the sunny road, and the black color of the road is reflected by sunlight and sparkles.
Show answer

Answer
④ It refers to the phenomenon wherein the snow melts and seeps into the sunny road, and the black color of the road is reflected by sunlight and sparkles.

Reference
It is one of the icing phenomena on the road surface. It is expressed as black ice or clear ice and refers to the phenomenon where a thin film of ice is formed as if coating the road surface. This involves snow and moisture intertwined with soot and dust in the air, seeping through the cracks in the asphalt, and then freezing to black. In the cold winter, it mainly occurs on roads that are shady and have low temperatures, such as on bridges, entrances and exits of tunnels, shady roads, and shaded mountain corners. It is not easily identified with the naked eye, so the risk of an accident is very high.
