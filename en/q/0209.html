209. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is the correct way of driving when the pedestrian signal at a diagonal crosswalk is green?

  1. ①When intending to go straight, you must stop just before the stop line.
  2. ②If there are no pedestrians, you can increase your speed and turn right.
  3. ③If there are no pedestrians, you can increase your speed and turn left.
  4. ④You can proceed in the direction where pedestrians are not crossing.
Show answer

Answer
① When intending to go straight, you must stop just before the stop line.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules, Annex 6, 532-2 Diagonal Crosswalk Marking, Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules, Article 6, Paragraph 2, Annex 2, Red Light Among the types of signals displayed by traffic signals and their meanings
