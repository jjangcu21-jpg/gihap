829. Which of the following are the desirable ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Switch to the first lane and proceed quickly.
  2. ②Turn on the hazard lights to alert other drivers to the presence of your vehicle.
  3. ③Since the road is wet, reduce your speed and drive slowly.
  4. ④Match your speed to the speed of the vehicle in front of you and drive while keeping an eye on it.
  5. ⑤Switch to the road shoulder and pass the vehicles in front.
Show answer

Answer
② Turn on the hazard lights to alert other drivers to the presence of your vehicle.
③ Since the road is wet, reduce your speed and drive slowly.

Reference
Hazard anticipation. Under Article 19(2) of the Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules, reduce speed in rain, fog or snow. If the national or city/provincial police chief sets a limit using a variable speed-limit sign under Appendix 6 I.1(타), follow that sign; it takes priority over conflicting fixed signs. Reduce the maximum speed by 20% when rain wets the road or snow is less than 20 mm deep. Reduce it by 50% when visibility is 100 m or less due to heavy rain, snow or fog, when the road is frozen, or when snow is at least 20 mm deep. On foggy roads, damp surfaces lengthen braking distance compared with clear weather, so avoid situations requiring sudden braking. Some drivers become anxious when the vehicle ahead disappears and follow even a fast vehicle too closely to keep it in sight. Instead, follow the reduced-speed rules even when the vehicle ahead is not visible, and slow further when conditions require. Use lights so other drivers can see you.
