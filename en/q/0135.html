135. Which of the following is NOT an appropriate method of preventing retaliatory driving?

  1. ①Turn on the emergency flashers in case of emergency braking.
  2. ②Turn off the head-beam headlights when a car approaches from the opposite direction.
  3. ③Turn on the high-beam headlights when driving at high speed.
  4. ④Wait for three seconds if the vehicle in front does not start moving right away.
Show answer

Answer
③ Turn on the high-beam headlights when driving at high speed.

Reference
Ways to prevent retaliatory driving include turning on the turn signal when changing lanes, turning on the emergency flashers, giving way and being considerate, being considerate for 3 seconds when starting late, and not irritating with the horn or high beam headlights.
