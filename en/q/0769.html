769. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①If an emergency vehicle is approaching, vehicles must move over to the left or right side of the road to yield the right of way.
  2. ②Follow behind the emergency vehicle as it will allow you to travel faster.
  3. ③Speed up to prevent the emergency vehicle from changing lanes.
  4. ④If the emergency vehicle is returning from an urgent business, it does not activate its strobe lights or siren, so there is no need to yield.
  5. ⑤If you see an emergency vehicle behind, speed up so that it cannot overtake you.
Show answer

Answer
① If an emergency vehicle is approaching, vehicles must move over to the left or right side of the road to yield the right of way.
④ If the emergency vehicle is returning from an urgent business, it does not activate its strobe lights or siren, so there is no need to yield.

Reference
When an emergency vehicle on duty approaches an intersection, move clear of the intersection and stop. Elsewhere, yield so that it can pass first. Following the emergency vehicle or speeding up to keep it from overtaking you is unsafe.
