297. A child crosses a street in a school zone with an infant. Which of the following is the correct driving behavior?

  1. ①Stop temporarily to protect children and infants.
  2. ②Slow down as a child crosses the street while protecting the infant.
  3. ③Drive fast as the child and infant are on the opposite side of the road.
  4. ④Since children and infants walk slowly, keep a safe distance and drive while avoiding them.
Show answer

Answer
① Stop temporarily to protect children and infants.

Reference
For the safety of pedestrians crossing, stop temporarily and continue on when the crossing is completed.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
