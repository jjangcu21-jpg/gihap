587. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following is NOT subject to a traffic penalty if a bicycle rider violates traffic laws?

  1. ①Violating traffic lights
  2. ②Crossing the centerline
  3. ③Obstructing pedestrians on a crosswalk
  4. ④Exceeding the speed limit
Show answer

Answer
④ Exceeding the speed limit

Reference
The Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act imposes a fine of 30,000 won under Appendix 8 for violating a traffic signal, 30,000 won for violating the center line, and 30,000 won for obstructing pedestrians crossing a crosswalk. Speeding regulations are governed by Article 17 of the Road Traffic Act (Speeds of automobiles, etc. and streetcars) ① The road speed of automobiles, etc. (excluding personal mobility devices; hereinafter the same shall apply in this Article) and streetcars shall be determined by Ordinance of the Ministry of Public Administration and Security.
