799. Which of the following are the safe ways to drive when trying to turn right in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Since it is difficult to have clear visibility on the right, come to a stop and then turn right.
  2. ②Since there are no pedestrians on the crosswalk, proceed through swiftly.
  3. ③Stop before the stop line to avoid obstructing the movement of oncoming vehicles.
  4. ④Since you entered the intersection first, proceed to turn right.
  5. ⑤Honk to prevent pedestrians from entering the crosswalk.
Show answer

Answer
① Since it is difficult to have clear visibility on the right, come to a stop and then turn right.
③ Stop before the stop line to avoid obstructing the movement of oncoming vehicles.

Reference
Risk prediction. Crosswalks installed on roads are crosswalks without traffic lights. At this time, the driver can see the driver trying to turn left in the opposite direction and the child running toward the crosswalk on the right. Also, if you want to turn right, in accordance with road traffic laws, you must not cross the path of other cars and horses, and you must anticipate pedestrians who may be present in the blind spot where the crosswalk on the right is obscured by cars that violate stopping and parking rules on the right, stop, and pay attention before making a right turn.
