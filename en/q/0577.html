577. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following are the TWO correct statements regarding cycling?

  1. ①Unless allowed by a safety sign, two or more cyclists must not ride side by side on a road.
  2. ②When crossing a crosswalk, a cyclist must dismount and push his/her bicycle across.
  3. ③A cyclist must not use a sidewalk even if the road is not accessible due to damage, construction, or other interferences.
  4. ④Where there is no bicycle track, a cyclist must ride close to the center of the road.
Show answer

Answer
① Unless allowed by a safety sign, two or more cyclists must not ride side by side on a road.
② When crossing a crosswalk, a cyclist must dismount and push his/her bicycle across.

Reference
In places where there is a separate bicycle path, you must ride on that bicycle path. In places where there is no bicycle path, you must ride along the right edge of the road. When riding along the edge of the road (excluding sections where bicycles are prohibited by safety signs), you must slow down or stop temporarily if it interferes with the passage of pedestrians.
