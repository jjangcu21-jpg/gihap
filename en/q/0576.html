576. Which of the following is not a high-risk personal mobility device/sports equipment stipulated by the Ordinance of the Ministry of Interior and Safety to require children to wear protective gear when riding on the road?

  1. ①Kick scooters
  2. ②Self-balancing scooters (segway)
  3. ③Roller skates
  4. ④Skateboards
Show answer

Answer
② Self-balancing scooters (segway)

Reference
High-risk amusement devices as prescribed by Ordinance of the Ministry of Public Administration and Security in Article 13 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act refer to kickboards, roller skates, inline skates, skateboards, and similar amusement devices, and personal mobility devices such as electric two-wheeled parallel vehicles are prohibited from being driven by children under Article 11 (4) of the Road Traffic Act.
