94. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is NOT true about regulations and proper operation of PMDs?

  1. ①The driver must turn on headlights and daytime running lights when driving at night.
  2. ②Among the PMDs, not more than one person can drive an electric kick scooter.
  3. ③A personal mobility device includes self-balancing scooters, electric kick scooters, electric bicycles, electric unicycles, and mobility scooters.
  4. ④An electric bicycle powered by a motor can carry two persons.
Show answer

Answer
③ A personal mobility device includes self-balancing scooters, electric kick scooters, electric bicycles, electric unicycles, and mobility scooters.

Reference
Article 50 of the Road Traffic Act (Specific Driver Compliance Matters) Paragraph 9 When riding on the road at night, drivers of bicycles, etc. must turn on their headlights and taillights or wear light-emitting devices such as luminous strips. Paragraph 10 The driver of a personal mobility device shall not drive a personal mobility device with passengers in excess of the passenger capacity prescribed by Ordinance of the Ministry of Public Administration and Security. Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules Article 33-3 (Riding capacity of personal mobility devices) In the case of electric kickboards and electric two-wheeled parallel vehicles: 1 person, in the case of bicycles that can be moved only by electric motor power: 2 people.
