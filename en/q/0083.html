83. Which of the following is NOT a desirable driver attitude?

  1. ①Because the traffic situation is not changing, there is no need to make plans ahead.
  2. ②Equip the vehicle with a fire extinguisher to take action immediately in case of fire.
  3. ③Remove lighters and other flammable items from the vehicle.
  4. ④Drive courteously, especially for novice drivers.
Show answer

Answer
① Because the traffic situation is not changing, there is no need to make plans ahead.

Reference
If you discover a dangerous element on the road due to a broken down vehicle, etc., turn on the emergency lights to inform the following vehicle of the situation ahead in advance, drive slowly to safely exit the danger zone, and if it is determined that help is needed, take secondary accident prevention measures and take action.
