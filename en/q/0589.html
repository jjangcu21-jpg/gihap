589. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following statements regarding the names of the roads/lanes that can be used by bicycles are INCORRECT? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Exclusive bicycle track
  2. ②Bicycle priority lane
  3. ③Bicycle and motorcycle lane
  4. ④Bicycle priority road
Show answer

Answer
② Bicycle priority lane
③ Bicycle and motorcycle lane

Reference
Roads on which bicycles can travel include bicycle-only paths, bicycle-only lanes, bicycle priority roads, and bicycle/pedestrian roads.
