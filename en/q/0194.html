194. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is correct regarding pedestrians using the sidewalk?

  1. ①A person who rides a two-wheeled vehicle up to 125cc on the sidewalk can be considered a pedestrian.
  2. ②A person riding a bicycle can be considered a pedestrian.
  3. ③A person who uses a wheelchair can be considered a pedestrian.
  4. ④A person who rides a 49cc motorcycle can be considered a pedestrian.
Show answer

Answer
③ A person who uses a wheelchair can be considered a pedestrian.

Reference
Article 2 (Definition) of the Road Traffic Act, item 10, “Sidewalk” (步道) refers to a portion of a road where the boundary is marked with a curb line, safety sign, or similar artificial structure so that pedestrians (including strollers and wheelchairs for pedestrian assistance prescribed by Ordinance of the Ministry of the Interior and Safety; hereinafter the same shall apply) can pass.
