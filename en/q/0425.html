425. Which of the following is NOT true about icy roads during winter months?

  1. ①Icy roads are more common on concrete roads than on asphalt.
  2. ②Ice melts slower on asphalt roads than on concrete.
  3. ③The friction coefficient of an asphalt surface increases to 1.6 when dry.
  4. ④Under the same conditions, the road friction coefficients of frozen concrete and asphalt surfaces are equal.
Show answer

Answer
③ The friction coefficient of an asphalt surface increases to 1.6 when dry.

Reference
① Freezing occurs more on roads paved with concrete than asphalt. ② Ice melts slower on asphalt-paved roads than on concrete. ③, ④ Under the same frozen conditions, the road surface friction coefficient of concrete and asphalt paved roads is 0.3, which is less than half the friction coefficient of a dry road surface.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
