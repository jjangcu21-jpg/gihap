683. Which of the following are incorrect ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①In the first lane, you can turn right at the green traffic light.
  2. ②In the first lane, you can turn left at the green left-turn signal.
  3. ③In the second lane, you can go straight at the green traffic light.
  4. ④In the third lane, you should stop before the stop line and turn right at the red traffic light.
  5. ⑤In the third lane, go straight at the green traffic light and stop at the safety zone installed within the intersection.
Show answer

Answer
① In the first lane, you can turn right at the green traffic light.
⑤ In the third lane, go straight at the green traffic light and stop at the safety zone installed within the intersection.

Reference
When drivers of all vehicles wish to turn right at an intersection, they must first turn right while driving slowly along the right edge of the road (Road Traffic Act, Article 25, Paragraph 1). If you are driving in lane 3 and there is a construction site ahead in lanes 3 and 4, you should slow down, turn on the turn signal, and change your route to lane two in advance. All vehicles must not enter places where entry is prohibited, such as safety zones.
