530. Which of the following is subject to mandatory education on special traffic safety?

  1. ①A first-time applicant seeking to obtain a driver's license
  2. ②A driver with 30 demerit points
  3. ③A driver who completed proactive driving training
  4. ④A driver whose license is suspended for reckless driving
Show answer

Answer
④ A driver whose license is suspended for reckless driving

Reference
Article 73 of the Road Traffic Act (Traffic Safety Education) Anyone who wishes to obtain a driver's license for the first time must receive traffic safety education. People with less than 40 demerit points can receive traffic law education.
