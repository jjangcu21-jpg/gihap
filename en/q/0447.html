447. If your vehicle breaks down on an expressway at night, in addition to a safety tripod sign, etc. you must install road flare signals to provide visibility for ( ) and alert other motorists that your car has broken down. Choose the correct one to fill in the brackets.

  1. ①200 m in every direction.
  2. ②300 m in every direction.
  3. ③400 m in every direction.
  4. ④500 m in every direction.
Show answer

Answer
④ 500 m in every direction.

Reference
In accordance with Article 40 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act (Signs for broken down vehicles), safety tripods and additional red flashing signals, electric lights or flame signals that can be identified from 500 meters in all directions must be installed on highways at night.
