876. What does this safety sign indicate?

  1. ①Detour to the left side of the road.
  2. ②Road merges to the right.
  3. ③Road narrows.
  4. ④The right lane ends.
Show answer

Answer
④ The right lane ends.

Reference
Appendix 6 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act. 119. No right-hand lane Installed when the right-hand lane disappears on a road with two or more lanes each way.
