212. Which of the following is the most appropriate way of turning left at an intersection without any traffic control?

  1. ①On general roadways, turn on the direction indicator immediately before the intersection and turn left.
  2. ②Slow down and follow the centerline of the road, and turn left by going around the central island in the outer lane of the intersection.
  3. ③Turning left on the outer lane of the intersection is not allowed even when permitted by the commissioner of the City/Do (Province) police agency.
  4. ④You must slow down. You may or may not stop at your discretion as you enter.
Show answer

Answer
④ You must slow down. You may or may not stop at your discretion as you enter.

Reference
When turning left on an ordinary road, signal at least 30 metres before the turn. Drive slowly along the center line and turn inside the center of the intersection. At locations designated by the city or provincial police chief, a turn outside the center is permitted. You must always proceed slowly when turning left; whether a temporary stop is required depends on the situation.
