580. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following is the correct behavior of a cyclist?

  1. ①If the bicycle impedes the passage of pedestrians, slow down or stop temporarily.
  2. ②A child riding a bicycle is not allowed to do so on the sidewalk.
  3. ③Dragging a bicycle is not allowed in sections where the passage of bicycles is prohibited.
  4. ④Up to two bicycles can pass side by side in the roadside area.
Show answer

Answer
① If the bicycle impedes the passage of pedestrians, slow down or stop temporarily.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 13-2 (Special Provisions for Passage Methods for Bicycles, etc.)
