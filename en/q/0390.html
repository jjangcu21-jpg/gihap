390. Which TWO correctly describe how to change lanes?

  1. ①Change lanes while maintaining speed if the car behind you in the lane you are about to enter is far back enough.
  2. ②Slow down when changing lanes if the car behind you in the lane you are about to enter is far back enough.
  3. ③Let the car behind you in the lane you are about to enter pass if it is approaching.
  4. ④Change lanes quickly if the car behind you in the lane you are about to enter is approaching.
Show answer

Answer
① Change lanes while maintaining speed if the car behind you in the lane you are about to enter is far back enough.
③ Let the car behind you in the lane you are about to enter pass if it is approaching.

Reference
When there is a distance from the car behind you, change lanes while maintaining your speed, and when approaching, slow down and let the car behind you pass first.
