978. Which of the following is NOT a dangerous situation found in the video? (Refer to the website) (Video of bus slowing down and crossing the centerline)

  2. ②Limited visibility with the road ahead obscured
  3. ③Pedestrians entering the crosswalk

Show answer

Answer
④ Passengers on the sidewalk after exiting the bus

Reference
Pedestrians waiting to cross on a sidewalk connected to a crosswalk without a traffic light may enter suddenly against the driver's judgment. Therefore, when approaching a crosswalk, you must drive slowly and stop if there is a pedestrian crossing the crosswalk or about to cross. Crossing the center line due to a vehicle in front slowing down on a road curved to the right violates Article 13 of the Road Traffic Act, and this act is a direct cause of traffic accidents. Therefore, if the center line is a solid yellow line, you must drive on the right while maintaining a safe distance from the vehicle in front. Drivers following a tall large bus or large cargo vehicle cannot confirm the road conditions ahead, so they must drive slowly, keep a wide distance between vehicles, and maintain a space to brake or stop. In the video in question, it cannot be said that it is a dangerous situation for passengers to get off the bus and walk on the sidewalk.
