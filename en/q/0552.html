552. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following are TWO INCORRECT statements regarding the punishment of a driver (excluding personal mobility device)?

  1. ①A person who drives under the influence of alcohol and whose blood alcohol concentration is 0.2% or more – At least 1 year to 2 years of imprisonment or criminal fine of up to KRW 10 million
  2. ②A person who committed risky behavior jointly - Up to 2 years of imprisonment or criminal fine of up to KRW 5 million
  3. ③A person who engaged in reckless driving - Up to 1 year of imprisonment or criminal fine of up to KRW 5 million
  4. ④Driving a motorcycle without a license – A criminal fine of up to KRW 500,000 or detention
Show answer

Answer
① A person who drives under the influence of alcohol and whose blood alcohol concentration is 0.2% or more – At least 1 year to 2 years of imprisonment or criminal fine of up to KRW 10 million
④ Driving a motorcycle without a license – A criminal fine of up to KRW 500,000 or detention

Reference
① Driving with a blood alcohol concentration of 0.2% or more (one violation): imprisonment for 2–5 years or a fine of KRW 10–20 million. ② Joint dangerous driving: imprisonment for up to 2 years or a fine of up to KRW 5 million. ③ Reckless driving: imprisonment for up to 1 year or a fine of up to KRW 5 million. ④ Driving a motor-driven bicycle without a license: a fine of up to KRW 300,000 or detention.
