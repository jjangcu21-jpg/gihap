645. Which of the following is the safest way to drive a trailer truck loaded with cargo, traveling at a speed of 50 km/h on a road with one lane in one direction that curves to the right?

  1. ①Slowing down can cause the vehicle to overturn. It is safer to drive at an increased speed into the curve.
  2. ②Drive into the other lane to reduce the turning radius.
  3. ③Drive into the curve at the current speed so as not to hold up the traffic behind.
  4. ④Slow down and safely enter the curve to prevent the vehicle from rolling over due to centrifugal force.
Show answer

Answer
④ Slow down and safely enter the curve to prevent the vehicle from rolling over due to centrifugal force.

Reference
On curved roads, there is a risk of the vehicle overturning due to centrifugal force, so reduce speed and enter safely.
