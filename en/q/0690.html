690. In the given road situation, which of the following are the two safest ways of driving?

  1. ①Since the traffic signal is red, wait patiently.
  2. ②Since the pedestrians are crossing slowly, sound the horn to incite them to walk faster.
  3. ③Stop past the stop line in front of the adjacent vehicle to ensure a smooth change of lane.
  4. ④Anticipate the signal change and accelerate to make a quick start and allow a smooth flow of traffic.
  5. ⑤Stop at the stop line and wait for the signal.
Show answer

Answer
① Since the traffic signal is red, wait patiently.
⑤ Stop at the stop line and wait for the signal.

Reference
Appendix 2 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act, Types and Meanings of Signals Displayed by Signals, Article 27 (Protection of Pedestrians) Paragraph 1 of the Road Traffic Act. When a pedestrian is passing or is about to cross a crosswalk, the driver of any car or streetcar must temporarily stop in front of the crosswalk (referring to the stop line where a stop line is installed) so as not to obstruct or endanger pedestrians crossing (Explanation ②, ③, ⑤). Because there may be pedestrians suddenly jumping out from the sidewalk, anticipating and making a sudden start are dangerous driving methods (④ Explanation).
