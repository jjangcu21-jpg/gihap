985. Which of the following correctly matches the Road Traffic Act violations committed by the main driver shown in the video? (Refer to the website)

Show answer

Answer
④ Violations of the no-lane changing zone, no parking and stopping area, pedestrian protection duty, and signal

Reference
Article 5 of the Road Traffic Act. Paragraph 1. Pedestrians, drivers of cars, horses, and trams passing on the road must follow signals or instructions displayed by traffic safety facilities and signals or instructions given by any of the following persons (“no.” omitted). Article 27 of the Road Traffic Act. Paragraph 1. When pedestrians (including drivers of bicycles, etc. who get off their bicycles, pull or carry bicycles, etc. pursuant to Article 13-2, Paragraph 6) are passing or are about to cross a crosswalk, the driver of any car or streetcar must temporarily stop in front of the crosswalk (referring to the stop line where a stop line is installed) so as not to obstruct pedestrians crossing or endanger them. Article 19 (Securing safety distance, etc.) Paragraph 3. The driver of any vehicle must not change the course of the vehicle if there is a risk of interfering with the normal passage of other vehicles traveling in the direction being changed. Article 2 of the Road Traffic Act, “Parking” refers to keeping the car in a stopped state due to the driver waiting for passengers, loading cargo, the car breaking down, or for other reasons, or leaving the car in a state where the driver cannot drive the car immediately. “Stopping” refers to a state other than parking where the driver stops the vehicle for no more than 5 minutes. Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 6] Types, methods of making, and installation/management standards of safety signs Road markings 516-2 No stopping/parking signs. The place where the main character's car stopped in the video is a section of road where double-yellow road markings are installed, so stopping and parking are prohibited.
