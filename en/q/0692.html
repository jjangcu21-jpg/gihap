692. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Drive slowly or stop temporarily before proceeding so that the pedestrians on the road can walk safely.
  2. ②Continue driving with caution, as pedestrians may appear between parked vehicles.
  3. ③Drivers are not required to protect pedestrians as it is not a school zone.
  4. ④Parking briefly at the intersection corner is possible when visiting the store on the left.
  5. ⑤Keep honking to pass through, as there are many parked cars and pedestrians on the unlaned roads of residential areas.
