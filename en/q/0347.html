347. Which of the following correctly describes a Dutch Reach (method of opening the door) when exiting a vehicle?

  1. ①When exiting the vehicle, grab the handle with the hand farthest from the window, check if there is oncoming traffic from the rear, and open the door.
  2. ②When exiting the vehicle, grab the handle with the hand closest to the window, check if there is oncoming traffic from the front, and open the door.
  3. ③The Dutch Reach prevents the driver from moving the vehicle with a door open.
  4. ④The Dutch Reach is a traffic safety campaign started in the UK.
Show answer

Answer
① When exiting the vehicle, grab the handle with the hand farthest from the window, check if there is oncoming traffic from the rear, and open the door.

Reference
Dutch Reach is a traffic safety campaign that started in the Netherlands in the 1960s to prevent accidents with bicycles approaching from behind the sides of passenger cars. This is a method of opening the car door by holding the handle with the hand away from the window when the driver or passenger opens the car door to get out of the car. When you open the door like this, your body naturally rotates at least 45 degrees, allowing you to visually see the back, preventing accidents that occur with bicycles or motorcycles approaching from the rear of the car. An open-door car accident refers to an accident that occurs while the driver opens the car door and starts.
