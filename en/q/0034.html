34. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following is NOT a valid reason for postponing license renewal or the regular aptitude test?

  1. ①If the person is staying overseas
  2. ②If the person is unable to move due to an illness
  3. ③If the person is serving as a noncommissioned officer or higher in the Army, Navy, or Air Force pursuant to the Military Personnel Management Act
  4. ④If the person falls victim to a disaster or a calamity
Show answer

Answer
③ If the person is serving as a noncommissioned officer or higher in the Army, Navy, or Air Force pursuant to the Military Personnel Management Act

Reference
Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act, Article 55(1): 1. Staying abroad. 2. Having suffered a disaster. 3. Being unable to move because of illness or injury. 4. Being deprived of physical liberty by law. 5. Serving in the military as an enlisted member, including converted service as a conscripted police officer or firefighter, or performing alternative service under the relevant Alternative Service Act. 6. Other substantial reasons considered unavoidable under generally accepted social norms. The Korean source lists item 5 twice; the overlapping wording is combined here.
