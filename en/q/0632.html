632. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is NOT true about getting on or loading in a vehicle?

  1. ①The driver must not load more persons than the number prescribed by the Presidential Decree in the vehicle that he/she is driving.
  2. ②The driver must ensure the safety of passengers, such as making sure that the doors are properly closed so that no one falls off the vehicle while driving.
  3. ③The driver must tightly fasten or cover all cargo on his/her vehicle to prevent them from falling off.
  4. ④For safety reasons, the driver must place an infant or an animal on his/her lap while driving.
Show answer

Answer
④ For safety reasons, the driver must place an infant or an animal on his/her lap while driving.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 39 (Methods and Restrictions on Riding or Loading) Drivers of all vehicles must not drive in conditions that may impede safety, such as operating the driving controls while holding infants, young children or animals, or placing objects around the driver's seat.
