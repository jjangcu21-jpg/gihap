361. What is the most appropriate method of turning left at an intersection under the Road Traffic Act?

  1. ①Slow down along the centerline and turn left through the inner side of the intersection’s center.
  2. ②Left turns are allowed at a red light at a signalized intersection if there is an “unprotected left turn” sign.
  3. ③At a signalized intersection, a left-turning vehicle has priority over a vehicle traveling straight ahead.
  4. ④On a one-way road with three lanes, turning left from the right lane is allowed if the traffic is light.
Show answer

Answer
① Slow down along the centerline and turn left through the inner side of the intersection’s center.

Reference
Articles 25 and 26 of the Road Traffic Act When drivers of all vehicles wish to turn left at an intersection, they must drive slowly along the center line of the road in advance and turn left using the inside of the center of the intersection. If there is an unprotected left turn safety sign, you must turn left at the straight ahead light, and in case of simultaneous entry, vehicles going straight have priority over vehicles turning left.
