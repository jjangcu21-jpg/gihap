401. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is NOT true about a designated lane on an expressway? (Assuming smooth traffic flow and no exclusive bus lanes)

  1. ①Lane 1 of an expressway with three lanes in one direction can be used by passenger vehicles, light vans, vans, mini-buses, and buses to overtake other vehicles.
  2. ②You can use the lane on the left of the designated lane to overtake another vehicle.
  3. ③All vehicles can use the lane on the left of their respective designated lanes.
  4. ④The penalty for not using the designated lane for drivers of passenger vehicles is 10 points.
Show answer

Answer
③ All vehicles can use the lane on the left of their respective designated lanes.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 9] All vehicles may travel in the lane to the right of the designated lane. Appendix 8 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act. No. 39. Passenger cars, etc. 40,000 won, Appendix 28 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act. 3. No. 21. 10 penalty points
