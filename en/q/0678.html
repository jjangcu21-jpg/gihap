678. Which of the following statements regarding the driving skills test for a Class I driver’s license for small towing vehicles is correct?

  1. ①The participant is required to score at least 90 out of 100 to pass.
  2. ②The participant is required to pass the bent course, curved course, change of direction, and driving tests.
  3. ③The pass criteria for the small towing vehicle test course is within 5 minutes for each course.
  4. ④The participant will lose 5 points every time he or she fails to touch the confirmation line on each test course.
Show answer

Answer
① The participant is required to score at least 90 out of 100 to pass.

Reference
Article 66 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act, Enforcement Rules of the Same Act [Appendix Table 24] Functional Test Scoring Criteria and Passing Criteria The functional test for a small tow vehicle license must pass a bending course, a curved course, and a change-of-direction course, and 10 points will be deducted for each course if it exceeds 3 minutes, touches the detection line, or does not touch the confirmation line of the change-of-direction course. The passing standard is 90 points or higher.
