651. Which of the following is the part that connects the tractor and the trailer of a tow truck?

  1. ①A coupler
  2. ②A kingpin
  3. ③An outrigger
  4. ④A boom
Show answer

Answer
① A coupler

Reference
Coupler: A device that connects a tractor (towing vehicle) and a trailer (towed vehicle). The rear of the tractor is semicircular at the top, becoming narrower toward the center, and has a groove of about 20 centimeters. The right side of the coupler consists of a coupler operating handle and spring lock.
