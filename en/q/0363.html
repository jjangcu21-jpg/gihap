363. In which of the following locations is the installation of a roundabout recommended for ensuring smooth traffic flow and safety on the road?

  1. ①Locations where traffic volumes are not very high, but there are many intersection accidents
  2. ②Locations where there are one or more access roads at the intersection with three or more lanes in one direction
  3. ③Locations where the traffic volume at the roundabout exceeds its capacity
  4. ④Locations where the presence of a roundabout in a segment necessary for signal coordination would reduce the effectiveness of coordination
Show answer

Answer
① Locations where traffic volumes are not very high, but there are many intersection accidents

Reference
Roundabout Design Guidelines (August 2022, Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport) 1) Cases where roundabout installation is recommended ① Cases where the traffic volume level is too high to operate as a non-signalized intersection and too small to operate as a signalized intersection, reducing the efficiency of signal operation ② Cases where the traffic volume level is not high, but many traffic accidents occur at intersections ③ Cases where it is difficult for drivers to recognize the right of way ④ Y-shaped, T-shaped or other unusually shaped intersections ⑤ Intersections within traffic calming project areas 2) Cases where roundabout installation is not recommended ① Cases where the traffic volume level of the roundabout exceeds handling capacity ② Cases where roundabout design standards are not met ③ Cases where variable lanes are operated during peak times ④ Cases where intersections are within a section where signal interlocking is in effect ⑤ Cases where at least one access road at the intersection has three or more lanes each way case
