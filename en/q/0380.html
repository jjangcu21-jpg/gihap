380. Which of the following hand signals is used to indicate your intent to turn right?

  1. ①Extend your left arm out to the left and bend your forearm vertically upward.
  2. ②Extend your left arm out to the left, parallel to the road.
  3. ③Extend your right arm out to the right and hold it parallel to the road, and wave the extended arm forwards and backwards.
  4. ④Stick your left arm out of the vehicle and bend your forearm downward at an angle of 45°.
Show answer

Answer
① Extend your left arm out to the left and bend your forearm vertically upward.

Reference
When the driver wants to turn right, he extends his left arm outside the left side of the car body, bends his elbow, and raises it vertically.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
