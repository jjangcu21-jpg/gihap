104. Vehicle A is moving slowly on the right side of the expressway's main lane. In this case, what is the best method of entering the main lane?

  1. ①Slowly increase the speed and enter, but make sure you do so after Vehicle A passes.
  2. ②Accelerate and enter using an empty roadside.
  3. ③Stop at the end of the acceleration lane and enter after Vehicle A passes.
  4. ④Continue driving on the acceleration lane at the same speed as Vehicle A.
Show answer

Answer
① Slowly increase the speed and enter, but make sure you do so after Vehicle A passes.

Reference
When attempting to enter a highway, the driver of a car (excluding emergency vehicles) must not obstruct the passage of other cars traveling on the highway.
