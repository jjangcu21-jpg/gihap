853. Which of the following are the TWO safest ways to proceed in the given situation?

  1. ①Continue driving in the first lane according to the “Lane 2 ends” sign.
  2. ②Make a sudden stop to avoid the construction area.
  3. ③Switch to the second lane in advance as the road narrows ahead.
  4. ④Turn on the hazard lights to alert others to the construction area.
  5. ⑤Keep driving in the first lane and merge into the second lane right before the construction zone.
Show answer

Answer
③ Switch to the second lane in advance as the road narrows ahead.
④ Turn on the hazard lights to alert others to the construction area.

Reference
If it is unavoidable due to construction, I express my intention and change course safely so that other drivers can fully predict my driving behavior. Additionally, when driving on roads where conditions are difficult to predict, such as construction zones, you must drive slowly or stop temporarily and drive with caution.
