Answer

Reference
It is necessary to be prepared for driving where there are always dangers that cannot be seen. This is not simply a sign of danger, but an attitude of preparing for the worst. Drivers can easily prepare for sufficiently predictable risks when they appear, but they tend to neglect risks they have not learned or experienced. In particular, traffic accidents in residential areas occur when risks are neglected.
