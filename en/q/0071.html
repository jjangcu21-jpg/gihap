71. According to the Motor Vehicle Management Act, the period for regular vehicle inspection is from ( ) days before to ( ) days after the expiration date of the inspection validity. Which of the following fills in the blanks correctly?

  1. ①90, 31
  2. ②80, 41
  3. ③60, 51
  4. ④50, 61
Show answer

Answer
① 90, 31

Reference
The period of regular inspection under Article 77, Paragraph 2 of the Enforcement Rules of the Automobile Management Act shall be from 90 days before the expiration date of the inspection validity period (referring to the expiration date if the inspection validity period is extended or postponed pursuant to the provisions of Article 75) to 31 days after. If a conformity judgment is received in the regular inspection within this period, the periodic inspection is deemed to have been performed on the expiration date of the inspection validity period.
