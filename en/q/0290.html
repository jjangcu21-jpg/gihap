290. Which of the following are true about the special protection for school buses? (Select TWO)

  1. ①The method of overtaking a school bus for children is the same as the method prescribed for overtaking other kinds of vehicles.
  2. ②When children are getting on or off the bus, a car coming from the opposite direction on a road without a centerline should check that it is safe and drive slowly.
  3. ③When children are getting on or off the bus, a car coming from the opposite direction on a road with one lane in one direction should stop momentarily, check that it is safe, and drive slowly.
  4. ④When children are getting on or off the bus, vehicles in the same lane and adjacent lanes should stop momentarily, check that it is safe, and drive slowly.
Show answer

Answer
③ When children are getting on or off the bus, a car coming from the opposite direction on a road with one lane in one direction should stop momentarily, check that it is safe, and drive slowly.
④ When children are getting on or off the bus, vehicles in the same lane and adjacent lanes should stop momentarily, check that it is safe, and drive slowly.

Reference
When a children's school bus is stopped on the road and a device, such as a flashing light, is in operation to indicate that children or infants are getting on or off, vehicles traveling in the lane where the children's school bus is stopped and the lane immediately next to the lane must stop temporarily, check for safety, and then drive slowly. Also, on roads without a center line or roads with one lane each way, drivers of vehicles traveling in the opposite direction must also stop temporarily and check for safety before reaching the children's school bus and then drive slowly.
