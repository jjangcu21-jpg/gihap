870. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following correctly describes this road safety sign?

  1. ①Regulation sign
  2. ②Priority sign for vehicles going straight
  3. ③Road sign for merging with left lane
  4. ④Prohibition on turning left
Show answer

Answer
③ Road sign for merging with left lane

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 6] Left merge road sign (Caution sign No. 108) Notifies that there is a left merge road.
