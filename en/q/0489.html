489. Which of the following is true about anger control techniques for preventing road rage and car crashes?

  1. ①An anger control technique that is a mindfulness-based practice designed to help you defuse stress is called the STOP technique.
  2. ②An anger control technique during which you try to push out the negative thoughts and focus on happy thoughts for 1–2 minutes is called the time-out technique.
  3. ③An anger control technique during which you question your passive recognition of the belief system and distorted thoughts that trigger anger is called the reminiscence technique.
  4. ④An anger control technique during which you repeatedly contract and relax your arms, legs, abdomen, chest, shoulders, and other body parts is called the contract-relax stretching technique.
Show answer

Answer
④ An anger control technique during which you repeatedly contract and relax your arms, legs, abdomen, chest, shoulders, and other body parts is called the contract-relax stretching technique.

Reference
Traffic Safety Rules 2021 Revised 2nd Edition Behavioral techniques to control anger include the time-out technique, the stop button technique, and tension relaxation training techniques. ① The time-out technique is an anger management method that allows you to get out of a situation where your emotions are boiling over for a while, gain some time, and find peace of mind. ② An anger control method that involves stopping negative thoughts that cause anger and concentrating on a happy scene you have been thinking about for 1-2 minutes is called the stop button technique. ③ The experience recall questioning technique is not an anger management method. ④ The method of tensing each part of the body, such as arms, legs, lower abdomen, chest, and shoulders, as much as possible and then relaxing them to repeat a comfortable state is called a tension relaxation training technique.
