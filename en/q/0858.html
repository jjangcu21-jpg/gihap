858. In this situation, which of the following are the two safest ways of proceeding?

  1. ①Sound the horn continuously to pass the parked vehicles quickly as they may start to move suddenly.
  2. ②Drive slowly and carefully as pedestrians may suddenly emerge from between the parked cars.
  3. ③Sound the horn to encourage the cyclist ahead to pass quickly.
  4. ④Drive carefully and pay attention to the surroundings, taking into account speed bumps, cyclists, and situations with limited visibility.
  5. ⑤Accelerate quickly as there is sufficient distance between you and the cyclist.
Show answer

Answer
② Drive slowly and carefully as pedestrians may suddenly emerge from between the parked cars.
④ Drive carefully and pay attention to the surroundings, taking into account speed bumps, cyclists, and situations with limited visibility.

Reference
There are cars parked on both sides and a bicycle crossing the road in front, so this is a section where an unexpected situation can easily occur. Children or pedestrians may suddenly jump out from between parked vehicles, so be careful and drive slowly. And the safest way to drive is to drive carefully, taking into account bicycles in front, speed bumps, and limited visibility. On the other hand, driving quickly while honking the horn or accelerating even though the distance from the bicycle is sufficient are incorrect driving behaviors that can lead to dangerous situations.
