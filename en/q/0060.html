60. Under the Motor Vehicle Management Act and its Enforcement Decree, what is the validity period for the initial inspection conducted on a new non-commercial passenger car?

  1. ①1 year
  2. ②2 years
  3. ③4 years
  4. ④5 years
Show answer

Answer
④ 5 years

Reference
Enforcement Rules of the Automobile Management Act [Appendix 15-2] The initial inspection validity period for non-commercial passenger vehicles is 5 years.
