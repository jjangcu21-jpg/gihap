66. Under the Motor Vehicle Management Act and its Enforcement Decree, what is the standard validity period of a temporary driving permit issued when purchasing a new car?

  1. ①Within 10 days
  2. ②Within 15 days
  3. ③Within 20 days
  4. ④Within 30 days
Show answer

Answer
① Within 10 days

Reference
The validity period of the temporary operation permit under Article 7, Paragraph 2, Item 1 of the Enforcement Decree of the Automobile Management Act is within 10 days.
