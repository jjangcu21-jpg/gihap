56. According to the Act on the Promotion of and Support for Commercialization of Autonomous Vehicles, which of the following is NOT true about autonomous vehicles?

  1. ①The types of autonomous vehicles can be divided into fully autonomous vehicles and semi-autonomous vehicles.
  2. ②Fully autonomous vehicles refer to vehicles that can operate solely on autonomous driving systems without the need for a driver or any intervention from the driver or passengers.
  3. ③Semi-autonomous vehicles refer to vehicles that cannot operate solely on autonomous driving systems or which require continuous attention from the driver or passengers, necessitating their intervention.
  4. ④Autonomous vehicles are limited to passenger vehicles, and this law does not apply to buses or trucks.
Show answer

Answer
④ Autonomous vehicles are limited to passenger vehicles, and this law does not apply to buses or trucks.

Reference
Under Article 2(1), item 1 and Article 2(2) of the Act on Promotion and Support of Commercialization of Autonomous Vehicles: 1. A partially autonomous vehicle needs driver or passenger intervention, for example because the system cannot drive alone or requires continuous driver supervision. 2. A fully autonomous vehicle can operate using the autonomous driving system alone and needs no driver or driver/passenger intervention. ④ Autonomous vehicles are not limited to passenger cars; they also include passenger vans/buses and freight vehicles.
