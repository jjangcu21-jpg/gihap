407. A passenger vehicle is driving in Lane 2 on an expressway with three lanes in one direction according to the Road Traffic Act. What is the correct lane for overtaking? (Assuming smooth traffic flow and no exclusive bus lanes)

  1. ①The first lane
  2. ②The second lane
  3. ③The third lane
  4. ④Any of the above
Show answer

Answer
① The first lane

Reference
You can overtake using the first lane.
