782. Which of the following are the safe ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Quickly drive through the intersection while using high-beam headlights to warn the bicycle rider.
  2. ②Drive through the intersection at a lower speed but stay cautious as the bicycle may enter abruptly.
  3. ③Anticipate that the bicycle will stay in its lane and drive through the intersection.
  4. ④Quickly drive through the intersection while repeatedly honking to warn the bicycle rider not to do anything dangerous.
  5. ⑤Because the bicycle is on the car lane, pass through while ensuring that the bicycle has sufficient space on the side.
Show answer

Answer
② Drive through the intersection at a lower speed but stay cautious as the bicycle may enter abruptly.
⑤ Because the bicycle is on the car lane, pass through while ensuring that the bicycle has sufficient space on the side.

Reference
Risk prediction. Proceeding in accordance with the Road Traffic Act cannot be said to be a violation. However, in the situation in question, since there are no cars or horses passing through the intersection, there is a high possibility that cyclists will jaywalk without anticipating the entry of other cars. Also, even if you do not jaywalk, the bicycle that passed the intersection is located on the second lane, so it can be said that a safe driving method is for drivers passing the intersection to create a safe space to the side of the bicycle and drive slowly.
