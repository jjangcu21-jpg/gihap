701. Which of the following are incorrect ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Stop just before the stop line to travel straight.
  2. ②To make a U-turn, make the turn safely from the second lane through the intersection if there are no vehicles ahead.
  3. ③Stop briefly before the crosswalk to drop off a passenger.
  4. ④To turn right, stop before the stop line and slowly turn right.
  5. ⑤If you are using a crosswalk, you must dismount from the bicycle and walk with it.
Show answer

Answer
② To make a U-turn, make the turn safely from the second lane through the intersection if there are no vehicles ahead.
③ Stop briefly before the crosswalk to drop off a passenger.

Reference
If there is a signal installed at the intersection that only displays three-color lights of green, yellow, and red, and there is no sign indicating an unprotected left turn or U-turn, in principle, left turns or U-turns by vehicles and horses are not permitted. Therefore, if you did not stop at the stop line when the red light was on at the above intersection but turned left or made a U-turn and proceeded, this should be considered a case of driving in violation of the traffic signal under Article 5 of the Road Traffic Act, unless there are special circumstances (Supreme Court ruling 95Do3093, May 31, 1996). Bicyclists can use the bicycle crossing (Article 15-2, Paragraph 2 of the Road Traffic Act) or get off their bicycles and move to the crosswalk as pedestrians according to pedestrian signals. Stopping and parking are prohibited within 10 m of the edge of a railway crossing or a crosswalk (Road Traffic Act, Article 32, Paragraph 5).
