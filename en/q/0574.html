574. There is a pedestrian walking his/her bicycle to cross the street ahead of you. Which of the following is the safest way to approach?

  1. ①Swiftly drive past the pedestrian from whichever side is more open.
  2. ②Use the headlights and horn to let the pedestrian know you are approaching.
  3. ③Fully stop the vehicle at a certain distance from the pedestrian.
  4. ④Make the pedestrian stop as you have the right of way.
Show answer

Answer
③ Fully stop the vehicle at a certain distance from the pedestrian.

Reference
When there is someone crossing the road ahead while pushing a bicycle, the safest way to drive is to pause at a safe distance and allow them to cross safely.
