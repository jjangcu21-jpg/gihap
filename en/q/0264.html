264. Stopping and parking are prohibited within ( ) meters of fire-extinguishing water supply system, emergency firefighting equipment, and firefighting systems under the Road Traffic Act. Which of the following best fills the parentheses?

  1. ①5
  2. ②6
  3. ③8
  4. ④10
Show answer

Answer
① 5

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 32 (Prohibition of Stopping and Parking) Drivers of all vehicles must not stop or park their vehicles in any of the following places. However, this does not apply to cases where this Act, an order under this Act, or instructions from a police officer are followed or a temporary stop is made to prevent danger. 6. Places within 5 meters from each of the following items: A place where fire-fighting water facilities or emergency fire-fighting equipment are installed in accordance with Article 10 of the Framework Act on Fire Services. A place where facilities prescribed by Presidential Decree are installed as fire-fighting facilities pursuant to Article 2 (1) 1 of the Act on Installation and Management of Fire-fighting Facilities.
