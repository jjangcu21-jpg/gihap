Road signs · 2 pts

928. Which of the following vehicles cannot pass through the road where this safety sign is installed?

  1. ①Electric bicycle
  2. ②Electric two-wheeled parallel vehicle
  3. ③Personal mobility device
  4. ④Motorcycle (excluding personal mobility devices)
Show answer

Answer
④ Motorcycle (excluding personal mobility devices)

Reference
Annex 6 of the Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules, instruction sign No. 302, and bicycle-only road sign indicate that it is a bicycle-only road or exclusive area. Road Traffic Act Article 2 (Definitions). Bicycles, etc. refer to bicycles and personal mobility devices. Bicycle refers to bicycles and electric bicycles defined in Article 2, Paragraph 1 and 1-2 of the Act on Promotion of Bicycle Use. Article 2-2 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act (Standards for personal mobility devices) 1. Electric kickboard 2. Electric two-wheeled parallel vehicle 3. Bicycle that can be moved only by electric motor power
