540. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following is the correct sum of the "administrative fine imposed on a guardian who let a child drive a personal mobility device (PMD) on a road" and "traffic penalty imposed on a driver who drove a PMD under the influence (excluding refusal to take a field sobriety test)"?

  1. ①KRW 100,000
  2. ②KRW 200,000
  3. ③KRW 300,000
  4. ④KRW 400,000
Show answer

Answer
② KRW 200,000

Reference
Appendix 6 of the Road Traffic Act Enforcement Decree: a guardian who allows a child to drive a personal mobility device on a road faces an administrative fine of 100,000 won. Appendix 8: for riding while intoxicated, the traffic penalty is 100,000 won for a personal mobility device and 30,000 won for a bicycle.
