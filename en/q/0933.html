Road signs · 2 pts

933. Which of the following statements about the activated left-turn signal shown on the safety sign is incorrect?

  1. ①When a vehicle stops within the rectangular marking (detection line) on the road, the sensor is activated and detects the vehicle.
  2. ②When there are no left-turning vehicles, extra time can be allocated to the straight-through signal.
  3. ③It helps improve traffic flow, and air pollution is expected to be reduced.
  4. ④To operate it, the driver must stop and press a switch installed on the left-hand side of the road, and then wait briefly.
Show answer

Answer
④ To operate it, the driver must stop and press a switch installed on the left-hand side of the road, and then wait briefly.

Reference
The safety sign is a “left turn response signal” sign and corresponds to an instruction sign (Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 6] No. 335). When a vehicle stops within a square mark (detection line) on the road, the left turn signal detects the vehicle and starts giving a left turn signal. When there are no left-turning vehicles, more time can be given to the signal to go straight, which is expected to smooth the flow of traffic and reduce air pollution. In addition, as idling time is reduced, economic benefits such as reduced fuel costs for drivers can be expected (Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport press release, Korea Road Traffic Authority promotional material).
