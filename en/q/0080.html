80. Which of the following is correct regarding the precautions to take when getting in and out of a car?

  1. ①When getting in or out, check if there is a car coming from behind.
  2. ②Get out of the car immediately after completely opening the door.
  3. ③When a person exits a vehicle from the back seat, the driver should look straight forward.
  4. ④When leaving the driver's seat temporarily, you need not turn off the car's engine.
Show answer

Answer
① When getting in or out, check if there is a car coming from behind.

Reference
When drivers get on and off, check to see if there are any vehicles coming from behind.
