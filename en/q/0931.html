931. Which two statements about A and B among the following are incorrect? (Choose two)

  1. ①A is a guide sign indicating a pedestrian-priority road, and B is a road marking indicating a pedestrian-priority road.
  2. ②On a pedestrian-priority road, pedestrians can use the entire road.
  3. ③On a pedestrian-priority road, pedestrians have priority so they may intentionally obstruct vehicle traffic.
  4. ④On a pedestrian-priority road, only pedestrians are allowed to use the road. A B
Show answer

Answer
③ On a pedestrian-priority road, pedestrians have priority so they may intentionally obstruct vehicle traffic.
④ On a pedestrian-priority road, only pedestrians are allowed to use the road. A B

Reference
A is a pedestrian priority road indication sign, and B is a road surface sign with the same meaning (Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 6]). On pedestrian priority roads, pedestrians can travel all parts of the road. On the other hand, even if pedestrians have priority, pedestrians must not intentionally obstruct the passage of vehicles on the road (Article 8, Paragraph 3 of the Road Traffic Act). ④ is an explanation of the pedestrian-only road (Article 2, Paragraph 31 of the Road Traffic Act).
