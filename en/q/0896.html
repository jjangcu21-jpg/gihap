896. What does this safety sign signify?

  1. ①You can park momentarily to load cargo.
  2. ②You can stop momentarily to drop off a passenger.
  3. ③The sign is installed at a section where parking and stopping are prohibited.
  4. ④Two-wheeled vehicles are allowed to park.
Show answer

Answer
② You can stop momentarily to drop off a passenger.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 6] Regulatory sign 219, no parking sign, installed in areas where parking of cars is prohibited, in front of a section or place on the road, or on the right side of the road at a necessary point.
