623. Which of the following statements regarding the driving characteristics of a large truck is correct?

  1. ①The stability of a large truck is reduced by the heavy weight and long wheelbase.
  2. ②The straight-driving stability of a large truck can be momentarily reduced at high speeds due to vehicle body vibration.
  3. ③The steering wheel recovery of a large truck is better than that of a compact passenger car.
  4. ④The higher driving position in a large truck enables improved field of vision in poor weather conditions.
Show answer

Answer
② The straight-driving stability of a large truck can be momentarily reduced at high speeds due to vehicle body vibration.

Reference
The high driver's seat of a large truck does not improve visibility in abnormal weather, and the self-centering action of the steering wheel is less smooth than that of a small passenger car, and its stability is higher than that of a passenger car due to its heavy weight and long wheelbase.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
