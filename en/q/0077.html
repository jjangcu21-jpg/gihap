77. Which of the following is the appropriate permeability of visible rays through the front windshield of motor vehicles (other than children’s school buses)?

  1. ①40%
  2. ②50%
  3. ③60%
  4. ④70%
Show answer

Answer
④ 70%

Reference
According to Article 28 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act, the standard for visible light transmittance of automobile window glass must be 70% for the front window and 40% for the window glass on the left and right sides of the driver's seat.
