674. Which of the following may be painted or marked on a recovery vehicle?

  1. ①Vehicle painting and signs similar to those used on a traffic control vehicle
  2. ②Vehicle painting and signs similar to those used on a crime investigation vehicle
  3. ③Vehicle painting and signs similar to those used on an emergency vehicle
  4. ④Indication of the driver's phone number to be called in case of an emergency
Show answer

Answer
④ Indication of the driver's phone number to be called in case of an emergency

Reference
Pursuant to Article 42 (Restrictions on Similar Signs and Prohibition of Operation) of the Road Traffic Act, Article 27 of the Enforcement Decree of the same Act, Article 27 (Scope of Similar Signs, Painting, etc.), Article 42 (2) of the Act, paints and signs restricted to automobiles, etc. (excluding personal mobility devices) are as follows. 1. Coloring or signs that can be mistaken for emergency vehicles 2. Pictures, symbols or text that are repulsive to others, such as displaying profanity or depicting obscene acts
