384. Which of the following is the correct method of turning left?

  1. ①Turn on the left turn signal at least 30m (100m on expressways) away from where you want to turn left.
  2. ②Turn on the left turn signal at least 10m (100m on expressways) away from where you want to turn left.
  3. ③Turn on the left turn signal at least 20m (80m on expressways) away from where you want to turn left.
  4. ④Turn on the left turn signal when you reach the location where you want to turn left.
Show answer

Answer
① Turn on the left turn signal at least 30m (100m on expressways) away from where you want to turn left.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Decree, Appendix 2 (Timing and method of signaling): When changing lanes, activate the direction indicator at least 30 meters before the point where you intend to change lanes.
