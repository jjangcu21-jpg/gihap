772. Which of the following are the most incorrect ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①To turn right, stop just before the stop line, and then turn right.
  2. ②When turning right, proceed slowly along the right edge of the road before turning.
  3. ③Drivers turning right must pay attention to pedestrians, bicycle riders, and others who are stopping or proceeding, depending on the signal.
  4. ④If a passenger is getting out of the car, you can stop briefly in front of a fire hydrant.
  5. ⑤On city streets, it is unnecessary to turn on the headlights because the surroundings are well-lit even at night.
Show answer

Answer
④ If a passenger is getting out of the car, you can stop briefly in front of a fire hydrant.
⑤ On city streets, it is unnecessary to turn on the headlights because the surroundings are well-lit even at night.

Reference
When drivers of all vehicles wish to turn right at an intersection, they must first turn right while driving slowly along the right edge of the road. In this case, the driver of the car turning right must pay attention to pedestrians or bicycles stopping or proceeding according to the signal (Article 25, Paragraph 1 of the Road Traffic Act). “Slow driving” refers to driving at a slow speed that allows the driver to immediately stop the car or streetcar (Road Traffic Act, Article 2, Paragraph 28). From sunset until sunrise, drivers of all cars or trams must turn on headlights, side lights, tail lights, and other lights as prescribed by Presidential Decree (Article 37, Paragraph 1, Subparagraph 1 of the Road Traffic Act).
