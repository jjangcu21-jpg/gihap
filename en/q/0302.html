302. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is true about the operation of the yellow and red strobe lights installed on a school bus for the purpose of allowing children or infants to get on or off the bus?

  1. ①The red indicator light(s) must be flashing when stopping the bus.
  2. ②The red indicator light(s) must be flashing when braking.
  3. ③The yellow indicator light(s) must be flashing when attempting to stop on the road.
  4. ④Both yellow and red indicator light(s) must be flashing when parking.
Show answer

Answer
③ The yellow indicator light(s) must be flashing when attempting to stop on the road.

Reference
Article 48 of the Rules on Performance and Standards for Automobiles and Automobile Parts (Other Standards for Lighting) Paragraph 4, No. 5 5. When attempting to stop or start on the road, the following standards must be met a. When stopping on the road, the driver must be able to operate the yellow or amber light to flash. When the hatch for boarding and disembarking children is opened after the flashing of item A, the red indicator light will automatically flash. When the hatch is closed for departure, the yellow or amber light will automatically flash again. When item c is flashing, the red light, yellow light, or amber light must not flash at the same time. Road Traffic Act, Article 53, Paragraph 1. ① A person driving a children's school bus must operate devices such as flashing lights in accordance with Article 51, Paragraph 1 only when children or infants are getting on or off the bus, and must display a sign in accordance with Article 51, Paragraph 3 only when driving with children or infants on board.
