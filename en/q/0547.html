547. Which of the following has a different traffic penalty regarding the violation of laws by a driver (over the age of 13) of a personal mobility device?

  1. ①Driving without a driver's license
  2. ②Refusing to take a breath test as required by a police officer
  3. ③Driving under the influence of alcohol
  4. ④Driving in a state wherein there is risk of being unable to drive normally due to the influence of drugs
Show answer

Answer
② Refusing to take a breath test as required by a police officer

Reference
Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act [Appendix 8] ①, ③, ④ are fines of 100,000 won, ② are fines of 130,000 won
