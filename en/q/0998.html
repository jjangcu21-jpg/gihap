998. Glare may occur when driving on a curved road at night. What should the driver do? (Refer to the website)

Video questions play in the Gihap app or on the official site.

Official video ↗
  1. ①The driver should look at the right edge of the road.
  2. ②The driver should increase his/her speed in order to get out of the glare.
  3. ③The driver should decelerate by slamming on the brakes.
  4. ④The driver should look at the left edge of the road.
Show answer

Answer
① The driver should look at the right edge of the road.

Reference
Article 19 of the Road Traffic Act (securing a safe distance, etc.), Article 20 of the Road Traffic Act (obligation to give way), Article 31 of the Road Traffic Act (places to slow down or stop temporarily), Article 37 of the Road Traffic Act (lighting of cars and trams) ○ At night, there is a limit to judging risks because you can only see within the range of the headlights, so it is important to reduce speed and secure sufficient space and a safe distance to cope with sudden movements of other traffic.
