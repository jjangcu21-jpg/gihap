551. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which TWO belong to the category of “vehicles”?

  1. ①Drilling equipment (truck-mounted)
  2. ②A road stabilizer
  3. ③A bicycle
  4. ④A stroller
Show answer

Answer
① Drilling equipment (truck-mounted)
② A road stabilizer

Reference
Pursuant to Article 2 of the Road Traffic Act and the proviso to Article 26, Paragraph 1 of the Construction Machinery Management Act, among construction machines, dump trucks, asphalt spreaders, road stabilizers, concrete mix trucks, concrete pumps, and boring machines (truck-loaded) are included in automobiles.
