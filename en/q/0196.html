196. When a cargo falls from a vehicle and injures a pedestrian, necessitating a two-week period of hospitalization, which two penalties among the following will be imposed on the driver?

  1. ①Criminal offense charges are imposed on the driver regardless of the victim’s wishes to file charges.
  2. ②No charges are filed if the driver comes to an agreement with the victim.
  3. ③No charges are filed if the driver has a full-coverage insurance policy.
  4. ④The driver is charged with a criminal offense regardless of the presence of a full-coverage insurance policy.
Show answer

Answer
① Criminal offense charges are imposed on the driver regardless of the victim’s wishes to file charges.
④ The driver is charged with a criminal offense regardless of the presence of a full-coverage insurance policy.

Reference
Under Article 3(2), item 12 of the Act on Special Cases Concerning the Settlement of Traffic Accidents, driving without taking the necessary measures to prevent cargo from falling, in violation of Article 39(4) of the Road Traffic Act, is subject to criminal punishment even when the driver has comprehensive insurance.
