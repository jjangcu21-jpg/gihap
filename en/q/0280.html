280. According to the Road Traffic Act, how many hours of safety education are required for persons subject to education regarding school buses for children?

  1. ①1 hour or more
  2. ②3 hours or more
  3. ③5 hours or more
  4. ④6 hours or more
Show answer

Answer
② 3 hours or more

Reference
Safety education for children's school buses is conducted for at least 3 hours through lectures and audio-visual education on children's behavioral characteristics for traffic safety, laws and regulations related to the operation of children's school buses, analysis of major accident cases of children's school buses, and other matters necessary to protect children while driving, boarding and disembarking, etc. (Article 31-2 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act).
