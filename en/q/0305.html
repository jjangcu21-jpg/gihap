305. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following children is NOT walking in a safe manner?

  1. ①A child walking on the edge of a roadway that is clearly separated from the sidewalk
  2. ②A child walking on the edge of a one-way road while facing the oncoming traffic
  3. ③A child walking on the edge of a roadway that does not have a clear separation from the sidewalk while facing the oncoming traffic
  4. ④A child walking on the right-hand side of a sidewalk
Show answer

Answer
① A child walking on the edge of a roadway that is clearly separated from the sidewalk

Reference
Pedestrians must use the sidewalk on roads where the sidewalk and roadway are separated. Where they are not separated, pedestrians must walk along the road edge or designated roadside area. On sidewalks, walking on the right is the general rule (Road Traffic Act Article 8).
