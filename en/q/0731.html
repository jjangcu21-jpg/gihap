731. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Since the road is slippery, switch to the third lane and slow down before exiting the tunnel.
  2. ②Speed up as you exit the tunnel since you do not know the situation outside the tunnel.
  3. ③Switch to the first lane and speed up, as lane changes are allowed inside the tunnel.
  4. ④Slow down and drive slowly, as the road outside the tunnel may be slippery.
  5. ⑤Be cautious of light adaptation when exiting the tunnel.
Show answer

Answer
④ Slow down and drive slowly, as the road outside the tunnel may be slippery.
⑤ Be cautious of light adaptation when exiting the tunnel.

Reference
When drivers of all vehicles wish to turn right at an intersection, they must first turn right while driving slowly along the right edge of the road. In this case, the driver of the car turning right must pay attention to pedestrians or bicycles stopping or proceeding according to the signal (Article 25, Paragraph 1 of the Road Traffic Act). If you want to turn right, operate the turn signal 30m before reaching the intersection (Appendix 2 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act). Cutting in front of a car that is stopped or moving slowly to prevent danger is a violation of cutting in (Articles 23 and 22, Paragraph 2, No. 3 of the Road Traffic Act). If you suddenly come out of a dark place into a bright place, you may experience a light adaptation phenomenon that takes time for your eyes to adjust to the bright light, so you need to slow down and take a safe driving stance to prepare for unexpected situations.
