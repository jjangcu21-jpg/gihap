688. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Drive at a speed of less than 30 km/h.
  2. ②To enter the underground parking lot of a commercial building 10 meters ahead on the right, stop temporarily and drive slowly to ensure safety.
  3. ③To enter the underground parking lot of a commercial building 10 meters ahead on the left, stop temporarily and drive slowly to ensure safety.
  4. ④When intending to turn left, turn on the turn signal in advance, drive slowly, and turn left using the outer part of the intersection's center.
  5. ⑤To drive a personal mobility device (PMD) at the downtown area, drive on the sidewalk.
Show answer

Answer
① Drive at a speed of less than 30 km/h.
② To enter the underground parking lot of a commercial building 10 meters ahead on the right, stop temporarily and drive slowly to ensure safety.

Reference
To enter the commercial underground parking lot on the left 10 meters ahead, you must drive while crossing the center line. When drivers of all vehicles wish to turn left at an intersection, they must drive slowly along the center line of the road in advance and turn left using the inside of the center of the intersection (Road Traffic Act, Article 25, Paragraph 2). When driving a personal mobility device, you must drive on the carriageway if there is no dedicated road.
