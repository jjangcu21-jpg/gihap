30. Which of the following correctly describes the renewal period of a driver’s license under the Road Traffic Act (for drivers under 65 years of age only)?

  1. ①Within two months before or after one's birthday in the year that marks the tenth anniversary since passing the driver’s license test.
  2. ②Within four months before or after one's birthday in the year that marks the tenth anniversary since passing the driver’s license test.
  3. ③Within six months before or after one's birthday in the year that marks the tenth anniversary since passing the driver’s license test.
  4. ④Within eight months before or after one's birthday in the year that marks the tenth anniversary since passing the driver’s license test.
Show answer

Answer
③ Within six months before or after one's birthday in the year that marks the tenth anniversary since passing the driver’s license test.

Reference
Road Traffic Act, Article 87, effective 1 January 2026: License holders must renew through the city or provincial police chief within the prescribed period. For the first renewal, count 10 years from passing the test under Article 83(1) or (2); use 5 years for those aged at least 65 but under 75 on that test date, 3 years for those aged 75 or older, and 3 years for a Class 1 ordinary license holder blind in one eye. The renewal window is the 6 months before and the 6 months after the birthday in the year when that interval ends. Subsequent renewals use the same intervals counted from the preceding renewal date, with age assessed on that date, and the same birthday-based window.
