193. Which of the following is the best method of crossing the road for a pedestrian when the green light at the crosswalk is flashing?

  1. ①A pedestrian who is not yet in the crosswalk must wait for the next green light to start crossing.
  2. ②Stand still in the middle of the crosswalk.
  3. ③Cross the road without waiting for the next signal.
  4. ④The pedestrian can start crossing the road as long as the light does not turn red.
Show answer

Answer
① A pedestrian who is not yet in the crosswalk must wait for the next green light to start crossing.

Reference
<Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules Annex 2 Types of signals displayed by signals and meaning of signals> Flashing green light: Pedestrians must not start crossing, and pedestrians crossing must quickly complete the crossing or stop crossing and return to the sidewalk.
