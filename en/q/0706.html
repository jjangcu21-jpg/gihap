706. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Honk to induce the bus ahead to increase speed.
  2. ②Check your surroundings and move into the first lane.
  3. ③Turn on the hazard lights, and then speed up to switch to the first lane.
  4. ④You need not pay attention to pedestrians who may appear in front of the bus as you are switching to the first lane.
  5. ⑤If there is a vehicle behind in the first lane, do not force yourself to change lanes but wait behind the bus.
Show answer

Answer
② Check your surroundings and move into the first lane.
⑤ If there is a vehicle behind in the first lane, do not force yourself to change lanes but wait behind the bus.

Reference
Continuous sounding of the horn without a justifiable reason should be avoided. When changing directions, turn on the turn signal and check the safety of the front and rear vehicles before proceeding. If visibility is poor, pay attention to the safety of pedestrians.
