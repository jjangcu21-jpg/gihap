320. The driver of a passenger car slammed on the brakes after spotting an elderly person crossing a crosswalk without pedestrian signal lights but unfortunately hit him/her, and he/she was hospitalized for two weeks. Which TWO are correct among the following statements?

  1. ①The driver is not responsible at all as there were no pedestrian signal lights.
  2. ②The driver is subject to civil liability.
  3. ③Only the elderly person is subject to criminal prosecution for crossing illegally.
  4. ④The driver is subject to criminal liability.
Show answer

Answer
② The driver is subject to civil liability.
④ The driver is subject to criminal liability.

Reference
Drivers are subject to civil and criminal liability for crosswalk traffic accidents.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
