974. Which of the following is the correct behavior for the driver in the video? (Refer to the website)

Video questions play in the Gihap app or on the official site.

Official video ↗
  1. ①Drive at low speed behind the police car.
  2. ②Immediately report the police car driver’s violation.
  3. ③Overtake if there is a safe space in the left lane.
  4. ④Overtake if there is a safe space in the right lane.
Show answer

Answer
① Drive at low speed behind the police car.

Reference
In the video, when a police car drives down a roadway in a zigzag pattern, it gives signals and instructions that cause a virtual traffic jam. This technique is also called a traffic brake. The purpose of this is to prevent traffic accidents that may lead to secondary or tertiary accidents in cases of dropped objects on the road, follow-up measures after traffic accidents, and other dangerous situations. Therefore, drivers of other cars must reduce their speed, drive slowly, do not mistake the actions of police car drivers for violations, and must follow signals and instructions.
