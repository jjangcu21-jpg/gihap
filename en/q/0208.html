208. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is NOT a circumstance that requires stopping temporarily while driving?

  1. ①When a child crosses the road without a guardian
  2. ②When the vehicle traffic light is flashing a red light
  3. ③When a child is sitting or standing on the road
  4. ④When the vehicle traffic light is flashing yellow
Show answer

Answer
④ When the vehicle traffic light is flashing yellow

Reference
Article 49 of the Road Traffic Act (Matters to be observed by all drivers) Paragraph 1 No. 2, Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules Appendix 2 When the vehicle traffic light is a flashing yellow light, you can proceed while paying attention to other traffic or safety signs.
