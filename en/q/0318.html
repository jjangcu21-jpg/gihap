318. Which of the following is true about the general characteristics of senior citizens who are considered people with mobility impairments?

  1. ①Their reflexes are slow but they are quick in making decisions from experience.
  2. ②They have poor eyesight but high hearing sensitivity.
  3. ③They may not be quick in responding to unexpected situations, but their cognitive ability is heightened in such cases.
  4. ④They lose mobility as their health conditions deteriorate.
Show answer

Answer
④ They lose mobility as their health conditions deteriorate.

Reference
General characteristics of the elderly include slowed reflexes, weakened vision and hearing, and as the physical condition ages, the ability to respond to emergencies and cognitive abilities gradually decline.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
