665. Which of the following is an inappropriate measure on the part of the driver of a recovery vehicle at the scene of an accident with injured persons?

  1. ①Check the injured for consciousness.
  2. ②Check the injured for breathing.
  3. ③Check the injured for bleeding.
  4. ④Load the crashed vehicle onto the towing vehicle and negotiate the towing cost.
Show answer

Answer
④ Load the crashed vehicle onto the towing vehicle and negotiate the towing cost.

Reference
At the accident scene, you must not induce the parties involved in the accident vehicle not to deal with the accident or urge them to reach an agreement regarding the accident.
