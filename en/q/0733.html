733. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①You can change lanes without using the turn signal in lanes where lane changes are allowed.
  2. ②A car traveling in the first lane can switch to the second lane.
  3. ③You can merge from the on-ramp directly into the first lane.
  4. ④A car traveling in the second lane can switch to the first lane.
  5. ⑤You can switch from the second lane to the entry lane.
Show answer

Answer
② A car traveling in the first lane can switch to the second lane.
④ A car traveling in the second lane can switch to the first lane.

Reference
The vehicle's direction must not be changed in places where safety signs are installed and direction changes are specifically prohibited. You can change course in the white dotted line section, but you must not change course in the solid white line section. In sections with double line markings with solid white and dotted lines, course changes are only possible on the side with the dotted line. When changing directions, you must turn on the turn signal, and it is safe to proceed in one lane at a time, paying attention to safety.
