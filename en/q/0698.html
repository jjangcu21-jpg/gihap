698. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Making a U-turn at an intersection with three-color traffic lights is allowed even if there is a no U-turn sign, as long as it does not interfere with other vehicles.
  2. ②You cannot change lanes as the solid white zigzag line restricts lane changes.
  3. ③You should slow down as the solid white zigzag line means reduce speed.
  4. ④If you want to turn right while traveling in the first lane, turn on the turn signal and move to the third lane at once if there are no trailing vehicles.
  5. ⑤Come to a sudden stop before the stop line and wait, as the traffic signal ahead may change to red even if it is currently green.
Show answer

Answer
② You cannot change lanes as the solid white zigzag line restricts lane changes.
③ You should slow down as the solid white zigzag line means reduce speed.

Reference
At intersections with three-color traffic lights, U-turns are not permitted unless there is a U-turn sign, regardless of whether it interferes with vehicles or horses. The solid white line in the form of a zigzag has both the meaning of a direction change limit and a slowdown. If you want to turn right, it is safe to change direction in advance by one lane at a time. If this is not possible, it is safer to proceed using a P-turn. The traffic light ahead may change to red, so it is safe to anticipate this and reduce your speed and drive slowly, but stopping suddenly even when the light is green is dangerous as there is a risk of collision with the vehicle behind you.
