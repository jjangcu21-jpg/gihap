345. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is NOT true about idling and parking restrictions for a personal mobility device?

  1. ①An area located within 10 m from the edge of an intersection or an area located within 5 m from the corner of a road
  2. ②An area located within 10 m from a crosswalk or an area located within 10 m from the edge of a railway crossing
  3. ③An area located within 10 m from any side of a safety zone or an area located within 10 m from bus stop columns
  4. ④An area located within 5 m from a place where emergency firefighting equipment is installed or an area located within 5 m from a fire-extinguishing water supply system
Show answer

Answer
① An area located within 10 m from the edge of an intersection or an area located within 5 m from the corner of a road

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 32, item 2: Stopping and parking are prohibited within 5 meters of the edge of an intersection.
