169. A vehicle is about to go across a sidewalk to enter a building. Which of the following is the correct sequence?

  1. ①Slow down → turn on the turn signal → enter quickly
  2. ②Stop → honk → enter quickly
  3. ③Slow down → check both sides → enter slowly
  4. ④Stop → check both sides → enter slowly
Show answer

Answer
④ Stop → check both sides → enter slowly

Reference
Article 13 of the Road Traffic Act (Passage of vehicles and horses) Paragraph 2 Drivers of vehicles and horses must pause immediately before crossing the sidewalk, look to the left and right, and then cross so as not to impede the passage of pedestrians.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
