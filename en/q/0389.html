389. According to the Motor Vehicle Management Act, which of the following are true about the criteria for a bus and the precautions when turning left behind a bus? (Select TWO)

  1. ①A bus refers to a vehicle with seating capacity of 36 or more persons. When driving behind a bus, you must drive slowly, keeping a safe distance from the bus so that you can see the traffic lights.
  2. ②A mini-bus refers to a vehicle with seating capacity of 16 ~ 35 persons. When you are driving behind a mini-bus and the mini-bus driver turns on a turn signal, slow down and catch up to the mini-bus so that other vehicles will not cut in front of you.
  3. ③A van refers to a vehicle with seating capacity of 15 or fewer persons. Since vans are more prone to overturning due to their high center of gravity compared to passenger vehicles, you must keep a safe distance from them.
  4. ④A light van refers to a vehicle with engine displacement below 1,200 cc. Although light vans have the same center of gravity as passenger vehicles, you must keep a safe distance from them.
Show answer

Answer
① A bus refers to a vehicle with seating capacity of 36 or more persons. When driving behind a bus, you must drive slowly, keeping a safe distance from the bus so that you can see the traffic lights.
③ A van refers to a vehicle with seating capacity of 15 or fewer persons. Since vans are more prone to overturning due to their high center of gravity compared to passenger vehicles, you must keep a safe distance from them.

Reference
The signal must be given when you reach a point of at least 30 meters (at least 100 meters on an expressway) before reaching the point where you want to perform the action (the edge of the intersection if turning left), and you must reduce your speed in advance and enter the first lane or left turn lane slowly.
