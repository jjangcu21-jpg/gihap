365. What is the term used to refer to the road surface markings of white broken lines designed to guide left turns at intersections?

  1. ①Turn guidelines
  2. ②Extension lines
  3. ③Instruction lines
  4. ④Regulation lines
Show answer

Answer
① Turn guidelines

Reference
It is highly likely that a side collision accident occurs while proceeding at an intersection due to a lack of understanding of the guidance lines. (Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules Appendix 6 Ⅰ․ General standards 2. Road surface markings, Ⅱ․ Individual standards 5. Road surface markings)
