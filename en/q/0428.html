428. Which of the following are the TWO most appropriate explanations regarding driving on a gravel road?

  1. ①Hold the steering wheel as loosely as possible to minimize impact on the arm.
  2. ②Increase speed while driving to ensure that the wheels are in contact with the road surface as much as possible.
  3. ③Slow down to ensure that gravel is not projected onto pedestrians or other vehicles or horses.
  4. ④Slightly lower tire pressure is better for driving than the appropriate level of air pressure.
Show answer

Answer
③ Slow down to ensure that gravel is not projected onto pedestrians or other vehicles or horses.
④ Slightly lower tire pressure is better for driving than the appropriate level of air pressure.

Reference
Gravel roads have uneven surfaces, and gravel can cause tire damage or increase steering movement. Reduce the speed while manipulating the steering wheel as little as possible, maintain a constant speed by using a low gear, and the slightly lower the appropriate air pressure of the tires, the better the traction and the minimized shock, which is advantageous for driving.
