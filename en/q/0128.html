128. Which TWO statements are correct regarding drunk driving?

  1. ①If a person disagrees with the result of a breathalyzer test, he/she may undergo the test again.
  2. ②The Road Traffic Act does not contain any provision for punishing acts that interfere with alcohol testing.
  3. ③A person who interferes with an alcohol test after riding a bicycle while drunk may be subject to punishment.
  4. ④A driver of a motor vehicle who refuses to comply with a police officer’s request to take an alcohol test, despite being reasonably suspected of being intoxicated, will have his/her driver’s license revoked.
Show answer

Answer
③ A person who interferes with an alcohol test after riding a bicycle while drunk may be subject to punishment.
④ A driver of a motor vehicle who refuses to comply with a police officer’s request to take an alcohol test, despite being reasonably suspected of being intoxicated, will have his/her driver’s license revoked.

Reference
① According to Article 44 (3) of the Road Traffic Act, measurement may be requested through methods such as blood collection. ② Article 44, Paragraph 5 of the Road Traffic Act, introduced to increase the accuracy and fairness of driving crackdowns and reduce social damage caused by drunk driving [2024. 1. 3. Partial revision, effective June 4, 2025] ③ Article 156, 12-2 of the Road Traffic Act ④ Article 93 (Cancellation/Suspension of Driver’s License) of the Road Traffic Act, Paragraph 1, No. 3
