795. Which of the following are the most dangerous ways to drive? (Select TWO)

  1. ①You may collide with a car making a quick left turn in the lane where you are driving.
  2. ②You may collide with a car turning right from the road on your right.
  3. ③You may collide with a car turning right from the opposite road.
  4. ④You may collide with a car making a U-turn on the opposite road.
  5. ⑤You may collide with a car trying to turn right from the third lane behind you.
Show answer

Answer
② You may collide with a car turning right from the road on your right.
④ You may collide with a car making a U-turn on the opposite road.

Reference
When the signal turns yellow or red, drivers increase their speed to quickly exit the intersection. At this time, there is a possibility of an accident with a car making an unreasonable right turn on the right side of the road. You may also collide with an oncoming vehicle that enters early in anticipation of a left-turn signal, starts abruptly when that signal appears, or makes a U-turn. A vehicle going straight against the signal may not be able to see the car making a U-turn because it is obscured by a left-turning vehicle on the opposite road, and conversely, a vehicle making a U-turn may not be able to see the car going straight violating the signal because it is obscured by a left-turning car, resulting in accidents. Therefore, obeying signals at intersections is essential for safe driving.
