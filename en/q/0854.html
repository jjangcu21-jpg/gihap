854. Which of the following are the safe ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Drive in the left lane to avoid a collision with the bicycle.
  2. ②Slow down sufficiently before reaching the bicycle.
  3. ③Accelerate to maintain communication with the trailing vehicle.
  4. ④Slow down in preparation for pedestrians entering the roadway and watch out for pedestrians.
  5. ⑤Drive through the edge of the road to protect pedestrians.
Show answer

Answer
② Slow down sufficiently before reaching the bicycle.
④ Slow down in preparation for pedestrians entering the roadway and watch out for pedestrians.

Reference
Risk prediction. The situation seen in the picture in question is a situation where pedestrians and bicycles are passing in the road edge area. In this situation, the speed of the bicycle is generally faster than the speed of the pedestrian, and the cyclist's driving behavior is to overtake the pedestrian. Traffic accidents occur frequently, such as collisions that occur while bicycles are overtaking on the left or right, sudden entry into the roadway, or falls. Therefore, if there are pedestrians or bicycles at the edge of the road, it is advisable for drivers to reduce their speed in advance and prepare to stop by predicting that pedestrians and bicycles will enter the roadway. Also, there are frequent cases of crossing the center line and driving on the left to avoid pedestrians and bicycles, which is not a desirable behavior.
