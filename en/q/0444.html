444. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following describes a situation in which a driver of a non-commercial vehicle is required to turn on the headlights, sidelights, taillights, and license plate lights at the same time?

  1. ①When stopping on a road at nighttime
  2. ②When stopping on a road with minimal visibility due to fog
  3. ③When the vehicle is being towed away due to a parking violation
  4. ④While driving inside a tunnel
Show answer

Answer
④ While driving inside a tunnel

Reference
Article 37 of the Road Traffic Act (Lighting of cars and trams) ① In any of the following cases, the driver of any vehicle shall turn on the headlights, side lights, tail lights, and other lights as prescribed by Presidential Decree. 1. When driving a car on the road at night, or stopping or parking a car on the road due to a breakdown or other unavoidable reasons. 2. When driving a car on a road when it is foggy, raining, or snowing, or stopping or parking a car on a road due to a breakdown or other unavoidable reasons. 3. When driving in a tunnel, or stopping or parking a car on a road inside a tunnel due to a breakdown or other unavoidable reasons. Article 19 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act (Driving a car on the road at night) Lights when driving, etc.) ① The types of lights that the driver of a car must turn on when driving a car on the road pursuant to each subparagraph of Article 37 (1) of the Act shall be classified according to the following categories. 1. Car: headlights, side lights, tail lights, number lights and interior lights 3. Cars towed: tail lights, side lights and number lights ② The types of lights that the driver of a car must turn on when stopping or parking on the road pursuant to each subparagraph of Article 37 (1) of the Act shall be classified according to the following categories. 1. Cars (excluding two-wheeled vehicles): tail lights and side lights
