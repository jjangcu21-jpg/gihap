93. According to the Road Traffic Act, if an infant is on board a vehicle (except for two-wheeled vehicles), using an infant safety harness is required. Which of the following is the age criteria for an infant?

  1. ①8 years or below
  2. ②Below 8 years
  3. ③Below 6 years
  4. ④6 years or below
Show answer

Answer
③ Below 6 years

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 11 (Protection of Children, etc.) Guardians of infants and young children (referring to persons under 6 years of age) shall not allow children to play on roads with frequent traffic.
