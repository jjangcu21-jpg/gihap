507. What will happen if the driver of a passenger car causes a traffic accident that damages only parked or stopped vehicles and, even upon becoming aware of the situation, fails to provide personal information to the victims?

  1. ①No punishment
  2. ②An administrative fine of KRW 100,000 is imposed.
  3. ③A traffic penalty of KRW 120,000 is notified.
  4. ④A criminal fine of up to KRW 300,000 or detention is imposed.
Show answer

Answer
③ A traffic penalty of KRW 120,000 is notified.

Reference
A person who causes a traffic accident that damages only a parked or stopped car and fails to provide personal information to the victim is subject to a fine of up to 200,000 won, detention, or a minor fine under Article 156 (Penalty Provisions) of the Road Traffic Act. In reality, according to Table 8 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act, passenger cars, etc. are subject to a fine of 120,000 won.
