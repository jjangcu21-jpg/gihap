881. What is the safe approach to driving on a road with this safety sign?

  1. ①Pass through the crossing slowly when the railroad crossing signal is green.
  2. ②Enter the crossing and drive through quickly when the railroad crossing barrier is about to come down.
  3. ③If a warning bell sounds before entering the railroad crossing, increase speed to pass through.
  4. ④Pass through the crossing while frequently changing gears when the railroad crossing barrier is going up.
Show answer

Answer
① Pass through the crossing slowly when the railroad crossing signal is green.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 6] Railroad Crossing Sign (Caution Sign No. 110) Notifies that there is a railroad crossing.
