511. Which of the following is the punishment imposed on a driver who has been criminally charged for assaulting a traffic police officer?

  1. ①40 demerit points
  2. ②100 demerit points
  3. ③Revocation of the driver’s license
  4. ④Request for a summary trial
Show answer

Answer
③ Revocation of the driver’s license

Reference
When a driver is booked for assaulting a police officer or a city/county/district public official in charge of enforcement, the driver's license will be revoked. (Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules Appended Table 28 2. Individual Standards for Cancellation 16)
