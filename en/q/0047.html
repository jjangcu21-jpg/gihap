47. Which of the following is NOT an appropriate method of charging an EV?

  1. ①When charging, use a charger and an adapter that are compatible with the specifications.
  2. ②During charging, do not disconnect the charging connector arbitrarily. End charging with the charging completion button.
  3. ③Be careful not to use the charger with wet hands; prevent water from entering the charging device.
  4. ④When using a portable charger to charge, you can use a household power strip or an extension cord.
Show answer

Answer
④ When using a portable charger to charge, you can use a household power strip or an extension cord.

Reference
When using a power strip or extension cord that does not meet the standards to charge an electric vehicle, there is a risk of fire due to high power.
