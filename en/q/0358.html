358. At an intersection with no traffic control, what is the safest method of turning left?

  1. ①Drive slowly and turn left with care even if there are other vehicles that have entered the intersection ahead of you.
  2. ②Yield to cars entering from wider roads.
  3. ③Yield to cars passing through straight across, but take the right of way over cars trying to turn right.
  4. ④Go slowly along the centerline of the road beforehand and turn left using the outside center of the intersection.
Show answer

Answer
② Yield to cars entering from wider roads.

Reference
Article 26 of the Road Traffic Act: You must yield the lane to the vehicle that enters first, vehicles turning left must yield the right of way to vehicles going straight and turning right, and you must turn left inside the center of the intersection.
