569. Which of the following are the TWO correct definitions based on those stipulated by the Road Traffic Act?

  1. ①A "lane" refers to the part of a road where all vehicles can travel and which is demarcated by a line of curbstones, a safety sign, or a similar artificial structure.
  2. ②A "vehicle line" refers to a boundary marked by a safety sign in order to indicate separate roadways.
  3. ③A "roadway" refers to the part of a road marked by a line to have vehicles and horses travel in a single file.
  4. ④A "sidewalk" refers to the part of a road demarcated by a line of curbstones for thoroughfare by pedestrians.
Show answer

Answer
② A "vehicle line" refers to a boundary marked by a safety sign in order to indicate separate roadways.
④ A "sidewalk" refers to the part of a road demarcated by a line of curbstones for thoroughfare by pedestrians.

Reference
Article 2 of the Road Traffic Act
