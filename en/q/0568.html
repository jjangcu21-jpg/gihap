568. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following are the TWO correct statements regarding emergency vehicles?

  1. ①An emergency vehicle used for the control of speeding violations must have a structure defined in the "Standards on the Safe Operation of Vehicles."
  2. ②Emergency vehicles used for security services for domestic and foreign VIPs are not required to use a siren or a light bar.
  3. ③A normal vehicle is not considered an emergency vehicle even if its headlights and emergency lights are turned on to indicate that it is being used for emergency purposes.
  4. ④As a rule, ambulances have traffic priority and special status according to the law only when they are using their siren or they have turned on their emergency vehicle lighting.
Show answer

Answer
② Emergency vehicles used for security services for domestic and foreign VIPs are not required to use a siren or a light bar.
④ As a rule, ambulances have traffic priority and special status according to the law only when they are using their siren or they have turned on their emergency vehicle lighting.

Reference
Article 3 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act (Matters to be complied with for emergency vehicles)
