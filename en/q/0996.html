996. Which of the following is NOT considered a hazardous factor/situation as shown in the video? (Refer to the website)

  4. ④Vehicles violating the overtaking rules inside the tunnel
Show answer

Answer
④ Vehicles violating the overtaking rules inside the tunnel

Reference
Light adaptation refers to the process in which the visual system suddenly loses sensitivity when suddenly moving from a dark place to a bright place. Dark adaptation can be said to be the opposite process of light adaptation. Due to this characteristic, the traffic situation ahead cannot be easily confirmed when entering or exiting an underpass or tunnel. Therefore, when entering or exiting an underpass or tunnel, the driver must reduce the speed of the car in advance and have a driving attitude that prepares for the traffic situation ahead. In particular, in sub-zero temperatures, (fine) dust, sand, and water on the road become entangled to form thin ice, which is difficult for drivers to visually identify while driving. This thin ice (aka black ice) is likely to form on bridge-type roadways (bridges) or tunnel entrances and exits that do not have geothermal heat. Therefore, drivers need to be careful about this. In the video, no car committed a violation of overtaking in the tunnel or bridge section.
