470. Which of the following statements regarding wearing a seat belt is correct?

  1. ①Fix the seat belt above the waist to protect against the shock from a traffic accident.
  2. ②Firefighters who are dispatched to suppress fire need not wear a seat belt.
  3. ③It is safest for children to take the front seat and wear a seat belt.
  4. ④If a driver does not ensure that children under the age of 13 wear a seat belt, an administrative fine of KRW 30,000 is imposed.
Show answer

Answer
② Firefighters who are dispatched to suppress fire need not wear a seat belt.

Reference
Appendix 6 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act, a fine of 60,000 won if the passenger is under 13 years of age, Article 31 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act (Reasons for not wearing seat belts) No. 4, when an emergency vehicle is being driven for its original purpose.
