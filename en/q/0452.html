452. Which of the following is the safest action in the event of a fire in a tunnel?

  1. ①Lock the car's doors to prevent theft and evacuate the tunnel.
  2. ②Stay in the car as the tunnel will be filled with smoke from the fire.
  3. ③Switch off the engine, leave the key in the ignition to allow for the car to be moved if necessary, and quickly evacuate to safety.
  4. ④Make a U-turn and drive away from the exit.
Show answer

Answer
③ Switch off the engine, leave the key in the ignition to allow for the car to be moved if necessary, and quickly evacuate to safety.

Reference
In the case of a fire in a tunnel, evacuation is the top priority, so underestimating the risk and staying in the vehicle is a dangerous action. You must turn off the engine, leave the key in the ignition, quickly get out, and evacuate.
