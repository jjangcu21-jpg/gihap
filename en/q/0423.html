423. Which of the following is NOT a proper way of driving in fog?

  1. ①Honk when driving around a curve or on a hill.
  2. ②If the visibility in fog is less than 70 m, reduce speed to less than half of the speed limit.
  3. ③Try to maximize visibility by turning on fog lamps and high-beam headlights.
  4. ④Even in case of vehicle failure or minor accident, move the vehicle to the side of the road.
Show answer

Answer
③ Try to maximize visibility by turning on fog lamps and high-beam headlights.

Reference
When you turn on your high beam lights, fine water particles in the fog can refract and disperse the light, obstructing the other driver's view, so it is better to keep the fog lights and low beams on.
