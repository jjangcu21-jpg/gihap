493. Which of the following are proper ways to take photographs at the scene of an accident? (Select TWO)

  1. ①If there is a black box that successfully captured the accident, there is no need to take any photos.
  2. ②Photos of spilled engine oil and coolant are not needed as their stains will last a long time on the road.
  3. ③Take photos of the overall accident scene from a distance and work your way toward the point of collision, taking photos along the way.
  4. ④Mark the scene by spray painting or taking photos of the direction of the tires.
Show answer

Answer
③ Take photos of the overall accident scene from a distance and work your way toward the point of collision, taking photos along the way.
④ Mark the scene by spray painting or taking photos of the direction of the tires.

Reference
Close-up and long-distance photos of the damaged area must be taken, and even photos of the direction in which the vehicle's wheels are turned must be taken to help identify the accident later.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
