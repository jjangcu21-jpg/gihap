516. When a driver subject to summary judgment pays the total sum of the due penalty in addition to ( ) of the traffic penalty and submits a document confirming the payment before the announcement of the summary judgment, the police chief is required to cancel the summary judgment on the driver. Select one that fills in ( ) correctly.

  1. ①20/100
  2. ②30/100
  3. ③50/100
  4. ④70/100
Show answer

Answer
③ 50/100

Reference
If the defendant for whom a summary judgment was requested pays an amount of 50/100 plus the amount of the penalty notified before the summary judgment is pronounced and submits documents proving the payment, the police chief or the Jeju Special Self-Governing Province Governor must cancel the request for a summary judgment against the defendant. (Road Traffic Act Article 165 (Handling of Failure to Comply with Notice Disposition, etc.))
