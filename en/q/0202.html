202. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is the most appropriate method of using a road with no clear distinction of a sidewalk but has a centerline for a pedestrian?

  1. ①Walk along the center of the roadway.
  2. ②Walk along the right side of the roadway.
  3. ③Walk along the roadside area.
  4. ④Use all areas of the road to walk on.
Show answer

Answer
③ Walk along the roadside area.

Reference
Article 8 of the Road Traffic Act (Pedestrian Passage) ① Pedestrians must travel on the edge of the road or edge area on roads with a center line (including roads divided into lanes in the case of one-way traffic) among roads that are not separated from sidewalks and roadways. ② Pedestrians may pass through all parts of the road in any of the following areas. In this case, pedestrians must not intentionally obstruct the progress of the carriage. 1. Roads without a center line among roads that are not divided into sidewalks and roadways (in the case of one-way traffic, limited to roads that are not divided into lanes; the same applies hereinafter) 2. Pedestrian priority roads
