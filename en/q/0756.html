756. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①You should drive at a speed of 30 km/h or less.
  2. ②You may drive fast to pass through, as there are no vehicles driving ahead.
  3. ③Parking is prohibited, but stopping is allowed.
  4. ④When passing through a crosswalk, honk and go through it quickly regardless of whether there are children present.
  5. ⑤Be cautious of children who may suddenly appear, even if fences are installed to prevent jaywalking.
Show answer

Answer
① You should drive at a speed of 30 km/h or less.
⑤ Be cautious of children who may suddenly appear, even if fences are installed to prevent jaywalking.

Reference
Because it is within a child protection zone, the maximum speed must be within 30 km/h, and you must pay attention to the movements of children and look ahead carefully. Accidents in child protection zones are caused by failure to follow laws, such as failure to drive safely, violation of pedestrian protection obligations, illegal parking and stopping, and traffic signal violations. When pedestrians cross, the driver must stop, wait until they finish crossing, check that it is safe, and then proceed.
