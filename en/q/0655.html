655. Which of the following statements regarding the characteristics of a trailer is correct?

  1. ①The heavy weight of a trailer makes its braking distance shorter than that of passenger cars.
  2. ②A trailer has a high risk of rolling over during sudden lane changes.
  3. ③The high seating position of a trailer makes vehicles ahead appear to be closer than they actually are.
  4. ④The large size of a trailer has no impact on the difference of radius between the inner wheels.
Show answer

Answer
② A trailer has a high risk of rolling over during sudden lane changes.

Reference
① The heavier the vehicle, the longer the braking distance. ② Because the body of the vehicle is long, the risk of tipping over or overturning is high, and a jackknife phenomenon may occur when changing lanes suddenly. (Jackknife phenomenon - a phenomenon in which the container attached to the back does not go in the direction of the lane change even if the front of the trailer changes lanes suddenly, but jumps out in the same direction it was traveling in.) ③ The trailer has a high driver's seat, so the vehicle in front appears to be farther than the actual distance between vehicles, so there is a tendency not to secure a safe distance (drivers mistakenly believe that a safe distance has been secured). ④ Because the car body is long and large, there is a large amount of inner wheel displacement.
