732. Which of the following road safety facilities shown in the given situation and their interpretations are incorrect? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Vehicle width limit sign - Restricts the passage of vehicles exceeding the width indicated on the sign (including the width of the loaded cargo)
  2. ②No motorcycle or two-wheeled vehicle passage sign - Prohibits the passage of motorcycles and two-wheeled vehicles
  3. ③Automobile road sign - Indicates that the road is an automobile road or a vehicle-only area
  4. ④Traffic light (flashing red light) - You can drive slowly while paying attention to other traffic or safety signs.
  5. ⑤Centerline - Indicates that vehicles should keep to the right side where the sign is posted.
Show answer

Answer
① Vehicle width limit sign - Restricts the passage of vehicles exceeding the width indicated on the sign (including the width of the loaded cargo)
④ Traffic light (flashing red light) - You can drive slowly while paying attention to other traffic or safety signs.

Reference
Vehicle height restriction sign - Restricts the passage of vehicles exceeding the height indicated on the sign (including the height of loaded cargo) (Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 6] Serial No. 221) The meaning of a flashing red light among vehicle traffic lights is that cars and horses can proceed while paying attention to other traffic after temporarily stopping just before a stop line or crosswalk or just before an intersection when there is a stop line or crosswalk. (Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules) [Appendix 2]).
