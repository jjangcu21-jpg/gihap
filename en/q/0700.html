700. In the given situation, which of the following are the two safest ways of proceeding?

  1. ①Slow down gradually and stop immediately before the stop line as the signal is yellow.
  2. ②Check the rear of lanes 1 and 2, and if there are no vehicles, switch to lane 1 to make a U-turn.
  3. ③Let the white vehicle and the bus in lanes 4 and 5 pass you before changing lanes to turn right.
  4. ④Do not change lanes as you are driving on a section of road marked by a solid white line.
  5. ⑤Accelerate to follow the vehicle ahead as the signal ahead is yellow.
Show answer

Answer
① Slow down gradually and stop immediately before the stop line as the signal is yellow.
④ Do not change lanes as you are driving on a section of road marked by a solid white line.

Reference
All drivers must follow the instructions given by traffic safety signs and signals (Article 5 of the Road Traffic Act). When the light is yellow, cars and horses must stop immediately before the stop line or crosswalk or just before the intersection, and if even part of the car or horse has already entered the intersection, they must proceed quickly out of the intersection (Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 2]). Additionally, changing lanes is prohibited in the solid white line section, so attempting to change lanes and make a U-turn or right turn is against the law and is a dangerous driving behavior. Option ⑤ ignores the yellow signal and increases speed to pass through the intersection. This is not only against the law, but also can result in a major traffic accident as collisions within the intersection are high.
