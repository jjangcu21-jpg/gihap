11. Which of the following is a correct definition (standard) of a “motorcycle” under the Road Traffic Act?

  1. ①Displacement of up to 50 cc; maximum rated output power of up to 0.59 kW
  2. ②Displacement of less than 50 cc; maximum rated output power of less than 0.59 kW
  3. ③Displacement of up to 125 cc; maximum rated output power of up to 11 kW
  4. ④Displacement of less than 125 cc; maximum rated output power of less than 11 kW
Show answer

Answer
③ Displacement of up to 125 cc; maximum rated output power of up to 11 kW

Reference
Road Traffic Act, Article 2, item 19(b): A motorized bicycle is either (a) a two-wheeled motor vehicle under Article 3 of the Motor Vehicle Management Act with an engine displacement of no more than 125 cc, or a maximum rated output of no more than 11 kW if electric; or (b) another vehicle fitted with a motor within those same limits. Category (b) excludes electric bicycles under Article 2, item 1-2 of the Act on Promotion of Bicycle Use and outdoor mobile robots under item 21-3.
