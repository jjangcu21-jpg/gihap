268. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following actions by an emergency vehicle on an emergency rescue mission is considered an offense?

  1. ①Driving at 100 km/h on a general road with two lanes in one direction
  2. ②Overtaking a vehicle inside a tunnel where lanes are marked with a solid white line
  3. ③Cutting in front of a vehicle at an intersection to turn right
  4. ④Failing to report or take the necessary actions in the event of a traffic accident resulting in personal injury because the vehicle is on an emergency rescue mission
Show answer

Answer
④ Failing to report or take the necessary actions in the event of a traffic accident resulting in personal injury because the vehicle is on an emergency rescue mission

Reference
Speed limits, prohibitions on overtaking, and prohibitions on cutting in are not applied to emergency vehicles. (Road Traffic Act Article 30 (Special Provisions for Emergency Vehicles), Road Traffic Act Article 54 (Measures in Case of Accident) Paragraph 5)
