437. Which of the following is NOT safe driving in rain?

  1. ①You must reduce speed to less than half of the speed limit depending on the driving condition.
  2. ②You must drive with reasonable consideration so as not to splash water on pedestrians.
  3. ③It is not good to drive in idle creep (a state where the tires move either forward or in reverse even when the accelerator and brake pedals are not applied) when it starts to rain.
  4. ④It is safer to drive with the headlights and taillights on even during the day.
Show answer

Answer
③ It is not good to drive in idle creep (a state where the tires move either forward or in reverse even when the accelerator and brake pedals are not applied) when it starts to rain.

Reference
① Depending on the situation, drive at a 20% slower speed than the speed limit on rainy roads, or at least 50% slower when visibility is 100 meters or less due to heavy rain, etc. ② To avoid splashing water on pedestrians, drive at a distance of at least 1 meter. ③ In the early stages of rain, it is best to drive in a creep state where the wheels roll without pressing the accelerator or brake pedals to prevent skidding caused by dust or impurities on the road surface becoming entangled in rainwater. ④ Even when driving during the day, it is a good idea to turn on the taillights and headlights.
