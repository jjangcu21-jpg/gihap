773. Which of the following are the most incorrect ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Bicycle riders may ride their bicycle when crossing a crosswalk.
  2. ②A bicycle rider who is a child may ride on the sidewalk.
  3. ③A bicycle rider must wear a helmet.
  4. ④Bicycle riders must turn on their front and rear lights or wear other light-emitting devices when riding on the road at night.
  5. ⑤School buses can park or stop on the left side of the road for the convenience of children getting on and off the bus.
Show answer

Answer
① Bicycle riders may ride their bicycle when crossing a crosswalk.
⑤ School buses can park or stop on the left side of the road for the convenience of children getting on and off the bus.

Reference
Drivers of bicycles, etc. must use separate bicycle lanes (including dedicated lanes installed for bicycles only in accordance with Article 15, Paragraph 1; hereinafter the same shall apply in this Article) on separate bicycle paths (Road Traffic Act, Article 13-2, Paragraph 1). Drivers of bicycles, etc. must ride along the right edge of the road in areas where bicycle lanes are not installed (Article 13-2, Paragraph 2 of the Road Traffic Act). Notwithstanding Paragraph 1 and Article 13 Paragraph 1, drivers of bicycles, etc. may pass on sidewalks if they are children, the elderly, or the physically disabled. In this case, drivers of bicycles, etc. must drive slowly on the roadway side of the sidewalk’s center or a place designated by a safety sign, and must stop temporarily if they interfere with the passage of pedestrians (Road Traffic Act, Article 13-2, Paragraph 4, No. 1). When driving on bicycle paths and roads pursuant to the Road Act, drivers of bicycles, etc. must wear life-protection equipment prescribed by Ordinance of the Ministry of Public Administration and Security, and must also require their fellow passengers to wear them (Article 50, Paragraph 4 of the Road Traffic Act). When riding on the road at night, drivers of bicycles must turn on their headlights and taillights or wear luminescent devices such as luminous bands (Article 50, Paragraph 9 of the Road Traffic Act). When stopping on the road, drivers of all vehicles must stop at the right edge of the roadway (Article 11, Paragraph 1, Item 1 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act).
