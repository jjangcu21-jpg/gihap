478. Which of the following are the TWO correct statements regarding the number of passengers in a vehicle?

  1. ①On expressways, it is not allowed to carry more people than the defined number of passengers for the vehicle.
  2. ②The number of passengers specified on the vehicle registration certificate is the number of passengers excluding the driver.
  3. ③When permission is obtained from the head of the police station having jurisdiction over the place of departure, the vehicle may operate in excess of the designated number of passengers.
  4. ④If the number of passengers is exceeded, permission must be obtained from the head of the police station having jurisdiction over the destination.
Show answer

Answer
① On expressways, it is not allowed to carry more people than the defined number of passengers for the vehicle.
③ When permission is obtained from the head of the police station having jurisdiction over the place of departure, the vehicle may operate in excess of the designated number of passengers.

Reference
Drivers of all vehicles must not allow passengers to board, load or drive vehicles in excess of operational safety standards regarding the number of passengers, load weight, and load capacity. However, this does not apply when permission has been obtained from the police chief having jurisdiction over the place of departure. (Article 39 of the Road Traffic Act)
