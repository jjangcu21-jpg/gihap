352. Which of the following are the TWO safest methods of parking on a steeply sloped road?

  1. ①Engage the parking brake and put the vehicle into reverse gear, regardless of the direction of the slope.
  2. ②Engage the parking brake and turn the steering wheel toward the roadside (meaning toward the side closest to the vehicle).
  3. ③Engage the parking brake and place a wheel stopper or other device that can prevent the vehicle from slipping in the downhill direction.
  4. ④For manual transmission vehicles, engage the parking brake and leave the gear in neutral.
Show answer

Answer
② Engage the parking brake and turn the steering wheel toward the roadside (meaning toward the side closest to the vehicle).
③ Engage the parking brake and place a wheel stopper or other device that can prevent the vehicle from slipping in the downhill direction.

Reference
Under Road Traffic Act Enforcement Decree Article 11(3), when parking on a slope, apply the brake, place a chock or similar object on the downhill side of the wheel to prevent rolling, and turn the steering toward the nearest road edge. Choose a gear that prevents rolling down the slope rather than always selecting reverse. Avoid leaving the transmission in neutral.
