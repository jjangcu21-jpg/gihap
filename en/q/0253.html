253. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following measures must be taken when an emergency vehicle driving with an emergency siren on approaches?

  1. ①When an emergency vehicle approaches an intersection, drivers of other vehicles must stop at the left edge of the intersection.
  2. ②On the road other than the intersection, other vehicles should yield so that emergency vehicles can pass first.
  3. ③Drive faster than the emergency vehicle.
  4. ④Stop temporarily and wait until the emergency vehicle passes.
Show answer

Answer
② On the road other than the intersection, other vehicles should yield so that emergency vehicles can pass first.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 29 (Priority Passage for Emergency Vehicles) ④ When an emergency vehicle approaches at or near an intersection, drivers of cars, horses and trams must stop temporarily to avoid the intersection. ⑤ When an emergency vehicle approaches from a place other than that specified in paragraph (4), the drivers of all cars and streetcars must give way to allow the emergency vehicle to pass first.
