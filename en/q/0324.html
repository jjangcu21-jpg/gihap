324. Which of the following is NOT a safety precaution to be taken by a driver driving through a silver zone at night?

  1. ①Watch out for glare from another car’s headlights as you may not be able to see the pedestrians.
  2. ②Drive at high speed because there are no elderly people around at night.
  3. ③Drive through while looking out for any elderly crossing the road illegally.
  4. ④Drive carefully because elderly people wearing black can be hard to spot.
Show answer

Answer
② Drive at high speed because there are no elderly people around at night.

Reference
As there may be elderly people passing by even at night, pass slowly.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
