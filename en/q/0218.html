218. Which of the following falls under the violation of “tailgating,” an act of obstructing passage by other cars by not allowing them to pass even when the traffic light has changed after entering an intersection with a green light?

  1. ①A violation of means for passing through intersections
  2. ②A violation of a temporary stop
  3. ③A violation of means for changing lanes
  4. ④A violation of measures for preventing congestion
Show answer

Answer
① A violation of means for passing through intersections

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 25(5): Do not enter a signal-controlled intersection if traffic ahead would force you to stop inside it (beyond the stop line, if present) and obstruct other vehicles or trams. This violation is known as blocking the intersection.
