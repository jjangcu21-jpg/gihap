834. Which of the following are the safe ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Switch to the right lane, ignoring the combination of broken and solid white lines.
  2. ②Turn on the right-turn signal to let other drivers know.
  3. ③Accelerate rapidly when changing lanes to the right.
  4. ④Change lanes right before the exit while slowing down.
  5. ⑤Changing lanes is not allowed if doing so may obstruct other vehicles.
Show answer

Answer
② Turn on the right-turn signal to let other drivers know.
⑤ Changing lanes is not allowed if doing so may obstruct other vehicles.

Reference
When changing lanes where the road markings permit it, signal to surrounding drivers with your hand, turn indicators, or lights until the maneuver is complete. You must not change your route if there is a risk of interfering with the normal passage of other vehicles. Articles 19 and 38 of the Road Traffic Act - The driver of any vehicle must not change the course of the vehicle if there is a risk of impeding the normal passage of other vehicles coming in the direction to be changed. - When the driver of any vehicle attempts to turn left, turn right, cross, U-turn, slow down, stop, or reverse, or change course while proceeding in the same direction, he or she must signal with his hand, a direction indicator, or a light until the action is completed.
