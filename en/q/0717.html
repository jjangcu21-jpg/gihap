717. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①The yield sign on the right is the one that entering vehicles must follow.
  2. ②The driver must overtake to the left side of the car ahead when intending to do so within a roundabout.
  3. ③All vehicles may use the exit to 1 o'clock without restriction.
  4. ④Pass immediately as vehicles traveling through the roundabout have the right of way over vehicles entering the roundabout.
  5. ⑤The driver must turn on the turn signal when exiting a roundabout.
Show answer

Answer
① The yield sign on the right is the one that entering vehicles must follow.
⑤ The driver must turn on the turn signal when exiting a roundabout.

Reference
Overtaking at intersections is prohibited (Article 22, Paragraph 3, No. 1 of the Road Traffic Act). When entering a roundabout, the driver of a vehicle must yield the right of way to another vehicle that is already proceeding (Article 25-2, Paragraph 2 of the Road Traffic Act). A car that is already turning has no obligation to give way. Parking and stopping are prohibited at intersections (Article 32, Paragraph 1 of the Road Traffic Act). When turning right or changing course to the right, you are required to turn on the right turn signal (Appendix 2 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act), and when you are going to exit a roundabout, you are required to turn on the turn signal (Article 38, Paragraph 1 of the Road Traffic Act). When crossing the bridge, you must follow the travel restriction signs.
