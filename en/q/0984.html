984. Which of the following pairs of vehicles complies with the Road Traffic Act? (Refer to the website)

Show answer

Answer
③ The black motorcycle and the black passenger car

Reference
Article 5 of the Road Traffic Act. Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 6] Types of safety signs, methods of making them, and installation and management standards. If you are approaching an intersection and check the green light and the desire to enter the intersection leads to acceleration and high speed, you cannot stop just before the intersection even if you check the yellow light. This directly leads to a serious violation of Article 5 of the Road Traffic Act (Duty to follow signals and instructions). Therefore, drivers should expect that the longer the green light stays on, the sooner it will change to yellow, so they must slow down when approaching an intersection and prepare to stop when the yellow light turns on.
