419. Which of the following is the most appropriate action to take when the brakes are not working on a downhill road?

  1. ①Turn the ignition off at once.
  2. ②Jump out of the car after shifting to low gear.
  3. ③Reduce speed by zigzagging.
  4. ④Run the car into a guard rail or a wall after reducing speed by shifting to low gear.
Show answer

Answer
④ Run the car into a guard rail or a wall after reducing speed by shifting to low gear.

Reference
If the brakes rupture and do not stop, there is a high possibility that a major accident will occur due to a collision or collision with an oncoming vehicle. When the brakes rupture, do not panic, shift to a lower gear, slow down, and stop by hitting the guardrail or wall. This is the way to prevent a secondary accident.
