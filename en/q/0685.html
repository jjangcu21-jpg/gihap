685. Which of the following pairings of information from the given situation and appropriate driving methods are incorrect? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Crosswalk – Pay attention to both sides, be cautious of pedestrians.
  2. ②Pedestrians on the roadway – Slow down and drive with caution for safety.
  3. ③Horizontal dual-color traffic lights – Continue driving on the lane marked with a red X.
  4. ④Reversible lane – Lane direction may change depending on the situation.
  5. ⑤Broken yellow centerline – This line cannot be crossed even when trying to overtake a vehicle.
Show answer

Answer
③ Horizontal dual-color traffic lights – Continue driving on the lane marked with a red X.
⑤ Broken yellow centerline – This line cannot be crossed even when trying to overtake a vehicle.

Reference
Under Article 14(1) of the Road Traffic Act, a city or provincial police chief may install reversible lanes on roads where traffic volumes in the two directions differ significantly by time of day. Signals indicate the travel direction so that more lanes can serve the busier direction. Horizontal two-color signals are installed at the entrance, middle, and exit of the reversible-lane section (Enforcement Rules, Appendix 3). A downward green arrow permits travel in the designated lane; a red X prohibits it (Appendix 2). A solid yellow center line must not be crossed. A broken yellow center line permits a temporary move into the opposite lane with attention to oncoming traffic, followed by a return to the original direction of travel (Appendix 6, item 501).
