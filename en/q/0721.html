721. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Keep driving while ensuring the pedestrian's safety, even if the pedestrian is jaywalking.
  2. ②Keep driving as there is no need to protect a jaywalking pedestrian.
  3. ③Honk loudly to prevent the pedestrian from jaywalking.
  4. ④Turn on the hazard lights to alert the trailing vehicles of the dangerous situation.
  5. ⑤Stop directly in front of the jaywalker and teach him/her a lesson.
Show answer

Answer
① Keep driving while ensuring the pedestrian's safety, even if the pedestrian is jaywalking.
④ Turn on the hazard lights to alert the trailing vehicles of the dangerous situation.

Reference
Even when a pedestrian is jaywalking, the driver must keep a safe distance and take care to avoid an accident. Additionally, it is safe to turn on the emergency flashers to alert following vehicles of danger.
