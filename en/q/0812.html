812. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①The black car driver uses the P space to pass quickly.
  2. ②The black car driver stops behind the school bus.
  3. ③The white car driver proceeds slowly while paying attention to the school bus.
  4. ④The white car driver stops before reaching the school bus, and then proceeds slowly.
  5. ⑤The white car driver honks continuously to indicate that he/she is going straight.
Show answer

Answer
② The black car driver stops behind the school bus.
④ The white car driver stops before reaching the school bus, and then proceeds slowly.

Reference
Article 51 (Special protection of children's school buses) ① When a children's school bus is stopped on the road and devices such as flashing lights are operating to indicate that children or infants are getting on and off, the driver of a car traveling in the lane where the children's school bus is stopped and the lane immediately next to the lane must stop temporarily before reaching the children's school bus and check for safety before driving slowly. ② In the case of paragraph 1, on roads where a center line is not installed or on a road with one lane each way, the driver of a car traveling in the opposite direction must also stop temporarily before reaching the children's school bus, check for safety, and then drive slowly. ③ Drivers of any vehicle may not overtake a children's school bus traveling on the road with a sign indicating that it is carrying children or infants.
