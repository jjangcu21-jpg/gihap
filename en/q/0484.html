484. Which TWO vehicles are allowed to continue driving while asking a passenger to take actions during a traffic accident?

  1. ①A passenger car that is carrying an injured person to a hospital
  2. ②A fire truck returning to the fire station after extinguishing a fire
  3. ③A tow vehicle going to the scene of a traffic accident
  4. ④A postal vehicle transporting mail.
Show answer

Answer
① A passenger car that is carrying an injured person to a hospital
④ A postal vehicle transporting mail.

Reference
Article 54 (Measures in Case of Accident) Paragraph 5 of the Road Traffic Act Drivers of emergency vehicles, vehicles carrying injured persons, mail vehicles, and streetcars may, in emergency cases, continue driving after having their passengers take action against the accident or report it to the police.
