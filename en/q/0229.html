229. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is INCORRECT regarding the method and speed to be followed by motor vehicles and horses?

  1. ①If there is another vehicle coming straight from the opposite direction when you attempt to turn left at an intersection with no traffic lights, you shall yield the route to the vehicle coming straight.
  2. ②A vehicle making a stop on a road with no distinction between the roadway and the sidewalk should stop at a distance of at least 50 cm from the right edge of the road.
  3. ③A vehicle signaling its intent to turn right at an intersection shall not be impeded from proceeding by the trailing vehicle.
  4. ④The minimum speed on exclusive roads for motor vehicles is 40 km/h.
Show answer

Answer
④ The minimum speed on exclusive roads for motor vehicles is 40 km/h.

Reference
① Article 26, Paragraph 4 of the Road Traffic Act, ② Article 11, Paragraph 1, No. 1 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act, ③ Article 25, Paragraph 4 of the Road Traffic Act, ④ The minimum speed on automobile roads is 30 kilometers per hour. (Article 19, Paragraph 1, No. 2 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act)
