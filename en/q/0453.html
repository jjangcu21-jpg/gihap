453. From a safety perspective, which of the following is the most inappropriate way of driving when passing through a tunnel?

  1. ①Before entering the tunnel, remove your sunglasses to allow your eyes to adapt to the light, and turn on your headlights as you would at nighttime.
  2. ②If the lanes inside the tunnel are marked with solid white lines, do not change lanes while in the tunnel.
  3. ③Keep a safe following distance from the car ahead, and be ready for sudden stops.
  4. ④Before entering the tunnel, check the road information at the entrance to the tunnel.
Show answer

Answer
① Before entering the tunnel, remove your sunglasses to allow your eyes to adapt to the light, and turn on your headlights as you would at nighttime.

Reference
To prevent accidents due to dark adaptation (a phenomenon in which things that were initially invisible become visible over time when entering a dark place from a bright place) and light adaptation (a phenomenon in which a person gradually adapts to bright light when coming out of a dark place), when passing through a tunnel, you must slow down by 10 to 20% compared to usual and turn on headlights, side lights, and tail lights. In addition, to prevent freezing and secondary accidents, it is necessary to secure a safer distance than on regular roads and prepare for sudden braking.
