751. If the accelerator pedal gets stuck in the driver's seat mat and continues to accelerate, which of the following are the ways to stop safely or slow down? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Press the brake pedal as hard as you can.
  2. ②Continuously press the hazard light button.
  3. ③Keep honking firmly while driving.
  4. ④Continuously operate the electronic parking brake (EPB).
  5. ⑤Steer the steering wheel strongly to the left and right.
Show answer

Answer
① Press the brake pedal as hard as you can.
④ Continuously operate the electronic parking brake (EPB).

Reference
Even if the accelerator pedal is continuously operated because it is caught in an object (mat, foreign material, etc.), when the brake pedal is operated, the brake override (BOS, Brake Override System) function is activated and the brake signal is given priority to decelerate and stop. The electronic parking brake (EPB) includes an emergency braking function, so it can be decelerated or operated if continuously operated. - It is possible to stop or decelerate in the same way even in unintentional acceleration situations. ※ What is an unintentional acceleration situation? ∘Accelerator pedal stuck due to mechanical defect ∘Accelerator pedal floor mat jammed ∘If external object (water bottle, shoe, wet tissue, etc.) gets caught in the accelerator pedal, etc., and the accelerator pedal does not return.
