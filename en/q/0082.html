82. Which of the following statements about overtaking is correct under the Road Traffic Act?

  1. ①Inside tunnels, overtaking is allowed during daytime but banned during nighttime.
  2. ②Drivers can overtake the car ahead either on its left or on its right after turning on the headlights and honking.
  3. ③Overtaking is not allowed on bridges or at intersections.
  4. ④A driver may stop, proceed slowly, or overtake a vehicle as directed by a police officer.
Show answer

Answer
③ Overtaking is not allowed on bridges or at intersections.

Reference
Article 21 (Methods of passing, etc.) ① Drivers of all vehicles must pass on the left side of the vehicle in front when passing another vehicle. Article 22 (Time and place of prohibition of overtaking) ①, ②, ③ ④ 2. Cars that are stopping or moving slowly according to the instructions of a police officer.
