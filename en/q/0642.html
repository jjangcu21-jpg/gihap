642. Which of the following terms refers to the phenomenon wherein a towed vehicle pushes the tow truck that is in front in a linear motion during rapid deceleration or sudden braking, and the joints are folded as in an “L” shape?

  1. ①Swing-out
  2. ②Jack knife
  3. ③Hydroplaning
  4. ④Vapor lock
Show answer

Answer
② Jack knife

Reference
A ‘jack knife’ is a phenomenon that can occur when the braking force of the tractor is greater than the braking force of the trailer in road environments such as wet roads. It refers to a phenomenon in which the connection between the tractor and the trailer folds like a knife while pushing the tractor with the inertial movement of the trailer. ‘Swing-out’ refers to the movement of the trailer swinging left and right like a pendulum when only the wheels of the trailer are braked for some unforeseen reason. ‘Hydroplaning’ refers to a condition in which steering ability is lost or impossible because the tires do not contact the road surface when driving on a wet road surface at high speed. ‘Vapor lock’ refers to a phenomenon in which air bubbles are generated in the brake fluid, preventing the brakes from operating properly.
