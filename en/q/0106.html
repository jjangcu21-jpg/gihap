106. What is the safest driving method when entering an intersection with no traffic lights and where both the left and right sides are not visible?

  1. ①Drive slowly depending on the surrounding situation and pass through the intersection after checking that it is safe.
  2. ②Enter while honking and flashing headlights, and drive slowly while passing through.
  3. ③Make a temporary stop to make sure that it is safe, and then pass through the intersection according to right-of-way standards.
  4. ④Since a vehicle entering the intersection first has priority, quickly pass through the intersection while checking the surroundings.
Show answer

Answer
③ Make a temporary stop to make sure that it is safe, and then pass through the intersection according to right-of-way standards.

Reference
The rule is to drive slowly at intersections without traffic lights, but if traffic at the intersection is frequent or you cannot check left and right due to obstacles, you must stop temporarily and check for safety before passing.
