136. Which of the following is NOT an appropriate way of reporting retaliatory driving?

  1. ①Calling 120
  2. ②Calling 112
  3. ③Reporting to the smartphone app Safety e-Report
  4. ④Reporting the incident to the Cyber Police Agency
Show answer

Answer
① Calling 120

Reference
If you are subject to retaliatory driving, you can report it to 112, the National Police Agency or city/provincial police agency website, or the Safety Report Center.
