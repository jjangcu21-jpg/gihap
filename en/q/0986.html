986. You are passing through a residential area. Which of the following is the LEAST related to things or places to be cautious of while driving? (Refer to the website)

Official video ↗
  1. ①Illegally parked vehicle
  2. ②Vehicle passing on the opposite side of the road
  3. ③Crosswalk with no traffic lights
  4. ④Pedestrians talking on the sidewalk on the left side of the road
Show answer

Answer
④ Pedestrians talking on the sidewalk on the left side of the road

Reference
When driving at night, pedestrians or bicycles may suddenly enter from in front or behind an illegally parked car. You need to be careful because you may momentarily lose your vision due to the headlights of a car passing on the opposite side of the road. Pedestrians crossing at a crosswalk without traffic lights may not be visible due to the lights of oncoming cars, so when approaching a crosswalk without traffic lights, you must slow down, check the crosswalk before entering, and stop if a pedestrian is crossing or about to cross. The pedestrians on the left sidewalk seen in the video cannot be said to be at high risk.
