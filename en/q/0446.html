446. Which of the following is NOT true about preventing a tire puncture and measures taken while driving?

  1. ①You must regularly check the tires for nails.
  2. ②You must regularly check tire pressures and tread depths.
  3. ③If the steering wheel pulls to one side, it is likely that a rear tire is punctured.
  4. ④While keeping the steering wheel straight ahead and in the driving gear state, use the engine brake to induce deceleration.
Show answer

Answer
③ If the steering wheel pulls to one side, it is likely that a rear tire is punctured.

Reference
If the steering wheel is tilted to one side, there is a high possibility that the front tire is punctured, so if you apply the foot brake, there is a high possibility that you will deviate from the path.
