786. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①To facilitate smooth traffic flow, wait ahead of the stop line at the red light before turning left.
  2. ②There is no need to pay attention to the red vehicle turning right on the opposite side of the road as vehicles turning left have the right of way.
  3. ③Be careful as the blind spots of your car may cause you to lose sight of the two-wheeled vehicle momentarily.
  4. ④Turn left following the white traffic guide lines on the intersection pavement.
  5. ⑤While turning left, turn on the right-turn signal and move directly into the third lane on the left side of the road.
Show answer

Answer
③ Be careful as the blind spots of your car may cause you to lose sight of the two-wheeled vehicle momentarily.
④ Turn left following the white traffic guide lines on the intersection pavement.

Reference
Drivers should always get into the habit of checking the intersection situation with their eyes, and when turning left, they should also pay attention to vehicles turning right on the opposite side of the road. When turning left, a safe left turn method is to follow the traffic guidance lines and start from the first lane into the first lane of the road on the left, and when starting from the second lane, enter the second lane of the left road. After entering the road on the left, you must sequentially change lanes in the white dotted line. Additionally, you should always be aware that when turning, you may momentarily lose sight of two-wheeled vehicles or pedestrians due to blind spots caused by the vehicle's A-pillar. On the contrary, it is wrong to think that other vehicles can always see you even if you are a two-wheeled vehicle driver or a car driver. Therefore, you must become a driver who obeys traffic laws, such as obeying the stop line.
