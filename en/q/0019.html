19. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is NOT true about an English driver’s license? (Limited to countries that have ratified the Geneva Convention or the Vienna Convention)

  1. ①When driving in a country where an English driver’s license is recognized, it is possible to drive without a separate notarized translation.
  2. ②The English driver's license can be used regardless of the length of stay in a country where an English driver’s license is recognized.
  3. ③You should bring your Korean driver's license, an international driver's license, and your passport in a country where an English driver’s license is not recognized.
  4. ④It is a license wherein the content of the driver's license is written in English on the back of the driver's license.
Show answer

Answer
② The English driver's license can be used regardless of the length of stay in a country where an English driver’s license is recognized.

Reference
English driver's license information (Korea Road Traffic Authority) The period during which you can drive varies from country to country, and most only allow for a short period of about 3 months, so if you are staying for a long period of time, you must obtain a driver's license from the relevant country.
