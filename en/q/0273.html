273. In which location should the driver come to a full stop, regardless of the existence of pedestrians?

  1. ①A road with no centerline among the roads where the roadway and sidewalk are not distinguishable
  2. ②Front of a crosswalk in a school zone with no traffic light
  3. ③Pedestrian-first road
  4. ④A place other than a road
Show answer

Answer
② Front of a crosswalk in a school zone with no traffic light

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 27(6): When passing pedestrians on a road without separate sidewalk and roadway and without a center line, on a pedestrian-priority road, or in an off-road area, drivers must keep a safe distance and proceed slowly. If they would obstruct pedestrians, they must slow down or stop to let them pass safely. (Amended January 11, 2022.) Article 27(7): At an unsignalized crosswalk in a child protection zone, stop before the crosswalk or its stop line regardless of whether pedestrians are crossing.
