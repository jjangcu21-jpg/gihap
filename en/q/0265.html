265. Which of the following can be designated as an emergency vehicle by the commissioner of the City/Do (Province) police agency at the request of the organization or the person intending to use the vehicle?

  1. ①A fire truck
  2. ②A gasworks vehicle used for emergency gas leak repair
  3. ③An ambulance
  4. ④A blood bank vehicle
Show answer

Answer
② A gasworks vehicle used for emergency gas leak repair

Reference
Article 2 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act (Types of Emergency Vehicles) ① In subparagraph 2, subparagraph 2 of Article 2 of the Road Traffic Act, “vehicles prescribed by Presidential Decree” refers to any of the following vehicles used for emergency purposes. However, automobiles under Nos. 6 through 11 are limited to those designated by the city/provincial police chief upon application by the person or institution using them. 6. Automobiles used for emergency work to prevent danger in organizations engaged in electric, gas, and other public services. 7. Automobiles used in emergency work for emergency prevention or recovery by organizations performing civil defense services. 8. Automobiles used for road management, used for emergency operations to prevent hazards on the road or to crack down on vehicles whose operation is restricted. 9. Automobiles used for emergency operations such as telegraph and telephone repair work. 10. Urgent mail. Cars used for transportation 11. Cars used for radio surveillance services
