765. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Keep honking to proceed, even if there are no pedestrians.
  2. ②Temporarily stop to watch out for pedestrians and vehicles before entering the intersection, as there is an alley behind a parked truck on the right side.
  3. ③Proceed slowly if there are no pedestrians, as there is no traffic signal at the crosswalk.
  4. ④Slow down to reduce the risk of an accident, as there are vehicles crossing the centerline ahead.
  5. ⑤When intending to turn right, do so without stopping as there is a stop sign on the right, but no traffic light.
Show answer

Answer
② Temporarily stop to watch out for pedestrians and vehicles before entering the intersection, as there is an alley behind a parked truck on the right side.
④ Slow down to reduce the risk of an accident, as there are vehicles crossing the centerline ahead.

Reference
Intersections without signals where it is impossible to check left or right must be entered after a temporary stop, and crosswalks without signals in child protection zones must be stopped temporarily regardless of the presence or absence of pedestrians (Road Traffic Act, Article 27, Paragraph 7). Because there is a vehicle ahead that crosses the center line, you must slow down to avoid accidents and avoid driving with the horn blaring without a justifiable reason. If there is a “Stop” sign, you must stop temporarily before turning right.
