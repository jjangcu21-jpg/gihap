331. Which of the following is NOT true about a silver zone?

  1. ①When driving in a silver zone, look around with caution for any potential hazards.
  2. ②A silver zone sign is a road sign that informs drivers of their duty to protect the seniors within the designated zone.
  3. ③A silver zone sign is installed in the center of the road within said zone.
  4. ④If the driver of a passenger vehicle obstructs a pedestrian crossing a crosswalk in a silver zone at 10 AM, the traffic penalty is KRW 120,000.
Show answer

Answer
③ A silver zone sign is installed in the center of the road within said zone.

Reference
Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act [Appendix 10] 2 If a passenger vehicle driver obstructs a pedestrian crossing the crosswalk in a senior citizen protection zone, a fine of 120,000 won will be imposed. Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 6] 323 Elderly protection signs instruct the protection of the elderly within the elderly protection zone and are installed on both sides of the road in the elderly protection zone.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
