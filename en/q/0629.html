629. Which of the following is the penalty imposed on the driver of a large bus who fails to control a passenger dancing inside the vehicle?

  1. ①Traffic penalty of KRW 90,000 and 30 demerit points
  2. ②Traffic penalty of KRW 100,000 and 40 demerit points
  3. ③Traffic penalty of KRW 110,000 and 50 demerit points
  4. ④Traffic penalty of KRW 120,000 and 60 demerit points
Show answer

Answer
② Traffic penalty of KRW 100,000 and 40 demerit points

Reference
For driving while allowing passengers to cause a disturbance inside the vehicle, a fine of 100,000 won and 40 demerit points will be imposed. Article 49 of the Road Traffic Act (Matters to be observed by all drivers, etc.), Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act (Appendix 8) Criminal acts and amount of offense (drivers), Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act (Appendix Table 28) Standards for driver's license cancellation/suspension
