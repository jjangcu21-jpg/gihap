245. Which of the following is the policy that limits the maximum speed in the city center to 50 km/h while lowered to 30 km/h on back roads (including residential areas) in order to reduce traffic accidents?

  1. ①New Deal Policy
  2. ②Safety Speed 5030
  3. ③Campaign for Unity in Order to Reduce Traffic Accidents
  4. ④Intelligent Transportation System (ITS)
Show answer

Answer
② Safety Speed 5030

Reference
The ‘New Deal Policy’ is a policy that was promoted to overcome the Great Depression (Doosan Encyclopedia). ‘One Mind Contest to Reduce Traffic Accidents’ is an event to raise traffic safety awareness hosted by the Korea Road Traffic Authority and sponsored by the Ministry of Public Administration and Security and the National Police Agency. ‘Intelligent transportation system’ refers to a transportation system that combines technologies such as electronics, information, communication, and control into the transportation system (Doosan Encyclopedia). The traffic safety policy in question is ‘Safe Speed ​​5030’. On April 16, 2019, in accordance with the revision of Article 19 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act, motor vehicles and streetcars are restricted to within 50 kilometers per hour on general roads in residential, commercial, and industrial areas in accordance with the provisions of Article 36, Paragraph 1, Item 1, A through C of the National Land Planning and Utilization Act, and within 60 kilometers per hour on routes or sections designated as particularly necessary by the city/provincial police chiefs. The travel speed was set. Effective date (April 17, 2021)
