697. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①You should slow down and stop before the stop line safely as the traffic light ahead is red.
  2. ②There is no need to pay special attention to children's safety on the current road, as there is a sign indicating the end of the school zone.
  3. ③When intending to turn left, watch out for trailing vehicles in the first lane in advance and change lanes safely.
  4. ④When intending to turn right, use the sidewalk to avoid vehicles waiting for the signal in the third lane.
  5. ⑤Stopping briefly is allowed as the solid yellow line on the right side of the road indicates that stopping is allowed but parking is prohibited.
Show answer

Answer
① You should slow down and stop before the stop line safely as the traffic light ahead is red.
③ When intending to turn left, watch out for trailing vehicles in the first lane in advance and change lanes safely.

Reference
If the traffic light ahead is red, slow down in advance and stop safely before the intersection. If you want to change course to the left, you must check if there is a vehicle traveling in the left lane behind before driving. If there is a child protection zone cancellation sign, the zone extends up to that sign. Since lane 3 allows straight travel and right turns, it is better to stop at the rear and wait until there is space to turn right. The solid yellow line on the right side of the road indicates a no parking zone, so stopping is also prohibited.
