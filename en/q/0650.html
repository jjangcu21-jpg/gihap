650. Which of the following must be displayed on the back or right side of a special vehicle for towing?

  1. ①Total vehicle weight and maximum permitted cargo weight
  2. ②Weight calculated by adding the vehicle's weight and maximum seating capacity
  3. ③Weight calculated by adding the total vehicle weight and maximum seating capacity
  4. ④Total vehicle weight, maximum permitted cargo weight, maximum permitted cargo volume, and cargo manifest
Show answer

Answer
② Weight calculated by adding the vehicle's weight and maximum seating capacity

Reference
Article 19 of the Rules on the Performance and Standards of Motor Vehicles and Motor Vehicle Parts requires the specified gross vehicle weight and maximum load to be marked on the rear of a truck (for a tank truck: gross vehicle weight, maximum load, maximum loading volume, and name of the cargo). For a special towing vehicle, the vehicle weight plus the weight corresponding to its passenger capacity must be marked on its rear or right side. Other vehicles must display their specified maximum load on the rear. However, vehicles with a gross vehicle weight of less than 15 tonnes may omit the gross vehicle weight marking.
