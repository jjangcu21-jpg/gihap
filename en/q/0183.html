183. Which of the following is NOT classified as a person corresponding to one who is visually impaired?

  1. ①Infant or child
  2. ②An individual who is unable to walk without a prosthetic limb
  3. ③An individual who has trouble maintaining bodily balance
  4. ④A deaf person
Show answer

Answer
① Infant or child

Reference
A person equivalent to a blind person under Article 11, Paragraph 2 of the Road Traffic Act and Article 8 of the Enforcement Decree of the same Act means a person who falls under any of the following items. 1. A person who cannot hear 2. A person who has a disability in the body’s balance function 3. A person who cannot walk without using a prosthetic leg, etc.
