372. Which of the following are the most appropriate ways of navigating a roundabout? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Before entering a roundabout, come to a complete stop and enter when there are no approaching vehicles from the left.
  2. ②At a roundabout, the traffic circulates clockwise.
  3. ③There is no need to use turn signals when entering or exiting a roundabout.
  4. ④Even after entering a roundabout, you should continue to be cautious of other vehicles and proceed with care.
Show answer

Answer
① Before entering a roundabout, come to a complete stop and enter when there are no approaching vehicles from the left.
④ Even after entering a roundabout, you should continue to be cautious of other vehicles and proceed with care.

Reference
Turns at roundabouts must be counterclockwise, and turn signals must be activated when entering or exiting.
