138. You may be subject to a penalty according to the Criminal Act if you overtake a vehicle over the centerline or perform any retaliatory driving such as sudden braking. In this case, what is the correct punishment that will be imposed on you?

  1. ①You will be sentenced to imprisonment for up to 7 years or criminal fine of not more than KRW 10 million.
  2. ②You will be sentenced to imprisonment for up to 10 years or criminal fine of not more than KRW 20 million.
  3. ③You will be sentenced to imprisonment for a limited term of more than 1 year.
  4. ④You will be sentenced to imprisonment for a limited term of more than 18 months.
Show answer

Answer
① You will be sentenced to imprisonment for up to 7 years or criminal fine of not more than KRW 10 million.

Reference
According to Article 284 (Special Intimidation) of the Criminal Act, a person who commits the crime of intimidation under the Criminal Act by using a dangerous vehicle, a car, shall be punished by imprisonment for up to 7 years or by a fine not exceeding 10 million won.
