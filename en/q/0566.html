566. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following is NOT a required document for the verification of aptitude test requirements?

  1. ①A health examination report as per the National Health Insurance Act
  2. ②A written diagnosis issued by a doctor in accordance with the Medical Service Act
  3. ③Notice of Physical Examination Results for Military Service under the Military Service Act
  4. ④An eyesight test report issued by the president of the Korean Optometric Association
Show answer

Answer
④ An eyesight test report issued by the president of the Korean Optometric Association

Reference
Article 45 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act (Standards of aptitude required for driving automobiles, etc.) ② The Korea Road Traffic Authority may determine whether the aptitude test standards under each subparagraph of paragraph 1 are met with the following documents. 1. Any of the following documents issued within the two years preceding the date of application for a driver's license test: A physical examination certificate issued by a clinic, hospital, or general hospital. Notification of health checkup results. A medical certificate issued by a doctor. Notification of results of military service judgment physical examination (including physical examination for active duty service)
