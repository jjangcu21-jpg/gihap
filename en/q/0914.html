Road signs · 2 pts

914. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following is the correct explanation of this road safety sign?

  1. ①Safety sign directing vehicles to make a U-turn after turning left
  2. ②Safety sign directing vehicles to turn left or make a U-turn
  3. ③Safety sign directing that vehicles turning left have priority over vehicles making a U-turn
  4. ④Safety sign directing that vehicles making a U-turn have priority over vehicles turning left
Show answer

Answer
② Safety sign directing vehicles to turn left or make a U-turn

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 6] Direction sign 309-2 left turn and U-turn sign instructs the car to turn left or U-turn. Priority for left turns or U-turns is not specified. This safety sign is installed on the right side or center of the road at the point where the car will turn left or U-turn.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
