186. Which of the following is an INCORRECT method of traveling for a pedestrian?

  1. ①Walk on the left side of a sidewalk.
  2. ②Pedestrians are allowed to use any part of the pedestrian-first road.
  3. ③Pedestrians must walk on the sidewalk on roads where the sidewalk and roadways are separated.
  4. ④For roads without a clear distinction of the sidewalks but have a centerline, pedestrians must use the roadside area to walk.
Show answer

Answer
① Walk on the left side of a sidewalk.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 8 (Pedestrian Passage) Pedestrians must, in principle, walk on the right side of the sidewalk.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
