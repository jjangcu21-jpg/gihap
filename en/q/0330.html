330. Which of the following is NOT a behavior for which a traffic penalty is imposed when it occurs in a silver zone between 8 AM and 8 PM?

  1. ①Violation of traffic signal
  2. ②Violation of parking rules
  3. ③Obstruction of pedestrians in a crosswalk
  4. ④Invasion of the centerline
Show answer

Answer
④ Invasion of the centerline

Reference
Appendix 10 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act: Illegal acts and amounts in children’s protection areas and elderly and disabled people’s protection areas
