242. The following describes a Hi-pass lane. Which is correct?

  1. ①A Hi-pass lane is always found in lane 1; drivers intending to take it must change lanes accordingly and pass through the toll road safely.
  2. ②Since the Hi-pass lane for trucks is exclusive to trucks and is colored blue, there is no need to slow down to pass through the toll road.
  3. ③Drivers must reduce speed in advance since the speed limit of a multi-lane Hi-pass toll road is 30 km/h.
  4. ④Drivers must observe the speed limit of a multi-lane Hi-pass toll road. If the Hi-pass device malfunctions upon entry, drivers can pay the toll upon exit.
Show answer

Answer
④ Drivers must observe the speed limit of a multi-lane Hi-pass toll road. If the Hi-pass device malfunctions upon entry, drivers can pay the toll upon exit.

Reference
Truck Hi-Pass guidance lines are orange; ordinary Hi-Pass lane guidance lines are blue. The speed limit in multi-lane Hi-Pass sections varies by section, from 50 to 80 km/h.
