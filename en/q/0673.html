673. A recovery vehicle was caught driving at 145 km/h on an expressway with a 100 km/h speed limit. Which of the following corresponds to the correct demerit points and traffic penalty?

  1. ①70 demerit points and traffic penalty of KRW 140,000
  2. ②60 demerit points and traffic penalty of KRW 130,000
  3. ③30 demerit points and traffic penalty of KRW 100,000
  4. ④15 demerit points and traffic penalty of KRW 70,000
Show answer

Answer
② 60 demerit points and traffic penalty of KRW 130,000

Reference
Rescue vehicles fall under Article 19, Paragraph 1, Subparagraph 3, Subparagraph 3 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act, and have a maximum speed of 80 kilometers per hour on an expressway with two lanes per direction running at 100 kilometers per hour. They are classified as special vehicles according to Annex 1 of the Enforcement Rules of the Automobile Management Act, and special cars are included in vans according to Annex 8 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act and Annex 28 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act, so ② is the correct answer.
