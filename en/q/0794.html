794. Which of the following are the biggest risk factors when making an unprotected left turn in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①There may be a vehicle coming at high speed from the opposite second lane.
  2. ②A vehicle may be stopped behind a truck in the opposite lane, waiting to turn left.
  3. ③A vehicle behind you may suddenly switch to the second lane.
  4. ④A pedestrian on the left side of the road may cross the crosswalk.
  5. ⑤A truck may make an unprotected left turn from the opposite first lane.
Show answer

Answer
① There may be a vehicle coming at high speed from the opposite second lane.
④ A pedestrian on the left side of the road may cross the crosswalk.

Reference
When making an unprotected left turn, you must pay attention to vehicles going straight on the green light on the opposite side of the road. The speed of those vehicles may be faster than expected, and you may not be able to see the vehicles going straight on from the second lane because of the van in the first lane on the other side. Also, if there is a crosswalk on the left side of the road, you must be careful about pedestrians walking.
