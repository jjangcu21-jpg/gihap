203. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is the correct method of passing for a motor vehicle or a horse that is permitted to pass through a pedestrian-only road?

  1. ①If there are any pedestrians, proceed slowly.
  2. ②Proceed while honking.
  3. ③Drive at the walking speed of pedestrians or make a temporary stop.
  4. ④If there are no pedestrians, proceed quickly.
Show answer

Answer
③ Drive at the walking speed of pedestrians or make a temporary stop.

Reference
<Article 28, Paragraph 3 of the Road Traffic Act> Drivers of cars and horses permitted to pass on pedestrian-only roads must drive the cars or horses at the speed of pedestrians or stop temporarily so as not to endanger pedestrians or impede the passage of pedestrians.
