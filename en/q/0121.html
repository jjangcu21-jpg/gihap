121. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is incorrect regarding drivers who have been issued a conditional driver’s license requiring the installation of an alcohol ignition interlock device?

  1. ①When intending to drive a vehicle, the driver must install an alcohol ignition interlock device and register it with the commissioner of the city or provincial police.
  2. ②Driving a vehicle that is not equipped with an alcohol ignition interlock device is prohibited.
  3. ③Driving a vehicle with an alcohol ignition interlock device that does not meet the installation standards is permitted.
  4. ④The driver must submit the driving records of the vehicle with the alcohol ignition interlock device to the commissioner of the city or provincial police at least twice a year.
Show answer

Answer
③ Driving a vehicle with an alcohol ignition interlock device that does not meet the installation standards is permitted.

Reference
① Article 50-3 Paragraph 1 of the Road Traffic Act ②③ Article 50-3 Paragraph 3 of the Road Traffic Act A person who has received a conditional driver's license for the installation of a drunk driving prevention device shall not drive a car, etc. that is not equipped with a drunk driving prevention device or is equipped with a drunk driving prevention device that does not meet the installation standards. ④ Article 50-3, Paragraph 6 of the Road Traffic Act
