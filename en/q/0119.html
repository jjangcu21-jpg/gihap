119. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is NOT true about the passenger capacity of personal mobility devices?

  1. ①An electric kick scooter can carry one person.
  2. ②A self-balancing scooter can carry one person.
  3. ③An electric bicycle powered by a motor can carry one person.
  4. ④The traffic fine for violating the passenger capacity of a personal mobility device is KRW 40,000.
Show answer

Answer
③ An electric bicycle powered by a motor can carry one person.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules Article 33-3 (Riding Capacity for Personal Mobility Devices) In the case of bicycles that can be moved solely by electric motor power, the ridership capacity is two. If this is violated, a fine of 40,000 won will be imposed.
