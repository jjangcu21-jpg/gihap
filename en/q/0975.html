Answer
① The vehicle that just entered the safety zone suddenly switches to the right lane

Reference
Safety zones are established in places where entry is prohibited according to the Road Traffic Act. Therefore, drivers should not enter the safety zone. However, some drivers experience delayed route decisions at junctions (JCT:JUCTION) and interchanges (IC:INTERCHANGE), instantaneous changes in judgment, or distraction due to phone use, and make dangerous changes to the direction of travel belatedly. At this time, you experience violations that violate your safety zone. Therefore, if you see another car moving slowly or stopped in a safety zone with its hazard lights flashing, anticipate that it may suddenly enter your lane, stay alert, and prepare to respond.
