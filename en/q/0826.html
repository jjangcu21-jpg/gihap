826. Which of the following are the TWO most appropriate ways to proceed in the given situation?

  1. ①Yield the right of way to the oncoming delivery vehicle.
  2. ②Proceed as the passenger car driver has the priority.
  3. ③Flash the high-beam headlights repeatedly to prevent the other driver from proceeding.
  4. ④Watch out for people getting out of parked vehicles.
  5. ⑤Stop and wait until the oncoming driver gives way.
Show answer

Answer
① Yield the right of way to the oncoming delivery vehicle.
④ Watch out for people getting out of parked vehicles.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 20 (Obligation to Yield the Way) ① Drivers of all vehicles (excluding emergency vehicles) must yield the right of way by moving to the right edge of the road if they intend to drive at a slower speed than the vehicle following behind. However, this does not apply to roads with traffic separations. ② When vehicles other than emergency vehicles face each other on a narrow road, the vehicles falling under each of the following categories must give way by moving to the right edge of the road. 1. If cars are facing each other on a narrow road on a slope, a car going up. 2. If a car carrying people or carrying goods and a car without passengers and not carrying goods are driving facing each other on a narrow road other than a narrow road with a slope, a car without passengers and not carrying goods.
