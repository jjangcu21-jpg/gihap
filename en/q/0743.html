743. Which of the following are the most incorrect ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Only buses can travel on exclusive bus lanes.
  2. ②Passenger vehicles may use the first lane to overtake the trucks ahead.
  3. ③Maintain a safe distance from the trucks ahead, and be cautious of falling objects.
  4. ④Vehicles traveling in the variable lane must switch to the third lane.
  5. ⑤5-ton trucks are not allowed to exit onto Oksan IC.
Show answer

Answer
① Only buses can travel on exclusive bus lanes.
② Passenger vehicles may use the first lane to overtake the trucks ahead.

Reference
On weekdays, the bus lane on the Gyeongbu Expressway operates only from Osan IC to the southern end of Hannam Bridge. On weekdays, the first lane of the Gyeongbu Expressway other than this section becomes the first lane of the expressway rather than a bus-only lane. Passenger cars trying to overtake and light, compact, and medium-sized vans trying to overtake can pass in the first lane of the highway (Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 9]). Overtaking on bridges is prohibited (Article 22, Paragraph 3, No. 3 of the Road Traffic Act).
