559. Which of the following is correct regarding a motorized bicycle under the Road Traffic Act?

  1. ①The term "motorcycle" refers to all two-wheeled vehicles.
  2. ②Under the Automobile Management Act, a motorcycle is defined as a two-wheeled vehicle with displacement of up to 250 cc.
  3. ③A car embedding a motor with displacement of 150 cc or more is considered to be a motorcycle.
  4. ④In the case of electricity-powered vehicles, a vehicle using a motor with maximum rated output of 11 kW or less (excluding electric bicycles) is considered to be a motorcycle.
Show answer

Answer
④ In the case of electricity-powered vehicles, a vehicle using a motor with maximum rated output of 11 kW or less (excluding electric bicycles) is considered to be a motorcycle.

Reference
Article 2, Paragraph 19 of the Road Traffic Act, “Motorized bicycle” refers to a vehicle that falls under any of the following items. (a) Among two-wheeled vehicles under Article 3 of the Automobile Management Act, two-wheeled vehicles with an engine displacement of 125 cc or less (if powered by electricity, a maximum rated output of 11 kilowatts or less). Other vehicles equipped with a motor with an engine displacement of 125 cc or less (if powered by electricity, a maximum rated output of 11 kilowatts or less) (excluding electric bicycles)
