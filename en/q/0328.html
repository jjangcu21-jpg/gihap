328. Which of the following is an INCORRECT method of driving when approaching a crosswalk with traffic lights in a silver zone?

  1. ①If an elderly person crosses the road after the signals change, wait for the person to finish crossing before taking off.
  2. ②Drivers must anticipate signal changes and drive accordingly to ensure safe driving.
  3. ③Drive slowly to come to a safe stop when the signals change.
  4. ④Take extra precautions as the elderly walk at a slower pace.
Show answer

Answer
② Drivers must anticipate signal changes and drive accordingly to ensure safe driving.

Reference
Pedestrian protection is the top priority. When approaching a crosswalk with a traffic light, stop temporarily to ensure safety, and proceed according to the vehicle progress signal after the crosswalk signal changes.
