506. You fail to pay a traffic penalty for which you have been notified in writing even by its second deadline. Which TWO are applicable?

  1. ①You must request a summary judgment immediately.
  2. ②Your license will be suspended for 40 days if no summary judgment is reached.
  3. ③An administrative fine will be charged.
  4. ④No summary judgment needs to be requested if you pay the traffic penalty along with an additional 30% as delay penalty.
Show answer

Answer
① You must request a summary judgment immediately.
② Your license will be suspended for 40 days if no summary judgment is reached.

Reference
If the second payment deadline has passed for a person who received a traffic penalty notice, a request for summary proceedings must be made without delay. Failure to undergo those proceedings results in a 40-day license suspension. If the person pays the original penalty plus 50%, summary proceedings are not requested. (Road Traffic Act, Article 165)
