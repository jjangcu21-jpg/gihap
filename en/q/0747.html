747. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Proceed quickly as there is a vehicle notifying about the construction ahead in the first lane.
  2. ②Gradually slow down while paying attention to the situation ahead.
  3. ③Turn on the hazard lights to alert oncoming traffic of the hazard.
  4. ④Make a sudden lane change into the third lane to avoid the construction vehicle.
  5. ⑤Honk repeatedly to alert others and pass through, as vehicles traveling on the expressway have the right of way over construction vehicles.
Show answer

Answer
② Gradually slow down while paying attention to the situation ahead.
③ Turn on the hazard lights to alert oncoming traffic of the hazard.

Reference
Cars travel at high speeds on highways, so if there are work vehicles or construction guidance vehicles on the road, you must reduce your speed in advance and drive safely. You must avoid suddenly changing to the next lane or suddenly accelerating or decelerating.
