649. When driving a vehicle towed using a camping trailer on the road, which of the following is the phenomenon wherein the towed vehicle shakes like a fish tail due to physical factors such as cross-wind?

  1. ①Jack knife phenomenon
  2. ②Swaying
  3. ③Hydroplaning
  4. ④Wheel alignment
Show answer

Answer
② Swaying

Reference
Most major accidents when driving a caravan are caused by the sway phenomenon, which causes the caravan to shake like a fish's tail, also known as the fishtail phenomenon.
