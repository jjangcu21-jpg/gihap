460. If the hood of the car starts generating smoke and catches fire on an expressway, which of the following are the TWO most appropriate measures to take?

  1. ①Stop immediately when you notice that there is a problem.
  2. ②Move to the roadside, turn off the engine, and evacuate the vehicle.
  3. ③If it is early enough to extinguish the fire, use the fire extinguisher fitted in the vehicle.
  4. ④If you fail to extinguish the fire, call 119 and wait right next to the vehicle.
Show answer

Answer
② Move to the roadside, turn off the engine, and evacuate the vehicle.
③ If it is early enough to extinguish the fire, use the fire extinguisher fitted in the vehicle.

Reference
What to do if a vehicle fire occurs while driving on the highway: 1. Move the vehicle to the shoulder of the road. 2. Turn off the engine and quickly get out of the vehicle. 3. If it is possible to extinguish the initial fire, extinguish the fire using the fire extinguisher provided in the vehicle. 4. If the initial fire extinguishment fails, evacuate far away as the vehicle may explode. 5. Report a vehicle fire to 119, etc.
