165. Which of the following is correct regarding the duty to protect pedestrians?

  1. ①There is no need to protect a drunken pedestrian who is jaywalking.
  2. ②It is okay to impede the passage of a pedestrian crossing a road with traffic signals.
  3. ③When the green light for pedestrians is flashing, a car can proceed.
  4. ④When turning right at an intersection with traffic signals, the driver shall not impede the passage of a pedestrian who is obeying the signal.
Show answer

Answer
④ When turning right at an intersection with traffic signals, the driver shall not impede the passage of a pedestrian who is obeying the signal.

Reference
Drunken pedestrians who jaywalk are also subject to protection. Even if the green signal is flashing on the pedestrian signal, you must not neglect pedestrian protection, and when turning right at an intersection with a traffic light, you must not interfere with pedestrians following the signal.
