610. Which of the following is NOT appropriate when charging an FCEV?

  1. ①Turning off the engine before charging
  2. ②Smoking a cigarette near the hydrogen charging station
  3. ③Not operating charging equipment on your own
  4. ④Starting the engine after charging your car
Show answer

Answer
② Smoking a cigarette near the hydrogen charging station

Reference
Smoking is prohibited in hydrogen vehicle refueling station facilities except in designated areas. 「High Pressure Gas Safety Management Act」 Enforcement Rules Annex 5, KGS Code FP216/FP217 2.1.2 Depending on the distance to the fire, a detour distance of at least 8m must be provided from the exterior of the gas facility to the place where fire is handled.
