1. Which of the following procedures is correct when applying for a mobile driver’s license?

  1. ①Applications can be made using any app.
  2. ②Applications must be made using a smartphone under the applicant’s name with identity verification.
  3. ③Applications can only be made at the traffic civil service office of a police station.
  4. ④A mobile driver’s license cannot be issued at a driver’s license test center.
Show answer

Answer
② Applications must be made using a smartphone under the applicant’s name with identity verification.

Reference
Article 85-2 of the Road Traffic Act (Issuance of mobile driver's license and verification of driver's license, etc.), Article 78-2 of the Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules (Application for mobile driver's license, etc.)
