366. Which of the following are the TWO correct methods of turning left safely at an intersection?

  1. ①Watch out for stopping vehicles from the opposite direction.
  2. ②Watch out for right-turning vehicles from the opposite direction.
  3. ③Watch out for right-turning vehicles from the same direction.
  4. ④Watch out for left-turning vehicles in the lane to your side.
Show answer

Answer
② Watch out for right-turning vehicles from the opposite direction.
④ Watch out for left-turning vehicles in the lane to your side.

Reference
Vehicles making an unprotected left turn at an intersection must turn left while paying attention to vehicles turning right and vehicles on the side also turning left in the same direction. (Article 25 of the Road Traffic Act)
