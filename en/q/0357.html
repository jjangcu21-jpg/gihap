357. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following are TWO locations that allow a motor vehicle to park?

  1. ①5 m from the corner of a road
  2. ②At a distance of 7 m or more from where a fire-extinguishing water facility is installed
  3. ③At a distance of 7 m or more from where emergency fire-extinguishing equipment is installed
  4. ④5 m from a safety zone
Show answer

Answer
② At a distance of 7 m or more from where a fire-extinguishing water facility is installed
③ At a distance of 7 m or more from where emergency fire-extinguishing equipment is installed

Reference
Places where parking is prohibited: ① Within 10 meters from a crosswalk ② Within 5 meters from where fire-fighting water facilities are installed ③ Within 5 meters from where emergency fire extinguishers are installed ④ Within 10 meters on each side of the safety zone (Road Traffic Act Article 32) Article 34-2 (Special provisions for places where stopping or parking is prohibited) ① In any of the following cases: Notwithstanding Article 32 subparagraph 1, 4, 5, 7, or 8, or Article 33 subparagraph 3, you may stop or park. 1. When a bicycle is stopped or parked at an electric bicycle charging station or bicycle parking device among bicycle facilities under Article 2, Paragraph 2 of the Act on the Promotion of Bicycle Use. 2. When a city/provincial police chief permits stopping or parking of bicycles, etc. with a safety sign at the request of the mayor, etc. ② A city/provincial police chief allows stopping or parking by specifying the area, time, method, and type of vehicle with a safety sign. At certain places, you may stop or park notwithstanding Article 32, Subparagraphs 7 and 8, or Article 33, Subparagraph 3.
