5. What is the period of validity of a learner’s permit according to the Road Traffic Act?

  1. ①6 months from the date of issuance
  2. ②1 year from the date of issuance
  3. ③2 years from the date of issuance
  4. ④3 years from the date of issuance
Show answer

Answer
② 1 year from the date of issuance

Reference
Road Traffic Act, Article 81: A learner's permit is valid for one year from its issue date. However, it ceases to be valid if its holder obtains a Class 1 or Class 2 ordinary driver's license, even before that year has elapsed.
