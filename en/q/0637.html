637. What is the maximum permitted speed when a passenger vehicle with total weight of 5,000 kg is towing a vehicle with total weight of 1,500 kg on a road that is not an expressway?

  1. ①50 km/h
  2. ②40 km/h
  3. ③30 km/h
  4. ④20 km/h
Show answer

Answer
③ 30 km/h

Reference
According to Article 20 of the Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules, when a car with a gross weight of less than 2,000 kilograms is towed by a car with a gross weight of three times or more, the speed limit is 30 kilometers per hour.
