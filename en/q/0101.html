101. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following cases requires driving at a slow speed?

  1. ①When the traffic signal at the intersection is flashing in red
  2. ②Upon passing an intersection where traffic is not controlled and is frequent
  3. ③Upon passing an intersection where traffic is not controlled
  4. ④Upon changing lanes near an intersection
Show answer

Answer
③ Upon passing an intersection where traffic is not controlled

Reference
① You must pause. ③ When passing through an intersection where traffic is not being controlled, you must pass slowly.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
