768. Which of the following driving methods violate the law in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Driver B - Drives at 100 km/h.
  2. ②Driver A - Drives at 70 km/h.
  3. ③Driver B - Overtakes at an intersection.
  4. ④Driver A - Overtakes at an intersection.
  5. ⑤Driver B - Does not maintain a safe distance with the vehicle ahead.
