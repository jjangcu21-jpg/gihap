199. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following methods of passage used by motor vehicles or horses is NOT correct?

  1. ①When the roadway and sidewalk are separated, be sure to use the roadway.
  2. ②Drive slowly just before crossing a crosswalk, check left and right, and cross in a manner that does not impede the passage of pedestrians.
  3. ③Be sure to use the right-side roadway.
  4. ④When it is a one-way road, you can drive in the center or left portion of the road.
Show answer

Answer
② Drive slowly just before crossing a crosswalk, check left and right, and cross in a manner that does not impede the passage of pedestrians.

Reference
<Article 13, Paragraph 2 of the Road Traffic Act> In the case of the proviso to Paragraph 1, the driver of a car or horse must pause immediately before crossing the sidewalk, look at the left and right sides, and then cross so as not to impede the passage of pedestrians.
