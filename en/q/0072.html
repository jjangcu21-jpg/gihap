72. Under the Guarantee of Compulsory Motor Vehicle Liability Security Act, what is the punishment criterion for a car owner who fails to purchase mandatory insurance? (When the car has not been operated)

  1. ①An administrative fine of up to KRW 3 million
  2. ②An administrative fine of up to KRW 5 million
  3. ③Up to 1 year of imprisonment or a criminal fine of up to KRW 10 million
  4. ④Up to 2 years of imprisonment or a criminalfine of up to KRW 20 million
Show answer

Answer
① An administrative fine of up to KRW 3 million

Reference
Article 48 of the Automobile Damage Compensation Guarantee Act (Fines) ③ A fine not exceeding 3 million won shall be imposed on any person who falls under any of the following items. 1. A person who fails to subscribe to mandatory insurance in accordance with the provisions of Article 5, Paragraphs 1 to 3. Article 46 (Penalty Provisions) ② Any person who falls under any of the following items shall be punished by imprisonment for not more than one year or by a fine not exceeding 10 million won. 2. A car owner who operates a car that is not covered by mandatory insurance in violation of the main text of Article 8.
