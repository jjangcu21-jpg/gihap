990. When entering and passing through an intersection by turning left, which of the following descriptions matches the situation shown in the video? (Refer to the website)

Video questions play in the Gihap app or on the official site.

Official video ↗
  1. ①The driver turns left according to the intersection signal.
  2. ②The driver’s slow driving causes another driver to overtake.
  3. ③The overtaking driver yields priority when entering the intersection.
  4. ④The overtaking driver enters the intersection according to the flashing red light of the signal.
Show answer

Answer
① The driver turns left according to the intersection signal.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules, Appendix 2: Flashing red signal. Vehicles must first stop immediately before the stop line or pedestrian crossing, if present, or immediately before the intersection, then may proceed with care for other traffic. Article 13 of the Road Traffic Act. Paragraph 3. The driver of a car or horse must drive on the right side of the center (if a center line is installed, this refers to the center line; hereinafter the same shall apply) of the road (referring to the roadway in the case of a road with a separate sidewalk and roadway). Paragraph 5. Drivers of cars and horses must not enter places where entry is prohibited by safety signs, such as safety zones. Road Traffic Act Article 22 (Time and place of prohibition on overtaking). Paragraph 3. The driver of any vehicle may not overtake another vehicle in any of the following places: 1. Intersection 2. In a tunnel 3. On a bridge 4. A bend in the road, near the crest of a slope, or down a steep slope, etc. Places designated as safety signs by the city/provincial police chief as deemed necessary to prevent danger on the road and ensure traffic safety and smooth traffic flow.
