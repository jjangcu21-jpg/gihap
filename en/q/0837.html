837. Which of the following are the TWO safest ways to proceed in the snowy road conditions?

  1. ①Follow the tire tracks of the vehicle ahead when the lane markings are not visible due to heavy snow.
  2. ②Maintain a greater safe distance than usual because braking distances shorten on snowy or icy roads.
  3. ③Be prepared for chain collisions caused by slippage accidents in tunnel entrances and exits, which are frequent freezing zones during heavy snowfall.
  4. ④Reduce your speed by 20% of the speed limit on icy roads.
  5. ⑤Beware of a temporary loss of vision due to adaptation to the dark after passing through the tunnel.
Show answer

Answer
① Follow the tire tracks of the vehicle ahead when the lane markings are not visible due to heavy snow.
③ Be prepared for chain collisions caused by slippage accidents in tunnel entrances and exits, which are frequent freezing zones during heavy snowfall.

Reference
② In snow, rain, or fog, the braking distance becomes longer, so you should leave a greater safety distance than usual. ④ On icy roads, the maximum speed must be reduced by 50/100. ⑤ When passing through a tunnel during heavy snowfall, you may experience temporary vision loss due to light adaptation as you are suddenly exposed from a dark place to a bright place.
