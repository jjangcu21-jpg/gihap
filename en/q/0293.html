293. Which of the following is the correct method of driving considering the protection of school buses for children?

  1. ①If you are facing and approaching a school bus for children on a road with one lane in one direction, you must stop before reaching the bus, check that it is safe, and drive on slowly.
  2. ②If a school bus for children has switched on flashers indicating that children are getting off, you must take another lane and drive past the bus quickly.
  3. ③If there is no centerline and you are facing and approaching a school bus for children, you must keep driving at the same speed.
  4. ④You can overtake a school bus for children, even if it has signs indicating that children are on board.
Show answer

Answer
① If you are facing and approaching a school bus for children on a road with one lane in one direction, you must stop before reaching the bus, check that it is safe, and drive on slowly.

Reference
Article 51, Paragraph 2 of the Road Traffic Act (Special Protection of Children's School Buses) ① When a children's school bus is stopped on the road and devices such as flashing lights are operating to indicate that children or infants are getting on or off, the driver of a car traveling in the lane where the children's school bus is stopped and the lane right next to the lane must stop temporarily before reaching the children's school bus, check for safety, and then slow down. ② In the case of paragraph 1, on roads where a center line is not installed and roads with one lane each way, the driver of a car traveling in the opposite direction must also stop temporarily and check for safety before reaching the children's school bus and then drive slowly. ③ Drivers of any vehicle may not overtake a children's school bus traveling on the road with a sign indicating that it is carrying children or infants.
