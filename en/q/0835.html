835. Among the vehicles driving on the expressway, which TWO are violating the designated lane rules?

  1. ①A (a passenger car overtaking another vehicle)
  2. ②B (a large 36-seater bus)
  3. ③C (a 1-ton cargo truck)
  4. ④D (a large 26-seater bus)
  5. ⑤E (a passenger car driving normally)
Show answer

Answer
② B (a large 36-seater bus)
③ C (a 1-ton cargo truck)

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules, Appendix 9: On an expressway with 2 lanes in each direction, lane 1 is for all motor vehicles overtaking, and lane 2 is for all motor vehicles. With 3 or more lanes in each direction, lane 1 is for overtaking passenger cars and light, small, or medium passenger vans/buses. The left-side lanes are for those same vehicle categories. The right-side lanes are for large buses, trucks, special vehicles, and construction machinery under Article 2, item 18(b). On expressways, exclude lane 1 and divide the remaining lanes into two halves: the half nearer lane 1 forms the left-side lanes. If the number remaining is odd, exclude the middle lane from the left-side lanes. The right-side lanes are all lanes other than lane 1 and the left-side lanes.
