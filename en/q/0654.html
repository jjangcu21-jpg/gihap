654. Which of the following is a towing vehicle designed in such a way that a towed vehicle is partially loaded so that most of the vehicle weight and its cargo weight are shifted to the towing vehicle?

  1. ①A pull trailer
  2. ②A semi-trailer
  3. ③A low-bed trailer
  4. ④A center-axle trailer
Show answer

Answer
② A semi-trailer

Reference
① Full trailer - A towed vehicle with a structure that supports most of the weight of the car and its cargo with the axle of the car ② Semi-trailer - A towed car with a structure in which part of the car and its cargo are carried on top of the tow car and a significant portion of the weight of the car and its cargo is shared with the tow car ③ Low-floor trailer - It is suitable for the transportation of heavy goods and has the structure of a semi-trailer, and most of the top ground clearance is 1,100 mm. or less, and is manufactured lower than the height of the upper part of the coupler of the towing car. ④ Center axle trailer - The center of gravity in an evenly loaded state is in front of the center of the vehicle axle, the connection device with the towing car is not bent in the vertical direction, and a towed car with one or more axles as a structure that shares a load less than 10% of the total weight of the vehicle or 1,000 kilograms to the towing car.
