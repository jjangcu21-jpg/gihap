81. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following correctly connects the situation and the proper action that drivers must take?

  1. ①School zone - Honk to inform children of the vehicle passing by.
  2. ②Railroad crossing - Increase speed to pass quickly when a crossing barrier is about to go down.
  3. ③Intersection without signals - When turning right, drive slowly along the right side of the road and turn right.
  4. ④Night driving - Adjust the headlights to high beam to caution drivers coming from the opposite direction.
Show answer

Answer
③ Intersection without signals - When turning right, drive slowly along the right side of the road and turn right.

Reference
If a child passes by on the pedestrian path in front of the school, you must stop temporarily, and if the barrier at a railroad crossing is about to lower, you must not enter. Additionally, when driving at night, headlights should be adjusted downward to avoid interfering with the driving of vehicles on the other side.
