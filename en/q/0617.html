617. Which of the following is NOT the correct explanation on preventing loads on a truck from falling?

  1. ①For cargo that is easy to roll, use fixed neck or cargo support.
  2. ②When loading construction equipment, etc., use wires and ropes.
  3. ③When there is space in the front, rear, and on the left and right sides of the loading box, use a detent, etc.
  4. ④Traffic penalty of KRW 50,000 and demerit of 10 points are imposed for violation of fall prevention of the load.
Show answer

Answer
④ Traffic penalty of KRW 50,000 and demerit of 10 points are imposed for violation of fall prevention of the load.

Reference
Under Appendix 8 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act, failure to secure cargo against falling incurs a traffic fine of KRW 50,000 for trucks over 4 tonnes and KRW 40,000 for trucks of 4 tonnes or less. Appendix 28 of the Enforcement Rules assigns 15 penalty points for this violation.
