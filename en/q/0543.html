543. According to the Toll Road Act and its Enforcement Decree, what is the correct standard for imposing additional tolls on vehicles that pass through tollgates without paying tolls on toll roads?

  1. ①An amount equivalent to five times the toll fee can be imposed.
  2. ②An amount equivalent to 10 times the toll fee can be imposed.
  3. ③An amount equivalent to 20 times the toll fee can be imposed.
  4. ④An amount equivalent to 30 times the toll fee can be imposed.
Show answer

Answer
② An amount equivalent to 10 times the toll fee can be imposed.

Reference
When traveling on a toll road without paying the toll, an amount equivalent to 10 times the toll may be imposed. (Article 20 of the Toll Road Act and Article 14 of the Enforcement Decree of the Toll Road Act)
