723. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Honk repeatedly to warn the pedestrian standing on the left side and proceed quickly.
  2. ②Proceed without anticipating any potential hazards.
  3. ③Proceed slowly in preparation for a vehicle entering from the right side of the road ahead.
  4. ④Speed up and pass through quickly as the intersection has no traffic lights.
  5. ⑤Stop temporarily before the stop line before the crosswalk.
Show answer

Answer
③ Proceed slowly in preparation for a vehicle entering from the right side of the road ahead.
⑤ Stop temporarily before the stop line before the crosswalk.

Reference
Avoid continuously honking your horn without a valid reason, and when entering an intersection without a traffic signal, you must drive slowly and pay attention to the surroundings. When a pedestrian is passing or is about to cross a crosswalk, the driver must temporarily stop in front of the crosswalk (referring to the stop line where a stop line is installed) so as not to interfere with the pedestrian's crossing or pose a danger.
