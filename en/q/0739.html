739. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Check the location and speed of vehicles in the third lane, and change lanes after ensuring safety.
  2. ②Slow down as much as possible when entering the third lane.
  3. ③Make a sudden lane change even if you have not secured a safe distance in the third lane.
  4. ④Turn on the turn signal in advance and check for safety before changing lanes.
  5. ⑤Turn on the turn signal as you begin changing lanes, since other drivers will not yield if the signal is turned on in advance.
Show answer

Answer
① Check the location and speed of vehicles in the third lane, and change lanes after ensuring safety.
④ Turn on the turn signal in advance and check for safety before changing lanes.

Reference
When changing the course of a car, you must not change the course if there is a risk of interfering with the normal passage of other cars coming in the direction you want to change. On highways and automobile-only roads, before changing course, you must turn on the turn signal from 100 meters before the intended lane-change point and secure a safe distance before changing course.
