869. What does this safety sign signify?

  1. ①You should drive with caution and proceed on the right side of the road because there is an obstacle in the middle of the road.
  2. ②You should drive with caution because a median strip is about to start.
  3. ③You should drive with caution because a median strip is about to end.
  4. ④You should drive with caution and headlights on because there is a tunnel is ahead.
Show answer

Answer
① You should drive with caution and proceed on the right side of the road because there is an obstacle in the middle of the road.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules Appended Table 6. 121. Right-hand traffic sign Notifies that there is a point where traffic must be made in the right-hand direction of the road.
