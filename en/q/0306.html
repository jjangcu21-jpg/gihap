306. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is NOT correct regarding the protection of children?

  1. ①If a child is crossing a road without a crosswalk, drivers must drive slowly.
  2. ②If a child is standing in a safety zone, drivers must drive slowly while maintaining a safe distance.
  3. ③If a child is walking in a narrow alley, drivers must drive slowly while maintaining a safe distance.
  4. ④If a child is crossing a crosswalk, drivers shall make a temporary stop in front of the crosswalk.
Show answer

Answer
① If a child is crossing a road without a crosswalk, drivers must drive slowly.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 27 (Protection of Pedestrians) ① When pedestrians (including drivers of bicycles, etc. who get off their bicycles and drag or carry their bicycles as prescribed in Article 13-2 (6)) are passing or are about to pass on a crosswalk, the driver of any car or tram must temporarily stop in front of the crosswalk (referring to the stop line where a stop line is installed) so as not to interfere with or endanger the crossing of pedestrians. ② When the driver of any car or streetcar intends to turn left or right at an intersection where traffic is being controlled, he or she must not obstruct the passage of pedestrians crossing the road in accordance with signals or instructions from traffic signals or police officials. ③ Drivers of all vehicles must not obstruct the passage of pedestrians crossing roads at or near intersections where traffic control is not in place. ④ Drivers of all vehicles must drive slowly and at a safe distance when there is a pedestrian in a safety zone installed on the road or when passing by a pedestrian on a narrow road without a lane. ⑤ When a pedestrian is crossing a road on which a crosswalk is not installed pursuant to Article 10 (3), the driver of any car or streetcar must temporarily stop at a safe distance to allow the pedestrian to cross safely. ⑥ Drivers of all vehicles must drive slowly at a safe distance when passing by pedestrians in any of the following places, and when they obstruct the passage of pedestrians, they must slow down or stop temporarily to allow pedestrians to pass safely. 1. A road without a center line among roads where sidewalks and roadways are not distinguished 2. Pedestrian priority roads 3. Places other than roads ⑦ The driver of any car or tram must temporarily stop in front of a crosswalk without a signal (referring to the stop line if a stop line is installed) among the crosswalks installed within a child protection zone under Article 12 (1), regardless of whether a pedestrian is crossing.
