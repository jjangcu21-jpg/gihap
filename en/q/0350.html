350. Which one of the following is the correct amount of administrative fine imposed on those who stack objects on or obstruct the entry to parking spaces reserved for persons with disabilities?

  1. ①KRW 40,000
  2. ②KRW 200,000
  3. ③KRW 500,000
  4. ④KRW 1,000,000
Show answer

Answer
③ KRW 500,000

Reference
Anyone who interferes with parking in a parking space exclusively for the disabled will be subject to a fine of 500,000 won (Article 17 (5) of the Act on Guarantee of Convenience for the Disabled, Elderly, Pregnant Women, etc., Annex Table 3 of the Enforcement Decree).
