802. When driving on an urban side street, which of the following are the two safest ways of driving?

  1. ①Watch out for bicycles and dogs being walked, as they may suddenly enter the roadway.
  2. ②Honk to let the cyclist know that you want to pass.
  3. ③Children may suddenly come into the center of the road, so slow down.
  4. ④Increase speed to avoid the bicycle and pass by quickly.
  5. ⑤Flash your headlights as a warning signal to the car coming from the opposite direction.
Show answer

Answer
① Watch out for bicycles and dogs being walked, as they may suddenly enter the roadway.
③ Children may suddenly come into the center of the road, so slow down.

Reference
Back roads in downtown areas are prone to accidents due to the mixing of various types of traffic, including pedestrians, bicycles, and pets. Drivers must drive carefully to avoid these risks. In particular, children may suddenly jump out onto the road, so you should reduce your speed and drive slowly (③). On the other hand, acts that threaten pedestrians or other drivers by using horns or headlights unnecessarily (②, ⑤) and speeding up to pass quickly (④) may both violate the law and are unsafe. Therefore, the correct answers are ① and ③.
