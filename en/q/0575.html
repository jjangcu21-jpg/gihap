575. Which of the following is the amount of penalty imposed on a person who rides a bicycle on a narrow road within a school zone where a lane is not installed and who does not slow down while passing by a pedestrian without securing a safe distance?

  1. ①KRW 100,000
  2. ②KRW 80,000
  3. ③KRW 40,000
  4. ④KRW 20,000
Show answer

Answer
③ KRW 40,000

Reference
Article 27 (4) of the Road Traffic Act: Drivers of all vehicles must drive slowly at a safe distance when there is a pedestrian in a safety zone installed on the road or when passing next to a pedestrian on a narrow road without a lane. Road Traffic Act Enforcement Decree, Schedule 10, fine: 40,000 won
