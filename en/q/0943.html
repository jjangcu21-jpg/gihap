943. What does this road surface marking indicate?

  1. ①A bicycle-only lane.
  2. ②A crossing for bicycles.
  3. ③Bicycles must be parked at a bicycle parking lot.
  4. ④Two or more bicycles can travel side by side on a bicycle track.
Show answer

Answer
② A crossing for bicycles.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules, Appendix 6, road marking 534: Bicycle crossing. Marked where bicycles need to cross the road; at intersections with pedestrian crossings, it is placed alongside the pedestrian crossing.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
