337. Which of the following is the most desirable driving behavior of an elderly driver?

  1. ①Always drive slowly for safety reasons when traveling long distances, regardless of the type of road.
  2. ②Drive a vehicle without taking a rest until you reach your destination to save time.
  3. ③Keep an eye on the road situation and drive by complying with the speed limit.
  4. ④Drive mainly at night when there is less traffic.
Show answer

Answer
③ Keep an eye on the road situation and drive by complying with the speed limit.

Reference
Elderly drivers should avoid driving long distances, for long periods of time, or driving late at night.
