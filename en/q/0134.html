134. Which of the following are TWO correct descriptions with regard to fatigued driving and driving under the influence of drugs?

  1. ①Fatigued driving is not likely to lead to falling asleep at the wheel.
  2. ②Fatigued driving is dangerous because it leads to poor focus, judgment, and reflexes.
  3. ③Driving under the influence of drugs and causing an accident resulting in injury are subject to punishment.
  4. ④If your driver's license is revoked because of a hit-and-run event while driving under the influence of drugs, it is possible to get the license back after three years.
Show answer

Answer
② Fatigued driving is dangerous because it leads to poor focus, judgment, and reflexes.
③ Driving under the influence of drugs and causing an accident resulting in injury are subject to punishment.

Reference
Driving while tired or taking medication can easily cause a decrease in attention, judgment, and reaction speed, as well as drowsy driving. In particular, if a person causes a traffic accident and causes injury while driving while unable to drive normally due to the influence of drugs such as drugs, he or she will be punished under Article 5-11 of the Act on the Aggravated Punishment, etc. of Specific Crimes (Dangerous Driving, Injury, etc.). In case ④, 5 years must elapse.
