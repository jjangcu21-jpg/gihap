443. Under the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is not an appropriate action on the part of a driver if the car is unable to operate due to a breakdown on the expressway at night?

  1. ①A red flashing signal, electric lantern or spark signal that can be identified from 500 m in all directions must be installed.
  2. ②When installing a sign, it must be installed in a location where the driver of a vehicle approaching from the rear can see it.
  3. ③The driver must take necessary measures, such as moving the vehicle off the expressway.
  4. ④The safety tripod sign must be installed 200 m from the point where the broken-down vehicle is located.
Show answer

Answer
④ The safety tripod sign must be installed 200 m from the point where the broken-down vehicle is located.

Reference
Article 66 of the Road Traffic Act, Article 40 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act, Indication of broken down vehicles
