378. Which of the following is the safest method of driving at an expressway interchange?

  1. ①As interchanges tend to be congested, apply sudden brakes to discourage vehicles behind you from following closely and prevent potential accidents accordingly.
  2. ②As drivers tend to become less aware of speed at an interchange, stop momentarily before continuing.
  3. ③If the desired interchange has already been passed, use the next interchange.
  4. ④Accelerate quickly to enter an interchange.
Show answer

Answer
③ If the desired interchange has already been passed, use the next interchange.

Reference
Sudden braking increases the risk of an accident with a vehicle behind. If you suddenly slow down near an interchange and come out onto a public road, your sense of speed will become dull, so you must slow down in advance and drive at the speed limit for the public road. If you pass the interchange you need to advance through, you must use the next interchange to exit.
