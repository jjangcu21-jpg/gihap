533. What is the penalty for a driver who, upon being stopped for drink driving, and who is known to have refused a breathalyzer test 3 years previously, refuses to comply once again?

  1. ①Imprisonment with labor for at least 1 year but not more than 6 years, or criminal fine of at least KRW 5 million but not more than KRW 30 million
  2. ②Imprisonment with labor for at least 2 years but not more than 6 years, or criminal fine of at least KRW 5 million but not more than KRW 20 million
  3. ③Imprisonment with labor for at least 3 years but not more than 5 years, or criminal fine of KRW at least 10 million but not more than KRW 30 million
  4. ④Imprisonment with labor for at least 1 year but not more than 5 years, or criminal fine of at least KRW 5 million but not more than KRW 20 million
Show answer

Answer
① Imprisonment with labor for at least 1 year but not more than 6 years, or criminal fine of at least KRW 5 million but not more than KRW 30 million

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 148-2(1): A person convicted of violating Article 44(1) or (2) and sentenced to a fine or a heavier penalty who violates either provision again within 10 years after that sentence became final is subject to the following penalties, including when the previous sentence has lost effect. This applies to drivers of motor vehicles, etc. or trams, excluding personal mobility devices. 1. Violation of Article 44(2): imprisonment for 1–6 years or a fine of 5–30 million won. 2. Violation of Article 44(1) with blood alcohol concentration at least 0.2%: imprisonment for 2–6 years or a fine of 10–30 million won. 3. Violation of Article 44(1) with blood alcohol concentration at least 0.03% but below 0.2%: imprisonment for 1–5 years or a fine of 5–20 million won.
