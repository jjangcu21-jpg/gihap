356. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following are TWO locations that allow a motor vehicle to make a stop or park?

  1. ①Within 50 m from the main entrance in a school zone
  2. ②Within 10 m from a crosswalk
  3. ③Within a distance of 5 m from where emergency fire-extinguishing equipment is installed
  4. ④Within 10 m from a safety zone in all four directions
Show answer

Answer
③ Within a distance of 5 m from where emergency fire-extinguishing equipment is installed
④ Within 10 m from a safety zone in all four directions

Reference
Places where stopping and parking are prohibited Drivers of all vehicles must not stop or park their vehicles in any of the following places. However, this does not apply when following this Act, orders under this Act, or instructions from police officials, or when temporarily stopping to prevent danger. 1. Intersections, pedestrian crossings, railway crossings, and sidewalks on roads with separate sidewalks and roadways (excluding on-street parking lots installed across a roadway and a sidewalk in accordance with the Parking Lot Act) 2. A place within 5 meters from the edge of an intersection or a corner of a road 3. A place within 10 meters on all sides of the safety zone on a road with a safety zone 4. A stopping place for buses and passenger vehicles A place within 10 meters from the place where the indicating pole, sign, or line is installed. However, this does not apply if the driver of a bus passenger car stops or parks the car to pick up or drop off passengers at a stop along the operating route during the operating hours of the bus passenger car. 5. A place within 10 meters from the edge of a crossing or a crosswalk. 6. A place within 5 meters from the following places: A place where fire-fighting water facilities or emergency fire-fighting equipment are installed in accordance with Article 10 of the Framework Act on Fire Services. Places where facilities prescribed by Presidential Decree are installed as fire-fighting facilities pursuant to Article 2 (1) 1 of the Act on Fire Prevention, Installation/Maintenance of Firefighting Facilities, and Safety Management 7. Places designated by the Commissioner of the City/Provincial Police Agency as deemed necessary to prevent danger on the road and ensure safety and smooth flow of traffic 8. Children's protection areas designated by the mayor, etc. pursuant to Article 12 (1) Article 34-2 (No stopping or parking) Special provisions for prohibited places) ① In any of the following cases, you may stop or park notwithstanding Article 32 subparagraph 1, 4, 5, 7, 8, or Article 33 subparagraph 3. 1. When a bicycle is stopped or parked at an electric bicycle charging station or bicycle parking device among bicycle facilities under Article 2, Paragraph 2 of the Act on the Promotion of Bicycle Use. 2. When a city/provincial police chief permits stopping or parking of bicycles, etc. with a safety sign at the request of the mayor, etc. ② A city/provincial police chief allows stopping or parking by specifying the area, time, method, and type of vehicle with a safety sign. At certain places, you may stop or park notwithstanding Article 32, Subparagraphs 7 and 8, or Article 33, Subparagraph 3.
