856. Which of the following are the TWO safest ways to proceed in the given situation?

  1. ①Drive close to the left-hand side of the bicycle to intimidate and dissuade it from turning left.
  2. ②Cross the centerline to overtake the bicycle.
  3. ③Slow down and wait until the bicycle safely moves off the road.
  4. ④Maintain a safe distance because the bicycle may turn left.
  5. ⑤Honk to hurry the bicycle along.
Show answer

Answer
③ Slow down and wait until the bicycle safely moves off the road.
④ Maintain a safe distance because the bicycle may turn left.

Reference
In the case of quiet country roads, there is less vehicle traffic, so be aware that many cyclists cross the center line. Therefore, if you spot a bicycle ahead while driving on a country road, you should drive slowly and look ahead carefully to monitor the cyclist's movements. ③ Drivers of automobiles, etc. must pay attention to drivers of bicycles, etc. going in the same direction, and when passing by them, they must secure the necessary distance to avoid collision with bicycles, etc. (Article 19, Paragraph 2 of the Road Traffic Act).
