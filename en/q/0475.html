475. Which of the following statements regarding the speed limit of a vehicle trying to overtake another vehicle is correct?

  1. ①There is no speed limit applied to a vehicle overtaking another one.
  2. ②A vehicle overtaking another vehicle can exceed the legal speed limit of the road by 50%.
  3. ③Vehicles are allowed to overtake other vehicles regardless of the speed limit, depending on the driver's driving skill.
  4. ④Overtaking other vehicles is allowed only when performed within the speed limit of the road.
Show answer

Answer
④ Overtaking other vehicles is allowed only when performed within the speed limit of the road.

Reference
A vehicle may overtake another vehicle only while staying within the speed limit for that road.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
