864. Which of the following are the safe ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Prepare to stop as the pedestrian in the front may rapidly turn left.
  2. ②To go straight, slowly enter the intersection as it is currently empty.
  3. ③Prior to entering the intersection, stop to check for vehicles coming from both sides.
  4. ④Honk repeatedly to alert the bicycle riders and pedestrians nearby.
  5. ⑤Drive along the right side of the road to induce the pedestrians to move to the edge of the road.
Show answer

Answer
① Prepare to stop as the pedestrian in the front may rapidly turn left.
③ Prior to entering the intersection, stop to check for vehicles coming from both sides.

Reference
Risk prediction. Article 31 of the Road Traffic Act (Places to slow down or stop temporarily) Paragraph ② Drivers of all cars or trams must stop temporarily at any of the following places. 1. Uncontrolled intersections where the view to the left or right is obstructed or traffic is frequent Article 25 (Intersection Passage Method) ① When drivers of all vehicles intend to turn right at an intersection, they must turn right while driving slowly along the right edge of the road in advance. In this case, the driver of a car turning right must pay attention to pedestrians or bicycles that stop or proceed according to the signal. ② If the driver of any vehicle intends to turn left at an intersection, he or she must drive slowly along the center line of the road in advance and turn left using the inside of the center of the intersection. However, drivers may pass outside the center of the intersection at locations specifically designated as necessary by the city/provincial police chief based on intersection conditions. ③ Notwithstanding paragraph 2, if a driver of a bicycle, etc. intends to turn left at an intersection, he/she must first move to the right edge of the road, drive slowly, and turn left using the edge of the intersection. ④ If there is a car that signals with a hand, a direction indicator, or a light to turn right or left pursuant to the provisions of paragraphs 1 through 3, the driver of the car behind the car must not obstruct the progress of the car in front that gave the signal. ⑤ Do not enter a signal-controlled intersection if traffic ahead would force you to stop within it (beyond the stop line, if present) and obstruct other vehicles or trams. ⑥ When attempting to enter an intersection where a safety sign indicating temporary stop or yield is installed without traffic control, the driver of any vehicle must temporarily stop or yield so as not to impede the progress of other vehicles. Article 49 of the Road Traffic Act (Matters to be observed by all drivers, etc.) Paragraph 1, No. 8. Drivers shall not commit any of the following acts without justifiable grounds and generate noise that may cause harm to others. The act of starting a car quickly or rapidly increasing its speed. An act of increasing the rotation speed of the engine of a car, etc. without transmitting it to the wheels of the car. Sounding the horn repeatedly or continuously
