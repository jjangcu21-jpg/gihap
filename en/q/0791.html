791. You want to turn right in the given situation. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Turn right while slowing down without coming to a full stop before the intersection stop line.
  2. ②Always check for pedestrians at the crosswalk with a traffic light just before the intersection.
  3. ③It is not necessary to check for vehicles going straight from the left to the right side of the road.
  4. ④Be cautious of a two-wheeled vehicle following you as seen in the right side-view mirror.
  5. ⑤It is not necessary to check for pedestrians at the crosswalk with a traffic light immediately after a right turn.
Show answer

Answer
② Always check for pedestrians at the crosswalk with a traffic light just before the intersection.
④ Be cautious of a two-wheeled vehicle following you as seen in the right side-view mirror.

Reference
Among vehicle signals, a red signal means that cars and horses must stop just before the stop line, crosswalk, or intersection. When a vehicle wishes to turn right, it must first stop right before the stop line, crosswalk, or intersection and then turn right without interfering with the traffic of other vehicles proceeding according to the signal. Nevertheless, cars and horses cannot turn right when the three-color right turn light is red. Therefore, you must temporarily stop at the stop line just before the intersection and check whether there are pedestrians on the crosswalk and whether there is a pedestrian signal, and you must also be careful about pedestrians on the crosswalk immediately after turning right. In addition, there are cases where the two-wheeled vehicle visible in the right side mirror drives straight between the sidewalk and your car, so it is necessary to prepare by turning on the right turn signal in advance. Also, when turning right, you need to be careful about vehicles going straight on the left side of the road or vehicles turning left on the opposite side of the road.
