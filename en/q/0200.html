200. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following guidelines for the protection of pedestrians is NOT correct?

  1. ①When a pedestrian is using the crosswalk, the driver must make a temporary stop before the crosswalk.
  2. ②The driver shall not impede the passage of pedestrians who are crossing the road according to the signals or directions given by a police officer.
  3. ③A driver shall not impede the passage of pedestrians who are crossing the road at an intersection.
  4. ④When a pedestrian is crossing a road without a crosswalk, the driver must drive slowly while maintaining a safe distance.
Show answer

Answer
④ When a pedestrian is crossing a road without a crosswalk, the driver must drive slowly while maintaining a safe distance.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 27 (Protection of Pedestrians) ① When pedestrians (including drivers of bicycles, etc. who get off their bicycles and drag or carry their bicycles as prescribed in Article 13-2 (6)) are passing or are about to pass on a crosswalk, the driver of any car or tram must temporarily stop in front of the crosswalk (referring to the stop line where a stop line is installed) so as not to interfere with or endanger the crossing of pedestrians. ② When the driver of any car or streetcar intends to turn left or right at an intersection where traffic is being controlled, he or she must not obstruct the passage of pedestrians crossing the road in accordance with signals or instructions from traffic signals or police officials. ③ Drivers of all vehicles must not obstruct the passage of pedestrians crossing roads at or near intersections where traffic control is not in place. ⑤ When a pedestrian is crossing a road on which a crosswalk is not installed pursuant to Article 10 (3), the driver of any car or streetcar must temporarily stop at a safe distance to allow the pedestrian to cross safely.
