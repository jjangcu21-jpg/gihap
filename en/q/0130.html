130. What penalty is a driver subjected to if caught by the police while driving in a physical state of being unable to drive normally (due to illness or fatigue)?

  1. ①Administrative fine
  2. ②Suspension of the driver's license
  3. ③Detention or criminal fine
  4. ④No penalty
Show answer

Answer
③ Detention or criminal fine

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 154 (Penalties): A fine of up to 300,000 won or detention is imposed. No. 3: A person who, in violation of Article 45, drives a car, etc. or a streetcar in conditions where there is a risk of not being able to drive normally due to overwork or illness (however, cases of driving a personal mobility device are excluded)
