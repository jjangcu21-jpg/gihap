73. According to the Motor Vehicle Management Act, what is the registration that needs to be done when the ownership of a car is changed through inheritance or other means?

  1. ①New registration
  2. ②Transfer of ownership registration
  3. ③Modification registration
  4. ④Cancellation registration
Show answer

Answer
② Transfer of ownership registration

Reference
Article 12 of the Automobile Management Act When the ownership of a car is changed through sale, inheritance, public sale, auction, etc., the transferee must register the transfer of ownership within the legal deadline.
