859. Which of the following are the TWO safest ways to proceed in the given situation?

  1. ①Overtake the black vehicle on the right.
  2. ②Drive side by side in the same lane as the black vehicle.
  3. ③Immediately switch to the third lane to allow the motorcycle to exit toward Goyang.
  4. ④Slow down and maintain a safe distance because the white vehicle may change lanes.
  5. ⑤Drive up to the section with the white broken line where lane changes are allowed, and then enter the third lane.
Show answer

Answer
④ Slow down and maintain a safe distance because the white vehicle may change lanes.
⑤ Drive up to the section with the white broken line where lane changes are allowed, and then enter the third lane.

Reference
③ In the case of a double white line consisting of one solid and one broken line, it is installed at a necessary point within a section or place where roads separate and merge. This means that cars can change course on the side with the dotted line, but that change of course is restricted on the side with the solid line.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
