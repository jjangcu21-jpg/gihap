982. Which of the following driver behaviors constitutes a violation of the Road Traffic Act? (Refer to the website)

Video questions play in the Gihap app or on the official site.

Official video ↗
  1. ①The driver turns on the turn signal to indicate the direction.
  2. ②The driver approaches the right edge of the road in advance to turn right.
  3. ③The driver exercises caution to avoid colliding with the car in front.
  4. ④The driver turns right in accordance with the traffic signal ahead.
Show answer

Answer
④ The driver turns right in accordance with the traffic signal ahead.

Reference
The main character driver used the turn signal in accordance with the Road Traffic Act. Also, in order to turn right according to the intersection traffic method, I used the right lane of the road in advance. Of course, to prevent a collision with the car in front, I drove slowly and ensured an appropriate distance between cars, and there was no collision. However, the main character driver had to stop at the stop line just before the crosswalk when the car's red light turned on, but he followed the car that was turning right first in front. This is a violation of the obligation to follow signals and instructions under Article 5 of the Road Traffic Act. When there are vehicles trying to turn right one after another, when the first vehicle stops temporarily, whether the second vehicle must also stop temporarily is as follows. According to the National Police Agency inquiry response (June 8, 2023), in accordance with Article 27, Paragraph 1 of the Road Traffic Act and the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act [Appendix 2], all vehicles are required to stop when there is a red light ahead. Therefore, after the first vehicle (A) stops temporarily, the second vehicle (B) that follows immediately must also stop temporarily.
