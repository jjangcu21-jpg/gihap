993. The driver is traveling on a road with one lane in one direction. Which of the following is a dangerous situation? (Refer to the website)

Video questions play in the Gihap app or on the official site.

Official video ↗
  1. ①The driver caused the trailing car to overtake by maintaining a safe distance from the agricultural machine ahead.
  2. ②The driver caused the trailing car to overtake by quickly decelerating and driving slowly.
  3. ③On local roads, the detection of agricultural machines passing or crossing roads may be delayed.
  4. ④The driver obstructed the trailing car from overtaking safely.
Show answer

Answer
③ On local roads, the detection of agricultural machines passing or crossing roads may be delayed.

Reference
Paragraph ③ of Article 13 of the Road Traffic Act: ‘The driver of a car or horse must drive on the right side of the center (if a center line is installed, refers to the center line; hereinafter the same shall apply) of the road (referring to the roadway in the case of a road with a separate sidewalk and roadway).’ In the video, the driver in question is driving on the right side of the center line, and the driver of the car or horse following him must also follow this. Therefore, since the driver is traveling in compliance with road traffic laws, it cannot be said that the driver's keeping a safe distance and driving slowly caused the driver of the following car to violate the center line. The drivers of the cars following failed to comply with Article 13 of the Road Traffic Act, violated the center line, and committed overtaking, which is prohibited in principle. When driving on local roads with bends or hills, agricultural machinery may not be easily spotted, so you must slow down.
