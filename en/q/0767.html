767. Which of the following descriptions regarding the compulsory disposition of vehicles that obstruct emergency fire engine traffic are incorrect? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Illegally parked vehicles that interfere with firefighting activities can be moved.
  2. ②Parked vehicles within parking lines that interfere with firefighting activities can be removed.
  3. ③Objects that obstruct the passage of firefighting vehicles can be moved or removed.
  4. ④The driver of a vehicle that has been towed due to illegal parking may claim compensation for any losses.
  5. ⑤The driver of a vehicle parked within a designated parking space but has been towed may claim compensation for damages.
Show answer

Answer
④ The driver of a vehicle that has been towed due to illegal parking may claim compensation for any losses.
⑤ The driver of a vehicle parked within a designated parking space but has been towed may claim compensation for damages.

Reference
Article 25 of the Framework Act on Fire Services (Compulsory Dispositions, etc.) ② When the fire department chief, fire chief or fire chief deems it urgent to rescue people or prevent a fire from spreading, he/she may take measures pursuant to paragraph (1) on fire-fighting objects and land other than fire-fighting objects or land pursuant to paragraph (1). ③ When the fire chief, fire chief, or fire chief is urgently dispatched for fire-fighting activities, he/she may remove or move parked or stopped vehicles and objects that interfere with the passage of fire trucks and fire-fighting activities. ④ The head of the fire headquarters, fire chief or fire brigade may request support for towing vehicles and manpower from relevant organizations, such as the competent local government, in order to remove or move parked or stopped vehicles that interfere with firefighting activities under paragraph 3, and the head of the relevant organization in receipt of the request shall cooperate therewith unless there is a justifiable reason. ⑤ The city/provincial governor may pay expenses to those who provide towing vehicles, manpower, etc. pursuant to paragraph 4, as prescribed by city/provincial ordinance. Article 49-2 (Compensation for Loss) Paragraph 1 3. A person who has suffered a loss due to a disposition pursuant to Article 25 Paragraph 2 or Paragraph 3. However, in cases under paragraph 3, compensation excludes losses involving unlawful obstruction of fire-truck passage or firefighting activities.
