404. Which among the following is permitted to use the exclusive bus lane on a general roadway?

  1. ①A 12-seater passenger vehicle carrying 6 passengers
  2. ②A passenger vehicle for domestic tourists that is transporting 25 tourists
  3. ③A 12-seater commuter passenger vehicle carrying employees on designated routes
  4. ④A taxi temporarily using the lane so that passengers can board/leave the taxi
Show answer

Answer
④ A taxi temporarily using the lane so that passengers can board/leave the taxi

Reference
When a taxi temporarily passes to pick up or drop off a passenger to the extent that it does not impede the passage of vehicles in the exclusive lane. In this case, the taxi driver must leave the dedicated lane as soon as the passenger gets on or off. (Road Traffic Act Enforcement Decree Article 10, Paragraph 2, Annex Table 1 of the same Enforcement Decree)
