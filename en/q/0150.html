150. Which of the following is an act of reckless driving for which you can be penalized?

  1. ①Stopping or parking on the roadside of an expressway
  2. ②Driving while under the influence of alcohol
  3. ③Driving in an exclusive lane on a general road
  4. ④Driving over the centerline
Show answer

Answer
④ Driving over the centerline

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 46-3 (Prohibition of Reckless Driving) Violation of signals or instructions, violation of center line, violation of speed, violation of prohibition on crossing, U-turn, and reversing, failure to secure safety distance, violation of prohibition on changing lanes, violation of prohibition on sudden braking, violation of overtaking rules or the prohibition on obstructing overtaking, generation of noise without justifiable reasons, violation of overtaking rules on expressways, prohibition on crossing, U-turn, and reverse on highways, etc.
