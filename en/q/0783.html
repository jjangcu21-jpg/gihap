783. Which of the following are the anticipated hazards when driving through an intersection in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①The black vehicle in lane 3 can turn right.
  2. ②The white vehicle in lane 2 can make a sudden shift to lane 1.
  3. ③A jaywalking pedestrian may suddenly appear from behind the bridge pier.
  4. ④All vehicles must stop since a pedestrian may try to cross the street at the last second.
  5. ⑤The vehicle behind may try to overtake you.
Show answer

Answer
② The white vehicle in lane 2 can make a sudden shift to lane 1.
③ A jaywalking pedestrian may suddenly appear from behind the bridge pier.

Reference
In an environment where bridge piers are installed on the road, you must drive while anticipating dangers such as motorcycles and pedestrians entering from the left and right sides of the bridge pier.
