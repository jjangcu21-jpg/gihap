643. Which of the following is NOT a correct description of towing a vehicle according to the Road Traffic Act?

  1. ①A driver may tow a two-wheeled vehicle using a large towing vehicle with a Class 1 driver’s license for large motor vehicles.
  2. ②A driver may drive a special vehicle for towing that weighs 3.5 tons or less with a small tow truck license.
  3. ③A driver may tow a broken down passenger car using a 10-ton truck with a Class 1 driver’s license for ordinary motor vehicles.
  4. ④A driver may tow a 1,500 kg vehicle using a 5,000 kg vehicle driving at 30 km/h.
Show answer

Answer
① A driver may tow a two-wheeled vehicle using a large towing vehicle with a Class 1 driver’s license for large motor vehicles.

Reference
■ Explanation Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 18] - Large tow trucks, small tow trucks, and rescue vehicles cannot be driven with a Class 1 large-sized license. - Large tow vehicle: Special towing vehicle, a vehicle that can be driven with a Type 2 regular license - Small towing vehicle: A special towing vehicle with a gross weight of 3.5 tons or less, a vehicle that can be driven with a Type 2 regular license - A towed vehicle can be towed by a car that can be driven by a person holding a Type 1 large license, Type 1 regular license, or Type 2 regular license (excluding two-wheeled vehicles under Article 3 of the Automobile Management Act). In this case, in order to tow a towed vehicle weighing up to 3 tons and exceeding 750 kilograms in total weight, you must have a license to drive the towing vehicle and a small tow vehicle license or a large tow vehicle license. To tow a towed vehicle exceeding 3 tons, you must have a license to drive the towing vehicle and a large tow vehicle license.
