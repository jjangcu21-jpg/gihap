531. How much is the fine for leaving a passenger car parked for 2 hours or more at an intersection, a crosswalk, or a railroad crossing (excluding child, elderly, or disabled protection zones)?

  1. ①KRW 40,000
  2. ②KRW 50,000
  3. ③KRW 60,000
  4. ④KRW 70,000
Show answer

Answer
② KRW 50,000

Reference
According to Table 6 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act, a fine of 50,000 won is imposed on employers, etc. of passenger cars parked at intersections, crosswalks, level crossings, or on the sidewalks of roads separated from sidewalks and roadways for two hours or more.
