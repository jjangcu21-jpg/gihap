781. Which of the following are the safe ways to drive when you are trying to go straight in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Do not enter the intersection until the school bus has departed.
  2. ②Since the school bus is stopped, you should pass it on the left.
  3. ③Follow the hand signals of the school bus driver and pass it on the left.
  4. ④Enter the intersection and wait behind the school bus.
  5. ⑤Be prepared for pedestrians who may appear behind the oncoming truck.
Show answer

Answer
① Do not enter the intersection until the school bus has departed.
⑤ Be prepared for pedestrians who may appear behind the oncoming truck.

Reference
Article 13, Paragraph 3 of the Road Traffic Act. Drivers of cars and horses must drive on the right side of the center (if a center line is installed, refer to the center line; hereinafter the same shall apply) of the road (referring to the roadway in the case of a road with a separate sidewalk and roadway). Appendix 2 of the Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules. The flashing yellow light means that ‘vehicles and horses can proceed while paying attention to other traffic or safety signs’, so you must pay attention and proceed even when the children’s school bus in front has departed and can enter the intersection. Article 51 of the Road Traffic Act. ① When a children's school bus is stopped on the road and devices such as flashing lights are in operation to indicate that children or infants are getting on or off, the driver of a car traveling in the lane where the children's school bus is stopped and the lane immediately next to the lane must stop temporarily before reaching the children's school bus, check for safety, and then drive slowly. ② In the case of paragraph 1, on roads without a center line or with one lane in each direction, drivers of cars traveling in the opposite direction must also stop temporarily and check for safety before reaching the children's school bus and then drive slowly. ③ Drivers of any vehicle may not overtake a children's school bus traveling on the road with a sign indicating that it is carrying children or infants.
