638. Which of the following is NOT a correct description of towing a vehicle?

  1. ①When towing a vehicle weighing over three tons, the driver must possess a driver’s license to operate a tow truck and a class 1 large towing vehicle license.
  2. ②When a motor vehicle with gross weight of less than 2,000 kilograms is being towed by another vehicle whose gross weight is at least thrice that of the towed vehicle, it may travel at a speed of up to 30 kilometers per hour on ordinary roads.
  3. ③When towing a vehicle on a normal road using a vehicle that is not a tow truck, the driver can drive at a speed equal to the speed limit.
  4. ④When towing a vehicle using a vehicle that is not a tow truck, the driver must possess a driver's license for that vehicle.
Show answer

Answer
③ When towing a vehicle on a normal road using a vehicle that is not a tow truck, the driver can drive at a speed equal to the speed limit.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules, Article 19: On expressways with at least two lanes per direction, the maximum speed is 100 km/h and minimum 50 km/h. The maximum is 80 km/h for trucks with payload over 1.5 tonnes, special-purpose vehicles, vehicles carrying dangerous goods under Appendix 9 note 6, and construction machinery. Article 20: When a vehicle other than a towing vehicle tows another vehicle on roads other than expressways, the limit is 30 km/h if the towed vehicle has gross weight below 2,000 kg and the towing vehicle weighs at least three times as much; in other cases, including towing by a two-wheeled vehicle, it is 25 km/h. Appendix 18: A holder of a Class 1 large, Class 1 ordinary or Class 2 ordinary license may tow using a vehicle that license permits, excluding two-wheeled vehicles under Automobile Management Act Article 3. For a towed vehicle with gross weight over 750 kg and up to 3 tonnes, a license for the towing vehicle plus a small- or large-trailer towing license is required. Above 3 tonnes, a license for the towing vehicle plus a large-trailer towing license is required.
