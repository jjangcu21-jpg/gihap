309. What are the criminal penalties under the Act on the Aggravated Punishment, etc. of Specific Crimes for causing injury to a child in a child protection zone while driving?

  1. ①Imprisonment with labor for at least 1 year but not more than 15 years or a criminal fine of at least KRW 5 million but not more than KRW 30 million
  2. ②Imprisonment with labor (or for an indefinite term) for at least 5 years
  3. ③Imprisonment with labor for not more than 2 years or a criminal fine of not more than KRW 5 million
  4. ④Imprisonment with labor for not more than 5 years or a criminal fine of not more than KRW 20 million
Show answer

Answer
① Imprisonment with labor for at least 1 year but not more than 15 years or a criminal fine of at least KRW 5 million but not more than KRW 30 million

Reference
Article 5-13 of the Act on the Aggravated Punishment of Specific Crimes, Article 12 (3) of the Road Traffic Act, measures pursuant to Paragraph 1 of the same Article must be observed in child protection areas, and in case of injury according to Article 5-13 of the Act on the Aggravated Punishment of Specific Crimes, etc., imprisonment for not less than 1 year but not more than 15 years or a fine of not less than 5 million won but not more than 30 million won.
