787. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Stay as close as possible to the car ahead to pass through the busy intersection.
  2. ②Be prepared for a vehicle in the third lane to switch to the left lane suddenly.
  3. ③Enter the intersection to maintain smooth traffic flow, regardless of whether the vehicles ahead have stopped.
  4. ④Turn on the hazard lights and safely overtake by moving into the opposite first lane, and then continue straight ahead.
  5. ⑤If it appears that you may not pass through the intersection at the green light, stop just before the stop line.
Show answer

Answer
② Be prepared for a vehicle in the third lane to switch to the left lane suddenly.
⑤ If it appears that you may not pass through the intersection at the green light, stop just before the stop line.

Reference
No stopping zone signs at intersections indicate frequent congestion due to high traffic volume. Even if the signal is green, you must not forcefully tailgate and become stuck in the intersection, blocking the flow of other vehicles. If you expect congestion at the intersection you are entering, be a leisurely driver who stops before the stop line and starts at the next green light. You also need to be careful about vehicles that suddenly change direction from lane 3 ahead to the empty second lane.
