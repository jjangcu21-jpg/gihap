296. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is NOT true about school zones?

  1. ①The traffic penalty for a parking violation is the same as that for the silver zone.
  2. ②A "slow" sign can be installed in school zones.
  3. ③Parking and stopping are prohibited within a school zone.
  4. ④One will face a criminal penalty for causing an accident that leaves a child injured, regardless of any settlements.
Show answer

Answer
① The traffic penalty for a parking violation is the same as that for the silver zone.

Reference
① Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules Article 93 Paragraph 2 [Appendix 10] ② Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 6] 520 ③ Road Traffic Act Article 32 Paragraph 8 Stopping and parking are prohibited in child protection zones. ④ Proviso to Article 3, Paragraph 2 of the Special Act on Traffic Accidents: If an accident occurs within a child protection zone, criminal punishment will be imposed regardless of whether there was agreement or not.
