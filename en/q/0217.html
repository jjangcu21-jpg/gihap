217. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is correct regarding a change of lane?

  1. ①Because it can be dangerous on a bridge, a change of lane is often restricted by solid white lines.
  2. ②Broken white lines mark traffic lanes where a change of lane is limited.
  3. ③Do not change lanes in an area where lane change is prohibited, even if there is roadwork or other such obstructions that prevent you from traveling further.
  4. ④It is not against the law to change lanes in an area where lane change is prohibited, provided that it is done safely.
Show answer

Answer
① Because it can be dangerous on a bridge, a change of lane is often restricted by solid white lines.

Reference
If you cannot proceed due to damage to the road, etc., you must change lanes and drive. In places where changing lanes is prohibited, even if you change lanes safely, it is a violation of the law. Lane change prohibition lines are indicated with solid lines.
