541. Which of the following statements regarding the criteria for vehicle types with access to exclusive bus lanes on expressways is correct?

  1. ①11-seater vans with at least 6 occupants.
  2. ②9-seater cars with at least 5 occupants.
  3. ③7-seater cars with at least 6 occupants.
  4. ④All vans regardless of the number of passengers.
Show answer

Answer
① 11-seater vans with at least 6 occupants.

Reference
Cars that can travel in the highway bus-only lanes in Appendix 1 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act are passenger cars and vans with a seating capacity of 9 or more. However, passenger cars and vans with 9 or more seats and 12 or less seats are allowed to pass only if there are 6 or more people on board.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
