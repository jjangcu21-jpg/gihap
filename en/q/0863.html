863. Which of the following are the safe ways for the bicycle rider to cross in the given location? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Stay on the bicycle while waiting for the crosswalk light to turn green, and cross the street while riding the bicycle.
  2. ②Quickly cross the street as other bicycle riders are crossing as well.
  3. ③Get off the bicycle and wait in a safe location to prevent collision with other bicycle riders.
  4. ④In the case of children or senior persons riding a bicycle, they must cross the street when the crosswalk light is green.
  5. ⑤When the crosswalk light turns green, the bicycle rider must walk his/her bicycle to cross the street.
Show answer

Answer
③ Get off the bicycle and wait in a safe location to prevent collision with other bicycle riders.
⑤ When the crosswalk light turns green, the bicycle rider must walk his/her bicycle to cross the street.

Reference
Article 13-2 (Special Provisions for Passage Methods for Bicycles, etc.) Paragraph 6. When a bicycle rider crosses the road using a crosswalk, he or she must get off the bicycle and walk while pushing or carrying the bicycle.
