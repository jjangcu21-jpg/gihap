14. A person who fails the road driving test may retake the test ( ) after the date of failure. Which of the following belongs in ( )?

  1. ①1 day
  2. ②3 days
  3. ③5 days
  4. ④7 days
Show answer

Answer
② 3 days

Reference
In accordance with Article 49, Paragraph 4 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act, a person who fails the road driving test may retake the road driving test three days after the date of failure.
