394. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is a correct statement regarding the speed of motor vehicles?

  1. ①The minimum speed limit on expressways is 50 km/h.
  2. ②Automobile roads have a maximum speed limit, but no minimum speed limit.
  3. ③There are maximum and minimum speed limits on normal roads.
  4. ④The maximum speed limit on expressways with more than two lanes in one direction is the same for all types of vehicles.
Show answer

Answer
① The minimum speed limit on expressways is 50 km/h.

Reference
The minimum speed on highways is set at 50 kilometers per hour on all highways, and both maximum and minimum speeds are limited on automobile-only roads. There is no minimum speed limit on general roads, and the maximum speed on highways with two or more lanes each way is regulated differently depending on the vehicle type.
