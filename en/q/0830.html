830. Which of the following are the TWO most appropriate ways to proceed in the given situation?

  1. ①Drive as usual since there is not much snow.
  2. ②Be prepared in case a truck skids and spins on the snowy road.
  3. ③Drive 10% slower than the maximum speed limit.
  4. ④Turn on the hazard lights and drive slowly.
  5. ⑤Slow down by driving in a zigzag pattern.
Show answer

Answer
② Be prepared in case a truck skids and spins on the snowy road.
④ Turn on the hazard lights and drive slowly.

Reference
Slowdown regulations apply depending on weather conditions. Cases where driving must be reduced by 20/100 of the maximum speed are when the road surface is wet due to rain or when less than 20 millimeters of snow has accumulated. Cases where driving is required to be driven at a speed reduced by 50/100 of the maximum speed is when visibility is 100 meters or less due to heavy rain, heavy snow, or fog, when the road surface is frozen, or when snow has accumulated 20 mm or more. This is the case (Article 19, Paragraph 2 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act).
