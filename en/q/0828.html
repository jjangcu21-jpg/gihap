828. You are about to reenter the expressway after taking a break at a rest area. Which of the following are the TWO safest ways to proceed in the given situation?

  1. ①Stop briefly and merge into the highway only when there are no approaching vehicles.
  2. ②Accelerate quickly and cut across directly into the first lane.
  3. ③Check your left and merge after gaining sufficient speed.
  4. ④Continue driving on the shoulder.
  5. ⑤Do not interfere with the flow of other vehicles.
Show answer

Answer
③ Check your left and merge after gaining sufficient speed.
⑤ Do not interfere with the flow of other vehicles.

Reference
Article 65 of the Road Traffic Act: When entering a highway, drivers of automobiles (excluding emergency vehicles) must not obstruct the passage of other automobiles traveling on the highway. Additionally, since the speed of vehicles traveling on the highway is very fast, they must enter the highway with sufficient acceleration.
