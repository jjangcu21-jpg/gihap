816. Which of the following are the TWO most likely risk factors for a traffic accident in the given situation?

  1. ①Truck A attempting to back up
  2. ②Pedestrian B crossing behind the truck
  3. ③Vehicle C approaching from the rear
  4. ④Pedestrian D walking on the sidewalk
  5. ⑤Vehicle E attempting to turn right from the left-hand side
Show answer

Answer
① Truck A attempting to back up
② Pedestrian B crossing behind the truck

Reference
Drivers planning to pass through complex side roads need to pay attention to whether there are any hazards around pedestrians hidden by vehicles or couriers and cargo vehicles where cargo is frequently loaded and unloaded.
