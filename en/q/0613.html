613. Which of the following is the LEAST appropriate method of loading freight onto a truck?

  1. ①Freight is to be loaded starting at the center of the freight container to the left and right.
  2. ②The center of gravity of a truck is in the front, so freight should be loaded starting from the back.
  3. ③Relatively heavier freight is to be loaded on the bottom of the freight container.
  4. ④After loading all of the freight, fix the freight so that it does not fall out of the truck.
Show answer

Answer
② The center of gravity of a truck is in the front, so freight should be loaded starting from the back.

Reference
Among the cargo transporter qualification test textbooks, cargo handling tips, when loading cargo into the cargo container, load it in a balanced manner so that the weight does not shift towards the front or back.
