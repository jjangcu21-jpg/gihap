758. Which of the following are true about traffic safety signs in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①The "30" marked on the pavement indicates that the maximum speed limit on the road is 30 km/h.
  2. ②The yellow crosswalk markings are incorrect, as all crosswalks should be marked in white.
  3. ③Zigzag solid white lines indicate that you should slow down, and lane changes are allowed in those sections.
  4. ④An indication sign attached to the traffic light means that there is a crosswalk.
  5. ⑤Asphalt paved in red is used only in school zones.
Show answer

Answer
① The "30" marked on the pavement indicates that the maximum speed limit on the road is 30 km/h.
④ An indication sign attached to the traffic light means that there is a crosswalk.

Reference
30 on the road surface indicates the maximum speed limit, crosswalks can be marked in yellow in child protection zones, a zigzag-shaped solid white line indicates both a restriction on changing directions and a sign of slowing down, and a crosswalk sign displayed on a vehicle signal indicates that there is a crosswalk ahead, and red asphalt is used not only in child protection zones, but also in senior protection zones and disabled protection zones.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
