663. Which of the following is the most desirable action for a recovery vehicle driver?

  1. ①Reach the location of a vehicle breakdown as soon as possible and tow the vehicle immediately.
  2. ②When towing a vehicle, obey the laws and safely proceed with towing.
  3. ③Drive safely only when the towing cost by distance is expensive.
  4. ④Ignore signals while on the way to the location of a breakdown.
Show answer

Answer
② When towing a vehicle, obey the laws and safely proceed with towing.

Reference
Rescue truck drivers must respond quickly, but must drive in compliance with the laws and not ignore the owner's opinions or damage the accident scene.
