598. Which of the following is NOT true about an FCEV?

  1. ①When the vehicle catches fire, quickly evacuate and move far from the fire for safety.
  2. ②If the hydrogen leak detector turns on, immediately park in a safe location and turn off the engine.
  3. ③The driver of an FCEV is not required to take safety education.
  4. ④The driver of an FCEV can manipulate equipment at the hydrogen charging station.
Show answer

Answer
④ The driver of an FCEV can manipulate equipment at the hydrogen charging station.

Reference
Only drivers who plan to engage in large hydrogen vans (capacity of 36 or more passengers) must complete safety training (special training). Hydrogen vehicle hydrogen refueling station facilities must not be operated arbitrarily by drivers.
