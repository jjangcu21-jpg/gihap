219. Which of the following is correct regarding an acceleration lane on an expressway?

  1. ①An acceleration lane allows vehicles on an expressway to change lanes so that they can take an exit.
  2. ②An acceleration lane allows the vehicles entering an expressway to increase their speed.
  3. ③An acceleration lane allows increasing speed for vehicles trying to overtake other vehicles on an expressway.
  4. ④An acceleration lane is designed to lessen the effects of the lowered speed of large vehicles when driving uphill.
Show answer

Answer
② An acceleration lane allows the vehicles entering an expressway to increase their speed.

Reference
A speed change lane refers to an additional lane installed to safely decelerate or accelerate without interfering with the driving of other high-speed vehicles on the main road when a high-speed vehicle slows down and enters another road, or a low-speed vehicle flows into a group of high-speed vehicles. In general, the former is called a deceleration lane, and the latter is an acceleration lane [Commentary on Rules for Road Structure and Facility Standards (2020), Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport, p.40].
