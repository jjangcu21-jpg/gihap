742. Which of the following pairings of information from the given situation and safe driving methods are incorrect? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Falling objects on the road - Turn on the hazard lights to alert oncoming traffic of the hazard.
  2. ②First lane - Passenger vehicle drivers are allowed to overtake on weekdays.
  3. ③Colored guide lines on the third lane - This lane is used as an exclusive bus lane on weekdays.
  4. ④Rest area sign - Indicates a rest area ahead on the right.
  5. ⑤A road with three lanes in one direction - Truck drivers can travel in the first lane to overtake.
Show answer

Answer
③ Colored guide lines on the third lane - This lane is used as an exclusive bus lane on weekdays.
⑤ A road with three lanes in one direction - Truck drivers can travel in the first lane to overtake.

Reference
The green lines installed in lane 3 are not lanes but color guide lines on the road. The road color guide line is a line drawn along the middle of the lane to guide the driving direction of the car. Freight vehicles must travel in the right lane (lane 3 in the photo) on highways with three or more lanes each way, and may use the immediately adjacent lane to the left when overtaking (Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 9]).
