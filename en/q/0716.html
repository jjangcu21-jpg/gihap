716. In the given situation, which of the following are the two safest ways of driving?

  1. ①Since it is darker than during the day at sunset, turn on the high beams and sound the horn continuously while driving.
  2. ②Let the vehicles coming from the opposite direction pass so as to secure a clear field of vision, and then overtake quickly.
  3. ③Keep a safe distance from the truck ahead as sudden or unexpected situations may require the truck to make an emergency stop.
  4. ④Do not overtake as overtaking is prohibited due to the solid centerline.
  5. ⑤Sound the horn to incite the vehicle ahead to go faster.
Show answer

Answer
③ Keep a safe distance from the truck ahead as sudden or unexpected situations may require the truck to make an emergency stop.
④ Do not overtake as overtaking is prohibited due to the solid centerline.

Reference
When following a vehicle in front traveling in the same direction, the driver of any vehicle must ensure the necessary distance to avoid a collision with the vehicle in front if the vehicle in front suddenly stops (Article 19, Paragraph 1 of the Road Traffic Act). In particular, when driving behind a large cargo vehicle, it is necessary to leave a sufficient safety distance because it is difficult to secure forward visibility and an unexpected situation may occur. In addition, overtaking is prohibited in sections where the center line is installed as a solid line (Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules [Appendix 6] Center Line Marking No. 501). On the other hand, continuous use of high beam lights and horns may cause danger by obstructing the view of other drivers or generating unnecessary noise.
