572. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following is NOT true about drivers of personal mobility devices (PMDs)?

  1. ①A driver's license is not required to operate a PMD.
  2. ②It is not allowed to have more riders than the permitted capacity on a PMD.
  3. ③Drivers of PMDs must wear protective gear to operate the device.
  4. ④Drivers of PMDs must use the bicycle lane if there is any.
Show answer

Answer
① A driver's license is not required to operate a PMD.

Reference
Personal mobility devices are a part of motorized bicycles, so those who wish to drive them must obtain at least a motorcycle license (Article 2, Paragraph 19-2, Article 80 of the Road Traffic Act).
