41. Which TWO items should you check before getting in a vehicle?

  1. ①Condition of tire wear
  2. ②Whether there are obstacles in front of or behind your car
  3. ③Whether the dashboard readings are working properly
  4. ④Whether the brake pedal works normally
Show answer

Answer
① Condition of tire wear
② Whether there are obstacles in front of or behind your car

Reference
Whether the instrument panel and brake pedal work correctly is checked from the driver's seat after getting into the vehicle.
