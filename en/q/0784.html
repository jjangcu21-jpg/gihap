784. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①The traffic light is green, so you can speed up and quickly drive straight through the intersection.
  2. ②Turn right from the second lane with the hazard lights on to alert oncoming traffic.
  3. ③Drive cautiously, anticipating a hazard as a vehicle on the third lane ahead may suddenly switch to the left lane.
  4. ④Since the vehicle behind you is tailgating, increase your speed quickly to create a safe distance and swiftly pass through the intersection.
  5. ⑤Gradually increase your speed to pass through the intersection while keeping your eyes open for surrounding traffic.
Show answer

Answer
③ Drive cautiously, anticipating a hazard as a vehicle on the third lane ahead may suddenly switch to the left lane.
⑤ Gradually increase your speed to pass through the intersection while keeping your eyes open for surrounding traffic.

Reference
There may be vehicles driving straight ahead while suddenly changing course from the front of the first or third lane to the second lane, and it is difficult to check the road conditions on the left or right side of the intersection due to vehicles stopped in the first or third lane, making it difficult to spot vehicles driving in violation of the signal. Therefore, it is a good idea to get into the habit of driving slowly and checking the intersection situation with your eyes immediately after the signal changes before passing. Also, if you want to turn right, the safest way to drive is to turn right in the right turn lane. You must not change course on the solid white line.
