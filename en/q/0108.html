108. Under the Road Traffic Act, which of the following describes the safest attitude for a person driving a personal mobility device?

  1. ①When passing on a sidewalk, drive away from pedestrians.
  2. ②When driving after drinking alcohol, special attention is required.
  3. ③If there are a crosswalk and a bicycle crossing, use the bicycle crossing.
  4. ④When driving the device on a crosswalk, avoid pedestrians on the crosswalk.
Show answer

Answer
③ If there are a crosswalk and a bicycle crossing, use the bicycle crossing.

Reference
Article 15-2 of the Road Traffic Act (Installation of bicycle crossing roads). When crossing a road with a separate bicycle crossing on a bicycle (bicycle or personal mobility device), you must use the bicycle crossing. Article 13-2 of the Road Traffic Act (Special Provisions for Passage Methods for Bicycles, etc.) When the driver of a personal mobility device crosses the road using a crosswalk, he or she must dismount and drag or walk while holding the vehicle.
