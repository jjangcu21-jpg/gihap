514. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, what is the traffic penalty imposed on the driver of an automobile parked within 5 m of the transmission valve of a fire department standpipe used for fire-extinguishing purposes?

  1. ①KRW 40,000
  2. ②KRW 30,000
  3. ③KRW 20,000
  4. ④No penalty is imposed.
Show answer

Answer
① KRW 40,000

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Decree Annex 8 3-3 (Safety Signs Installed) Passenger Cars 80,000 won / 29 (Safety Signs Not Installed) Passenger Cars 40,000 won
