52. Which of the following is NOT true about a driver-assistance system?

  1. ①Lane Following Assist (LFA) is a system designed to assist the driver in driving to stay within the lane on the road.
  2. ②Adaptive Smart Cruise Control (ASCC) is a system that automatically adjusts the vehicle speed to maintain a safe distance from vehicles ahead.
  3. ③Active Blind Spot Detection (ABSD) is a system that assists drivers in changing lanes by warning them of vehicles in their blind spots.
  4. ④Autonomous Emergency Braking (AEB) is a system that prevents the wheels from locking up and reduces the braking distance.
Show answer

Answer
④ Autonomous Emergency Braking (AEB) is a system that prevents the wheels from locking up and reduces the braking distance.

Reference
There are various advanced car functions for safety, such as LFA, ASCC, ABSD, and AEB, and there are some that the driver can select depending on the car purchase option, and the driver can also choose to disable certain functions as needed while driving. AEB is a function that automatically applies emergency braking when the driver fails to apply the brakes in a dangerous situation or brakes too weakly to avoid a collision.
