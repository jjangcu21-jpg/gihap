148. A driver of a motor vehicle (excluding personal mobility devices) threatening another person by repeatedly committing a series of two or more of the acts below will be punished for reckless driving. Which of the following is NOT an example of reckless driving?

  1. ①Not keeping the eyes forward; operating a video display device while driving
  2. ②Exceeding the speed limit while overtaking a car on his/her right side
  3. ③Not securing sufficient safe distance and repeating sudden braking
  4. ④Exceeding the speed limit to obstruct a trailing car that is attempting to pass by
Show answer

Answer
① Not keeping the eyes forward; operating a video display device while driving

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 46-3 (Prohibition of Reckless Driving) Violation of signals or instructions, violation of center line, violation of speed, violation of prohibition on crossing, U-turn, and reversing, failure to secure safety distance, violation of prohibition on changing lanes, violation of prohibition on sudden braking, violation of overtaking rules or the prohibition on obstructing overtaking, generation of noise without justifiable reasons, violation of overtaking rules on expressways, prohibition on crossing, U-turn, and reverse on highways, etc.
