627. Which of the following is NOT true about the driving characteristics of large vehicles?

  1. ①Despite having a great level of stability due to their heavy weight and long wheelbase, the steering recovery is slow for large vehicles compared to small vehicles.
  2. ②Because the driver’s seat in a large vehicle is located higher than that of a small vehicle, the driver drives by looking only at the immediate front of the vehicle, so the stability of straight-line travel is improved.
  3. ③Cornering characteristics change significantly depending on the type and load placement of the cargo.
  4. ④The stability of a large vehicle varies significantly depending on the type and load placement of the cargo.
Show answer

Answer
② Because the driver’s seat in a large vehicle is located higher than that of a small vehicle, the driver drives by looking only at the immediate front of the vehicle, so the stability of straight-line travel is improved.

Reference
In large vehicles, when driving at high speeds, the body shakes and the straight-line stability may momentarily deteriorate. Moreover, because the driver's seat is high, when the weather is bad such as at night or in heavy rain or fog, the driver is forced to look directly in front of the car, which further deteriorates straight-line stability. In order to increase straight-line stability, you must place your gaze as far away as possible and use caution when operating the steering wheel.
