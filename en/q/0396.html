396. Which of the following statements regarding the centerline on a road is correct?

  1. ①Overtaking is allowed if the center is marked with a yellow solid line.
  2. ②The centerline of a reversible lane is the leftmost yellow broken line in the direction of traffic indicated by traffic signals.
  3. ③A bus stopped on a provincial road with one lane in one direction can be overtaken by crossing the yellow solid centerline.
  4. ④A centerline can be installed on roads at least 4.75 m wide.
Show answer

Answer
② The centerline of a reversible lane is the leftmost yellow broken line in the direction of traffic indicated by traffic signals.

Reference
Road Traffic Act, Article 2 item 5 and Article 13; Enforcement Rules, Appendix 6 item 501: Neither a double nor a single solid center line may be crossed. You must not overtake a bus stopped for passengers to board or alight. A center line is installed where the roadway is at least 6 metres wide.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
