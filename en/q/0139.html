139. If a passenger vehicle driver responds to a lane-change dispute by suddenly braking in front of the pursuing vehicle, leading to a subsequent collision with an unaware cargo truck and resulting in injury to the cargo truck driver, legal repercussions may arise under the provisions of the Criminal Act for engaging in retaliatory driving. What is the appropriate punishment standard for this? (The cargo truck driver is not seriously injured)

  1. ①You will be sentenced to imprisonment for a limited term in the range of 1 year - 10 years.
  2. ②You will be sentenced to imprisonment for a limited term in the range of 1 year - 20 years.
  3. ③You will be sentenced to imprisonment for a limited term in the range of 2 years - 10 years.
  4. ④You will be sentenced to imprisonment for a limited term in the range of 2 years - 20 years.
Show answer

Answer
① You will be sentenced to imprisonment for a limited term in the range of 1 year - 10 years.

Reference
The punishment for injuring someone through retaliatory driving is a violation of Article 258-2 (Special Injury) Paragraph 1 of the Criminal Act and shall be imprisonment for not less than 1 year but not more than 10 years.
