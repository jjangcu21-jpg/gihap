182. Which of the following is an INCORRECT method of crossing the road for a pedestrian?

  1. ①Pedestrians must take the shortest route when crossing a road without a crosswalk.
  2. ②Pedestrians must not cross the road around the front or rear of a vehicle.
  3. ③Pedestrians can climb over a lane partition placed to prevent illegal crossing on the road.
  4. ④Pedestrians can walk on the road if the sidewalk is closed for construction.
Show answer

Answer
③ Pedestrians can climb over a lane partition placed to prevent illegal crossing on the road.

Reference
Article 8 of the Road Traffic Act (Pedestrian Passage) ① Pedestrians must always use the sidewalk on roads where sidewalks and roadways are separated. However, this does not apply when crossing a road, when traffic on the sidewalk is prohibited due to road construction, etc., or in other unavoidable cases. Road Traffic Act Article 10 (Road Crossing) ② Pedestrians must cross the road where a crosswalk, underpass, overpass or other road crossing facility is installed pursuant to paragraph (1). However, in the case of persons with physical disabilities who cannot use road crossing facilities such as underpasses or overpasses, they may cross the road without using road crossing facilities in a way that does not interfere with other traffic. ③ Pedestrians must cross the road at the shortest distance on a road where a crosswalk pursuant to paragraph (1) is not installed. ④ Pedestrians must not cross directly in front or behind cars and streetcars. However, this does not apply when crossing a crosswalk or crossing a road according to signals or instructions from traffic lights or police officials.
