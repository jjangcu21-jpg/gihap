271. Which TWO are considered emergency vehicles?

  1. ①A vehicle being guided by a police car for emergency purposes
  2. ②An investigation vehicle used for non-investigatory purposes
  3. ③A tow truck returning after completing a rescue activity
  4. ④A vehicle transporting patients who are injured or in critical condition, or transporting blood for transfusion
Show answer

Answer
① A vehicle being guided by a police car for emergency purposes
④ A vehicle transporting patients who are injured or in critical condition, or transporting blood for transfusion

Reference
Article 2 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act (Types of Emergency Vehicles) ① In subparagraph 22 (d) of Article 2 of the Road Traffic Act (hereinafter referred to as the “Act”), “vehicle prescribed by Presidential Decree” means a vehicle that falls under any of the following items and is used for emergency purposes. However, vehicles falling under Nos. 6 through 11 are limited to those designated by the city/provincial police chief upon application by the person or institution using them. 1. Among police automobiles, automobiles used for criminal investigation, traffic control, and other urgent police duties. 2. Among automobiles for the ROK Armed Forces and the United Nations Forces in Korea, automobiles used to maintain order within the military or to encourage the orderly movement of units. 3. Among automobiles of investigative agencies, automobiles used for criminal investigation. 4. Among automobiles of facilities or institutions that fall under any of the following items, automobiles used for apprehending fugitives or escorting and guarding detainees or probationers a. Prison, juvenile prison or detention center B. A juvenile detention center or juvenile classification review center. Probation office 5. Automobiles used for official work to perform security duties for domestic and foreign dignitaries 6. Automobiles used for emergency work to prevent danger in organizations engaged in electric, gas, and other public services 7. Automobiles used for emergency prevention or recovery in organizations performing civil defense work 8. Automobiles used for road management, used for emergency operations to prevent hazards on the road or Cars used to crack down on cars whose operation is restricted 9. Cars used for emergency work such as repair work for telegraphs and telephones 10. Cars used for transporting urgent mail 11. Cars used for radio wave surveillance ② Cars that fall under any of the following items in addition to the cars specified in each item of paragraph 1 are considered emergency cars. 1. A vehicle being escorted by a police emergency vehicle under paragraph 1, item 1. 2. A ROK Armed Forces or United Nations Forces in Korea vehicle being escorted by an emergency vehicle of those forces under paragraph 1, item 2. 3. A car transporting patients or injured people whose lives are in danger or blood for transfusion.
