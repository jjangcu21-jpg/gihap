287. A vehicle just hit a child who suddenly emerged from an alleyway. The child appeared unharmed and claimed that he was fine. Which of the following is the correct action taken by the driver?

  1. ①The driver can leave since this is a crime that is not prosecuted against objection.
  2. ②The driver can leave since the child was not hurt, so this is not considered an accident.
  3. ③The driver must call the parents of the child and take other necessary measures before leaving the scene.
  4. ④Since the fault lies with the child, the driver can just take the child's contact information and leave.
Show answer

Answer
③ The driver must call the parents of the child and take other necessary measures before leaving the scene.

Reference
A driver who injures a child in a traffic accident must take necessary measures, including contacting the parents.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
