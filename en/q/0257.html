257. In order for fire trucks and ambulances to be granted a special privilege to overtake other vehicles or drive at a speed exceeding the limit in a no-passing zone, which of the following is the measure that the drivers of those vehicles should take?

  1. ①Drive while honking.
  2. ②Equip the cars in a manner required for safe driving according to the Motor Vehicle Management Act and turn on the sirens or flashing lights.
  3. ③Drive with the headlights on.
  4. ④Those vehicles can be granted such privileges without taking additional measures.
Show answer

Answer
② Equip the cars in a manner required for safe driving according to the Motor Vehicle Management Act and turn on the sirens or flashing lights.

Reference
In order for an emergency vehicle to receive special treatment, it must have a structure necessary for safe operation of the vehicle in accordance with the Automobile Management Act and must sound a siren or turn on warning lights.
