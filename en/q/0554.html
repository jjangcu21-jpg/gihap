554. According to the Road Traffic Act and its Enforcement Decree, which of the following are considered TWO violations of the legal speed limit by passenger cars?

  1. ①Driving at a speed of 85 km/h on a general road with two lanes in one direction.
  2. ②Driving at a speed of 90 km/h on Seohaean Expressway.
  3. ③Driving at a speed of 95 km/h on an automobile road.
  4. ④Driving at a speed of 75 km/h on an expressway with one lane in one direction.
Show answer

Answer
① Driving at a speed of 85 km/h on a general road with two lanes in one direction.
③ Driving at a speed of 95 km/h on an automobile road.

Reference
The maximum speed limit is 80 kilometers per hour on ordinary roads with two lanes in each direction, 90 kilometers per hour on automobile-only roads, 110 kilometers per hour on the West Coast Expressway, and 80 kilometers per hour on expressways with one lane in each direction. (Article 19 of the Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules)
