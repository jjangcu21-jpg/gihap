468. Which of the following is the best method of preventing the risk of collision caused by the sudden braking of a vehicle ahead?

  1. ①Step on the brake pedal to slow down.
  2. ②Turn the steering wheel sharply to the left and drive over the centerline to avoid hitting the vehicle ahead.
  3. ③Close your eyes to minimize the impact.
  4. ④Turn on both the wipers and the high-beam headlights.
Show answer

Answer
① Step on the brake pedal to slow down.

Reference
Ensure sufficient safety distance to prevent a collision with the car in front, and be prepared to brake at any time in case of danger. In case of an unavoidable collision, press the brake pedal hard to slow down to minimize damage. If you turn the steering wheel sharply to the left and cross the center line, an accident with a vehicle traveling on the opposite side may occur. Also, closing your eyes and operating the wipers and high beams has nothing to do with reducing the damage from a collision.
