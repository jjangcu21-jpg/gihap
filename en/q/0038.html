38. What is the standard for penalties imposed on a driver who knowingly uses a fake petroleum product as vehicle fuel?

  1. ①An administrative fine between KRW 50,000 and 100,000
  2. ②An administrative fine between KRW 500,000 and 1 million
  3. ③An administrative fine between KRW 2 million and 20 million
  4. ④No penalties
Show answer

Answer
③ An administrative fine between KRW 2 million and 20 million

Reference
Petroleum and Petroleum Substitute Fuel Business Act Enforcement Decree Annex 6 Fine Enforcement Standards If you use a petroleum product as a vehicle fuel knowing that it is a fake petroleum product, a fine ranging from KRW 2 million to KRW 20 million may be imposed depending on the amount used.
