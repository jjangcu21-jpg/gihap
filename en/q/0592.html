592. Which of the following correctly describes how an air-conditioner in a vehicle should be used and maintained?

  1. ①Turn on the air-conditioner to full and then turn it down to a lower level.
  2. ②Replace the coolant every six months.
  3. ③The ideal temperature setting for an air-conditioner is 16°C.
  4. ④Operate the air-conditioner in outer air inflow mode for greater cooling efficiency.
Show answer

Answer
① Turn on the air-conditioner to full and then turn it down to a lower level.

Reference
Air conditioner use is related to fuel consumption efficiency, and air conditioner refrigerant is an environmental pollutant that destroys the ozone layer, so it is desirable to reduce its use or use it efficiently as much as possible.
