811. You are entering an intersection and trying to go straight. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive? (Select TWO)

  1. ①The black car may reverse, so slow down, and be prepared.
  2. ②Be careful as the two-wheeled vehicle may change lanes on the left of the black car.
  3. ③Use the empty straight lane in the opposite direction to go straight, and then return to your original lane.
  4. ④Accelerate and pass the stopped black car in a straight line.
  5. ⑤Accelerate and pass the two-wheeled vehicle so that it cannot change lanes in front of you.
Show answer

Answer
① The black car may reverse, so slow down, and be prepared.
② Be careful as the two-wheeled vehicle may change lanes on the left of the black car.

Reference
In the situation presented, the black passenger car is in violation of Article 18 of the Road Traffic Act, prohibiting U-turns. Since the black passenger car's retreat lights are on, it is possible to predict its retreat behavior to secure the turning radius. Therefore, when a driver spots a car with the reverse lights on, he or she must be cautious and slow down or stop. A driver of a two-wheeled vehicle traveling in the right lane (second lane) is likely to change course to the left if he or she judges that the traffic ahead is obstructed. In this case, it is necessary to pay special attention to the two-wheeled vehicle on the right.
