657. Which of the following correctly describes a rule that should be followed by the driver of a trailer?

  1. ①Routine inspection of the vehicle must take place only at the end of a shift.
  2. ②Transport of cargo must not be refused unless there is a valid reason.
  3. ③The vehicle should be kept clean only when carrying high-priced cargo.
  4. ④Be sure to check the placement of covers and packaging used to hold cargo in place when the destination is reached.
Show answer

Answer
② Transport of cargo must not be refused unless there is a valid reason.

Reference
Before driving, drivers must secure covers and packaging to prevent cargo from leaving, keep it clean at all times, and regularly inspect and confirm the vehicle before and after driving.
