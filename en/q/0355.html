355. Which TWO constitute stopping?

  1. ①A taxi stopping at a taxi stand for the driver to go to the restroom
  2. ②A vehicle that is left unattended by its driver who is away to pick up a load to transport
  3. ③The vehicle stops while waiting for a traffic signal.
  4. ④The driver stops the car to ask a passerby for directions.
Show answer

Answer
③ The vehicle stops while waiting for a traffic signal.
④ The driver stops the car to ask a passerby for directions.

Reference
Stopping refers to a state other than parking where the driver stops the car for no more than 5 minutes. (Road Traffic Act, Article 2, Paragraph 25)
