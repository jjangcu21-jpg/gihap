822. Which TWO are most likely to lead to an accident in the given situation?

  1. ①A car driving ahead
  2. ②An electric scooter suddenly turning left to enter the road
  3. ③A truck preparing to move from the right-hand side
  4. ④A person working on the right-hand side of the road
  5. ⑤A motorcycle ahead on the left-hand side
Show answer

Answer
② An electric scooter suddenly turning left to enter the road
③ A truck preparing to move from the right-hand side

Reference
During the busy farming season, the movement of agricultural machinery, vehicles, and pedestrians becomes more active on suburban roads. In particular, on roads connected to farm road entrances, there is a high possibility of unexpected entry or unexpected behavior that does not comply with traffic laws, so it is necessary to look ahead and drive defensively.
