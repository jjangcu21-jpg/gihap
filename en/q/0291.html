291. Below are descriptions of a thoroughfare of bicycles. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is INCORRECT?

  1. ①The rider of a bicycle must pass along a bicycle track at a location with a separate bicycle track.
  2. ②The rider of a bicycle must pass along close to the right side of the road at a location where no bicycle track is constructed.
  3. ③The rider of a bicycle may pass along the roadside area (excluding a section where thoroughfare for bicycles is prohibited with safety markings).
  4. ④A rider of a bicycle who intends to cross the road at a crosswalk may do so while riding the bicycle.
Show answer

Answer
④ A rider of a bicycle who intends to cross the road at a crosswalk may do so while riding the bicycle.

Reference
Article 13-2 of the Road Traffic Act (Special provisions for bicycle passage methods)
