393. Which of the following instructions about an intersection with an unprotected left-turn sign is correct?

  1. ①You can turn left regardless of the signal as long as you look out for traffic.
  2. ②You can turn left at a red light while looking out for traffic.
  3. ③You can turn left at a green light while looking out for traffic.
  4. ④You can turn left at a yellow light while looking out for traffic.
Show answer

Answer
③ You can turn left at a green light while looking out for traffic.

Reference
In places where there is an unprotected left turn sign, you can turn left while paying attention to other traffic while the green light is on. If you are turning left at a green light and collide with a vehicle going straight on the opposite side, the vehicle turning left becomes the at-fault party in an ordinary-negligence traffic accident.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
