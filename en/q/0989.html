989. The driver is approaching and passing through an intersection. Which of the following is a violation of the Road Traffic Act? (Refer to the website) (Promotional issue_Passing through roundabout)

Video questions play in the Gihap app or on the official site.

Official video ↗
  1. ①Upon exiting, the driver turned on the correct turn signal.
  2. ②Upon entering, the driver turned on the correct turn signal.
  3. ③Upon exiting, the driver proceeded from the inner lane without changing lanes within the intersection.
  4. ④Upon entering, the driver immediately entered the inner lane without changing lanes within the intersection.
Show answer

Answer
② Upon entering, the driver turned on the correct turn signal.

Reference
The intersection in the video is a type of roundabout that enters into two lanes. In accordance with Article 25-2 of the Road Traffic Act, when entering or exiting a roundabout, you must activate the direction indicator in advance. The lights that must be turned on at this time must be on the left side when entering and the right side when exiting in accordance with the attached table of the Road Traffic Act. When entering a roundabout with two lanes, according to the road markings, you must turn from the left lane (lane 1) into the inner lane inside the intersection, and from the right lane (second lane) into the outer lane inside the intersection.
