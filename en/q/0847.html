847. Which of the following are the TWO safest ways to proceed in the given situation?

  1. ①Make a right turn since there is no risk of collision with the children on the crosswalk.
  2. ②Be prepared for children who may dart out onto the crosswalk unexpectedly.
  3. ③Turn right only after the children have finished crossing.
  4. ④Honk to hurry the children crossing the street.
  5. ⑤Stop on the crosswalk to prevent other children from entering the crosswalk.
Show answer

Answer
② Be prepared for children who may dart out onto the crosswalk unexpectedly.
③ Turn right only after the children have finished crossing.

Reference
Even when the crosswalk signal is almost over, pedestrians, especially children, sometimes change direction and go back. Also, since there are pedestrians who forcefully try to cross while the signal is flashing, it is safe to proceed after checking for the presence of pedestrians even if the vehicle signal is green. When facing a crosswalk after turning right at an intersection, if there are pedestrians, you must stop temporarily and pass after the pedestrians have finished crossing (Article 27 of the Road Traffic Act).
