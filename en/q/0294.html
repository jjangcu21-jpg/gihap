294. Which of the following are TWO correct statements regarding the registration of school buses for children?

  1. ①To operate a school bus, one must report to the Korea Road Traffic Authority beforehand and receive a report certificate.
  2. ②A school bus for children must be a vehicle with maximum passenger capacity of nine or more (1 child is considered 1 passenger).
  3. ③If applying to renew the registration of a school bus for children because the old one needs to be replaced, one must submit the old registration together with the renewal form.
  4. ④The registration certificate of a school bus for children must be attached in a place where it will be easily visible on the top left side of the windows.
Show answer

Answer
② A school bus for children must be a vehicle with maximum passenger capacity of nine or more (1 child is considered 1 passenger).
③ If applying to renew the registration of a school bus for children because the old one needs to be replaced, one must submit the old registration together with the renewal form.

Reference
Article 52 of the Road Traffic Act (Reporting of children's school buses, etc.) ① Anyone who wishes to operate a children's school bus must report it to the chief of the local police station in advance and obtain a report certificate. Article 35 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act (Reporting Procedures for Children's School Buses, etc.) ③ The children's school bus reporting certificate must be affixed to an easily visible location in the upper right corner of the front window glass of the vehicle. ④ When reapplying because the children's school bus report certificate is worn out and unusable, the application for reissuance of the children's school bus report certificate must be attached and submitted with the report certificate that the children's school bus report certificate has become worn out and unusable.
