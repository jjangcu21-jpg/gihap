486. Which of the following is NOT appropriate for preventing road kill at night?

  1. ①Reduce speed when traveling on national or mountain roads with fewer vehicles or people.
  2. ②If you spot an animal on the road, slow down or stop on the shoulder to avoid a collision.
  3. ③If you spot an animal on the road, turn off the headlights and honk lightly to scare it away.
  4. ④To spot animals on the road more easily, drive closer along the shoulder.
Show answer

Answer
④ To spot animals on the road more easily, drive closer along the shoulder.

Reference
The risk of a roadkill accident is when an animal suddenly appears and you cannot respond, so it is best to drive at a reduced speed on roads where the animal is likely to appear.
