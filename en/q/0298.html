298. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is correct regarding school zones?

  1. ①If a car accident occurs while a child is jaywalking, the driver is exempted from all liability.
  2. ②If a cyclist hits a child while driving, only civil liability exists as a bicycle is not a vehicle.
  3. ③Upon seeing a child suddenly running onto the road, stop the vehicle instead of reducing the speed.
  4. ④The chief of a police station may set the speed limit of a vehicle at 50km or less per hour.
Show answer

Answer
③ Upon seeing a child suddenly running onto the road, stop the vehicle instead of reducing the speed.

Reference
You must stop temporarily, and securing the safety of pedestrians is a priority.
