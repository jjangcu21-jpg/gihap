129. Which of the following are true about drowsy and/or fatigued driving? (Select TWO)

  1. ①If drowsy while driving, you must consume more caffeine and change lanes more often to avoid monotonous driving.
  2. ②Your alertness improves on roads with fewer variations and potential hazards, thereby reducing the risk of drowsy driving.
  3. ③Avoid driving if you have taken cold medicines since they may cause drowsiness.
  4. ④Alcohol deactivates certain cerebral functions and increases the chance of drowsiness.
Show answer

Answer
③ Avoid driving if you have taken cold medicines since they may cause drowsiness.
④ Alcohol deactivates certain cerebral functions and increases the chance of drowsiness.

Reference
Driving on highways, where changes in the traffic environment are monotonous, results in less alertness than driving on city roads or general roads, and drowsy driving behavior increases regardless of sleep deprivation. Additionally, if you drink and drive, your cerebral function slows down, increasing the possibility of drowsy driving. In particular, cold medicine is not a prohibited drug under the Road Traffic Act, but it may contain ingredients that cause drowsiness, so be careful when driving after taking it. If you must drive, you need to ask a pharmacist about the ingredients before taking it.
