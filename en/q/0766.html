766. Which of the following descriptions regarding the exclusive use areas for fire engines under the Framework Act on Firefighting Services are incorrect? (Select TWO)

  1. ①They are installed in apartment buildings to facilitate firefighting operations.
  2. ②No one should park a vehicle in the designated area or block access to it in any way.
  3. ③The owner of an apartment building must install at least one in each building, without exception.
  4. ④Parking as shown in the photo is subject to an administrative fine.
  5. ⑤Removing or damaging the "exclusive use area" sign does not constitute obstruction of the exclusive use area.
Show answer

Answer
③ The owner of an apartment building must install at least one in each building, without exception.
⑤ Removing or damaging the "exclusive use area" sign does not constitute obstruction of the exclusive use area.

Reference
ⓛ,② Article 21-2 of the Framework Act on Fire Services (exclusive areas for fire trucks, etc.) ③ Article 7-13 of the Enforcement Decree of the Framework Act on Fire Services (standards and methods for installing fire vehicles-only zones) ④ Article 19 of the Enforcement Decree of the Framework Act on Fire Services (standards for imposition of fines) Annex 3 ⑤ Article 7-14 of the Enforcement Decree of the Framework Act on Fire Services (standards for sabotage of exclusive zones)
