793. Which of the following are the desirable ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①Slow down and keep a safe distance as the school bus may switch to the right lane.
  2. ②The school bus is slowing down, so drive safely to overtake the bus.
  3. ③Keep an eye on the electric kick scooter in lane 3 and switch to the right lane to overtake the school bus.
  4. ④Because you cannot check the front of the school bus, stay in the lane and reduce speed to maintain a safe distance.
  5. ⑤Honk at the school bus driver to inform him/her that the bus is violating the minimum speed limit.
Show answer

Answer
① Slow down and keep a safe distance as the school bus may switch to the right lane.
④ Because you cannot check the front of the school bus, stay in the lane and reduce speed to maintain a safe distance.

Reference
Article 51 of the Road Traffic Act (Special Protection of Children's School Buses) ① When a children's school bus is stopped on the road and devices such as flashing lights are operating to indicate that children or infants are getting on or off, the driver of a car traveling in the lane where the children's school bus is stopped and the lane immediately adjacent to the lane must stop temporarily before reaching the children's school bus, check for safety, and then drive slowly. ② In the case of paragraph 1, on roads without a center line or roads with one lane in each direction, drivers of cars traveling in the opposite direction must also stop temporarily and check for safety before reaching the children's school bus and then drive slowly. ③ Drivers of any vehicle may not overtake a children's school bus traveling on the road with a sign indicating that it is carrying children or infants. Rules on performance and standards of automobiles and automobile parts. Article 48 (Other standards for lighting) Paragraph 4. Vans for transporting children must be equipped with indicator lights that meet the following standards. No. 5. When attempting to stop or start on the road, the following standards must be met. When stopping on the road, the driver must be able to operate the yellow or amber light to flash. Because the yellow flashing lights of the children's school bus are in operation, the children's school bus may be in the process of stopping at the right edge of the road. Therefore, the speed of children's school buses may decrease sharply, contrary to expectations. Also, because forward visibility is limited due to the height of the children's school bus, you should refrain from predicting that the traffic situation ahead will be safe.
