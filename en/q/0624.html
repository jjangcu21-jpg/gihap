624. Which of the following statements regarding blind spots and load change when braking a large truck is correct?

  1. ①The blind spots are not much different in the case of a vehicle with a hood and a vehicle without it.
  2. ②The braking force applied to the front and rear wheels is not influenced by change of load.
  3. ③The blind spot on the left side of the driver is much larger than the one on the right in the case of a large truck.
  4. ④The braking power of a large truck will vary depending on the cargo load.
Show answer

Answer
④ The braking power of a large truck will vary depending on the cargo load.

Reference
The blind spot of a large truck varies greatly depending on the presence or absence of a bonnet, and changes in load affect the braking force of the front and rear wheels, and the blind spot on the right side of the driver's seat is much wider than the left side of the driver's seat.

Reference text is a machine translation for study.
