155. Which of the following is NOT a desirable driving habit?

  1. ①Turn off the engine while refueling.
  2. ②Unless there is an urgent situation, do not apply the brake suddenly, instigating other vehicles to do the same.
  3. ③To predict potential hazards and practice defensive driving, observe the speed limit and keep a safe distance.
  4. ④To ensure driving stability, tire pressure should be 10% higher than the recommended standard regardless of the season.
Show answer

Answer
④ To ensure driving stability, tire pressure should be 10% higher than the recommended standard regardless of the season.

Reference
The appropriate tire pressure is 80% of the maximum pressure. Depending on the season, it is helpful for safety to inflate 10% lower in summer and 10% higher in winter.
