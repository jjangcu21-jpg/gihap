144. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following cannot be construed as reckless driving for a motorcycle (excluding personal mobility devices)?

  1. ①The driver repeatedly ignored traffic signals three times.
  2. ②The driver committed successive violations of exceeding the speed limit and disobeying instructions.
  3. ③The driver committed successive violations of ignoring traffic signals and crossing the centerline.
  4. ④The driver committed successive violations of crossing the centerline as well as the duty to protect pedestrians.
Show answer

Answer
④ The driver committed successive violations of crossing the centerline as well as the duty to protect pedestrians.

Reference
Article 46-3 of the Road Traffic Act: Violating signals or instructions, violating the center line, speeding, violating the prohibition on crossing, U-turn, and reversing, failing to secure a safety distance, violating the prohibition on changing course, violating the prohibition on sudden braking, violating overtaking rules or the prohibition on obstructing overtaking, making noise without justifiable reasons, violating the overtaking method on the highway, violating the prohibition on crossing, U-turn, and reversing on the highway, etc., or committing two or more acts in succession. You must not continue or repeat an act to threaten or harm others or create traffic hazards. Violation of pedestrian protection obligations is not included in the act of reckless driving.
