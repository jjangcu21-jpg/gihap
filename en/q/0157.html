157. According to the Road Traffic Act, which of the following is NOT true about a pedestrian?

  1. ①A person using a handcart (not motorized) that is 1 m or less in width is not considered a pedestrian.
  2. ②A person using a rollator that is 1 m or less in width is considered a pedestrian.
  3. ③A person riding a bicycle is not considered a pedestrian.
  4. ④A person using a walking frame that is 1 m or less in width is considered a pedestrian.
Show answer

Answer
① A person using a handcart (not motorized) that is 1 m or less in width is not considered a pedestrian.

Reference
“Sidewalk” refers to a portion of a road where the boundary is marked with a curb line, safety sign, or similar artificial structure so that pedestrians (including people using devices or devices prescribed by Ordinance of the Ministry of Public Administration and Security, such as strollers, wheelchairs for walking assistance, and walkers for the elderly, and outdoor mobile robots under item 21-3; hereinafter the same shall apply) can pass. (Road Traffic Act, Article 2, Subparagraph 10) Instruments and devices prescribed by Ordinance of the Ministry of Public Administration and Security with a width of 1 meter or less. Strollers, wheelchairs for walking assistance, walkers for the elderly, children's play equipment, non-powered carts, two-wheeled vehicles, etc. that are pulled or carried by the driver, devices used for road maintenance, etc., outdoor mobile robots under item 21-3, etc. (Article 2 of the Enforcement Rules of the Road Traffic Act)
