201. Which of the following is the correct method of passing through a crosswalk for a vehicle if traffic signals for both vehicles and pedestrians are out of order?

  1. ①If a pedestrian is crossing, pass through at a slow speed.
  2. ②If there are no pedestrians on the crosswalk, do not slow down but be sure to pass through quickly.
  3. ③The driver need not drive slowly, as the crosswalk has lost its function due to the breakdown of the signal lights.
  4. ④If a pedestrian is crossing, make a temporary stop just in front of the crosswalk.
Show answer

Answer
④ If a pedestrian is crossing, make a temporary stop just in front of the crosswalk.

Reference
<Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act, Article 27, Paragraph 1> When pedestrians (including drivers of bicycles, etc. who dismount from bicycles, pull or carry bicycles, etc. pursuant to Article 13-2, Paragraph 6) are passing or are about to cross a crosswalk, the driver of any car or tram must temporarily stop in front of the crosswalk (referring to the stop line where a stop line is installed) so as not to interfere with or endanger the crossing of pedestrians.
