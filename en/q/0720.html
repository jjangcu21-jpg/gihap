720. Which of the following are the safest ways to drive in the given situation? (Select TWO)

  1. ①The pedestrian is trying to walk across the roadway, so stop temporarily before proceeding to ensure the pedestrian's safety.
  2. ②Speed up as there is no obligation to protect pedestrians in an unlaned road.
  3. ③If there are vehicles following, turn on the hazard lights to alert them of the dangerous situation.
  4. ④Honk repeatedly to prevent the pedestrian from crossing the road.
  5. ⑤Make a sudden stop in front of the pedestrian to alert him/her.
Show answer

Answer
① The pedestrian is trying to walk across the roadway, so stop temporarily before proceeding to ensure the pedestrian's safety.
③ If there are vehicles following, turn on the hazard lights to alert them of the dangerous situation.

Reference
If a pedestrian is walking on a side road without a center line, the driver must leave a safe distance and slow down or stop temporarily to allow the pedestrian to walk safely. Additionally, it is safe to turn on the emergency flashers to alert vehicles behind of danger.
