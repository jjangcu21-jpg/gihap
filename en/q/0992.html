992. Which of the following should you pay attention to the most when anticipating danger on a back road? (Refer to the website)

Official video ↗
  1. ①Children who might suddenly come out from between stopped cars
  2. ②Left turn of the blue truck as seen in the rear-view mirror
  3. ③Right turn of the cyclist on the right
  4. ④Sudden braking of the car ahead
Show answer

Answer
① Children who might suddenly come out from between stopped cars

Reference
Article 27 of the Road Traffic Act (Pedestrian Protection), Article 49 of the Road Traffic Act (Matters to be observed by all drivers, etc.) When passing on a side road, you must be careful of pedestrians who may suddenly come out between cars. Side roads often do not have center lines, so you must be careful of cars suddenly changing lanes and entering at any time without turning on the left turn signal. When turning right or left, you must pay attention to risk factors, such as not being able to see surrounding vehicles or pedestrians due to blind spots in the car body pillars.
