521. Which of the following is the correct statement regarding the demerit points applied to a driver who causes an accident?

  1. ①When a traffic accident involving one death (within 72 hours) occurs due to the violation of a signal, a penalty of 105 demerit points is incurred.
  2. ②Demerit points are also applied for losses suffered by passengers of the victim's vehicle and the driver of the offending vehicle.
  3. ③Demerit points are calculated based on the cause of the accident, casualties if any, and damage to property.
  4. ④If the accident involves two vehicles, no demerit points are applied if both vehicles are responsible for the accident.
Show answer

Answer
① When a traffic accident involving one death (within 72 hours) occurs due to the violation of a signal, a penalty of 105 demerit points is incurred.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules Annex 28-3 Individual standards for suspension disposition
