418. Which of the following is the most inappropriate way to respond when your car has been flooded during localized heavy rainfall?

  1. ①Try to escape by opening the door on the opposite side of the rushing currents.
  2. ②If the car door won't open, use a sharp object (such as headrest, seatbelt lock, etc.) to strike the edge of the window to break it and attempt to escape.
  3. ③If you cannot break the window, do not panic; call 911 and quickly open the door to escape when the water levels inside and outside of the vehicle become the same (less than 30cm).
  4. ④Once you have escaped, take shelter in the lowest ground or hide under the vehicle.
Show answer

Answer
④ Once you have escaped, take shelter in the lowest ground or hide under the vehicle.

Reference
National Disaster and Safety Portal, Ministry of the Interior and Safety — public guidance: Move the vehicle to a safe place before water reaches two-thirds of the tyre height. If the vehicle is flooded, use the metal rods of the driver’s headrest to break a window and escape. If the window cannot be broken, wait until the difference between the water levels inside and outside is 30 cm or less, then escape quickly as soon as the door opens. With rainfall of 100 mm per hour, road signs at distances of 100 metres or more cannot be distinguished; move the vehicle to a safe place and wait for the rain to ease. Never enter an underpass where water is beginning to accumulate; if already inside, leave the vehicle and evacuate quickly. Do not enter a bridge when water is flowing over it; take a detour or wait somewhere safe. If trapped, open the door on the side opposite the current, or break a window to escape. Vehicle window glass has some elasticity, so striking the center requires great force; strike a corner instead (Korea Transportation Safety Authority official blog). After escaping a flooded vehicle, move to ground above the water level or climb onto the roof and wait for rescue (National Fire Agency official blog).
