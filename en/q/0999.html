999. Which of the following vehicles is driving in compliance with the road traffic regulations to protect pedestrians traversing a crosswalk without traffic signals? (Refer to the website)

  3. ③The brown car
  4. ④The red car
Show answer

Answer
① The white car

Reference
Road Traffic Act Article 27 (Pedestrian Protection) At crosswalks without traffic lights, you must be careful of vehicles passing between pedestrians even though there are pedestrians. Be careful because pedestrians may not be noticed due to cars parked illegally in the crosswalk. Be careful of pedestrians who suddenly start running and cross the street. Watch for pedestrians who cross assuming that vehicles will stop for them.
