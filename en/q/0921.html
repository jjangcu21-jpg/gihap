Road signs · 2 pts

921. Which of the following is true about using an intersection with this road sign?

  1. ①A truck apron cannot be situated on the sides of a traffic island.
  2. ②You must always turn on the turn signal when entering and leaving the intersection.
  3. ③You must turn on the turn signal when entering the intersection but not necessarily when leaving the intersection.
  4. ④A vehicle entering the intersection has the right of way to vehicles already making a turn.
Show answer

Answer
② You must always turn on the turn signal when entering and leaving the intersection.

Reference
Road Traffic Act Enforcement Rules, Appendix 6, instruction sign 304: Roundabout. When entering, yield to vehicles circulating counterclockwise. Use turn signals when entering and exiting. A truck apron around the central island allows large vehicles and semi-trailers to drive over it. Roundabouts and traffic circles are installed and operated according to the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport’s road structure/facility standards commentary and Roundabout Design Guidelines. Comparison from those guidelines: (1) Priority: at roundabouts, entering vehicles yield to circulating vehicles; at traffic circles, circulating vehicles yield. (2) Entry: roundabouts guide vehicles to enter slowly; traffic circles permit high-speed entry. (3) Circulating roadway: roundabouts are designed to discourage high speeds and avoid large turning radii; traffic circles have large circulating areas with high-speed travel. (4) Splitter islands: required at roundabouts to slow and separate traffic; optional at traffic circles. (5) Central island: roundabout islands are mostly no more than 50 m in diameter, and urban mini-roundabouts may have islands as small as 2 m; traffic circles have no diameter limit.
