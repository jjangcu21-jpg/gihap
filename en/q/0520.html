520. Which of the following is the correct pairing of traffic penalty and speed range imposed on the driver of an automobile caught speeding on an expressway?

  1. ①For exceeding the speed limit by over 60 km/h to 80 km/h: traffic penalty of KRW 120,000
  2. ②For exceeding the speed limit by over 40 to 60 km/h: traffic penalty of KRW 80,000
  3. ③For exceeding the speed limit by over 20 to 40 km/h: traffic penalty of KRW 50,000
  4. ④For exceeding the speed limit by less than 20 km/h: traffic penalty of KRW 20,000
Show answer

Answer
① For exceeding the speed limit by over 60 km/h to 80 km/h: traffic penalty of KRW 120,000

Reference
Appendix 8 of the Enforcement Decree of the Road Traffic Act
