436. Which of the following is an INCORRECT method of driving on snowy roads?

  1. ①Just clearing the windshield of piled up snow is enough for safe driving.
  2. ②The foot brake and engine brake must be used together.
  3. ③For safety, drive at 30km/h or below with snow chains on.
  4. ④Maintain a longer safe distance than usual.
Show answer

Answer
① Just clearing the windshield of piled up snow is enough for safe driving.

Reference
It is safe to drive after removing snow from all parts of the vehicle. In particular, there is a risk that snow on the front bonnet may block visibility, so it is more important to remove snow from the front before driving.
